# Mianx.ai Multi-Agent System Metrics

> **Metrics exist to improve governed decisions about Multi-Agent
> systems. They do not become decision authority merely because they
> are numerical.**
>
> Permanent measurement rule:
>
> ```text
> MEASUREMENT
> SIGNAL
> ≠
> ENTERPRISE
> TRUTH
> ```
>
> Therefore:
>
> ```text
> HIGH SCORE
> ≠
> HIGHER
> AUTHORITY
>
> HIGH SUCCESS RATE
> ≠
> PRODUCTION
> APPROVAL
>
> LOW INCIDENT RATE
> ≠
> SECURITY
> VERIFIED
>
> LOW COST
> ≠
> GOOD
> SYSTEM
>
> FAST LATENCY
> ≠
> CORRECT
> OUTCOME
>
> COMPLETION RATE
> ≠
> VERIFIED
> BUSINESS VALUE
> ```

# 2. Metrics Mission

The mission is:

> **Provide a consistent, auditable, scope-aware and evidence-bound
> method for observing whether Multi-Agent systems produce reliable
> enterprise value at acceptable quality, Security, cost, latency,
> resilience and governance levels without allowing dashboards,
> rankings, benchmarks or AI-generated analytics to become hidden
> authority or to overstate runtime maturity.**
