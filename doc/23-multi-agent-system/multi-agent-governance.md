# Mianx.ai Multi-Agent System Governance

> **This document defines the root Governance model for Mianx.ai
> Multi-Agent Teams and collective Agent behavior.**
>
> Its central rule is:
>
> ```text
> COLLECTIVE
> INTELLIGENCE
> MAY
> INCREASE
>
> WITHOUT
>
> COLLECTIVE
> AUTHORITY
> INCREASING
> IMPLICITLY.
> ```
>
> Multi-Agent Governance exists to ensure that:
>
> ```text
> WHO
> MAY PROPOSE
>
> WHO
> MAY RECOMMEND
>
> WHO
> MAY SELECT
>
> WHO
> MAY APPROVE
>
> WHO
> MAY EXECUTE
>
> WHO
> MAY VERIFY
>
> WHO
> MAY ACCEPT RISK
>
> WHO
> MAY CHANGE POLICY
>
> WHO
> MAY AUTHORIZE PRODUCTION
> ```
>
> remain explicit, separable, attributable and enforceable.

# 2. Governance Mission

The Governance mission is:

> **Ensure that Multi-Agent coordination creates enterprise capability
> without creating hidden authority, permission aggregation,
> untraceable delegation, ungoverned collective decisions, cross-scope
> data access, uncontrolled autonomy or Production actions outside
> explicitly authorized decision rights.**

enterprise authority where the organization has defined Founder
control.

```text

SYNTHESIZE

PREPARE
OPTIONS

PROVIDE
EVIDENCE
```

without becoming the Founder.
