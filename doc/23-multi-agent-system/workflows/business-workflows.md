# Mianx.ai Multi-Agent Business Workflows

> **Mianx.ai may automate and augment enterprise work.**
>
> It must not confuse process automation with business authority.
>
> Permanent:
>
> ```text
> BUSINESS
> WORKFLOW
> ≠
> BUSINESS
> AUTHORITY
> ```

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the governed architecture for business processes
performed with the participation of multiple Mianx.ai Agents and Teams.

The mission is:

> **Enable Mianx.ai to coordinate scalable AI-assisted enterprise
> processes while preserving real business ownership, human
