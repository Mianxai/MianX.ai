# Mianx.ai Multi-Agent Priority Management

> **Priority changes ordering. It does not change authority.**
>
> Permanent:
>
> ```text
> HIGHER
> PRIORITY
>
> MAY
> CHANGE
> WHEN
> WORK
> RUNS
>
> BUT
>
> MUST
> NOT
> CHANGE
> WHAT
> THE
> ACTOR
> IS
> AUTHORIZED
> TO
> DO
> ```

# 1. Purpose

This document defines governed Priority Management for Multi-Agent work
including:

```text
TASKS

TEAMS

PROJECTS

CUSTOMERS

TENANTS
```

Priority may influence ordering among eligible work.

It must never independently authorize execution.

---

# 2. Mission

The mission is:

> **Provide deterministic, attributable and fair ordering of
> already-governed Multi-Agent work while preventing urgency,
> deadlines, business value, executive labels, starvation or incidents
> from becoming privilege-escalation mechanisms.**
