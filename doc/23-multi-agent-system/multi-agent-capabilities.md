# Mianx.ai Multi-Agent System Capabilities

> **This document defines what a governed Mianx.ai Multi-Agent System
> may collectively be capable of doing.**
>
> It does not define collective authority.
>
> Permanent rule:
>
> ```text
> COLLECTIVE
> CAPABILITY
> ≠
> COLLECTIVE
> AUTHORITY
> ```
>
> Therefore:
>
> ```text
> TEAM
> CAN
> PRODUCE
> OUTCOME X
>
> ≠
>
> EVERY
> PARTICIPANT
> MAY
> PERFORM
> EVERY ACTION
> REQUIRED
> FOR X.
> ```

---

# 2. Capability Mission

The mission is:

> **Define the bounded collective behaviors and outcomes that multiple
> individually governed Mianx.ai Agents may provide together, while
> ensuring that every collective capability remains reducible to
> individually governed participants, explicit Task and scope context,
> action-specific authorization, controlled information exchange,
> Evidence, Audit and applicable Human or Founder governance.**
