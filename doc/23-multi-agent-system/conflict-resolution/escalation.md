# Mianx.ai Multi-Agent Conflict Escalation

> **Escalation transfers a decision need to an appropriate authority
> domain.**
>
> It does not transfer or create the authority itself.
>
> Permanent:
>
> ```text
> ESCALATION
> =
> GOVERNED
> REQUEST
> FOR
> DECISION /
> INTERVENTION
>
> NOT
>
> AUTHORITY
> CREATION
> ```

# 2. Escalation Mission

The mission is:

> **Route unresolved or authority-sensitive conditions to the smallest
> correct decision domain with sufficient context, Evidence and
> accountability while preventing urgency, severity, seniority,
> fallback or timeout from becoming implicit authorization.**
