# Mianx.ai Multi-Agent Conflict Resolution

> **Conflict Resolution selects and applies an authorized way to address
> a validated conflict.**
>
> It does not create the authority required to make that decision.
>
> Permanent:
>
> ```text
> RESOLUTION
> MECHANISM
>
> ≠
>
> DECISION
> AUTHORITY
> ```

---

```text
AGENTS
```

# 2. Conflict Resolution Mission

The mission is:

> **Resolve valid Multi-Agent conflicts through the smallest authorized
> decision domain and safest compatible mechanism while preserving
> policy, Security, scope, dissent, Evidence, reversibility and
> accountability.**
