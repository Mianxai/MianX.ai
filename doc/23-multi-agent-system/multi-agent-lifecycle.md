# Mianx.ai Multi-Agent System Lifecycle

> **This document defines how a Multi-Agent Team or collective
> execution arrangement moves through governed lifecycle states from
> definition through formation, operation, reconfiguration and eventual
> dissolution or retirement.**
>
> The lifecycle never creates authority merely because a Team advances
> to a later state.
>
> Permanent rule:
>
> ```text
> LIFECYCLE
> PROGRESSION
> ≠
> AUTHORITY
> PROGRESSION
> ```
>
> Therefore:
>
> ```text
> DEFINED
> ≠
> AUTHORIZED
>
> FORMED
> ≠
> READY
>
> READY
> ≠
> TASK AUTHORIZED
>
> ACTIVE
> ≠
> UNRESTRICTED
> AUTONOMY
>
> VERIFIED
> ≠
> PRODUCTION
> AUTHORIZED
> ```

# 2. Lifecycle Mission

The lifecycle mission is:

> **Ensure that every material stage of Multi-Agent Team existence and
> operation is explicit, versioned where required, scoped, attributable,
> reviewable, revocable and auditable, with security and authorization
> revalidated whenever material Team state changes.**
