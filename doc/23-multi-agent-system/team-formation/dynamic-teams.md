# Mianx.ai Multi-Agent Dynamic Teams

> **A Dynamic Team is a bounded collaboration structure composed of
> independently governed Agents.**
>
> A Team does not merge identities, permissions, credentials or Tenant
> authority.
>
> Permanent:
>
> ```text
> MANY
> AGENTS
>
> WORKING
> AS
> ONE
> TEAM
>
> ≠
>
> MANY
> PERMISSIONS
> MERGED
> INTO
> ONE
> SUPER-AGENT
> ```

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the governed Dynamic Team Formation architecture
for Mianx.ai.

It governs:

```text
TEAM
CREATION

MEMBER
SELECTION

MEMBER
INVITATION

MEMBER

LEADER /
COORDINATOR
SELECTION

TEAM
SUSPENSION

TEAM

```

while preserving individual Agent governance.

---

# 2. Mission

The mission is:

> **Create fit-for-purpose Teams from individually eligible Agents
> without allowing team composition, collaboration need, shared goals,
> leadership or combined capability to create a new Security authority
> that no participant independently possesses.**
