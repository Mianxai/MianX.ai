# Mianx.ai Automation Engine Detailed Governance Framework

> **Automation Governance defines who may decide, who may execute, what
> controls apply, what Evidence is required, and when Automation must
> stop or escalate.**
>
> Permanent:
>
> ```text
> GOVERNANCE
> DOCUMENTED
> ≠
> GOVERNANCE
> ENFORCED
> ```
>
> and:
>
> ```text
> SILENCE
> ≠
> APPROVAL
> ```

# 4. Governance Mission

The mission is:

> **Enable maximum safe Automation while preserving Founder authority,
> explicit decision rights, bounded AI autonomy, Project and Tenant
> isolation, accountability, reversibility, Evidence and mandatory
> escalation for high-risk actions.**
