# Mianx.ai Automation Engine Business Process Automation Framework

> **Business Process Automation is the governed conversion of repeatable
> business work into controlled execution systems.**
>
> It is not the blind replacement of people with software or AI.
>
> Permanent:
>
> ```text
> AUTOMATE
> PROCESS
> ≠
> REMOVE
> GOVERNANCE
> ```
>
> and:
>
> ```text
> AUTOMATE
> BROKEN
> PROCESS
> ≠
> FIX
> BROKEN
> PROCESS
> ```

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Business Process Automation Framework for:

```text
doc/24-automation-engine/business-process-automation/
```

and specifically:

```text
doc/24-automation-engine/business-process-automation/bpa-framework.md
```

It establishes the methodology through which business processes become
governed Automation candidates.

---

# 2. BPA Mission

The mission is:

> **Convert business operations into measurable, reliable, governable
> and increasingly autonomous processes while preserving explicit
> business ownership, human authority, Security, legal controls,
> Project isolation, Tenant isolation, exception handling and
> accountable business outcomes.**

> **Define the governed Business Workflow model through which approved

> structures, including Workflow identity, Process-to-Workflow mapping,
> business stages, activities, tasks, execution paths, decision points,
> branches, loops, parallel work, waits, handoffs, human tasks,
> Approval tasks, Multi-Level Approval, Human-in-the-Loop tasks, Agent
> tasks, Multi-Agent collaboration, deterministic system tasks, Rules,
> Events, Schedules, Jobs, Queues, Pipelines, external integrations,
> business Data contracts, business keys, Process and Workflow state
> separation, correlation, idempotency, exception paths, retry,
> compensation, escalation, deadlines, SLAs, workflow versions, Process
> version binding, Project/Tenant/environment scope, source-of-truth
> reconciliation, testing, simulation, rollout, observability, Audit,
> Evidence, Runtime Truth, verification scenarios and Production hard
> stops while preserving that a Business Workflow is an executable
> representation of a governed Business Process rather than the owner of
> business policy or authoritative domain state, Workflow completion
> does not automatically establish business outcome success, retries may
> not recreate Approval or authority, technical state must reconcile
> against authoritative business systems where required, Agent or
> Multi-Agent steps may not expand Process authority, and every running
> Business Workflow must remain bound to exact Process version, Workflow
> version, Project, Tenant, environment, controls, policy and Evidence.**
