# Product Roadmap

> **The Product Roadmap defines the long-term evolution of the Mianx.ai platform.**

---

# Purpose

This roadmap provides a strategic view of how Mianx.ai will evolve over multiple phases.

It aligns business strategy, engineering priorities, AI capabilities, and product development into one unified direction.

Goal

Build the foundation of the company.

Deliverables

- Documentation
- Governance
- Company Structure
- Product Definition
- System Architecture

Status

🟢 In Progress

Goal

Build the first working platform.

Major Deliverables

- Organizations
- Users
- Departments

- Dashboard

Enterprise-ready platform foundation.

- Inventory

- Assets
- Documents
- Projects

Target Outcome

A fully integrated ERP platform.

Create autonomous AI departments.

- Sales AI

AI-driven business execution.

---

# Roadmap Principles

Every phase must:

- Build upon previous phases
- Remain modular
- Maintain documentation
- Preserve backward compatibility
- Improve organizational knowledge

---

# Success Metrics

The roadmap is successful when:

- Every planned phase is delivered.
- AI adoption increases over time.
- Platform capabilities expand without unnecessary complexity.
- Customer value continuously improves.

---

# Risks

Potential roadmap risks:

- Scope expansion
- Resource limitations
- AI model evolution
- Technology changes
- Market shifts

Roadmaps should be reviewed regularly.

# Review Schedule

Roadmap reviews:

- Quarterly

- Major release review
