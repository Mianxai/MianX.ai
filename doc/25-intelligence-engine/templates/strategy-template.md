# Mianx.ai Intelligence Engine Strategy Template

> **This document defines the reusable governed Strategy artifact template for the Mianx.ai Intelligence Engine.**
>
> It standardizes how strategic work is framed, documented, challenged, evaluated, approved and handed off.
>
> **It does not grant strategic authority.**
>
> Completing this template does not prove that a Strategy is correct, feasible, approved, funded, safe, executable, implemented, verified or Production authorized.
>
> Strategy runtime, automated strategy generation, automated evaluation, approval enforcement, Project isolation, Tenant isolation, execution integration and Production capability remain `NOT_PROVEN` unless independently demonstrated.

---
