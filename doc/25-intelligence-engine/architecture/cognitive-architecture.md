# 2. Architecture Mission

The mission is:

> **Provide a composable, evidence-grounded, context-aware,
> uncertainty-honest and governable Intelligence architecture that can
> support many Projects, Tenants, Agents, Models and enterprise
> workflows while preserving strict authority and isolation
> boundaries.**
