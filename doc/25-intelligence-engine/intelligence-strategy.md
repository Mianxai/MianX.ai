# Mianx.ai Intelligence Engine Strategy

> **The Intelligence Engine strategy is to build decision intelligence
> in layers: first trustworthy context and evidence, then reasoning,
> then higher-order prediction, planning, recommendation and learning,
> and only afterward broader autonomy—while keeping authority,
> Security, Project scope, Tenant scope and Production authorization
> independent from intelligence quality.**

Permanent:

```text
STRATEGY
≠
IMPLEMENTATION
```

and:

```text
INTELLIGENCE
≠
AUTHORITY
```

and:

```text
HIGHER
INTELLIGENCE
CAPABILITY
≠
HIGHER
EXECUTION
AUTHORITY
```

The strategic mission is:

> **Create a reusable Intelligence Engine that compounds enterprise
