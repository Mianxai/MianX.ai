# Quality Compliance

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Enterprise Quality Compliance Framework for the MIANX-AI Platform.

Quality Compliance ensures that every product, engineering process, AI system, infrastructure component, business process, and operational activity complies with applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, security, and organizational quality requirements.

Compliance is treated as a continuous operational capability rather than a one-time certification activity.

---

# Objectives

The Quality Compliance Framework aims to:

- Ensure regulatory compliance.
- Ensure internal policy compliance.
- Reduce legal and operational risk.
- Maintain customer trust.
- Support certifications.
- Standardize compliance processes.
- Improve audit readiness.
- Enable continuous monitoring.
- Protect organizational reputation.
- Support enterprise growth.

---

# Scope

This framework applies to:

- Software Engineering
- AI Systems
- APIs
- Infrastructure
- DevOps
- Security
- Operations
- Data Management
- Documentation
- Business Processes
- Vendors
- Third-Party Services

- Compliance by Design
- Security by Design

- Risk-Based Decision Making
- Continuous Monitoring

- Transparency
- Accountability

# Best Practices

- Embed compliance into engineering.
- Automate compliance monitoring.
- Maintain complete evidence.
- Review policies regularly.
- Train employees continuously.
- Track regulatory changes.
- Perform regular assessments.
- Document every decision.
- Maintain audit readiness.
- Improve continuously.

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Manual compliance tracking only.
- Missing evidence.
- Outdated policies.
- Ignoring audit findings.
- Delayed CAPA implementation.
- Weak documentation.
- Inconsistent controls.
- Unapproved vendors.
- One-time compliance efforts.
- Lack of ownership.

---

# Governance

The Quality Compliance Framework is governed by:

- Executive Leadership
- Chief Technology Officer (CTO)
- Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)
- Head of Quality Engineering
- Compliance Committee

The framework shall be reviewed annually or whenever regulatory requirements, industry standards, or organizational policies change.
