# Quality Management System (QMS)

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Enterprise Quality Management System (QMS) for the MIANX-AI Platform.

The QMS establishes the policies, processes, procedures, standards, governance, and continuous improvement mechanisms required to consistently deliver high-quality products, services, AI systems, and operational processes.

The QMS aligns engineering excellence with international quality management principles and serves as the foundation for sustainable organizational growth.

---

# Objectives

The Quality Management System aims to:

- Standardize enterprise quality processes.
- Improve customer satisfaction.
- Ensure consistent product quality.
- Reduce defects.
- Improve operational efficiency.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Enable continuous improvement.
- Strengthen governance.
- Improve organizational maturity.
- Support long-term scalability.

---

# Scope

The QMS applies to:

- Software Engineering
- AI Engineering
- APIs
- Infrastructure
- DevOps
- Security
- Operations
- Customer Support
- Business Operations
- Documentation
- Internal Processes
- External Deliverables

# Quality Policy

The MIANX-AI Quality Policy is to:

- Deliver reliable products.
- Exceed customer expectations.
- Follow engineering standards.
- Maintain secure systems.
- Ensure compliance.
- Promote innovation.
- Continuously improve processes.

# Document Control

All controlled documents shall include:

- Title
- Version
- Owner
- Reviewer
- Approval Status
- Revision History
- Last Updated Date

Documents shall be:

- Version Controlled
- Reviewed
- Approved
- Archived
- Traceable

---

# Records Management

Quality records include:

- Test Reports
- Audit Reports
- Review Reports
- Inspection Records
- Risk Assessments
- CAPA Records
- Release Reports
- Customer Feedback

Retention periods shall follow organizational policies.

---

# Risk Management

Quality risks shall be:

- Identified
- Evaluated
- Prioritized
- Assigned
- Mitigated
- Monitored
- Reviewed

Risk evaluation considers:

- Probability
- Business Impact
- Technical Impact
- Customer Impact

---

# Corrective Actions (CAPA)

Workflow:

```text

↓

Root Cause Analysis

↓

Corrective Plan

↓

Implementation

↓

Verification

↓

Closure
```

# Preventive Actions

Preventive actions aim to eliminate potential issues before they occur.

Examples:

- Process improvements
- Additional automation
- Training
- Standard updates
- Monitoring enhancements

---

# Change Management

Quality-related changes require:

- Impact Assessment
- Risk Review
- Approval
- Validation
- Documentation Update
- Communication

# Management Reviews

Management reviews evaluate:

- KPI Performance
- Audit Results
- Customer Feedback
- Process Effectiveness
- Risk Status
- CAPA Effectiveness
- Improvement Opportunities

Frequency:

- Quarterly
- Annual Strategic Review

Audit objectives:

- Verify compliance.
- Evaluate effectiveness.
- Identify improvements.
- Ensure policy adherence.
- Validate documentation.

- Reliability
- Security
- Compliance

- Support Tickets
- Surveys

- Reviews

- Usage Analytics

# Roles & Responsibilities

- Approve Quality Policy
- Provide resources
- Review organizational performance

- Approve engineering standards
- Review quality performance

# Compliance

The QMS aligns with:

---

# Best Practices

- Build quality into every process.
- Automate repetitive tasks.
- Measure everything.
- Maintain document control.
- Resolve root causes.
- Encourage continuous learning.
- Review KPIs regularly.
- Promote cross-functional collaboration.
- Keep processes simple and repeatable.
- Continuously improve.

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Reactive quality management.
- Manual-only processes.
- Uncontrolled documentation.
- Ignoring audit findings.
- Undefined ownership.
- Poor traceability.
- Inconsistent standards.
- Lack of management reviews.
- Missing corrective actions.
- Ignoring customer feedback.

---

# Governance

The Quality Management System is governed by:

- Chief Technology Officer (CTO)
- Head of Quality Engineering
- Quality Governance Committee
- Executive Leadership

The QMS shall be reviewed annually and updated whenever organizational strategy, regulatory requirements, engineering practices, or business processes change.
