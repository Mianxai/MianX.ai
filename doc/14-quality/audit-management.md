# Audit Management

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Enterprise Audit Management Framework for the MIANX-AI Platform.

Audit Management provides a structured approach for planning, executing, documenting, reviewing, and continuously improving audits across engineering, security, operations, business processes, AI systems, infrastructure, and compliance.

Audits ensure that organizational activities comply with internal policies, regulatory requirements, industry standards, and enterprise quality objectives.

---

# Objectives

The Audit Management Framework aims to:

- Verify compliance.
- Improve governance.
- Identify risks.
- Detect process gaps.
- Strengthen accountability.
- Support continuous improvement.
- Ensure policy adherence.
- Improve operational excellence.
- Increase customer confidence.
- Reduce organizational risk.

---

# Scope

Audit Management applies to:

- Software Engineering
- AI Systems
- APIs
- Infrastructure
- DevOps
- Security
- Operations
- Business Processes
- Documentation
- Data Management
- Customer Support
- Enterprise Governance

---

# Audit Principles

Every audit shall be:

- Independent
- Objective
- Evidence-Based
- Risk-Oriented
- Transparent
- Repeatable
- Well Documented
- Ethical
- Confidential
- Focused on Improvement

# Audit Planning

Every audit shall define:

- Objectives
- Scope
- Standards
- Audit Team
- Timeline
- Resources
- Risk Areas
- Deliverables
- Approval

---

# Audit Scope

Scope shall specify:

- Systems
- Departments
- Products
- Processes
- Time Period
- Standards
- Evidence Required

---

# Audit Preparation

Preparation includes:

- Reviewing documentation
- Defining checklists
- Scheduling interviews
- Collecting previous audit reports
- Reviewing KPIs
- Identifying high-risk areas

---

# Evidence Collection

Evidence may include:

- Policies
- Procedures
- Documentation
- Configuration Files
- Logs
- Source Code
- Test Results
- Monitoring Reports
- Interviews
- System Demonstrations

Evidence shall be objective and verifiable.

---

# Audit Execution

Activities include:

- Interviews
- Process Observation
- Documentation Review
- Configuration Review
- Technical Validation
- Sampling
- Compliance Verification

---

# Audit Findings

Findings shall be classified as:

| Classification | Description |
|---------------|-------------|
| Critical | Immediate organizational risk |
| Major | Significant non-conformance |
| Minor | Limited impact |
| Observation | Improvement opportunity |
| Best Practice | Exemplary implementation |

---

# Non-Conformance Management

Every non-conformance shall include:

- Finding ID
- Description
- Standard Violated
- Evidence
- Impact
- Risk Level
- Owner
- Due Date

---

# Root Cause Analysis (RCA)

Critical and Major findings require RCA.

Process:

```text
Finding

↓

Investigation

↓

Root Cause

↓

Corrective Action

↓

Preventive Action

↓

Verification

↓

Closure
```

# Continuous Improvement

Audit Management continuously improves through:

- Lessons Learned
- Audit Retrospectives
- Process Optimization

---

# Best Practices

- Maintain auditor independence.
- Use standardized checklists.
- Base findings on objective evidence.
- Prioritize high-risk areas.
- Complete CAPA on time.
- Track recurring issues.
- Review audit metrics regularly.
- Automate evidence collection where possible.
- Maintain audit documentation.
- Share organizational learning.

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Subjective findings.
- Missing evidence.
- Delayed CAPA.
- Poor documentation.
- Incomplete audit scope.
- Ignoring repeat findings.
- Weak follow-up.
- Lack of auditor independence.
- Missing management reviews.
- Treating audits as one-time events.
