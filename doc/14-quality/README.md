# Quality

---

# Overview

The **Quality** documentation defines the Enterprise Quality Management System (QMS) for the MIANX-AI Platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that every product, service, AI agent, workflow, API, infrastructure component, and business process consistently meets enterprise standards for quality, reliability, security, maintainability, and customer satisfaction.

Quality is not limited to testing—it is integrated throughout the entire Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC), DevOps lifecycle, AI lifecycle, and business operations.

---

# Objectives

The Quality domain aims to:

- Establish enterprise quality standards.
- Standardize Quality Assurance (QA).
- Standardize Quality Control (QC).
- Improve software reliability.
- Reduce production defects.
- Increase customer satisfaction.
- Improve engineering excellence.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Enable continuous improvement.
- Build a quality-first engineering culture.

---

The Quality framework applies to:

# Core Principles

The MIANX-AI Quality Framework is based on:

- Customer First
- Quality by Design
- Shift Left Testing
- Continuous Quality
- Automation First
- Risk-Based Testing
- Continuous Improvement
- Measurable Quality
- Data-Driven Decisions
- Enterprise Governance
