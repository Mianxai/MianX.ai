# Quality Metrics

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Enterprise Quality Metrics Framework for the MIANX-AI Platform.

Quality Metrics provide objective measurements that evaluate software quality, engineering performance, AI reliability, operational excellence, customer satisfaction, and organizational maturity.

The framework enables leadership to make data-driven decisions and continuously improve products, services, and processes.

---

# Objectives

The Quality Metrics Framework aims to:

- Measure product quality.
- Monitor engineering performance.
- Improve release quality.
- Detect trends early.
- Support executive decision-making.
- Improve customer satisfaction.
- Reduce operational risk.
- Drive continuous improvement.
- Increase engineering productivity.
- Ensure enterprise scalability.

---

# Scope

This framework applies to:

- Software Engineering
- AI Engineering
- APIs
- Infrastructure
- DevOps
- Operations
- Security
- Customer Support
- Product Management
- Documentation
- Business Operations

---

# Quality Measurement Principles

All metrics shall be:

- Objective
- Measurable
- Actionable
- Consistent
- Repeatable
- Transparent
- Automated where possible
- Continuously Reviewed
- Business Aligned
- Customer Focused

# Best Practices

- Measure what matters.
- Automate metric collection.
- Use consistent definitions.
- Review trends instead of isolated values.
- Share dashboards across teams.
- Focus on actionable insights.
- Maintain data quality.
- Review KPIs regularly.
- Align metrics with business goals.
- Continuously refine measurements.

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Vanity metrics.
- Manual data collection.
- Inconsistent definitions.
- Ignoring trends.
- Measuring too many KPIs.
- Poor dashboard visibility.
- Missing ownership.
- Delayed reporting.
- Metrics without actions.
- Optimizing numbers instead of outcomes.

---

# Governance

The Quality Metrics Framework is governed by:

- Chief Technology Officer (CTO)
- Head of Quality Engineering
- Executive Leadership
- Engineering Leadership
- Quality Governance Committee

Quality metrics shall be reviewed monthly, while enterprise KPIs shall be reviewed quarterly as part of executive governance.
