# Quality Governance

---

# Purpose

This document defines the Enterprise Quality Governance Framework for the MIANX-AI Platform.

Quality Governance establishes the leadership structure, responsibilities, decision-making processes, quality policies, review mechanisms, and accountability required to ensure that every product, service, AI system, API, and operational process consistently meets enterprise quality standards.

---

# Objectives

The Quality Governance Framework aims to:

- Establish enterprise-wide quality ownership.
- Standardize quality decision-making.
- Define governance roles.
- Ensure compliance with quality standards.
- Improve transparency.
- Reduce quality risks.
- Drive continuous improvement.
- Enable measurable quality.
- Maintain engineering excellence.
- Align quality with business strategy.

---

# Governance Principles

The framework is based on the following principles:

- Quality is everyone's responsibility.
- Prevention is better than correction.
- Decisions shall be data-driven.
- Governance must be transparent.
- Continuous improvement is mandatory.
- Compliance is non-negotiable.
- Automation should be prioritized.
- Customer value comes first.
- Accountability must be clearly assigned.
- Quality must be measurable.

# Decision-Making Framework

Quality decisions shall be based on:

- Engineering metrics
- Customer feedback
- Audit findings
- Risk assessments
- Compliance status
- Performance indicators
- Business priorities

---

# Quality Reviews

Mandatory reviews include:

- Requirements Review
- Architecture Review
- Design Review
- Code Review
- Test Review
- Security Review
- Release Review
- Post-Release Review

No major release may bypass mandatory reviews.

---

# Risk Governance

Quality risks shall be:

- Identified
- Assessed
- Prioritized
- Mitigated
- Monitored
- Reviewed

Each risk shall have:

- Owner
- Impact
- Probability
- Mitigation Plan
- Review Schedule

---

# Compliance Governance

Compliance shall be monitored against:

- Internal Standards
- Engineering Standards
- Security Policies
- Regulatory Requirements
- Customer Contracts
- Industry Best Practices

---

# Audit Governance

Audits include:

- Internal Quality Audits
- Engineering Audits
- Process Audits
- Security Audits
- Documentation Audits
- Release Audits
- Compliance Audits

Audit findings shall be tracked until closure.

---

# Metrics Governance

Quality metrics shall measure:

- Product Quality
- Engineering Quality
- Test Coverage
- Defect Density
- Release Success
- Customer Satisfaction
- Platform Availability
- Compliance Status

Metrics shall be reviewed monthly.

# Best Practices

- Define clear ownership.
- Automate governance where possible.
- Use measurable KPIs.
- Review governance regularly.
- Encourage transparency.
- Maintain documentation.
- Perform regular audits.
- Resolve issues quickly.
- Learn from failures.
- Promote a quality culture.

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Undefined ownership.
- Manual governance only.
- Inconsistent standards.
- Missing audits.
- Ignored metrics.
- Weak accountability.
- Reactive quality management.
- Poor documentation.
- Skipped reviews.
- Lack of continuous improvement.
