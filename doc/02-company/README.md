# Company Documentation

> **The Company module defines what Mianx.ai is, how it is organized, and how it operates as an AI-native enterprise.**

---

# Purpose

The Company documentation establishes the organizational foundation of Mianx.ai.

It explains the company's identity, organizational structure, leadership model, operational philosophy, and long-term direction.

Every department, AI workforce, product, and operational process should align with the company structure defined in this module.

---

# Objectives

The Company module aims to:

- Define the organizational structure
- Explain how Mianx.ai operates
- Define leadership responsibilities
- Describe company departments
- Establish organizational scalability
- Align business operations with company strategy

---

# Scope

The Company documentation covers:

- Company Structure
- Organizational Model
- Leadership Model
- Departments
- Business Units
- Reporting Structure
- Decision-Making Structure
- Organizational Growth
- Future Expansion

---

# Company Philosophy

Mianx.ai is designed as an **AI-native enterprise**, where humans lead strategy and AI workforces execute operations.

The company is built around reusable systems, intelligent automation, continuous learning, and long-term scalability.

Every department contributes to a shared organizational intelligence that improves over time.
