# Company Structure

> **Mianx.ai is an AI-native enterprise where humans provide leadership and AI workforces execute operations through specialized departments.**

---

# Purpose

This document defines the organizational architecture of Mianx.ai.

It explains how the company is structured, how departments collaborate, how AI workforces are organized, and how responsibilities are distributed across the enterprise.

The structure is designed for scalability, automation, and long-term sustainability.

---

# Organizational Philosophy

Mianx.ai is built on a simple principle:

```text
Human Leadership
        │
        ▼
AI Executive Team
        │
        ▼
AI Departments
        │
        ▼
AI Teams
        │
        ▼
AI Specialists
```

Humans define direction.

AI executes operations.

Knowledge continuously improves the organization.

---

# Organizational Layers

Mianx.ai consists of five organizational layers.

## Layer 1 — Leadership

Responsible for long-term direction.

Responsibilities:

- Vision
- Strategy
- Innovation
- Partnerships
- Investment Decisions
- Risk Management

Primary Role:

Founder

---

## Layer 2 — Executive AI

Coordinates company-wide execution.

Responsibilities:

- Planning
- Department Coordination
- KPI Monitoring
- Decision Support
- Resource Allocation

Future AI Roles:

- CEO AI
- COO AI
- CTO AI
- CFO AI
- CPO AI

---

## Layer 3 — Business Departments

Responsible for business functions.

Departments include:

- Product
- Engineering
- Platform
- Marketing
- Sales
- Finance
- HR
- Operations
- Legal
- Customer Success
- Research

Each department operates through specialized AI workforces.

---

## Layer 4 — AI Teams

Every department contains multiple AI teams.

Example:

Engineering Department

- Backend Team
- Frontend Team
- Mobile Team
- DevOps Team
- QA Team
- AI Team
- ERP Team

Each team focuses on a specific domain.

---

## Layer 5 — AI Specialists

The execution layer.

Examples:

- API Engineer AI
- Database Engineer AI
- UI Engineer AI
- Flutter Engineer AI
- React Engineer AI
- Python Engineer AI
- DevOps Engineer AI
- Security Engineer AI

These specialists complete operational work.

---

# Communication Model

Departments collaborate through standardized workflows.

```text
Executive AI
      │
      ▼
Department AI
      │
      ▼
Team AI
      │
      ▼
Specialist AI
```

Knowledge flows upward and downward across all layers.

# Scalability Model

The company structure is modular.

New departments can be introduced without changing the overall architecture.

Examples:

- AI Consulting
- Cybersecurity
- Cloud Services
- Manufacturing
- Healthcare
- Education
- Government Solutions

Each new business unit follows the same organizational pattern.
