# Data Strategy

---

# Purpose

The Data Strategy defines how MIANX-AI will manage data as a strategic enterprise asset.

It establishes the long-term direction for collecting, storing, governing, securing, analyzing, and utilizing data across every application, AI agent, workflow, business process, and platform service.

The strategy ensures that data supports intelligent decision-making, enterprise automation, AI innovation, and sustainable business growth.

---

# Vision

To build one of the world's most intelligent, trusted, secure, AI-native, and scalable enterprise data platforms capable of powering autonomous businesses.

---

# Mission

Our mission is to transform enterprise data into actionable intelligence by providing a unified, governed, secure, AI-ready, and highly scalable data ecosystem.

---

# Strategic Objectives

The data strategy aims to:

- Treat data as a strategic enterprise asset
- Establish a single source of truth
- Enable AI-powered decision making
- Support enterprise analytics
- Improve operational efficiency
- Ensure data quality
- Protect sensitive information
- Maintain regulatory compliance
- Enable real-time intelligence
- Support long-term scalability

---

This strategy applies to:

- Business Data

# Data as a Strategic Asset

Every piece of data shall be:

- Owned
- Governed
- Protected
- Documented
- Auditable
- Searchable
- Versioned
- Traceable
- Measurable
- Valuable

Data is considered a core enterprise asset equal to software, infrastructure, and intellectual property.

# Best Practices

Platform teams should:

- Treat data as a product.
- Eliminate duplicate sources.
- Maintain clear ownership.
- Automate governance.
- Continuously monitor quality.
- Secure data by default.
- Build reusable data services.
- Document every dataset.

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Data silos
- Duplicate datasets
- Undefined ownership
- Poor documentation
- Manual governance
- Weak security controls
- Inconsistent schemas
- Missing metadata
- Untracked data changes
- Low-quality data
