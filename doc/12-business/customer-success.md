# Customer Success

---

# Purpose

The Enterprise Customer Success Framework defines how MIANX-AI helps customers achieve measurable business outcomes using its products and services.

Customer Success is responsible for ensuring every customer successfully adopts the platform, realizes business value, renews subscriptions, expands usage, and becomes a long-term advocate of MIANX-AI.

---

# Objectives

The Customer Success Framework aims to:

- Deliver customer value.
- Improve onboarding.
- Increase product adoption.
- Reduce customer churn.
- Improve renewals.
- Increase expansion revenue.
- Strengthen customer relationships.
- Improve customer satisfaction.
- Build customer loyalty.
- Create customer advocates.

---

# Scope

This framework applies to:

- Customer Onboarding
- Customer Education
- Product Adoption
- Customer Engagement
- Customer Health
- Customer Support Collaboration
- Renewals
- Expansion
- Customer Advocacy
- Success Analytics

# Customer Success Principles

MIANX-AI follows these principles:

- Customer First
- Outcome Driven
- Proactive Engagement
- Data Driven
- Continuous Improvement
- Transparency
- Long-Term Partnership
- AI Assisted
- Accountability
- Operational Excellence

# Customer Onboarding

Every customer shall receive:

- Welcome Process
- Account Setup
- Workspace Configuration
- User Training
- Documentation Access
- Product Walkthrough
- Success Plan
- Initial Health Assessment

Successful onboarding should minimize time-to-value.

---

# Customer Implementation

Implementation activities include:

- Requirements Validation
- Platform Configuration
- Integration
- Data Migration
- User Provisioning
- Security Configuration
- Acceptance Testing
- Go-Live Support

---

# Product Adoption

Customer Success monitors:

- Feature Usage
- Active Users
- Workflow Adoption
- Automation Usage
- AI Usage
- API Usage
- Training Completion
- Documentation Usage

Low adoption triggers proactive engagement.

---

# Customer Engagement

Engagement activities include:

- Business Reviews
- Success Meetings
- Product Updates
- Training Sessions
- Webinars
- Community Participation
- Executive Check-ins
- Success Planning

---

# Customer Health Score

Each customer receives a health score based on:

- Product Adoption
- Login Frequency
- Active Users
- Support Tickets
- Customer Satisfaction
- Renewal Status
- Payment History
- AI Usage
- Engagement
- Contract Value

Example:

| Score | Status |
|--------|---------|
| 90–100 | Excellent |
| 75–89 | Healthy |
| 60–74 | At Risk |
| Below 60 | Critical |

# Renewal Management

Renewal process includes:

```text
Health Review

↓

Usage Analysis

↓

Renewal Planning

↓

Executive Meeting

↓

Commercial Review

↓

Contract Renewal
```

# Best Practices

Customer Success teams should:

- Engage customers proactively.
- Monitor health continuously.
- Respond quickly to risks.
- Align product usage with business goals.
- Educate customers regularly.
- Collaborate with Sales and Support.
- Measure outcomes consistently.
- Build trusted relationships.

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Reactive customer management
- Poor onboarding
- Ignoring health scores
- Delayed renewals
- Weak customer communication
- Lack of documentation
- Missing executive engagement
- Untracked customer goals
- Poor adoption monitoring
- Limited customer feedback

---

# Governance

The Enterprise Customer Success Framework is governed by:

- Chief Customer Officer (CCO)
- Executive Leadership Team
- Customer Success Department
- Sales Leadership
- Product Leadership

The framework shall be reviewed quarterly and updated annually or whenever significant business, product, or customer lifecycle changes occur.
