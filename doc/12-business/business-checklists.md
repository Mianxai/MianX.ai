# Business Checklists

---

# Purpose

This document provides enterprise-wide operational checklists to ensure every business process within MIANX-AI is executed consistently, efficiently, securely, and in alignment with organizational objectives.

These checklists support operational excellence, governance, compliance, and continuous improvement.

---

# Business Strategy Checklist

Before executing strategic initiatives, verify:

- ☐ Vision is documented.
- ☐ Mission is approved.
- ☐ Business goals are defined.
- ☐ Annual objectives are established.
- ☐ KPIs are assigned.
- ☐ Budget is approved.
- ☐ Risks are identified.
- ☐ Success metrics are defined.
- ☐ Executive approval received.
- ☐ Review schedule established.
