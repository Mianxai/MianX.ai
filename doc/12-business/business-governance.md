# Business Governance

---

# Purpose

The Enterprise Business Governance Framework establishes how MIANX-AI is directed, managed, monitored, and continuously improved.

Business governance ensures that every strategic decision, operational process, investment, product initiative, AI system, and organizational activity aligns with the company's vision, mission, objectives, and long-term strategy.

The framework promotes accountability, transparency, ethical leadership, operational excellence, and sustainable business growth.

---

# Objectives

The Business Governance Framework aims to:

- Align business activities with company strategy.
- Define decision-making authority.
- Establish accountability.
- Improve transparency.
- Strengthen risk management.
- Ensure regulatory compliance.
- Improve operational efficiency.
- Support scalable growth.
- Enable data-driven decision making.
- Build long-term organizational resilience.

---

This framework applies to:

- Executive Leadership

# Accountability Framework

Every business function shall define:

- Business Owner
- Executive Sponsor
- Decision Authority
- Responsibilities
- Performance Indicators
- Review Frequency

No critical business activity shall exist without an assigned owner.

# Best Practices

Business leaders should:

- Make decisions based on data.
- Maintain transparent governance.
- Review strategic objectives regularly.
- Clearly assign ownership.
- Monitor KPIs continuously.
- Encourage innovation.
- Maintain ethical leadership.
- Continuously improve governance processes.

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Undefined responsibilities
- Poor communication
- Reactive decision-making
- Weak accountability
- Ignoring KPIs
- Inconsistent governance
- Lack of executive oversight
- Missing documentation
- Poor compliance management
- Short-term strategic thinking

# Governance

The Enterprise Business Governance Framework is governed by:

- Founder & CEO
- Executive Leadership Team
- Business Strategy Office

The framework shall be reviewed quarterly and comprehensively updated annually or whenever significant organizational, regulatory, or strategic changes occur.
