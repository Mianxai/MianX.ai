# Sales Management

---

# Purpose

The Enterprise Sales Management Framework establishes standardized processes for acquiring customers, managing opportunities, forecasting revenue, building long-term customer relationships, and scaling global sales operations across the MIANX-AI Platform.

The framework ensures every sales activity is measurable, repeatable, customer-centric, and aligned with the company's strategic business objectives.

---

# Objectives

The Sales Management Framework aims to:

- Increase recurring revenue.
- Improve lead conversion.
- Standardize sales operations.
- Strengthen customer relationships.
- Improve forecasting accuracy.
- Reduce sales cycle duration.
- Expand enterprise sales.
- Improve account growth.
- Support global expansion.
- Enable AI-assisted selling.

---

# Best Practices

Sales teams should:

- Keep CRM records current.
- Qualify leads consistently.
- Focus on customer outcomes.
- Build long-term relationships.
- Review pipelines weekly.
- Forecast conservatively.
- Collaborate with Marketing and Customer Success.
- Use AI insights to improve productivity.

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Poor CRM hygiene
- Unqualified opportunities
- Overly optimistic forecasts
- Ignoring customer needs
- Weak follow-up
- Discount-driven selling
- Lack of account planning
- Missing renewal planning
- Poor documentation
- Inconsistent sales processes

---

# Governance

The Enterprise Sales Management Framework is governed by:

- Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- Chief Revenue Officer (CRO)
- Sales Leadership Team
- Finance Department

The framework shall be reviewed quarterly and updated annually or whenever significant market, product, pricing, or organizational changes occur.
