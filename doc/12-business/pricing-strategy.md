# Pricing Strategy

---

# Purpose

The Enterprise Pricing Strategy defines how MIANX-AI prices its products, services, AI capabilities, enterprise solutions, APIs, and managed services.

The strategy ensures pricing is transparent, scalable, competitive, profitable, and aligned with customer value while supporting long-term business growth.

---

# Objectives

The Pricing Strategy aims to:

- Maximize customer value.
- Build predictable recurring revenue.
- Maintain competitive pricing.
- Support global expansion.
- Enable flexible purchasing options.
- Improve customer retention.
- Optimize profitability.
- Simplify pricing decisions.
- Support enterprise sales.
- Enable sustainable growth.

---

# Pricing Principles

MIANX-AI follows these pricing principles:

- Value-Based Pricing
- Transparent Pricing
- Simple Pricing Structure
- Predictable Billing
- Flexible Plans
- Enterprise Scalability
- Global Accessibility
- Sustainable Margins
- Customer-Centric Pricing
- Continuous Optimization

# Pricing Models

The platform supports multiple pricing models:

## Subscription Pricing

Recurring monthly or annual billing.

Suitable for:

- SaaS Platform
- AI Platform
- Workforce Platform
- Enterprise Software

---

## Usage-Based Pricing

Customers pay according to consumption.

Examples:

- AI Tokens
- API Calls
- GPU Hours
- Storage
- AI Agent Executions
- Workflow Runs

---

## Seat-Based Pricing

Pricing based on the number of users.

Examples:

- Team Plans
- Enterprise Workforce
- Organization Licenses

---

## Project-Based Pricing

One-time pricing for:

- Custom Development
- AI Implementations
- Integrations
- Consulting Projects

---

## Retainer Pricing

Recurring service agreements for:

- Managed Services
- AI Operations
- DevOps
- Security Operations
- Technical Support

---

## Hybrid Pricing

Combination of:

- Subscription
- Usage
- Enterprise Licensing
- Professional Services

# SaaS Subscription Plans

The recommended product hierarchy:

## Free

Designed for evaluation.

Features:

- Limited projects
- Basic AI usage
- Community support
- Limited storage

---

## Starter

Designed for individuals.

Features:

- Small projects
- Basic automation
- Standard support
- Limited integrations

---

## Professional

Designed for professionals and growing businesses.

Features:

- Advanced AI
- Team collaboration
- API access
- Higher limits
- Priority support

---

## Business

Designed for established organizations.

Features:

- Multiple workspaces
- Advanced security
- Automation
- AI workforce
- Analytics
- Integrations

---

## Enterprise

Designed for large organizations.

Features:

- Unlimited scalability
- Enterprise security
- SSO
- Dedicated support
- Custom AI
- Compliance
- SLA
- Enterprise APIs

---

## Enterprise Plus

Custom strategic partnerships including:

- Dedicated infrastructure
- Dedicated AI clusters
- Professional services
- Custom development
- Account management
- Executive support

# Best Practices

The organization should:

- Keep pricing simple.
- Focus on customer value.
- Offer scalable plans.
- Monitor competitor pricing.
- Review margins regularly.
- Measure pricing performance.
- Continuously optimize pricing.
- Maintain transparent billing.

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Overly complex pricing
- Hidden fees
- Frequent pricing changes
- Poor communication
- Unsustainable discounts
- One-size-fits-all pricing
- Ignoring regional markets
- Manual pricing management
- Weak approval controls
- Lack of pricing analytics

---

# Governance

The Enterprise Pricing Strategy is governed by:

- Founder & CEO
- Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
- Executive Leadership Team
- Finance Department
- Sales Department

The pricing strategy shall be reviewed quarterly and updated whenever significant market conditions, customer behavior, operating costs, or strategic objectives change.
