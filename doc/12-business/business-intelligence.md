# Business Intelligence

---

# Purpose

The Enterprise Business Intelligence (BI) Framework establishes how MIANX-AI transforms enterprise data into actionable business insights that support strategic planning, operational excellence, customer success, financial growth, and executive decision-making.

Business Intelligence provides a unified view of business performance across every department through trusted data, enterprise dashboards, AI-powered analytics, and standardized reporting.

---

# Objectives

The Business Intelligence Framework aims to:

- Enable data-driven decision making.
- Deliver real-time business visibility.
- Standardize enterprise reporting.
- Improve forecasting accuracy.
- Support executive leadership.
- Increase operational efficiency.
- Monitor business performance.
- Detect opportunities and risks.
- Enable AI-assisted analytics.
- Build a single source of truth.

---

This framework applies to:

- Executive Dashboards

# Best Practices

Business Intelligence teams should:

- Maintain trusted data.
- Automate reporting.
- Build reusable dashboards.
- Standardize KPIs.
- Monitor data quality.
- Enable self-service analytics.
- Validate AI insights.
- Continuously improve business visibility.

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Multiple versions of business data
- Manual spreadsheet reporting
- Poor data quality
- Missing KPI definitions
- Inconsistent dashboards
- Delayed reporting
- Weak data governance
- Excessive manual analysis
- Unvalidated AI recommendations
- Reporting without business context
