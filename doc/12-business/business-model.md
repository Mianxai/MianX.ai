# Business Model

---

# Purpose

The Enterprise Business Model defines how MIANX-AI creates value, delivers value, and captures value.

It explains how the company transforms innovative AI technologies into scalable products and services that solve customer problems while generating sustainable long-term revenue.

This document serves as the foundation for all business planning, product development, financial forecasting, investment decisions, and strategic growth.

---

# Objectives

The Business Model aims to:

- Define value creation.
- Identify target customers.
- Establish sustainable revenue.
- Build scalable operations.
- Maximize customer value.
- Strengthen competitive advantage.
- Enable global expansion.
- Support continuous innovation.
- Improve profitability.
- Ensure long-term sustainability.

---

# Vision Alignment

The business model supports the company's vision:

> **To become the world's leading AI-powered Autonomous Enterprise Platform.**

Every product, service, and business decision shall contribute toward achieving this vision.

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Dependence on a single revenue source
- Undefined customer segments
- Poor pricing strategy
- Weak customer relationships
- High operational costs
- Limited innovation
- Ignoring market changes
- Lack of scalability
- Poor financial planning
- Weak competitive positioning
