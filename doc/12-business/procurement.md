# Procurement Management

---

# Purpose

The Enterprise Procurement Management Framework establishes standardized processes for acquiring products, services, software, infrastructure, and professional services required to operate and grow MIANX-AI.

The framework ensures procurement activities are transparent, efficient, compliant, secure, financially responsible, and strategically aligned with business objectives.

---

# Objectives

The Procurement Management Framework aims to:

- Optimize procurement costs.
- Improve purchasing efficiency.
- Standardize procurement workflows.
- Reduce procurement risks.
- Ensure supplier quality.
- Maintain regulatory compliance.
- Improve contract management.
- Increase operational transparency.
- Support sustainable procurement.
- Enable AI-driven procurement automation.

---

# Scope

This framework applies to:

- Strategic Sourcing
- Purchasing
- Software Procurement

- Hardware Procurement

- Vendor Selection

# Procurement Methods

## Direct Purchase

Low-value purchases using approved vendors.

---

## Request for Information (RFI)

Used to collect market intelligence before procurement.

---

## Request for Quotation (RFQ)

Used when product specifications are well defined.

---

## Request for Proposal (RFP)

Used for complex procurement requiring technical and commercial evaluation.

---

## Competitive Tender

Used for major procurement projects requiring formal competition.

# Approval Workflow

```text
Requester

↓

Department Manager

↓

Budget Owner

↓

Procurement

↓

Finance

↓

Legal (if required)

↓

Executive Approval

↓

Purchase Order
```

Approval levels shall be based on procurement value and organizational policy.

# Purchase Orders

Each Purchase Order (PO) includes:

- PO Number
- Vendor
- Products / Services
- Quantity
- Pricing
- Delivery Terms
- Payment Terms
- Contract Reference

---

# Contract Coordination

Procurement coordinates with Legal for:

- Master Service Agreements
- License Agreements
- Subscription Agreements
- NDAs
- Statements of Work
- Support Agreements

---

# Delivery & Acceptance

Procurement verifies:

- Correct Delivery
- Product Quality
- Service Completion
- Acceptance Criteria
- Documentation
- Warranty Information

Only accepted purchases proceed to payment.

---

# Invoice Management

Invoices shall be matched against:

- Purchase Order
- Delivery Confirmation
- Contract
- Acceptance Record

Discrepancies must be resolved before payment.

---

# Payment Management

Payments follow:

- Contract Terms
- Finance Policies
- Tax Requirements
- Currency Rules
- Payment Schedule

# Best Practices

Procurement teams should:

- Follow standardized workflows.
- Encourage competitive sourcing.
- Maintain procurement transparency.
- Review supplier performance regularly.
- Monitor procurement risks.
- Align procurement with budgets.
- Use AI responsibly.
- Maintain complete documentation.

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Unauthorized purchasing
- Missing approvals
- Vendor favoritism
- Poor documentation
- Weak contract reviews
- Emergency purchasing without justification
- Ignoring supplier performance
- Duplicate purchases
- Manual procurement tracking
- Procurement outside approved policies

---

# Governance

The Enterprise Procurement Management Framework is governed by:

- Chief Procurement Officer (CPO)
- Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
- Executive Leadership Team
- Procurement Department
- Finance Department
- Legal Department

The framework shall be reviewed quarterly and updated annually or whenever significant regulatory, operational, financial, or strategic changes occur.
