# Business Documentation

---

# Purpose

The Business Documentation section defines how MIANX-AI operates as a company.

It documents every business process, strategy, revenue model, customer lifecycle, finance process, marketing strategy, sales methodology, partnerships, legal framework, and long-term business growth plan.

This documentation serves as the single source of truth for all business operations and ensures scalability, consistency, governance, and organizational alignment.

---

# Objectives

This documentation aims to:

- Define the business structure.
- Document business strategies.
- Standardize company operations.
- Support long-term scalability.
- Improve decision making.
- Define revenue generation.
- Establish customer lifecycle management.
- Create repeatable business processes.
- Improve governance.
- Build a world-class AI company.
