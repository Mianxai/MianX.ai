# Business Metrics

---

# Purpose

The Enterprise Business Metrics Framework defines the official Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), business scorecards, reporting standards, and measurement methodologies used throughout MIANX-AI.

Its purpose is to ensure every department measures success consistently using standardized, reliable, and transparent metrics that support strategic planning, operational excellence, and continuous improvement.

---

# Objectives

The Business Metrics Framework aims to:

- Establish enterprise-wide KPIs.
- Measure organizational performance.
- Improve strategic decision-making.
- Increase operational visibility.
- Enable continuous improvement.
- Standardize reporting.
- Support executive governance.
- Improve accountability.
- Enable AI-driven insights.
- Align business goals across departments.

---

# Scope

This framework applies to:

- Executive Metrics
- Financial Metrics
- Sales Metrics
- Marketing Metrics
- Product Metrics
- Engineering Metrics
- Customer Success Metrics
- Operations Metrics
- Security Metrics
- AI Performance Metrics

---

# Guiding Principles

Business metrics shall be:

- Accurate
- Objective
- Measurable
- Timely
- Actionable
- Transparent
- Consistent
- Auditable
- Automated
- Continuously Improved

# KPI Ownership

Each KPI shall include:

- KPI Name
- Description
- Formula
- Data Source
- Department Owner
- Executive Owner
- Target Value
- Warning Threshold
- Critical Threshold
- Review Frequency

No KPI shall exist without an assigned owner.

# Best Practices

Business teams should:

- Measure what matters.
- Use standardized KPIs.
- Validate data quality.
- Automate reporting.
- Review trends regularly.
- Align KPIs with strategy.
- Act on insights.
- Continuously refine metrics.

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Vanity metrics
- Duplicate KPIs
- Undefined ownership
- Manual reporting
- Poor data quality
- Inconsistent calculations
- Delayed reporting
- Ignoring trends
- Overcomplicated dashboards
- Metrics without action plans

# Governance

The Enterprise Business Metrics Framework is governed by:

- Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
- Chief Data Officer (CDO)
- Executive Leadership Team
- Business Intelligence Team

The framework shall be reviewed quarterly and updated annually or whenever strategic objectives, business models, or reporting requirements change.
