# Operations Governance

---

# Purpose

Operations Governance defines how operational activities across the MIANX-AI Platform are directed, controlled, monitored, and continuously improved.

It establishes clear ownership, decision-making authority, accountability, operational standards, governance committees, compliance mechanisms, and performance reviews to ensure every operational process is executed consistently, securely, and efficiently.

Operations Governance ensures that day-to-day operations align with the company's strategic objectives, engineering standards, security policies, legal obligations, and customer commitments.

---

# Objectives

The Operations Governance Framework aims to:

- Standardize operational decision-making
- Define ownership and accountability
- Improve operational consistency
- Ensure policy compliance
- Reduce operational risk
- Support audit readiness
- Strengthen service reliability
- Improve operational transparency
- Enable continuous improvement
- Support autonomous operations

---

# Scope

This framework applies to:

- Platform Operations
- Infrastructure Operations
- Service Operations

### Tactical

Examples:

- Process updates

### Operational

Examples:

- Incident handling
- Routine maintenance

# Best Practices

Operations teams should:

- Clearly define ownership.
- Document every operational process.
- Follow standardized governance policies.
- Review operational performance regularly.
- Use data to drive improvements.
- Automate governance workflows where practical.
- Maintain transparency across teams.
- Continuously improve operational maturity.

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Undefined ownership
- Manual approvals without records
- Inconsistent operational procedures
- Poor documentation
- Ignoring audit findings
- Weak escalation paths
- Reactive governance
- Missing KPI reviews
- Uncontrolled operational changes
- Lack of accountability
