# Operations

---

# Overview

The **Operations** documentation defines how the MIANX-AI Platform operates after software has been designed, developed, deployed, and released.

While Engineering focuses on building products and DevOps focuses on delivering them, Operations ensures that every service remains reliable, available, efficient, secure, and continuously improving throughout its lifecycle.

This documentation establishes enterprise standards for operational governance, IT Service Management (ITSM), operational processes, service delivery, maintenance, business continuity, customer support, operational monitoring, and day-to-day platform management.

It serves as the operational playbook for engineering teams, operations teams, AI workforce, and future autonomous enterprise systems.

---

# Mission

Create an autonomous, scalable, highly reliable operational ecosystem capable of supporting millions of users, thousands of AI agents, and hundreds of enterprise customers with minimal manual intervention.

---

The Operations framework aims to:

- Standardize operational processes
- Improve service reliability
- Ensure operational excellence
- Reduce operational risk

- Drive continuous operational improvement
