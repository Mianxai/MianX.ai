# Mianx.ai AI Operating System Goal Management Standard

> **This document defines the governed target-state Goal Management
> standard for the Mianx.ai AI Operating System.**
>
> **Goals express approved desired outcomes. Goals do not create authority
> merely because they exist, are strategically important, are marked
> urgent, are generated by an AI Agent, or are linked to a Founder,
> executive, Project, Customer, Tenant, Plan, Workflow, or Task.**
>
> **Founder sovereignty remains above autonomous Goal creation and
> mutation. Founder-reserved strategic direction cannot be replaced,
> expanded, superseded, or reinterpreted by Agent consensus, Planner
> output, Model reasoning, automated optimization, or metric pressure.**
>
> **Goal decomposition may convert approved outcomes into narrower child
> Goals. It must not silently broaden objective, authority, Project,
> Customer, Tenant, data, Model, Tool, side-effect, budget, or autonomy
> boundaries.**
>
> **Goal progress must be evidence-based. A high percentage, positive KPI,
> Agent statement, completed Plan, completed Workflow, completed Task, or
> generated report does not by itself prove the Goal outcome has been
> achieved.**
>
> **Goal Management connects strategy to Plans, Workflows, Tasks, Agents,
> Services, Decisions, Metrics, Milestones, and Human accountability while
> preserving Governance and scope boundaries.**
>
> **This document defines target-state requirements. It does not prove that
> a Goal Registry, Goal Graph, Goal Lifecycle Engine, Goal Alignment
> Engine, Goal Drift Detector, KPI engine, Milestone engine, Goal Approval
> Runtime, Goal Progress Runtime, or Production Goal Management system
> currently exists.**

---

# 104. PROPOSED

Goal exists but has not completed review.

---

# 105. REVIEWING

Goal is undergoing validation, alignment, risk, and authority review.

---

# 106. APPROVED

Required approval exists for current Goal Version and scope.

---

# 107. ACTIVE

Goal is currently authorized for managed pursuit.

---

# 108. BLOCKED

Goal cannot progress because a required dependency or condition is
unresolved.

---

# 109. AT_RISK

Goal remains Active but evidence indicates material risk of failure.

---

# 110. SUSPENDED

Goal pursuit is intentionally paused.

---

# 111. SUPERSEDED

A newer approved Goal replaces this Goal's active strategic role.

---

# 112. CANCELLED

Authorized decision terminated future pursuit.

---

# 113. FAILED

Goal success criteria cannot be satisfied under current approved
conditions or declared failure criteria were met.

---

# 114. COMPLETING

Goal execution activities appear finished, but outcome verification remains.

---

# 115. COMPLETED

Required success and completion validation have passed.

---

≠

# 226. Goal Anti-Gaming

Do not improve Goal metrics by:

- creating many trivial Goals;
- splitting one Goal into easy Child Goals merely to inflate completion;
- lowering Success Criteria after poor performance without approval;
- deleting failed Goals;
- hiding superseded Goals;
- redefining failure as success after the fact;
- choosing vanity KPIs;
- replacing outcome metrics with activity metrics;
- changing metric formulas without evidence;
- using stale metrics while claiming current success;
- counting Plan completion as Goal completion;
- counting Task completion as Goal completion;
- counting Agent self-report as outcome evidence;
- changing Customer/Tenant scope to find better metrics;
- ignoring mandatory constraints;
- hiding Goal Drift;
- changing Goal priority without authority;
- suppressing at-risk status;
- cancelling likely-to-fail Goals solely to improve success rate.
