# Mianx.ai AI Operating System Reasoning Strategies Standard

> **This document defines the governed target-state Reasoning Strategies
> standard for the Mianx.ai AI Operating System.**
>
> **A Reasoning Strategy is a governed method for how an approved Reasoning
> Request may be analyzed. A Strategy determines method, not enterprise
> authority.**
>
> **Strategy selection must consider the reasoning objective, evidence
> quality, uncertainty, risk, Project, Customer, Tenant, Model
> capabilities, Tool permissions, cost, time, latency, and execution
> constraints.**
>
> **No strategy is universally correct. Deduction, induction, abduction,
> analogy, causal reasoning, counterfactual reasoning, comparison,
> constraint reasoning, probability, scenario reasoning, diagnosis,
> critique, verification, Multi-Model analysis, and Multi-Agent analysis
> have different strengths, assumptions, and failure modes.**
>
> **More reasoning passes, Models, Agents, tokens, sources, or Tool calls
> do not automatically produce better reasoning. Strategy execution must be
> bounded by explicit budgets, stopping conditions, progress requirements,
> Security, Governance, and evidence quality.**
>
> **Self-critique and self-verification are useful techniques but do not
> provide independent verification merely because the same Model reviews
> its own output. Independent evidence and independent verification should
> be used where risk requires them.**
>
> **Multi-Model or Multi-Agent consensus does not create truth, approval,
> Founder authority, Customer authority, Tool authority, Model authority,
> or Production authorization.**
>
> **This document defines target-state requirements. It does not prove that
> a Strategy Registry, Strategy Selector, Strategy Executor, Multi-Pass
> Reasoner, Multi-Model Runtime, Multi-Agent Debate Runtime, Verification
> Runtime, Reasoning Budget Manager, Strategy Evaluation Runtime, or
> Production Reasoning Strategies system currently exists.**

# 253. Strategy Anti-Gaming

Do not improve Strategy metrics by:

- selecting the cheapest Strategy regardless of risk;
- suppressing expensive mandatory Verification;
- hiding Strategy switches;
- hiding failed strategies;
- stopping early to improve latency;
- extending reasoning to inflate "depth";
- adding unnecessary Models;
- adding unnecessary Agents;
- inflating Tool calls;
- reducing Human escalations artificially;
- converting unresolved contradictions into consensus;
- discarding minority evidence;
- changing weights until preferred option wins;
- re-running Models until a desired answer appears;
- counting self-verification as independent verification;
- hiding budget overruns;
- changing Strategy Version without history;
- using cross-Customer cached reasoning;
- lowering eligibility controls to improve success rate.
