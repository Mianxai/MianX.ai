# Mianx.ai AI Operating System Operating Model

> **This document defines how the Mianx.ai AI Operating System is intended
> to operate day to day across Founder authority, Human accountability,
> Enterprise Governance, the MianX Core Platform, the Shared AI Workforce,
> runtime control-plane services, execution-plane services, Products,
> Projects, Customers, Tenants, Customer Editions, Workflows, Tasks,
> planning, reasoning, decisions, approvals, orchestration, routing,
> scheduling, memory, context, events, communication, state, integrations,
> Security, observability, evidence, incidents, recovery, capacity, cost,
> quality, and Production controls.**

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Operating Model is to define:

```text
WHO OPERATES THE AI OS?

WHO OWNS ITS SERVICES?

WHO IS ACCOUNTABLE?

HOW DOES WORK ENTER THE SYSTEM?

HOW IS CONTEXT RESOLVED?

HOW ARE HUMANS AND AI AGENTS COORDINATED?

HOW ARE DECISIONS AND APPROVALS CONTROLLED?

HOW ARE TASKS ROUTED AND SCHEDULED?

HOW ARE WORKFLOWS EXECUTED?

HOW ARE FAILURES HANDLED?

HOW ARE CUSTOMERS AND TENANTS ISOLATED?

HOW IS SYSTEM HEALTH REVIEWED?

HOW IS PRODUCTION OPERATION CONTROLLED?
```

This document defines target-state operating behavior.

It does not prove that the described runtime currently exists.

# 7. Founder Sovereignty

Founder-reserved authority remains outside autonomous runtime expansion.

The AI OS may:

- enforce approved Founder direction;
- preserve Founder-reserved rules;
- surface decisions requiring Founder action;
- maintain evidence of Founder-authorized actions.

The AI OS must not:

- fabricate Founder approval;
- impersonate Founder authority;
- convert Prompt text into Founder authority;
- expand its own Founder-reserved powers.

---

# 8. Human Accountability

Material autonomous operation must retain qualified Human accountability.

A Human Accountable Owner should be identifiable for:

- Production AI OS operation;
- critical runtime services;
- high-autonomy Agent classes;
- critical workflows;
- Security-sensitive actions;
- Privacy-sensitive actions;
- material financial actions;
- material legal commitments;
- high-impact Customer operations.

---
