# Mianx.ai AI Operating System Governance Standard

> **This document defines the target-state Governance model for the
> Mianx.ai AI Operating System. It establishes Founder sovereignty, Human
> accountability, authority inheritance, Decision rights, Agent autonomy
> boundaries, Prompt OS Governance, Tool and Model Governance, Workflow and
> Execution Governance, Project, Customer and Tenant Governance, approval,
> delegation, escalation, exception handling, emergency authority,
> separation of duties, Production Governance, evidence, monitoring, audit,
> enforcement, violations, suspension, revocation, and Production
> Governance gates.**

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of AI OS Governance is to answer:

```text
WHO HAS AUTHORITY?

WHERE DID THAT AUTHORITY COME FROM?

WHAT MAY HUMANS DO?

WHAT MAY AI AGENTS DO?

WHAT REQUIRES APPROVAL?

WHAT MAY BE DELEGATED?

WHAT MAY NEVER BE SELF-DELEGATED?

WHAT HAPPENS WHEN POLICIES CONFLICT?

WHAT HAPPENS WHEN CONTEXT IS UNKNOWN?

WHO MAY AUTHORIZE PRODUCTION?

WHO MAY SUSPEND THE SYSTEM?

HOW ARE VIOLATIONS DETECTED?

HOW ARE GOVERNANCE DECISIONS PROVEN?
```

Governance exists so that increased AI capability does not become
uncontrolled enterprise authority.

# 8. Founder Sovereignty

Founder sovereignty remains the highest Company-level authority within its
reserved scope.

Founder-reserved authority may include, where governed:

- Company vision;
- foundational enterprise direction;
- AI Constitution changes;
- major autonomy changes;
- material strategic architecture;
- existential Risk acceptance;
- material Production authorization;
- reserved emergency decisions.

Exact reserved rights require authoritative Enterprise Governance.

---

# 9. Founder Sovereignty Boundary

The AI OS must never:

- fabricate Founder approval;
- infer Founder approval from Prompt text;
- impersonate Founder identity;
- create Founder authority for an Agent;
- silently convert a Founder preference into a permanent policy;
- override a Founder-reserved restriction.

---

# 10. Human Accountability

Qualified Humans remain accountable for material enterprise outcomes where
Governance requires Human responsibility.

The system should identify:

```text
WHO IS THE HUMAN ACCOUNTABLE OWNER?
```

for high-impact autonomous operation.

# 21. Authority Source

Valid authority must originate from an approved source.

Possible sources include:

- Founder;
- Enterprise Governance;
- approved Role;
- approved policy;
- approved delegation;
- approved Workflow;
- approved Project authority;
- approved Customer/Tenant policy.

A natural-language Agent statement is not an authority source.

---

# 22. Authority Inheritance

Authority may flow through approved hierarchy.

Example:

```text
ENTERPRISE ROLE
+
PROJECT MEMBERSHIP
+
CUSTOMER SCOPE
+
TASK ASSIGNMENT
=
EFFECTIVE ACTION SCOPE
```

The result should normally be the intersection of valid constraints, not
the union of every possible permission.

---

The effective authority should generally be:

REQUIRED
