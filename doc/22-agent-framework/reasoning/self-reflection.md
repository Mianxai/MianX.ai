# Mianx.ai Agent Self-Reflection

> **This document defines how one individual Mianx.ai Agent reviews its
> own attributable work after an output, decision, plan, Task attempt,
> execution attempt, failure, validation result, evaluation, or other
> governed event.**
>
> Self-Reflection should help the system answer:
>
> ```text
> WHAT WAS EXPECTED?
>
> WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED?
>
> WHAT EVIDENCE EXISTS?
>
> WHAT WAS CORRECT?
>
> WHAT WAS WRONG?
>
> WHAT WAS UNKNOWN?
>
> WHICH ASSUMPTIONS HELD?
>
> WHICH ASSUMPTIONS FAILED?
>
> WHICH CLAIMS WERE UNSUPPORTED?
>
> WAS CONFIDENCE JUSTIFIED?
>
> WAS ESCALATION MISSED?
>
> DID SECURITY OR SCOPE DRIFT OCCUR?
>
> WAS THE RESULT VERIFIED?
>
> WHAT SHOULD BE CORRECTED?
>
> WHAT SHOULD BE LEARNED?
>
> WHAT SHOULD BE PROPOSED
> FOR GOVERNED IMPROVEMENT?
> ```
>
> Self-Reflection must never conclude:
>
> ```text
> "I FOUND A BETTER WAY,
> THEREFORE
> I MAY MODIFY MYSELF
> AND USE IT."
> ```
>
> Permanent rule:
>
> ```text
> SELF-REFLECTION
> MAY
> IDENTIFY,
> EXPLAIN,
> CLASSIFY,
> RECOMMEND,
> AND PROPOSE.
>
> SELF-REFLECTION
> MUST NOT
> SELF-APPROVE,
> SELF-MODIFY,
> SELF-PROMOTE,
> ERASE FAILURE,
> OR
> EXPAND AUTHORITY.
> ```
>
> Runtime Reflection Engine, automatic Reflection triggers, error
> classifier, root-cause engine, calibration feedback loop, recurring
> pattern detector, Memory-candidate admission, learning integration,
> Prompt/Skill/Capability improvement pipelines, Project/Customer/Tenant
> reflection isolation, and Production Self-Reflection remain
> `NOT_PROVEN` unless implementation Evidence exists.

# 2. Self-Reflection Mission

The mission is:

> **Enable Mianx.ai Agents to review attributable outcomes and
> reasoning quality, expose errors and uncertainty, preserve negative
> Evidence, identify recurring weaknesses, and produce governed
> correction or improvement candidates without allowing retrospective
> intelligence to become a hidden self-modification or authority-
> escalation mechanism.**

# 5. What Is Self-Reflection?

Self-Reflection is:

> **A governed retrospective review of one Agent's attributable work,
> reasoning, decisions, plans or execution results against expected
> outcomes, Evidence, constraints and applicable policy.**

---

# 6. What Self-Reflection Is Not

It is not:

```text
SELF-TRAINING WITHOUT GOVERNANCE

SELF-MODIFICATION

SELF-PROMOTION

SELF-APPROVAL

SELF-ASSIGNED CAPABILITY

SELF-ASSIGNED TOOL ACCESS

SELF-WRITTEN POLICY

AUTOMATIC MEMORY TRUTH

AUTOMATIC ROOT-CAUSE PROOF

AUTOMATIC RISK ACCEPTANCE

HISTORY REWRITING

FAILURE DELETION

PRIVATE CHAIN-OF-THOUGHT ARCHIVING
```


# 27. Negative Evidence Preservation

Negative Evidence should not be deleted merely because it harms Agent
performance scores or contradicts prior conclusions.

```text
≠
```
