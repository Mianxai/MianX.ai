# Mianx.ai Agent Goal Planning

> **This document defines how an individual Mianx.ai Agent reasons about
> governed desired outcomes before those outcomes become Tasks or
> execution plans.**
>
> Goal Planning should answer:
>
> ```text
> WHAT OUTCOME IS DESIRED?
>
> WHO OR WHAT AUTHORIZED THIS OUTCOME
> TO ENTER THE PLANNING PROCESS?
>
> WHY DOES IT MATTER?
>
> WHAT SCOPE DOES IT BELONG TO?
>
> WHAT DOES SUCCESS MEAN?
>
> WHAT CONSTRAINTS APPLY?
>
> WHAT OTHER GOALS DOES IT DEPEND ON?
>
> WHAT GOALS CONFLICT WITH IT?
>
> WHAT PRIORITY DOES IT HAVE?
>
> WHAT EVIDENCE SUPPORTS THAT PRIORITY?
>
> IS IT FEASIBLE?
>
> WHAT RISKS EXIST?
>
> WHAT MUST BE APPROVED BEFORE EXECUTION?
>
> WHAT SHOULD BE DECOMPOSED NEXT?
> ```
>
> Goal Planning must never conclude:
>
> ```text
> "THIS IS A GOAL,
> THEREFORE
> I MAY EXECUTE IT."
> ```
>
> Permanent rule:
>
> ```text
> GOAL
> =
> GOVERNED
> DESIRED OUTCOME
>
> NOT
>
> EXECUTION AUTHORITY
> ```
>
> Runtime Goal Store, Goal lifecycle enforcement, Goal authority
> provenance, hierarchy enforcement, conflict detection, prioritization,
> feasibility resolution, Goal drift detection, Project/Customer/Tenant
> isolation, Task Planning handoff, and Production Goal enforcement
> remain `NOT_PROVEN` unless implementation Evidence exists.

# 2. Goal Planning Mission

The mission is:

> **Convert desired outcomes into explicit, scoped, traceable,
> prioritized, conflict-aware and evidence-aware Goal artifacts before
> detailed Task or Execution Planning begins, while preventing Agent
> self-created objectives, urgency, hierarchy, Customer pressure,
> Memory, or AI-generated suggestions from becoming hidden authority.**

# 5. Goal Planning vs Execution Planning

Goal Planning asks:

```text
WHAT OUTCOME
SHOULD BE PURSUED?
```

Execution Planning asks:

```text
HOW COULD
A BOUNDED OUTCOME
BE EXECUTED?
```

See:

```text
./execution-planning.md
```

---

# 6. Goal Planning vs Task Planning

Goal Planning operates at desired-outcome level.

Task Planning converts approved/bounded work into concrete Task units.

See:

```text
./task-planning.md
```

---

# 7. Goal Planning vs Strategy

Enterprise Strategy may define high-level direction.

An Agent Goal should remain traceable to an authorized strategic,
operational, Project, Customer, or Task context where required.

---

# 8. Strategy Boundary

```text
STRATEGICALLY ALIGNED
≠
AUTHORIZED TO EXECUTE
```

---

# 9. Goal Planning vs Reasoning

Reasoning may help interpret a Goal.

The required enterprise artifact is the explicit Goal definition,
rationale summary, assumptions, risks, dependencies and Evidence—not
private chain-of-thought.

---

# 10. Goal Definition

A Goal is:

> **A Versioned, governed statement of a desired future outcome inside a
> defined scope, together with sufficient provenance and constraints to
> determine whether and how it should proceed into further planning.**

---

# 11. Goal vs Objective

Within this framework, `Goal` is the governed Goal artifact.

An objective may be a human-language desired outcome before normalization.
