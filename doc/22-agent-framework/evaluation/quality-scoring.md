# Mianx.ai Agent Quality Scoring

> **This document defines how the quality of individual Mianx.ai Agent
> outputs, results, artifacts, decisions, recommendations, analyses,
> plans, communications, and other governed work products is evaluated
> and scored.**
>
> Quality scoring exists to answer:
>
> ```text
> HOW GOOD
> IS THIS SPECIFIC OUTPUT
> FOR ITS DEFINED PURPOSE
> UNDER ITS DEFINED REQUIREMENTS?
> ```
>
> It does not independently answer:
>
> ```text
> WAS THE ACTION AUTHORIZED?
>
> DID THE EXTERNAL SIDE EFFECT ACTUALLY OCCUR?
>
> IS THE AGENT SAFE FOR PRODUCTION?
>
> SHOULD THE AGENT HAVE MORE AUTONOMY?
>
> SHOULD THE AGENT RECEIVE MORE PERMISSIONS?
> ```
>
> Therefore:
>
> ```text
> QUALITY SCORE
> ≠
> AUTHORIZATION
>
> QUALITY SCORE
> ≠
> VERIFIED SUCCESS
>
> QUALITY SCORE
> ≠
> PRODUCTION FITNESS
>
> QUALITY SCORE
> ≠
> AUTONOMY
>
> QUALITY SCORE
> ≠
> AUTHORITY
> ```
>
> Most importantly:
>
> ```text
> HIGH AGGREGATE QUALITY SCORE
> MUST NEVER
> ERASE
> A CRITICAL SECURITY,
> AUTHORIZATION,
> ISOLATION,
> FABRICATED-EVIDENCE,
> OR CRITICAL-CORRECTNESS FAILURE
> ```
>
> Runtime scorers, rubrics, thresholds, judge Models, dashboards,
> calibration datasets, automated gates, and Production quality
> verification remain `NOT_PROVEN` unless supported by implementation
> and Evidence.

# 2. Quality Scoring Mission

The mission is:

> **Create a consistent, explainable, evidence-backed and
> risk-sensitive mechanism for assessing Agent output quality without
> converting subjective scoring, average scores, Model judgment, or
> Agent self-confidence into trusted truth or Security authority.**
