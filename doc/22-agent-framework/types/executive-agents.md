# Mianx.ai Executive Agents

> **This document defines the Executive Agent Type within the
> Mianx.ai individual-Agent Framework.**
>
> Executive Agents exist to support high-level:
>
> ```text
> STRATEGIC ANALYSIS
>
> GOAL INTERPRETATION
>
> PRIORITIZATION
>
> PORTFOLIO COORDINATION
>
> EXECUTIVE DECISION SUPPORT
>
> CROSS-FUNCTIONAL SYNTHESIS
>
> DELEGATION
>
> ESCALATION
>
> RISK VISIBILITY
>
> ORGANIZATIONAL ALIGNMENT
> ```
>
> They must never be interpreted as:
>
> ```text
> DIGITAL FOUNDERS
>
> UNRESTRICTED CEOs
>
> AUTOMATIC HUMAN EXECUTIVES
>
> PLATFORM ADMINISTRATORS
>
> UNIVERSAL APPROVERS
>
> UNLIMITED BUDGET OWNERS
>
> POLICY OVERRIDERS
>
> RISK ACCEPTORS
>
> CROSS-TENANT SUPERUSERS
> ```
>
> Permanent rule:
>
> ```text
> EXECUTIVE AGENT
> =
> STRATEGIC
> DECISION-SUPPORT
> AND
> COORDINATION
> AGENT TYPE
>
> NOT
>
> UNBOUNDED
> EXECUTIVE AUTHORITY.
> ```

# 5. What Is an Executive Agent?

An Executive Agent is:

> **An individual-Agent architectural Type designed to perform
> strategic synthesis, executive-level analysis, planning support,
> prioritization support, cross-functional coordination, bounded
> delegation and escalation under explicitly governed organizational,
> security, budget, decision, approval and Production constraints.**

---

```text
FOUNDER

CEO
```

A separate Role, authority and authorization model is required.

The Founder remains a separate Human authority.

