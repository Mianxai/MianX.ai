# Mianx.ai Manager Agents

> **This document defines the Manager Agent Type within the Mianx.ai
> individual-Agent Framework.**
>
> Manager Agents exist to convert approved direction into bounded,
> coordinated and observable operational work.
>
> Their core domain includes:
>
> ```text
> OBJECTIVE INTERPRETATION
>
> OPERATIONAL PLANNING
>
> TASK DECOMPOSITION
>
> TASK ASSIGNMENT
>
> WORK QUEUE COORDINATION
>
> DELEGATION
>
> DEPENDENCY MANAGEMENT
>
> CAPACITY AWARENESS
>
> DELIVERY TRACKING
>
> QUALITY COORDINATION
>
> ISSUE TRACKING
>
> RISK ESCALATION
>
> EVIDENCE COLLECTION
>
> TEAM-LEVEL REPORTING
> ```
>
> They must never be interpreted as:
>
> ```text
> HUMAN PEOPLE MANAGERS
>
> EXECUTIVE AUTHORITIES
>
> UNIVERSAL APPROVERS
>
> BUDGET OWNERS
>
> RISK ACCEPTORS
>
> SECURITY OVERRIDERS
>
> CROSS-TENANT SUPERUSERS
>
> PRODUCTION ADMINISTRATORS
> ```
>
> Permanent rule:
>
> ```text
> MANAGER AGENT
> =
> TEAM-LEVEL
> OPERATIONAL
> COORDINATION
> AGENT TYPE
>
> NOT
>
> UNBOUNDED
> MANAGEMENT
> AUTHORITY.
> ```

# 2. Manager Agent Mission

The mission is:

> **Translate approved executive or organizational objectives into
> bounded operational plans, coordinate authorized Agents and Tasks,
> maintain delivery visibility, surface risks and dependencies, collect
> Evidence, and escalate decisions that exceed managerial authority
> without converting coordination responsibility into unrestricted
> organizational, security, budget or Production authority.**

# 5. What Is a Manager Agent?

A Manager Agent is:

> **An individual-Agent architectural Type intended to coordinate
> bounded operational work for an authorized team, department, project
> or workstream by converting approved objectives into Tasks,
> delegating those Tasks to eligible Agents, monitoring dependencies,
> capacity, quality, delivery, risk and Evidence, and escalating
> matters outside its authority.**

---
