# Purpose

This document defines the official corporate stationery standards for Mianx.ai.

It ensures that every printed and digital business document maintains a consistent, professional, and enterprise-grade visual identity.

---

# Objectives

- Standardize corporate stationery.
- Maintain brand consistency.
- Improve professionalism.
- Support legal and business communications.
- Enable reusable document templates.

---

# Scope

These standards apply to all official stationery, including:

- Letterheads
- Business Cards
- Envelopes
- Invoices
- Quotations
- Purchase Orders
- Contracts
- Certificates

- Employee ID Cards

# Branding Standards

Every stationery item must use:

- Official Logo
- Approved Color Palette
- Official Typography
- Standard Layout
- Approved Icons (where applicable)

---

# Letterhead Standards

Every letterhead should include:

- Company Logo
- Company Name
- Address
- Phone Number
- Email
- Website
- Registration Information (if applicable)

Footer may include:

- Copyright
- Confidentiality Notice
- Social Media Links

---

# Business Card Standards

Front Side

- Logo
- Employee Name
- Designation
- Department

Back Side

- Contact Information
- Website
- QR Code (optional)
- Company Tagline

---

# Envelope Standards

Include:

- Logo
- Return Address
- Company Name
- Brand Colors

---

# Invoice Standards

Every invoice should contain:

- Invoice Number
- Customer Information
- Billing Address
- Products/Services
- Taxes
- Total Amount
- Payment Terms
- Company Information

---

# Quotation Standards

Every quotation should include:

- Quote Number
- Client Information
- Pricing
- Terms & Conditions
- Expiry Date
- Approval Section

---

# Contract Standards

Contracts should include:

- Company Branding
- Document Version
- Approval Signatures
- Legal Clauses
- Footer Information

---

# Certificate Standards

Certificates should contain:

- Logo
- Certificate Title
- Recipient Name
- Description
- Authorized Signature
- Date
- Verification Code (optional)

---

# Email Signature Standards

Every employee email signature should include:

- Full Name
- Designation
- Department
- Company Logo
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- Website
- Social Links (optional)

---

# Employee ID Card

Must include:

- Photo
- Employee Name
- Employee ID
- Department
- QR Code / Barcode
- Company Logo
- Expiry Date (if applicable)

---

# Visitor Badge

Must include:

- Visitor Name
- Host
- Visit Date
- Badge Number
- Company Logo

---

# Design Principles

All stationery should follow:

- Minimal Layout
- High Readability
- Consistent Alignment
- Professional Appearance
- Enterprise Branding

---

# Print Specifications

Recommended:

Paper Sizes

- A4
- A5
- Letter

Print Quality

- 300 DPI minimum

Color Mode

- CMYK for print
- RGB for digital

---

# Digital Formats

Maintain editable versions in:

- DOCX
- PPTX
- FIG
- AI

Export formats:

- PDF
- PNG
- SVG

# Version Control

Every stationery asset should include:

- Version
- Owner

---

# Accessibility

Digital stationery should:

- Support screen readers where applicable.
- Use readable typography.
- Maintain sufficient color contrast.
- Be printable without losing readability.

---

# Quality Checklist

Before release:

- [ ] Official logo used
- [ ] Brand colors verified
- [ ] Typography approved
- [ ] Contact information accurate
- [ ] Layout reviewed
- [ ] Print quality checked
- [ ] Source files archived
- [ ] Final approval completed

# Common Mistakes

Avoid:

- Outdated logos
- Incorrect company information
- Inconsistent spacing
- Low-resolution graphics
- Missing version control
- Unapproved templates

- Use centralized templates.
- Keep all stationery synchronized with brand updates.
- Archive previous versions instead of overwriting them.
- Store editable source files securely.
- Review templates annually or after major branding updates.
