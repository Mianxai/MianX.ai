# Purpose

This document defines the official standards for designing, producing, approving, storing, and distributing all Mianx.ai branded merchandise.

The goal is to ensure every physical product reflects the company's premium enterprise identity.

- Standardize merchandise production.
- Ensure premium product quality.
- Protect brand identity.
- Support marketing and employee engagement.
- Create reusable merchandise templates.

# Branding Standards

Every merchandise item must use:

- Official Logo
- Approved Brand Colors
- Official Typography
- Correct Logo Placement
- Approved Graphics

No unofficial branding is allowed.

---

# Logo Usage

The logo must:

- Maintain correct proportions.
- Follow minimum size rules.
- Respect clear space requirements.
- Use approved color variations.

Never:

- Stretch
- Rotate
- Recolor
- Add effects

---

# Color Standards

Use only:

- Official Brand Colors
- Approved Neutral Colors
- Approved Accent Colors

Production colors must match brand specifications.

---

# Typography Standards

Use only approved fonts.

Maintain:

- Consistent hierarchy
- Readable sizing
- Proper spacing

---

# Product Quality

Every product should meet enterprise-quality standards.

Requirements include:

- Durable materials
- High-resolution printing
- Accurate colors
- Long-lasting finishes
- Comfortable materials (for apparel)

---

# Printing Methods

Approved methods include:

- Screen Printing
- Embroidery
- Heat Transfer
- UV Printing
- Laser Engraving
- Digital Printing

The method should match the product type and expected durability.

---

# Apparel Standards

Applicable to:

- T-Shirts
- Hoodies
- Jackets
- Polo Shirts

Requirements:

- Premium fabric
- Consistent sizing
- Quality stitching
- Comfortable fit
- Durable branding

---

# Packaging Standards

Official packaging should include:

- Brand Identity
- Product Label
- Care Instructions (if applicable)
- Sustainability Information (where applicable)

---

# Sustainability

Whenever possible:

- Use recyclable packaging.
- Reduce plastic usage.
- Prefer sustainable materials.
- Minimize waste during production.

- Off-brand colors
