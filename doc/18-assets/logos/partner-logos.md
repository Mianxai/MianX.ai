# Purpose

This document defines the official standards for using logos belonging to external organizations within the Mianx.ai ecosystem.

It ensures that partner logos are used only with valid permission, correct attribution, approved visual treatment, complete licensing records, and appropriate security controls.

---

# Objectives

- Protect third-party intellectual property.
- Prevent unauthorized logo usage.
- Standardize partner logo storage.
- Maintain accurate licensing records.
- Support professional co-branding.
- Define approval and expiry requirements.
- Prevent outdated partnership claims.
- Preserve a complete audit trail.
- Enable safe use by AI agents and automation systems.

# Core Principle

A partner logo remains the property of its respective owner.

Mianx.ai may use a partner logo only when:

- Permission exists.
- The intended use is approved.
- Applicable terms are followed.
- The asset is current.
- The partnership status is valid.
- The logo is used without unauthorized modification.

Possession of a logo file does not automatically grant permission to publish it.

# Partnership Status

Every partner logo must be linked to an official relationship status.

Approved statuses may include:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Proposed | Relationship is under discussion |
| Pending Approval | Agreement or permission is under review |
| Active | Current approved relationship |
| Suspended | Usage is temporarily restricted |
| Expired | Agreement or permission has ended |
| Terminated | Relationship has been formally ended |
| Archived | Asset is retained for historical records |

Only logos linked to an **Active** relationship may be used in new public materials unless a documented exception exists.

# Permission Requirements

Before using a partner logo, confirm one of the following:

- Written permission
- Contractual usage rights
- Published partner brand guidelines
- Approved certification entitlement
- Valid sponsorship agreement
- Approved marketplace agreement
- Joint marketing agreement
- Official media kit terms

Permission evidence must be stored with the logo metadata.

---

# Permission Evidence

Acceptable evidence may include:

- Signed Agreement
- Email Approval
- Partner Portal Authorization
- Brand Usage License
- Marketing Addendum
- Sponsorship Contract
- Certification Record
- Official Public Brand Guidelines

A verbal approval should not be treated as sufficient for high-visibility public use.

---

# Permission Scope

Permission must clearly identify:

- Who may use the logo
- Where it may be used
- Why it may be used
- Approved audience
- Approved channels
- Approved territories
- Start date
- Expiry date
- Modification restrictions
- Attribution requirements
- Approval requirements
- Removal obligations

---

# Logo Source Priority

Use logo assets from the following sources in order of preference:

1. Official Partner Media Kit
2. Official Partner Brand Portal
3. Directly Supplied Partner Files
4. Contract-approved Shared Repository
5. Official Public Brand Guidelines

Do not use logos copied from:

- Search-engine previews
- Screenshots
- Social media images
- Unofficial websites
- Random icon libraries
- Third-party logo collections
- Low-resolution marketing graphics

---

# Accepted File Formats

Preferred vector formats:

- SVG
- EPS
- PDF
- AI

Accepted raster formats:

- PNG
- WebP
- JPG, only where transparency is unnecessary

Original source files should be preserved when legally permitted.

---

# Logo Integrity

Partner logos must be used exactly as approved.

Never:

- Redraw the logo.
- Change its colors.
- Change its proportions.
- Replace its typography.
- Add effects.
- Add shadows.
- Remove trademark symbols.
- Crop protected areas.
- Combine it with unofficial symbols.
- Convert it into a Mianx.ai-style logo.
- Recreate it using generative AI.

---

# Clear Space

Every partner logo must follow the clear-space requirements defined by the partner.

Where no explicit guidance exists:

- Maintain sufficient separation from nearby elements.
- Avoid visual crowding.
- Preserve recognizability.
- Request clarification for high-profile public use.

Mianx.ai internal spacing rules must not override stricter partner requirements.

---

# Minimum Size

Use the partner's approved minimum-size specification.

Where no minimum size is documented:

- Test readability.
- Preserve trademark details.
- Avoid reducing the logo beyond recognition.
- Obtain partner approval for prominent public usage.

---

# Background Usage

Use only partner-approved background variants.

Possible variants may include:

- Full-color on light background
- Full-color on dark background
- White reversed logo
- Black monochrome logo
- Approved single-color version

Do not automatically invert or recolor a partner logo through CSS filters.

---

# Color Usage

Partner colors must remain unchanged unless written permission allows an alternate treatment.

Avoid:

- Replacing partner colors with Mianx.ai colors
- Applying gradients
- Applying transparency
- Using low-contrast backgrounds
- Converting to monochrome without approval
- Matching all partner logos to one visual color

Visual consistency must never override legal brand requirements.

---

# Trademark Symbols

Registered trademark, trademark, and service-mark symbols must be preserved where required.

Examples may include:

```text
®

™

℠
```

Do not remove such marks merely to simplify a layout.

---

# Partner Name Usage

Use the official legal or approved display name.

Maintain:

- Correct capitalization
- Correct punctuation
- Correct product naming
- Correct trademark symbols
- Correct company suffix where required

Do not invent abbreviations or shorten names without approval.

---

# Co-branding Principles

Co-branding must communicate the relationship clearly without suggesting false ownership, endorsement, or control.

Approved relationship labels may include:

```text
Technology Partner

Integration Partner

Powered by

In Collaboration With

Supported by

Sponsored by

Certified Partner
```

The selected wording must accurately match the agreement.

---

# Co-branding Hierarchy

The visual hierarchy should reflect the actual relationship.

Example:

```text
Mianx.ai

in collaboration with

Partner Name
```

For partner-led material:

```text
Partner Name

with technology from

Mianx.ai
```

Hierarchy must be agreed upon before publication.

---

# Equal Partnership Layout

Where equal partnership is contractually approved:

```text
[Mianx.ai Logo]    [Partner Logo]
```

Requirements:

- Balanced visual weight
- Approved separator
- Correct clear space
- No unauthorized logo merging
- No new combined symbol
- No implied acquisition or ownership

---

# Unequal Partnership Layout

Where Mianx.ai is the primary brand:

```text
[Mianx.ai Primary Logo]

Technology Partner:

[Partner Logo]
```

The partner logo should remain visible but secondary.

---

# Logo Separation

Never merge two independent logos into a single permanent logo file unless a jointly approved lockup has been created.

Maintain separate:

- Source files
- Metadata
- Ownership
- Version records
- Usage permissions

---

# Multiple Partner Logos

When displaying multiple partner logos:

- Use a consistent visual area.
- Preserve each logo's proportions.
- Avoid making one logo appear artificially larger.
- Follow sponsorship tiers where applicable.
- Use neutral spacing.
- Maintain readable backgrounds.
- Confirm ordering rules.

Do not force all logos into identical dimensions if this distorts visual balance.

---

# Partner Logo Wall

A public partner logo wall must be governed carefully.

Before adding a logo:

- Verify active relationship.
- Confirm public usage permission.
- Confirm current logo version.
- Confirm approved relationship label.
- Confirm correct destination link.
- Confirm expiry date.
- Record approval.

Before removing a logo:

- Confirm relationship status.
- Preserve historical records.
- Remove public references by the agreed deadline.
- Archive the asset rather than deleting it.

---

# Client Logo Usage

Client logos require additional privacy and confidentiality controls.

Before public use, confirm:

- The client relationship may be disclosed.
- The client has approved the use.
- The project is not confidential.
- The case study is approved.
- The final layout is approved where required.
- The logo version is current.
- Removal conditions are documented.

A completed project does not automatically grant public logo usage rights.

In architecture diagrams, third-party logos may identify external services.

Requirements:

- Maintain readable labels.

- Respect internal confidentiality.

# Public Website Usage

Before publishing a partner logo on a public website:

- Verify active permission.

- Verify current logo.
- Verify approved text.

- Verify expiry date.

# Accessibility

Every published partner logo should include meaningful alternative text.

Recommended patterns:

```text

```

For relationship context:

```text

```

# Storage Structure

Recommended structure:

```text
partners/

└── partner-name/
    ├── approved/
    ├── light/
    ├── dark/
    ├── monochrome/
    ├── co-branding/
    ├── permissions/
    ├── guidelines/
    ├── metadata/
    ├── exports/
    ├── deprecated/
    └── source-files/
```

Create only the folders required for actual assets and records.

---

# Permission Document Storage

Permission evidence should be stored securely.

Examples:

```text
partners/
└── partner-name/
    └── permissions/
        ├── agreement-reference.md
        ├── approval-email-reference.md
        ├── usage-scope.md
        └── expiry-record.md
```

Sensitive contracts should be referenced securely rather than copied into public documentation folders.

---

# File Naming Convention

Use lowercase kebab-case.

Pattern:

```text
{partner-name}-{asset-type}-{variant}-{version}.{extension}
```

Examples:

```text
partner-name-logo-primary-v1.svg

partner-name-logo-white-v1.svg

partner-name-certification-badge-v2.png

partner-name-co-brand-lockup-v1.svg
```

Avoid:

```text
partner-logo-final.png

new-vendor-logo.svg

partner-logo-latest-final.ai

logo-from-google.png
```

---

LOGO-PARTNER-{PARTNER-CODE}-{NUMBER}

# Source File Requirements

For every partner logo, preserve:

- Original supplied file
- Original file name
- Source date
- Source organization
- Download or delivery reference
- Associated guidelines
- Permission evidence
- Internal approved copy
- Generated technical exports, if permitted

Do not modify the original supplied file.

---

# Original and Working Copies

Maintain clear separation between:

```text
Original Partner Asset

↓

Approved Working Copy

↓

Approved Export

↓

Published Usage
```

The original must remain unchanged.

---

# Export Rules

Technical exports may be created only when permitted.

Allowed technical changes may include:

- Format conversion
- Transparent-background export
- Size-specific raster export
- File optimization
- Metadata cleanup

Technical processing must not change:

- Shape
- Color
- Typography
- Spacing
- Trademark marks
- Proportions

---

# Export Quality Requirements

Before release, verify:

- Logo matches the original.
- Color values remain correct.
- Transparency behaves correctly.
- No clipping exists.
- No unauthorized whitespace removal occurred.
- Trademark symbols remain intact.
- File naming is correct.
- Metadata is complete.
- Permission scope permits the export.
- Target usage is approved.

---

# Versioning Policy

Use internal semantic versioning for the stored asset record.

| Change Type | Example | Meaning |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| Major | 2.0.0 | Partner supplied a significantly redesigned logo |
| Minor | 1.1.0 | New approved variant or co-branding layout |
| Patch | 1.0.1 | Technical export correction |

The partner's own versioning should be recorded separately where available.

---

# Relationship Expiry

When a partnership expires:

- Stop new public usage.
- Review existing published assets.
- Follow contractual removal timelines.
- Remove logo walls and partner claims where required.
- Archive source files and metadata.
- Update relationship status.
- Preserve historical records.
- Notify responsible teams.

---

# Permission Expiry

Permission may expire before the wider relationship ends.

In such cases:

- Suspend affected usage.
- Request renewal.
- Remove unauthorized public assets.
- Record the suspension.
- Preserve evidence.
- Re-enable only after renewed approval.

---

# Logo Update Process

When a partner supplies a new logo:

- Verify authenticity.
- Record receipt date.
- Compare with the current version.
- Review new brand guidelines.
- Update metadata.
- Generate permitted exports.
- Identify affected materials.
- Plan replacement.
- Archive the old version.
- Notify internal users.

---

# Deprecated Partner Logos

Deprecated partner logos must:

- Be moved out of active directories.
- Be marked clearly.
- Include a replacement reference.
- Include deprecation date.
- Include removal deadline.
- Remain available for historical audits.

Deprecated logos must not appear in new materials.

- Restrict repository access.

Avoid:

- Copying logos from image search.
- Assuming integration equals partnership.
- Publishing client logos without permission.
- Using expired certification badges.
- Recoloring third-party logos.
- Creating fake co-branding lockups.

- Storing logos without permission evidence.
- Failing to track expiry dates.
- Leaving terminated partners on the public website.
- Using confidential partner names in public material.
- Allowing AI agents to select unverified logo files.
- Treating internal vendor use as public marketing permission.
