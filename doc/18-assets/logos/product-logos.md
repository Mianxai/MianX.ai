# Purpose

This document defines the official standards for every product logo created under the Mianx.ai brand.

It ensures that each product maintains a recognizable identity while remaining visually connected to the parent Mianx.ai ecosystem.

---

# Objectives

- Standardize product logo creation.
- Maintain connection with the parent brand.
- Prevent inconsistent product identities.
- Support multiple products and platforms.
- Enable responsive logo usage.
- Preserve editable source files.
- Maintain complete product logo history.
- Support automated asset discovery by AI agents.

---

# Product Brand Architecture

Mianx.ai products should follow an endorsed brand architecture.

```text
Mianx.ai
│
├── Product A
├── Product B
├── Product C
└── Product D
```

Every product may have its own identity, but it must remain visibly associated with Mianx.ai.

# Primary Product Logo

The primary logo is the default representation of the product.

Use it for:

- Product Website
- Product Dashboard
- Launch Material
- Documentation
- Sales Material
- Product Presentations
- Marketplace Listings

---

# Secondary Product Logo

The secondary logo is used when space or layout limitations prevent use of the primary logo.

Use it for:

- Compact Headers
- Mobile Navigation
- Sidebars
- Small Cards
- Embedded Interfaces

---

# Product Symbol

The product symbol is a simplified visual mark.

Use it for:

- Application Icons
- Favicons
- Navigation Items
- Notifications
- Product Avatars
- Marketplace Cards

The symbol must remain recognizable at small sizes.

---

# Product Wordmark

The wordmark is the text-based product identity.

Use it for:

- Legal Documents
- Product Documentation
- Email Headers
- Reports
- Simple Layouts

---

# Product Naming Requirements

Before logo design begins, the product name must be:

- Approved by Product Leadership
- Reviewed by Brand
- Reviewed for duplication
- Reviewed for trademark risk
- Documented in the product registry

Temporary internal code names must not be used in public logo assets unless formally approved.

---

# Naming Pattern

Recommended product naming patterns:

```text
Mianx.ai [Product Name]
```

```text
[Product Name] by Mianx.ai
```

```text
[Product Name]
A Mianx.ai Product
```

The selected pattern must remain consistent across all product assets.

---

# Visual Relationship Rules

Product logos should share selected elements with the parent brand.

These may include:

- Typography
- Shape Language
- Grid System
- Spacing System
- Color Logic
- Corner Radius
- Icon Style
- Endorsement Placement

Products should not appear unrelated to the Mianx.ai ecosystem.

---

# Color System

Each product may receive an approved accent color.

The product accent color must:

- Complement the Mianx.ai palette.
- Remain accessible.
- Avoid conflict with semantic UI colors.
- Be documented in the product design system.
- Be approved by the Brand Team.

The corporate Mianx.ai logo must always retain its approved colors.

---

# Typography

Product logos must use:

- Approved brand typography, or
- A formally approved product type treatment.

Typography must remain:

- Readable
- Scalable
- Distinct
- Consistent
- Legally licensed

Do not use unlicensed or decorative fonts without approval.

---

# Shape Language

Product symbols should follow a shared visual system.

Recommended characteristics:

- Simple Geometry
- Clear Silhouette
- Balanced Proportions
- Minimal Detail
- Scalable Construction
- Recognizable Form

Avoid overly complex symbols that fail at small sizes.

---

# Logo Grid

Every product logo should be constructed on a documented grid.

The grid should define:

- Symbol Proportions
- Internal Spacing
- Wordmark Alignment
- Endorsement Position
- Clear Space
- Responsive Behavior

The original grid specification must be preserved with the source files.

---

# Clear Space

Maintain protected space around the product logo.

Recommended clear-space unit:

```text
X = height of the primary symbol element
```

Minimum protected area:

```text
1 × X on all sides
```

No text, icons, borders, or other graphics should enter this area.

---

# Minimum Size

The exact minimum size must be validated for each product logo.

The validation must confirm:

- Wordmark readability
- Symbol recognition
- Endorsement readability
- Small-screen performance
- Print clarity

Record approved minimum sizes in the product logo metadata.

---

# Responsive Logo System

Product logos should adapt to available space.

| Context | Recommended Variant |
|---------|---------------------|
| Large Marketing Page | Primary Logo |
| Desktop Header | Horizontal Logo |
| Mobile Header | Secondary Logo |
| Sidebar | Product Symbol |
| Browser Tab | Favicon |
| App Launcher | App Icon |
| Small Card | Symbol or Compact Logo |

---

# Light Background Usage

On light backgrounds, use:

- Primary Logo
- Dark Wordmark
- Approved Full-color Symbol

Ensure sufficient visual contrast.

---

# Dark Background Usage

On dark backgrounds, use:

- Light Logo
- Reversed Wordmark
- Approved Light Symbol Variant

Do not automatically invert logo files through CSS or image filters.

Use the officially exported dark-background version.

---

# Monochrome Usage

Use monochrome variants for:

- Single-color Printing
- Embossing
- Engraving
- Fax Documents
- Restricted Production Methods
- Legal Stamps

Only approved black, white, or single-color variants may be used.

---

# Application Icon Standards

Application icons should:

- Use the product symbol.
- Remain recognizable without the wordmark.
- Avoid small text.
- Respect platform safe areas.
- Be tested across required device sizes.
- Use platform-specific exports where necessary.

---

# Favicon Standards

Each product should maintain favicon assets for supported environments.

Typical exports may include:

```text
favicon.svg
favicon.ico
favicon-16.png
favicon-32.png
favicon-48.png
apple-touch-icon.png
```

Actual export requirements must be defined by the target platform.

---

# Product Status Indicators

Product status must not be permanently embedded in the master logo.

Where necessary, status labels may be added separately.

Approved examples:

```text
Alpha
Beta
Preview
Experimental
Deprecated
```

Status labels must be removable without modifying the official product logo.

---

# Co-branding

When a product appears with the Mianx.ai corporate logo:

- Maintain clear hierarchy.
- Use approved spacing.
- Avoid competing logo sizes.
- Keep both identities readable.
- Follow partnership and legal requirements.

Co-branding layouts require Brand Team approval.

---

# Third-party Integration Usage

When product logos appear with external services:

- Use approved exports.
- Follow the external platform's technical requirements.
- Preserve logo proportions.
- Avoid unapproved recoloring.
- Confirm licensing and partnership permissions.

---

```text
    ├── favicons/
    ├── exports/
    ├── metadata/
    └── source-files/
```

Folders should be created only when corresponding assets exist.

---

# File Naming Convention

Use lowercase kebab-case.

Pattern:

```text
{product-name}-{asset-type}-{variant}-{version}.{extension}
```

Examples:

```text
ai-workforce-logo-primary-v1.svg

automation-platform-logo-dark-v1.svg

client-portal-symbol-v1.svg

developer-platform-wordmark-v1.svg

data-platform-app-icon-512-v1.png
```

Do not use names such as:

```text
final-logo.svg

new-logo-2.png

latest-product-logo.svg

logo-fixed-final-final.svg
```

---

# Product Logo Identifier

Every product logo family should receive a unique identifier.

Recommended pattern:

```text
LOGO-PRODUCT-{PRODUCT-CODE}-{NUMBER}
```

Example:

```text
LOGO-PRODUCT-AIW-001
```

# Source Files

Maintain editable master files.

Approved source types may include:

- Figma
- Adobe Illustrator
- Master SVG
- Vector PDF

The source file must remain separate from generated exports.

Never edit a production export and treat it as the new master.

# Versioning Policy

Use semantic versioning for product logo families.

| Change Type | Version Example | Meaning |
|-------------|-----------------|---------|
| Major | 2.0.0 | Significant product rebrand |
| Minor | 1.1.0 | New approved variant |
| Patch | 1.0.1 | Small technical correction |

A major visual redesign must not overwrite the previous version.

---

# Deprecation Policy

When a product logo is retired:

- Mark it as deprecated.
- Record the replacement logo.
- Define a migration deadline.
- Notify affected teams.
- Update product repositories.
- Archive the old master and exports.
- Preserve historical records.

Deprecated logos must not be deleted.

---

# Product Rebranding

A rebrand requires:

- Business justification
- Product approval
- Brand impact assessment
- Migration plan
- Asset replacement plan
- Documentation updates
- Communication plan
- Release approval

# AI Workforce Usage

AI agents may:

- Search approved product logos.
- Select context-appropriate variants.
- Generate required approved export sizes.
- Validate naming and metadata.
- Detect deprecated asset usage.
- Report missing logo variants.

AI agents must not:

- Invent official product logos.
- Recolor approved logos.
- Modify the master symbol.
- publish draft assets.
- bypass approval workflows.
- use deprecated logos in new materials.

# Legal and Licensing

Before publishing a product logo, confirm:

- Product name approval
- Font licensing
- Illustration licensing
- Symbol originality
- Third-party asset permissions
- Trademark review where required
- Geographic usage restrictions where applicable

---

# Accessibility

Every product logo should:

- Remain identifiable at required sizes.
- Maintain suitable contrast.
- Avoid relying only on color for product recognition.
- Include meaningful alternative text in digital interfaces.
- Support high-resolution and zoomed displays.

Recommended alternative text pattern:

```text
{Product Name} logo
```

---

# Usage Rules

Always:

- Use approved logo exports.
- Select the correct background variant.
- Maintain proportions.
- Preserve clear space.
- Follow product metadata.
- Use the current active version.

Never:

- Stretch the logo.
- Rotate the logo.
- Add shadows or effects.
- Change product colors.
- Replace approved typography.
- Crop the logo.
- Combine it with unofficial symbols.
- recreate it manually.
- use a deprecated version for new work.

# Common Mistakes

Avoid:

- Creating unrelated product identities.
- Using multiple logos for the same product.
- Embedding temporary status in the master logo.
- Reusing another product's accent color without review.
- Publishing logos without source files.
- Using raster files as masters.
- Creating duplicate logo folders.
- Storing unofficial copies across project repositories.
- Failing to archive previous versions.

# Best Practices

- Establish the product name before designing the logo.
- Keep product identities connected to Mianx.ai.
- Use one approved master source.
- Prefer simple and scalable product symbols.
- Register every logo in the product logo registry.
- Generate exports through a controlled process.
- Never overwrite released logo versions.
- Review product identities during major product changes.
- Keep product, brand, and asset documentation synchronized.
