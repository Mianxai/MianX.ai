# Purpose

Marketing illustrations communicate the Mianx.ai brand across digital and physical channels.

Unlike product illustrations, marketing illustrations focus on storytelling, awareness, trust, engagement, education, and conversion.

This document establishes enterprise standards for creating consistent, scalable, and reusable marketing illustrations.

---

# Objectives

The Marketing Illustration System aims to:

- Strengthen brand identity
- Increase user engagement
- Improve conversion
- Support storytelling
- Explain complex services
- Increase campaign consistency
- Improve visual quality
- Support multi-channel marketing
- Enable scalable asset production
- Support AI-assisted illustration workflows

---

# Marketing Illustration Philosophy

Every marketing illustration should answer one question:

> **Why should someone care about this message?**

Marketing artwork should inspire action—not simply decorate a page.

---

# Composition

Recommended layout:

```text
Primary Subject

↓

Supporting Objects

↓

Background Shapes

↓

Whitespace
```

Avoid cluttered marketing visuals.

---

# Characters

Characters should appear:

- Diverse
- Inclusive
- Professional
- Collaborative
- Confident

Avoid exaggerated cartoon styles.

---

# Devices

Illustrated devices may include:

- Desktop
- Laptop
- Mobile
- Tablet
- Cloud
- Dashboard

Do not imitate specific commercial hardware.

---

# Color Usage

Recommended color balance:

| Color | Usage |
|--------|-------|
| Neutral | 60% |
| Brand Primary | 25% |
| Accent | 15% |

Marketing assets may use slightly stronger accents than product illustrations while remaining within the approved brand palette.

---

# Typography Integration

Illustrations should not permanently embed text.

If labels are required:

- Use official typography
- Keep text editable
- Support localization

---

# Responsive Design

Illustrations should adapt to:

- Desktop
- Laptop
- Tablet
- Mobile
- Social Media Formats

No important visual element should be cropped.

---

# Accessibility

Marketing illustrations should:

- Maintain sufficient contrast
- Avoid relying only on color
- Support alternative text
- Preserve readability on small screens

---

# Performance

Illustrations should:

- Use optimized SVG
- Export responsive assets
- Minimize file size
- Preserve sharpness at all resolutions

---

# AI-Assisted Creation

AI may assist with:

- Campaign concepts
- Mood boards
- Color exploration
- Composition
- Variant generation

Every AI-generated illustration must be reviewed by the Design Team before publication.

# Quality Checklist

Before approval:

- [ ] Supports campaign objective
- [ ] One clear message
- [ ] Brand compliant
- [ ] Responsive
- [ ] Accessible
- [ ] Performance optimized
- [ ] Source archived
- [ ] Metadata registered
- [ ] Export package generated
- [ ] Approved by Marketing & Brand Team

---

# Common Mistakes

Avoid:

- Stock-looking artwork
- Mixed illustration styles
- Multiple competing messages
- Decorative overload
- Off-brand colors
- Crowded layouts
- Embedded non-localizable text
- Low-resolution exports

---

# Best Practices

- Communicate one idea.
- Tell a visual story.
- Keep illustrations reusable.
- Design for every channel.
- Follow the official illustration style.
- Optimize exports.
- Archive master files.
- Review accessibility.
- Maintain version history.
- Preserve brand consistency.
