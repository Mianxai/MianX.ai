# Purpose

This document defines the official visual identity and branding standards for the Mianx.ai ecosystem.

Every digital product, website, mobile application, AI agent interface, document, presentation, marketing campaign, and communication must follow these standards to ensure a consistent and professional brand experience.

---

# Logo Usage Rules

Always:

- Use approved logo files.
- Maintain proportions.
- Preserve clear space around the logo.
- Use high-resolution versions.

Never:

- Stretch the logo.
- Rotate the logo.
- Add shadows or effects.
- Change logo colors.
- Modify typography.
- Place the logo on unreadable backgrounds.

---

# Logo Clear Space

Maintain minimum clear space equal to the height of the "M" in the logo on all sides.
