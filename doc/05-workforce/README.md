# Workforce

> The Workforce domain defines how people operate within the MIANX organization. It covers organizational structure, hiring, onboarding, roles, responsibilities, career growth, performance management, collaboration, and company culture.

The goal is to build a world-class team capable of designing, building, operating, and continuously improving the MIANX ecosystem.

---

# Purpose

The Workforce domain provides standards, policies, and operational guidance for managing people across every department within the organization.

---

# Objectives

The Workforce documentation aims to:

- Define organizational structure
- Standardize hiring processes
- Establish onboarding procedures
- Clarify roles and responsibilities
- Support employee growth
- Improve collaboration
- Encourage knowledge sharing
- Maintain operational consistency
- Build strong engineering culture
- Scale the organization efficiently
