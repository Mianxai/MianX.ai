# Candidate Screening

> This document defines the candidate screening process used at MIANX. Screening is the structured evaluation of applicants before interviews begin, ensuring that candidates meet the minimum requirements for a role while maintaining fairness, consistency, and efficiency.

The screening stage helps hiring teams focus interview efforts on candidates who demonstrate the highest potential for success.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of Candidate Screening is to establish a standardized process for reviewing applications, assessing qualifications, and determining which candidates advance to the interview stage.

---

# Objectives

The screening process aims to:

- Improve hiring quality
- Reduce hiring time
- Maintain fair evaluations
- Standardize candidate reviews
- Reduce recruitment bias
- Improve recruiter efficiency
- Support hiring managers
- Enhance candidate experience
- Ensure role alignment
- Build reliable hiring data

---

# Screening Principles

Candidate screening should always be:

- Objective
- Consistent
- Evidence-based
- Inclusive
- Transparent
- Efficient
- Confidential
- Documented

Candidates should be evaluated against job requirements—not personal assumptions or unrelated characteristics.

---

# Screening Workflow

```text
Application Received

↓

Application Validation

↓

Resume Review

↓

Qualification Assessment

↓

Experience Review

↓

Skills Evaluation

↓

Portfolio Review (If Applicable)

↓

Initial Recommendation

↓

Hiring Manager Review

↓

Interview Decision
```

Every screening decision should be documented.

---

# Screening Inputs

The following information may be reviewed:

- Resume / CV
- Cover Letter
- Portfolio
- GitHub or Technical Profile
- LinkedIn Profile
- Certifications
- Education
- Employment History
- Personal Projects
- References (if provided)

Only information relevant to the role should be considered.

---

# Eligibility Verification

Before detailed evaluation, verify:

- Application completeness
- Minimum qualifications
- Work authorization (where required)
- Employment eligibility
- Required documentation

Incomplete applications may be returned for clarification or rejected if essential information is missing.

---

# Resume Review

Resume evaluation should consider:

- Relevant experience
- Career progression
- Technical expertise
- Project impact
- Problem-solving experience
- Leadership responsibilities
- Communication quality
- Employment consistency

Career paths should be evaluated holistically rather than solely by years of experience.

---

# Skills Assessment

Review technical and professional skills relevant to the role.

Examples:

- Programming languages
- Frameworks
- Cloud platforms
- Databases
- DevOps practices
- AI / ML knowledge
- Security fundamentals
- Communication skills
- Team collaboration

Only role-specific skills should influence screening outcomes.

---

# Portfolio Review

For technical and creative roles, review:

- Personal projects
- Open-source contributions
- Technical blogs
- Research publications
- Design portfolio
- Product demonstrations
- Community contributions

Portfolio quality often provides stronger evidence than certifications alone.

---

# Experience Evaluation

Evaluate:

| Area | Examples |
|------|----------|
| Technical Experience | Technologies used |
| Project Complexity | Scale and responsibility |
| Leadership | Mentoring, ownership |
| Business Impact | Measurable outcomes |
| Collaboration | Teamwork and communication |
| Continuous Learning | Certifications, self-learning |

Experience should be evaluated based on relevance and impact rather than duration alone.

---

# Screening Scorecard

Example evaluation matrix:

| Category | Weight |
|----------|-------:|
| Technical Skills | 35% |
| Relevant Experience | 25% |
| Problem Solving | 15% |
| Portfolio / Projects | 10% |
| Communication | 10% |
| Learning Mindset | 5% |

Hiring teams may adjust weights depending on the role.

---

# Screening Outcomes

Possible outcomes:

| Decision | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Advance | Proceed to interview |
| Hold | Keep for future consideration |
| Request Information | Need additional clarification |
| Reject | Does not meet role requirements |

Every outcome should include documented reasoning.

---

# Hiring Manager Review

The Hiring Manager should verify:

- Role alignment
- Technical fit
- Team requirements
- Business priorities
- Interview readiness

The Hiring Manager makes the final decision on whether to invite a candidate for interviews.

---

# Candidate Communication

Candidates should receive timely updates regarding:

- Application confirmation
- Screening status
- Interview invitations
- Requests for additional information
- Final screening outcome

Professional and respectful communication should be maintained throughout the process.

---

# Documentation Requirements

Maintain records for:

- Applications received
- Screening scorecards
- Reviewer comments
- Screening decisions
- Candidate status
- Communication history
- Supporting documents

Documentation should be stored securely and retained according to company policy.

---

# Confidentiality

Candidate information should:

- Be accessed only by authorized personnel
- Be used solely for recruitment purposes
- Be protected from unauthorized disclosure
- Comply with privacy regulations
- Follow company data retention policies

Candidate privacy is a fundamental responsibility.

---

# Screening Metrics

Recommended metrics include:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Applications Received | Total applications |
| Screening Completion Time | Average review duration |
| Interview Conversion Rate | Candidates advancing to interviews |
| Qualified Candidate Rate | Percentage meeting requirements |
| Resume Review Accuracy | Screening quality |
| Candidate Response Time | Communication efficiency |
| Screening Consistency | Evaluation standardization |

Metrics should support continuous improvement of the recruitment process.

---

# Roles & Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| HR Team | Initial application review |
| Recruiter | Candidate screening |
| Hiring Manager | Screening approval |
| Department Head | Escalation and oversight |
| Candidate | Provide accurate information |

---

# Best Practices

Recommended:

- Use standardized scorecards
- Review applications consistently
- Focus on evidence rather than assumptions
- Evaluate relevant experience over job titles
- Respond to candidates promptly
- Protect candidate confidentiality
- Document every decision
- Continuously improve screening criteria

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Screening based on personal bias
- Ignoring transferable skills
- Rejecting candidates solely by years of experience
- Inconsistent evaluation standards
- Delayed application reviews
- Poor candidate communication
- Undocumented decisions
- Reviewing irrelevant personal information

---

# Future Evolution

Candidate screening at MIANX will evolve through:

- AI-assisted resume analysis
- Skills-based candidate matching
- Automated screening workflows
- Intelligent applicant ranking
- Competency-based evaluation
- Recruitment analytics dashboards
- Internal talent discovery
- Predictive hiring recommendations
