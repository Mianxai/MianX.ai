# Hiring Best Practices

> This document defines the hiring best practices adopted by MIANX. These practices provide guidance for recruiters, hiring managers, interviewers, and leadership teams to consistently recruit exceptional talent while maintaining fairness, efficiency, professionalism, and a positive candidate experience.

Best practices are intended to improve hiring outcomes, reduce unnecessary risks, and strengthen long-term organizational growth.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this document is to establish recommended hiring standards that promote high-quality recruitment decisions across every department.

---

# Objectives

This document aims to:

- Improve hiring quality
- Standardize recruitment practices
- Reduce hiring bias
- Improve candidate experience
- Increase hiring efficiency
- Support workforce growth
- Strengthen employer reputation
- Encourage evidence-based decisions
- Improve documentation quality
- Promote continuous improvement

---

# Hiring Philosophy

MIANX believes that hiring is a long-term investment rather than a short-term staffing activity.

Successful hiring focuses on:

- Capability
- Integrity
- Learning ability
- Ownership
- Collaboration
- Adaptability
- Professionalism
- Long-term potential

Technical knowledge can be developed, but attitude, ethics, and learning mindset are equally important.

---

# Before Opening a Position

Before recruiting:

- Confirm business need
- Review workforce planning
- Verify department budget
- Consider internal candidates
- Update the job description
- Define success criteria
- Establish interview requirements
- Prepare evaluation scorecards

Hiring should begin only after the role is clearly defined.

---

# Writing Effective Job Descriptions

Good job descriptions should:

- Clearly explain the role
- Describe expected outcomes
- List essential responsibilities
- Separate required and preferred qualifications
- Use inclusive language
- Avoid unnecessary requirements
- Explain career growth opportunities
- Reflect actual day-to-day responsibilities

Candidates should understand exactly what success looks like in the role.

---

# Candidate Sourcing

Use multiple sourcing channels, including:

- Employee referrals
- Company careers page
- Professional communities
- Universities
- Industry events
- Open-source communities
- Internal talent
- Recruitment partners

Diversified sourcing improves talent quality and resilience.

---

# Candidate Communication

Maintain clear communication throughout the hiring process.

Candidates should receive:

- Application confirmation
- Process timeline
- Interview instructions
- Status updates
- Offer information
- Final outcome notification

Professional communication strengthens employer reputation regardless of hiring outcome.

---

# Resume Screening

When reviewing applications:

- Focus on relevant experience
- Consider transferable skills
- Review project impact
- Evaluate learning ability
- Assess communication quality
- Avoid unnecessary assumptions

Years of experience should not be the sole measure of capability.

---

# Interview Best Practices

Interviewers should:

- Prepare before interviews
- Use structured questions
- Encourage discussion
- Listen actively
- Ask follow-up questions
- Document observations immediately
- Treat every candidate respectfully
- Finish interviews on time

Every candidate should experience a professional and welcoming interview.

---

# Technical Assessments

Technical assessments should:

- Reflect real-world work
- Match the role requirements
- Focus on engineering thinking
- Evaluate code quality
- Include practical scenarios
- Have reasonable time limits
- Avoid unnecessary complexity

Assessments should measure problem-solving rather than memorization.

---

# Candidate Evaluation

Evaluation should:

- Use standardized scorecards
- Consider all interview stages
- Focus on evidence
- Encourage independent interviewer feedback
- Resolve disagreements through discussion
- Document hiring rationale

Candidates should be evaluated against the role—not against one another.

---

# Hiring Decisions

Final hiring decisions should consider:

- Technical ability
- Communication
- Collaboration
- Ownership
- Learning mindset
- Leadership potential
- Business needs
- Long-term contribution

No single interview should determine the hiring outcome.

---

# Offer Management

Employment offers should:

- Be delivered promptly
- Clearly explain compensation
- Describe benefits
- Include employment terms
- Allow reasonable review time
- Support respectful negotiations

Transparent offers improve acceptance rates.

---

# Background Verification

Verification should:

- Obtain candidate consent where required
- Verify only relevant information
- Protect candidate privacy
- Document findings
- Resolve discrepancies fairly
- Follow legal requirements

Background verification should support—not replace—professional judgment.

---

# Documentation

Maintain records for:

- Job descriptions
- Interview scorecards
- Technical assessments
- Hiring recommendations
- Offer approvals
- Candidate communications
- Background verification
- Hiring metrics

Accurate documentation improves accountability and future hiring decisions.

---

# Candidate Experience

Every candidate should experience:

- Respectful treatment
- Clear communication
- Transparent expectations
- Fair evaluations
- Timely decisions
- Professional interactions
- Constructive feedback where appropriate

Even unsuccessful candidates should leave with a positive impression of MIANX.

---

# Diversity & Inclusion

Recruitment should promote:

- Equal opportunity
- Inclusive job descriptions
- Diverse sourcing channels
- Objective evaluation
- Consistent interview processes
- Accessible recruitment practices

Hiring decisions should always be based on qualifications, demonstrated capability, and organizational needs.

---

# Ethical Hiring

All hiring activities should:

- Respect confidentiality
- Protect candidate information
- Avoid discrimination
- Prevent conflicts of interest
- Follow company policies
- Comply with applicable laws

Ethics are fundamental to sustainable hiring.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Hiring teams should regularly:

- Review hiring metrics
- Analyze recruitment outcomes
- Conduct interviewer calibration sessions
- Update interview questions
- Improve assessment quality
- Collect candidate feedback
- Review hiring documentation
- Refine recruitment processes

Continuous improvement strengthens long-term hiring performance.

---

# Common Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Hiring without workforce planning
- Poorly defined job descriptions
- Unstructured interviews
- Bias-driven decisions
- Excessive interview rounds
- Delayed candidate communication
- Ignoring documentation
- Measuring only hiring speed
- Unrealistic job requirements
- Weak onboarding preparation

Avoiding these issues improves both hiring quality and organizational reputation.

---

# Future Evolution

The MIANX hiring framework will continue evolving through:

- AI-assisted recruiting
- Intelligent resume screening
- Automated interview scheduling
- Digital hiring scorecards
- Recruitment analytics dashboards
- Skills-based hiring frameworks
- Internal talent marketplaces
- Predictive workforce planning
- Global recruitment support
- Continuous hiring intelligence
