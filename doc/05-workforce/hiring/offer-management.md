# Offer Management

> This document defines the Offer Management process used by MIANX to prepare, approve, deliver, negotiate, and finalize employment offers. The objective is to ensure every employment offer is accurate, compliant, transparent, and provides an excellent candidate experience.

Offer Management begins after a successful hiring decision and concludes when the candidate either accepts or declines the employment offer.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of Offer Management is to establish a standardized process for preparing and managing employment offers while ensuring consistency, fairness, and legal compliance.

---

# Objectives

The offer management process aims to:

- Standardize offer preparation
- Ensure compensation consistency
- Improve candidate experience
- Reduce offer processing time
- Maintain legal compliance
- Protect confidential information
- Improve offer acceptance rates
- Enable accurate documentation
- Support onboarding readiness
- Strengthen employer reputation

---

# Offer Management Principles

Every employment offer should be:

- Accurate
- Transparent
- Fair
- Competitive
- Confidential
- Timely
- Legally compliant
- Properly documented

Offer decisions should align with company compensation policies and workforce planning.

---

# Offer Management Workflow

```text
Hiring Decision

↓

Compensation Review

↓

Internal Approval

↓

Offer Preparation

↓

Offer Delivery

↓

Candidate Review

↓

Negotiation (If Required)

↓

Final Offer

↓

Offer Acceptance

↓

Employment Documentation

↓

Onboarding
```

Each stage should be completed before moving to the next.

---

# Offer Components

Every employment offer should clearly define:

- Position title
- Department
- Reporting manager
- Employment type
- Work location
- Start date
- Compensation
- Benefits
- Probation period (if applicable)
- Working hours
- Leave policy
- Confidentiality obligations
- Employment conditions

The offer should clearly explain all employment terms.

---

# Compensation Review

Before preparing an offer, verify:

- Approved salary range
- Internal pay equity
- Budget availability
- Job level
- Benefits eligibility
- Bonus eligibility
- Equity or stock options (if applicable)

Compensation decisions should follow company policies and approved salary structures.

---

# Approval Process

Employment offers should receive approval from:

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Hiring Manager | Hiring recommendation |
| HR Team | Offer preparation |
| Finance Team | Budget verification |
| Department Head | Department approval |
| Founder / Executive Leadership | Executive approvals (where required) |

No offer should be sent without all required approvals.

---

# Offer Preparation

The HR team should prepare:

- Employment offer letter
- Compensation summary
- Benefits information
- Employment agreement
- Required legal documentation
- Candidate instructions

Documents should be reviewed for accuracy before delivery.

---

# Offer Delivery

Employment offers should be:

- Delivered professionally
- Sent securely
- Easy to understand
- Accompanied by clear next steps
- Time-bound with a response deadline

Candidates should have adequate time to review the offer.

---

# Candidate Questions

Candidates may request clarification regarding:

- Role responsibilities
- Compensation
- Benefits
- Remote work policies
- Career growth
- Working hours
- Company policies
- Start date

Questions should be answered promptly and consistently.

---

# Offer Negotiation

Negotiations may include:

- Salary
- Start date
- Benefits
- Remote work arrangements
- Relocation support
- Signing bonus (where applicable)
- Vacation allocation
- Equipment requirements

Negotiations should remain respectful, transparent, and within approved company policies.

---

# Offer Outcomes

Possible outcomes include:

| Outcome | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Accepted | Candidate accepts the offer |
| Accepted with Conditions | Additional agreed changes |
| Declined | Candidate rejects the offer |
| Expired | Response deadline passes |
| Withdrawn | Offer cancelled before acceptance |

All outcomes should be documented.

---

# Offer Acceptance

After acceptance:

```text
Offer Signed

↓

Employment Record Created

↓

HR Notification

↓

Equipment Request

↓

Account Provisioning

↓

Onboarding Schedule

↓

Employee Start Date
```

Acceptance officially begins the onboarding process.

---

# Employment Documentation

Maintain:

- Signed offer letter
- Employment agreement
- Compensation approval
- Benefits enrollment
- Identification documents (where required)
- Background verification records
- Tax documentation (where applicable)

Documentation should comply with company retention policies.

---

# Confidentiality

Offer information should:

- Be shared only with authorized personnel
- Remain confidential
- Be stored securely
- Follow privacy regulations
- Be protected from unauthorized disclosure

Compensation information should only be disclosed on a need-to-know basis.

---

# Candidate Experience

Offer Management should provide:

- Timely communication
- Professional interactions
- Clear documentation
- Transparent expectations
- Fast responses to questions
- Respectful negotiations

A positive offer experience improves employer reputation and offer acceptance rates.

---

# Offer Metrics

Recommended metrics include:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Offer Acceptance Rate | Accepted offers |
| Offer Decline Rate | Declined offers |
| Time to Offer | Hiring decision to offer delivery |
| Offer Approval Time | Internal approval duration |
| Negotiation Rate | Offers requiring negotiation |
| Candidate Satisfaction | Offer experience feedback |
| Offer Accuracy | Documentation quality |

Metrics should be reviewed regularly to improve the offer process.

---

# Roles & Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| HR Team | Offer preparation and communication |
| Hiring Manager | Candidate recommendation |
| Finance Team | Compensation validation |
| Department Head | Offer approval |
| Legal Team | Employment agreement review |
| Candidate | Review and respond to the offer |

---

# Documentation Requirements

Maintain records for:

- Compensation approvals
- Offer letters
- Employment agreements
- Candidate communications
- Negotiation history
- Signed documents
- Offer decisions
- Approval records

All documents should be securely stored and version controlled where applicable.

---

# Best Practices

Recommended:

- Prepare offers promptly after approval
- Use standardized templates
- Clearly explain compensation and benefits
- Allow reasonable review time
- Respond quickly to candidate questions
- Document all negotiations
- Maintain confidentiality
- Coordinate onboarding immediately after acceptance

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Sending offers without approval
- Verbal promises not included in the written offer
- Inconsistent compensation for equivalent roles
- Delayed communication
- Unclear employment terms
- Pressure tactics during negotiation
- Missing documentation
- Sharing confidential compensation information

---
