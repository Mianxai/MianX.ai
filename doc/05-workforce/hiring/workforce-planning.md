# Workforce Planning

> This document defines how MIANX plans, forecasts, and manages its workforce requirements. Workforce planning ensures that the organization has the right people, with the right skills, in the right roles, at the right time.

Workforce planning aligns hiring with business strategy, product roadmaps, financial planning, and long-term organizational growth.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of Workforce Planning is to proactively identify workforce needs, optimize resource allocation, and support sustainable company growth.

---

# Objectives

The workforce planning process aims to:

- Align hiring with business goals
- Forecast workforce demand
- Identify skill gaps
- Optimize team capacity
- Improve hiring efficiency
- Support financial planning
- Reduce hiring risks
- Enable organizational scalability
- Strengthen succession planning
- Improve employee utilization

---

# Workforce Planning Principles

Every workforce plan should be:

- Business-driven
- Data-informed
- Financially sustainable
- Flexible
- Transparent
- Scalable
- Regularly reviewed
- Aligned with company strategy

---

---

# Planning Inputs

Workforce planning considers:

- Company vision
- Annual objectives
- Product roadmap
- Customer demand
- Engineering roadmap
- Operational workload
- Budget availability
- Market conditions
- Employee feedback
- Historical hiring data

---

# Workforce Demand Forecasting

Forecast workforce demand based on:

- New product initiatives
- Infrastructure growth
- Customer acquisition
- Business expansion
- Regulatory requirements
- Support workload
- Technical debt
- Innovation projects

Forecasts should cover:

- Quarterly
- Annual
- Three-year outlook

---

# Capacity Planning

Each department should evaluate:

- Current headcount
- Active projects
- Team workload
- Delivery timelines
- Productivity trends
- Resource utilization

Capacity planning identifies when additional hiring or internal redistribution is required.

---

# Skill Gap Analysis

Identify:

- Missing technical skills
- Leadership gaps
- Emerging technology expertise
- Operational capabilities
- Business knowledge
- Specialized certifications

Gap analysis should guide hiring, training, and internal development.

---

# Headcount Planning

Headcount plans should define:

| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Current Headcount | Existing employees |
| Planned Headcount | Target workforce size |
| New Positions | Roles to be created |
| Replacement Hiring | Backfill positions |
| Internal Transfers | Planned movement between teams |
| Contractors | Temporary workforce requirements |

Every planned position requires documented business justification.

---

# Workforce Allocation

Resources may be allocated across:

- Engineering
- Product
- Design
- Platform
- DevOps
- Security
- QA
- Operations
- HR
- Finance
- Marketing
- Sales
- Customer Success

Allocation should balance business priorities with team capacity.

---

# Hiring Prioritization

Hiring requests should be prioritized based on:

| Priority | Example |
|----------|----------|
| Critical | Business continuity, security, compliance |
| High | Product delivery, customer commitments |
| Medium | Growth initiatives |
| Low | Future planning or optimization |

Critical positions receive expedited review and approval.

---

# Budget Planning

Hiring plans must align with:

- Annual operating budget
- Department budgets
- Compensation strategy
- Benefits costs
- Equipment expenses
- Training costs
- Recruitment costs

Finance and HR should jointly review workforce budgets.

---

# Succession Planning

Every critical leadership role should identify:

- Potential successors
- Required development plans
- Leadership readiness
- Knowledge transfer requirements

Succession planning reduces organizational risk.

---

# Workforce Review

Regular workforce reviews should assess:

- Hiring progress
- Team capacity
- Employee retention
- Performance trends
- Open positions
- Skill distribution
- Organizational health

Reviews should occur at least quarterly.

# Planning Metrics

Track:

- Headcount Growth
- Vacancy Rate
- Time-to-Fill
- Workforce Utilization
- Employee Retention
- Hiring Forecast Accuracy
- Internal Mobility Rate
- Hiring Cost
- Revenue per Employee
- Manager-to-Employee Ratio

Metrics should inform future planning cycles.

---

# Documentation Requirements

Maintain documentation for:

- Annual workforce plan
- Department hiring plans
- Capacity reports
- Skill gap assessments
- Budget approvals
- Hiring forecasts
- Organizational charts
- Workforce metrics

Documentation should be reviewed and updated regularly.

---

# Best Practices

Recommended:

- Plan hiring proactively
- Align workforce plans with business objectives
- Review capacity frequently
- Invest in internal talent development
- Balance hiring with automation
- Maintain realistic hiring timelines
- Document workforce assumptions
- Monitor workforce metrics continuously

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Reactive hiring
- Hiring without budget approval
- Ignoring capacity constraints
- Undefined workforce goals
- Overstaffing
- Understaffing
- Neglecting succession planning
- Planning without measurable data
