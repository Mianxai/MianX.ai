# Background Verification

> This document defines the Background Verification process used by MIANX to verify candidate information before employment begins. The purpose is to protect the company, employees, customers, and business operations while ensuring that hiring decisions are based on accurate and verified information.

Background verification is performed only after a candidate has successfully completed the interview process and received a conditional employment offer, where applicable.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of Background Verification is to validate candidate information, reduce hiring risks, ensure legal compliance, and maintain trust throughout the recruitment process.

---

# Objectives

The background verification process aims to:

- Verify candidate identity
- Confirm employment history
- Validate educational qualifications
- Verify professional certifications
- Reduce hiring risks
- Detect fraudulent information
- Ensure legal compliance
- Protect company assets
- Improve hiring confidence
- Maintain recruitment integrity

---

# Verification Principles

Every verification process should be:

- Lawful
- Respectful
- Confidential
- Objective
- Accurate
- Transparent
- Proportionate
- Documented

Verification should only collect information relevant to employment.

---

# Verification Workflow

```text
Conditional Offer

↓

Candidate Consent

↓

Identity Verification

↓

Employment Verification

↓

Education Verification

↓

Certification Verification

↓

Reference Checks

↓

Background Review

↓

Verification Report

↓

Hiring Decision

↓

Onboarding
```

Verification begins only after receiving the candidate's consent where required by applicable law.

---

# Candidate Consent

Before verification begins:

- Explain the verification process
- Inform the candidate what information will be verified
- Obtain required written consent
- Explain privacy protections
- Provide contact information for questions

Consent records should be securely retained.

---

# Identity Verification

Identity verification may include:

- Government-issued identification
- Passport
- National identity card
- Driver's license
- Work authorization documents

Identity verification ensures the candidate is legally eligible for employment where required.

---

# Employment Verification

Previous employment may be verified by confirming:

- Employer name
- Position title
- Employment dates
- Responsibilities (where available)
- Reason for leaving (where permitted)
- Employment status

Employment verification should rely on official sources whenever possible.

---

# Education Verification

Education verification may include:

- Degree confirmation
- Institution verification
- Graduation date
- Academic program
- Professional diplomas

Educational verification should only confirm information relevant to the role.

---

# Certification Verification

Where applicable, verify:

- Professional certifications
- Technical certifications
- Security certifications
- Cloud certifications
- Industry licenses

Expired or invalid certifications should be documented.

---

# Reference Checks

Professional references may be requested to verify:

- Work quality
- Professional conduct
- Collaboration
- Leadership
- Reliability
- Communication
- Technical capability

Reference checks should remain factual and professional.

---

# Additional Verification

Depending on the role and applicable laws, additional verification may include:

- Professional memberships
- Published research
- Open-source contributions
- Public technical portfolio
- Business ownership disclosures
- Conflict of interest declarations

Additional checks should be appropriate for the position.

---

# Verification Outcomes

| Result | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Verified | Information confirmed |
| Partially Verified | Minor discrepancies identified |
| Unable to Verify | Information unavailable |
| Verification Failed | Significant discrepancy identified |

Every outcome should include supporting documentation.

---

# Handling Discrepancies

If discrepancies are identified:

1. Review supporting evidence
2. Contact the candidate for clarification
3. Verify additional documentation if needed
4. Document findings
5. Escalate significant concerns
6. Make a final documented decision

Minor administrative differences should not automatically disqualify a candidate.

---

# Final Review

After verification:

```text
Verification Report

↓

HR Review

↓

Hiring Manager Review

↓

Legal Review (If Required)

↓

Final Employment Decision
```

The final hiring decision should consider the complete recruitment process rather than background verification alone.

---

# Confidentiality

Background verification information must:

- Be treated as confidential
- Be stored securely
- Be accessible only to authorized personnel
- Be retained according to company policy
- Be deleted when retention periods expire

Candidate privacy should always be respected.

---

# Data Protection

MIANX should:

- Collect only necessary information
- Minimize data retention
- Protect personal information
- Comply with applicable privacy regulations
- Secure verification records
- Restrict unauthorized access

Data protection is a core responsibility throughout the hiring process.

---

# Documentation Requirements

Maintain records for:

- Candidate consent
- Identity verification
- Employment verification
- Education verification
- Certification verification
- Reference checks
- Verification reports
- Final approval records

Documentation should remain confidential and auditable.

---

# Roles & Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| HR Team | Coordinate verification process |
| Candidate | Provide accurate information and required documents |
| Hiring Manager | Review verification outcome |
| Legal Team | Compliance guidance when necessary |
| Department Head | Final approval for critical roles |

---

# Verification Metrics

Recommended metrics include:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Verification Completion Rate | Successfully completed verifications |
| Average Verification Time | Time required to complete verification |
| Verification Accuracy | Confirmed information quality |
| Discrepancy Rate | Percentage of applications with discrepancies |
| Offer Withdrawal Rate | Offers withdrawn after verification |
| Candidate Satisfaction | Candidate experience during verification |
| Compliance Rate | Verification policy adherence |

Metrics should support continuous process improvement.

---

# Best Practices

Recommended:

- Obtain consent before verification
- Verify only job-relevant information
- Use reliable verification sources
- Document every verification step
- Protect candidate privacy
- Resolve discrepancies fairly
- Complete verification promptly
- Maintain consistent verification standards

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Conducting verification without consent (where required)
- Collecting unnecessary personal information
- Making assumptions without evidence
- Sharing confidential reports
- Using unofficial verification sources
- Delaying onboarding unnecessarily
- Applying inconsistent verification standards
- Retaining personal information longer than required
