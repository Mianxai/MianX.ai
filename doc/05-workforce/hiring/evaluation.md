# Candidate Evaluation

> This document defines the standardized candidate evaluation framework used by MIANX. It ensures every hiring decision is based on objective evidence, structured feedback, and consistent evaluation criteria rather than personal opinions or bias.

Candidate evaluation combines information gathered throughout the hiring process—including screening, interviews, technical assessments, and behavioral observations—to determine whether a candidate is the right fit for both the role and the organization.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of Candidate Evaluation is to establish a fair, repeatable, and evidence-based decision-making process for hiring across all departments.

---

# Objectives

The evaluation process aims to:

- Standardize hiring decisions
- Improve hiring quality
- Reduce unconscious bias
- Ensure fair candidate comparisons
- Measure technical competency
- Evaluate long-term potential
- Improve documentation quality
- Support hiring transparency
- Increase decision consistency
- Enable continuous hiring improvement

---

# Evaluation Principles

Every evaluation should be:

- Objective
- Structured
- Evidence-based
- Consistent
- Inclusive
- Confidential
- Role-specific
- Documented

Hiring decisions should reflect demonstrated ability rather than assumptions.

---

# Evaluation Workflow

```text
Resume Screening

↓

HR Interview

↓

Technical Assessment

↓

Technical Interview

↓

Behavioral Interview

↓

Leadership Review (If Required)

↓

Evaluation Scorecards

↓

Panel Discussion

↓

Hiring Recommendation

↓

Final Hiring Decision
```

Every stage contributes to the final evaluation.

---

# Evaluation Areas

Candidates should be evaluated across multiple competencies.

| Area | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Technical Skills | Job-specific knowledge |
| Problem Solving | Analytical thinking |
| Communication | Written and verbal communication |
| Collaboration | Teamwork and cooperation |
| Ownership | Accountability and initiative |
| Learning Ability | Curiosity and adaptability |
| Leadership | Guidance and influence |
| Professionalism | Ethics and conduct |
| Cultural Alignment | Alignment with company values |

No single category should determine the final outcome.

---

# Technical Evaluation

Review:

- Programming knowledge
- Architecture understanding
- Code quality
- System design
- Debugging ability
- Security awareness
- Testing practices
- Performance optimization

Technical evaluations should reflect real-world engineering work.

---

# Behavioral Evaluation

Assess:

- Team collaboration
- Conflict resolution
- Accountability
- Adaptability
- Decision-making
- Communication style
- Learning mindset
- Professional integrity

Behavioral interviews should rely on examples from past experience.

---

# Leadership Evaluation

For senior positions, evaluate:

- Strategic thinking
- Team leadership
- Mentorship
- Organizational impact
- Decision-making
- Cross-functional collaboration
- Change management
- Business understanding

Leadership potential should be considered alongside technical expertise.

---

# Evaluation Scorecard

Suggested scoring model:

| Category | Weight |
|----------|-------:|
| Technical Competency | 35% |
| Problem Solving | 20% |
| Communication | 10% |
| Collaboration | 10% |
| Ownership | 10% |
| Learning Ability | 5% |
| Leadership | 5% |
| Cultural Alignment | 5% |

Departments may adjust weights based on role requirements.

---

# Candidate Rating Scale

| Rating | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| 5 | Exceptional |
| 4 | Strong |
| 3 | Meets Expectations |
| 2 | Below Expectations |
| 1 | Does Not Meet Requirements |

Every rating should include written justification.

---

# Evaluation Decision Matrix

| Decision | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Strong Hire | Outstanding candidate with minimal concerns |
| Hire | Meets role requirements and demonstrates potential |
| Hold | Suitable candidate for future opportunities |
| No Hire | Does not currently meet role requirements |

The decision should reflect the overall evidence gathered throughout the hiring process.

---

# Panel Review

The interview panel should:

- Review all scorecards
- Discuss strengths and concerns
- Resolve scoring differences
- Identify potential risks
- Make a documented recommendation

Panel discussions should focus on evidence rather than personal opinions.

---

# Final Hiring Decision

The Hiring Manager should consider:

- Evaluation scorecards
- Technical assessment
- Interview feedback
- Team requirements
- Workforce planning
- Organizational priorities

Final approval may require Department Head or Executive approval depending on the role.

---

# Candidate Feedback

Where appropriate, provide candidates with:

- Hiring outcome
- General feedback
- Professional communication
- Future opportunities (if applicable)

Feedback should be respectful, constructive, and avoid disclosing confidential internal discussions.

---

# Documentation Requirements

Maintain records for:

- Evaluation scorecards
- Interview feedback
- Technical assessment results
- Panel recommendations
- Hiring decisions
- Candidate communication
- Approval records

Documentation should remain confidential and securely stored.

---

# Confidentiality

Evaluation information must:

- Be accessible only to authorized personnel
- Be protected against unauthorized disclosure
- Be retained according to company policy
- Comply with applicable privacy regulations

Confidentiality is essential for maintaining trust in the hiring process.

---

# Evaluation Metrics

Recommended metrics include:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Interview Pass Rate | Candidates progressing to next stage |
| Offer Rate | Offers extended after evaluation |
| Offer Acceptance Rate | Accepted employment offers |
| New Hire Success Rate | Performance after hiring |
| Evaluation Consistency | Reviewer agreement |
| Candidate Satisfaction | Recruitment experience |
| Time to Decision | Evaluation efficiency |

Metrics should be reviewed regularly to improve hiring quality.

---

# Roles & Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| HR Team | Coordinate evaluations |
| Hiring Manager | Final recommendation |
| Technical Interviewers | Technical scoring |
| Interview Panel | Structured feedback |
| Department Head | Final approval (where required) |
| Founder | Executive-level hiring approval |

---

# Quality Assurance

Evaluation quality should be maintained through:

- Standardized scorecards
- Interviewer training
- Calibration sessions
- Documentation reviews
- Hiring retrospectives
- Periodic process audits

Continuous improvement helps maintain fairness and consistency.

---

# Best Practices

Recommended:

- Base decisions on evidence
- Use standardized evaluation forms
- Document observations immediately
- Encourage independent scoring before panel discussions
- Consider long-term potential alongside current skills
- Maintain confidentiality
- Review hiring outcomes regularly
- Continuously improve evaluation criteria

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Making decisions based on intuition alone
- Allowing one interviewer to dominate decisions
- Ignoring documented evidence
- Inconsistent scoring methods
- Personal bias
- Undocumented hiring decisions
- Overvaluing credentials over demonstrated ability
- Comparing candidates against each other instead of role requirements

---

# Future Evolution

Candidate evaluation at MIANX will evolve through:

- AI-assisted evaluation summaries
- Competency-based hiring frameworks
- Predictive hiring analytics
- Structured interviewer calibration
- Skills intelligence platforms
- Workforce analytics integration
- Digital evaluation dashboards
- Continuous hiring quality measurement
