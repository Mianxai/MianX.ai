# Candidate Sourcing

> This document defines how MIANX identifies, attracts, and engages qualified candidates for current and future hiring needs. Candidate sourcing is a proactive process focused on building a sustainable talent pipeline rather than simply filling open positions.

The objective is to consistently attract high-quality candidates who align with MIANX's technical standards, values, and long-term vision.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of Candidate Sourcing is to establish a structured, scalable, and repeatable approach for identifying talent across multiple recruitment channels.

---

# Objectives

The sourcing process aims to:

- Build strong talent pipelines
- Reduce hiring time
- Improve candidate quality
- Expand talent reach
- Strengthen employer branding
- Support workforce planning
- Diversify sourcing channels
- Maintain candidate relationships
- Improve recruitment efficiency
- Enable long-term hiring success

---

# Sourcing Principles

Candidate sourcing should always be:

- Proactive
- Inclusive
- Ethical
- Data-driven
- Respectful
- Transparent
- Consistent
- Relationship-focused

Recruitment should prioritize long-term talent quality over short-term hiring speed.

---

```text
Workforce Planning

Candidate Identification

Candidate Outreach

Resume Screening
```

Candidate sourcing continues even when there are no immediate vacancies.

# Candidate Sources

MIANX may source candidates through:

## Internal Sources

- Internal promotions
- Internal transfers
- Employee referrals
- Internship conversions
- Previous applicants
- Talent community

Internal candidates should always be considered before external hiring when appropriate.

---

## External Sources

Potential external sources include:

- Company careers page
- Professional networking platforms
- University partnerships
- Technical communities
- Open-source contributors
- Industry conferences
- Recruitment agencies
- Community events
- Hackathons
- Developer forums

Using multiple sourcing channels reduces dependency on a single talent source.

---

# Employee Referral Program

Employee referrals are encouraged because they often result in:

- Faster hiring
- Better cultural alignment
- Higher retention
- Improved candidate quality

Referral guidelines should ensure:

- Equal opportunity
- Objective evaluation
- Transparent processes
- No preferential treatment

Every referred candidate must complete the standard hiring process.

---

# University Recruitment

MIANX may partner with educational institutions to recruit:

- Interns
- Graduate engineers
- Researchers
- Entry-level professionals

Activities may include:

- Career fairs
- Guest lectures
- Internship programs
- Research collaborations
- Student competitions

University hiring supports long-term workforce development.

---

# Community Engagement

Talent acquisition extends beyond active recruitment.

MIANX may participate in:

- Technical meetups
- Open-source projects
- Engineering blogs
- Community forums
- Conferences
- Workshops
- AI research communities
- Developer events

Community involvement strengthens employer reputation and attracts future candidates.

---

# Employer Branding

Employer branding should communicate:

- Company mission
- Engineering culture
- Career growth opportunities
- Learning environment
- Technical challenges
- Employee benefits
- Company values
- Innovation initiatives

Authentic branding improves candidate trust and engagement.

---

# Candidate Outreach

Outreach messages should be:

- Personalized
- Professional
- Respectful
- Relevant
- Concise
- Honest

Every outreach message should explain:

- Why the candidate was selected
- Role overview
- Company introduction
- Next steps
- Contact information

Mass, generic messaging should be avoided.

---

# Talent Pipeline

The recruitment team should maintain a talent pipeline including:

| Stage | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Prospective | Identified but not contacted |
| Contacted | Initial outreach completed |
| Interested | Candidate responded positively |
| Applied | Application received |
| Interviewing | Active recruitment |
| Offer Extended | Employment offer sent |
| Hired | Joined MIANX |
| Future Opportunity | Suitable for later roles |

Maintaining a talent pipeline reduces future hiring effort.

---

# Candidate Relationship Management

Relationships should continue even if candidates are not immediately hired.

Recruitment teams should:

- Maintain professional communication
- Share future opportunities
- Update candidate records
- Respect communication preferences
- Protect candidate privacy

Positive candidate experiences strengthen long-term recruiting success.

---

# Diversity & Inclusion

Candidate sourcing should encourage diversity by:

- Expanding sourcing channels
- Removing unnecessary barriers
- Using inclusive language
- Evaluating objectively
- Promoting equal opportunity

Hiring decisions should always be based on qualifications, capability, and organizational needs.

---

# Recruitment Ethics

Recruitment activities should:

- Respect candidate confidentiality
- Avoid misleading information
- Protect personal data
- Comply with employment laws
- Avoid discriminatory practices
- Maintain professional conduct

Ethical recruitment protects both candidates and the organization.

---

# Sourcing Metrics

Recommended metrics include:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Candidate Pipeline Size | Total active candidates |
| Qualified Candidate Rate | Candidates meeting requirements |
| Source Effectiveness | Successful hires by source |
| Response Rate | Candidate outreach responses |
| Referral Hiring Rate | Hires through referrals |
| Time to Source | Time to identify candidates |
| Cost per Candidate | Recruitment cost |
| Pipeline Conversion Rate | Progress through hiring stages |

Metrics should guide continuous improvement.

---

# Documentation Requirements

Maintain records for:

- Sourcing strategies
- Candidate outreach
- Talent pipelines
- Referral records
- Recruitment campaigns
- Candidate communication
- Source performance
- Recruitment metrics

Documentation should comply with company privacy and retention policies.

---

# Best Practices

Recommended:

- Build talent pipelines continuously
- Use multiple sourcing channels
- Personalize candidate outreach
- Invest in employer branding
- Encourage employee referrals
- Maintain candidate relationships
- Track sourcing performance
- Review sourcing strategies regularly

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Hiring only when positions become urgent
- Overreliance on one sourcing channel
- Generic outreach messages
- Ignoring internal candidates
- Poor candidate communication
- Outdated candidate databases
- Biased sourcing practices
- Failing to measure recruitment effectiveness

# Future Evolution

Candidate sourcing at MIANX will evolve through:

- AI-assisted candidate matching
- Skills-based talent discovery
- Automated sourcing workflows
- Talent intelligence platforms
- Predictive hiring analytics
- Global talent networks
- Internal talent marketplace
- AI-powered recruitment assistants
