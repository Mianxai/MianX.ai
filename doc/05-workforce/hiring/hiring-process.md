# Hiring Process

> This document defines the official hiring process used by MIANX. It establishes a consistent, transparent, and scalable recruitment workflow to ensure every hiring decision is based on merit, organizational needs, and long-term success.

The hiring process is designed to identify talented individuals who align with MIANX's technical standards, values, culture, and vision.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Hiring Process is to standardize recruitment across all departments while providing an excellent experience for candidates and hiring teams.

---

# Objectives

The hiring process aims to:

- Standardize recruitment workflows
- Ensure fair candidate evaluation
- Improve hiring quality
- Reduce hiring bias
- Shorten hiring time
- Improve candidate experience
- Support workforce planning
- Document hiring decisions
- Maintain compliance
- Enable organizational growth

---

# Hiring Principles

Every hiring decision should follow these principles:

- Hire for potential and capability
- Prioritize integrity
- Use objective evaluation
- Respect candidate time
- Maintain confidentiality
- Document every stage
- Eliminate unnecessary delays
- Make evidence-based decisions

---

# Phase 1 — Workforce Planning

Hiring begins only after identifying a legitimate business need.

Inputs include:

- Team growth
- New projects
- Skill gaps
- Employee departures
- Capacity planning
- Strategic initiatives

Output:

- Approved hiring request

---

# Phase 2 — Hiring Request

The Hiring Manager submits a request including:

- Position title
- Department
- Business justification
- Employment type
- Budget
- Required experience
- Target hiring timeline

Approval is required before recruitment begins.

---

# Phase 3 — Job Description

A standardized job description should include:

- Role overview
- Responsibilities
- Required skills
- Preferred qualifications
- Experience level
- Team information
- Benefits
- Hiring process overview

Job descriptions should be reviewed before publication.

---

# Phase 4 — Candidate Sourcing

Candidates may be sourced through:

- Company careers page
- Employee referrals
- Professional networks
- Recruitment agencies
- Universities
- Open-source communities
- Conferences
- Social platforms

The sourcing strategy depends on the role and hiring urgency.

---

# Phase 5 — Resume Screening

Applications are reviewed based on:

- Relevant experience
- Technical skills
- Portfolio or projects
- Communication
- Career progression
- Education (where relevant)

Screening should use consistent evaluation criteria.

---

# Phase 6 — Initial Interview

Purpose:

- Verify candidate background
- Understand motivation
- Assess communication skills
- Explain the role
- Discuss expectations

Typical duration:

30–45 minutes

---

# Phase 7 — Technical Assessment

Candidates may complete:

- Coding challenge
- System design exercise
- Portfolio review
- Practical assignment
- Problem-solving exercise

Assessments should reflect real-world job responsibilities rather than unrelated puzzles.

---

# Phase 8 — Technical Interview

The interview focuses on:

- Technical knowledge
- Problem-solving
- Architecture understanding
- Code quality
- Collaboration
- Learning ability

Interviewers should use structured evaluation forms.

---

# Phase 9 — Final Interview

The final interview evaluates:

- Team fit
- Leadership potential
- Company values
- Long-term career goals
- Cross-functional collaboration
- Organizational alignment

For senior positions, executive participation may be required.

---

# Phase 10 — Hiring Decision

The hiring panel reviews:

- Interview feedback
- Assessment results
- Technical evaluation
- Reference checks (if applicable)
- Overall recommendation

Possible outcomes:

- Hire
- Hold
- Reject

Every decision should be documented with supporting rationale.

---

# Phase 11 — Offer Management

The offer should include:

- Position
- Compensation
- Benefits
- Start date
- Employment terms
- Reporting manager

Candidates should have sufficient time to review the offer.

---

# Phase 12 — Background Verification

Verification may include:

- Employment history
- Education
- Professional references
- Legal requirements (where applicable)

Background verification should comply with local laws and company policies.

---

# Phase 13 — Offer Acceptance

Once accepted:

- Employment records are created
- Equipment requests begin
- Accounts are prepared
- Onboarding is scheduled

The onboarding team is notified automatically.

---

# Candidate Evaluation Criteria

Candidates should be evaluated across multiple dimensions:

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Technical Skills | Languages, frameworks, architecture |
| Problem Solving | Analysis, reasoning, debugging |
| Communication | Clarity, collaboration |
| Ownership | Initiative, accountability |
| Learning Ability | Curiosity, adaptability |
| Teamwork | Collaboration, feedback |
| Cultural Alignment | Values, professionalism |

No hiring decision should rely on a single interview.

---

# Roles & Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| HR | Recruitment coordination |
| Hiring Manager | Role ownership |
| Interview Panel | Candidate evaluation |
| Department Head | Final approval |
| Founder | Executive hiring approval |
| Candidate | Participate honestly and professionally |

---

# Hiring Metrics

The hiring process should monitor:

- Time-to-Hire
- Time-to-Fill
- Offer Acceptance Rate
- Candidate Satisfaction
- Interview-to-Offer Ratio
- New Hire Retention
- Hiring Quality
- Diversity Metrics

Metrics support continuous improvement.

---

# Documentation Requirements

The following should be documented:

- Hiring requests
- Interview schedules
- Evaluation forms
- Technical assessments
- Hiring decisions
- Offer letters
- Background verification
- Final hiring records

Documentation should be stored securely.

---

# Best Practices

Recommended:

- Use structured interviews
- Evaluate consistently
- Focus on evidence
- Respect candidate time
- Communicate promptly
- Document decisions
- Involve diverse interviewers
- Continuously improve interview quality

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Unstructured interviews
- Bias-based decisions
- Excessive interview rounds
- Poor candidate communication
- Undefined evaluation criteria
- Delayed hiring decisions
- Hiring without workforce planning
- Ignoring interview feedback

---

# Future Evolution

The hiring process will evolve through:

- AI-assisted resume screening
- Automated interview scheduling
- Skills-based candidate matching
- Predictive hiring analytics
- Interview intelligence
- Internal talent marketplace
- Global hiring support
- Workforce planning automation
