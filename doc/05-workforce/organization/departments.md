# Departments

> This document defines every major department within the MIANX organization, their responsibilities, ownership, collaboration model, and operational boundaries.

Departments provide organizational structure, accountability, specialization, and scalability while working together toward common business objectives.

---

# Purpose

The Departments document standardizes how organizational functions are divided and managed across MIANX.

---

# Objectives

The department structure aims to:

- Clearly define responsibilities
- Improve organizational efficiency
- Reduce ownership conflicts
- Encourage cross-functional collaboration
- Support business growth
- Enable operational scalability
- Standardize reporting
- Improve decision making
- Strengthen accountability
- Maintain organizational consistency

---

# Department Principles

Every department should operate according to these principles:

- Clear ownership
- Customer-focused mindset
- Documentation first
- Data-driven decisions
- Continuous improvement
- Collaboration over silos
- Accountability
- Operational excellence

# Cross-Department Collaboration

Departments collaborate through:

- Shared Documentation
- Weekly Meetings
- Project Teams
- Internal APIs
- Standard Workflows
- Company OKRs

Cross-functional work is encouraged wherever possible.

---

# Reporting Structure

Every department should have:

- Department Head
- Managers (where required)
- Team Leads
- Individual Contributors

Reporting lines should remain simple and transparent.

# Performance Indicators

Departments should define measurable KPIs including:

- Delivery Speed
- Quality
- Customer Satisfaction
- Operational Efficiency
- Cost Optimization
- Innovation
- Employee Engagement

KPIs should be reviewed quarterly.

---

# Best Practices

Recommended:

- Define clear ownership
- Document responsibilities
- Encourage collaboration
- Reduce unnecessary hierarchy
- Share knowledge openly
- Measure outcomes
- Continuously improve processes
- Align departmental goals with company strategy

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Overlapping ownership
- Department silos
- Unclear reporting lines
- Excessive bureaucracy
- Poor documentation
- Isolated decision making
- Undefined responsibilities
- Lack of accountability

---

# Future Evolution

As MIANX grows:

- Departments may split into specialized divisions.
- Regional offices may introduce localized leadership.
- AI-assisted operations will automate repetitive work.
- Organizational analytics will support workforce planning.
- Cross-functional programs will expand.
