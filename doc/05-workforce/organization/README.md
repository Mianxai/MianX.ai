# Organization

> This section defines the organizational structure of the MIANX organization. It establishes how teams are organized, how leadership operates, how departments collaborate, and how responsibilities are distributed across the company.

A well-defined organizational structure enables scalability, accountability, operational efficiency, and sustainable long-term growth.

---

# Purpose

The Organization documentation provides a standardized blueprint for building and managing teams across the entire MIANX ecosystem.

---

# Objectives

This section aims to:

- Define organizational hierarchy
- Establish reporting structures
- Clarify departmental responsibilities
- Improve collaboration
- Support business scalability
- Standardize decision making
- Encourage accountability
- Maintain operational consistency
- Enable efficient communication
- Build a sustainable organization

# Organizational Principles

The MIANX organization follows these principles:

- Ownership over hierarchy
- Transparency by default
- Documentation first
- Data-driven decisions
- Customer-focused execution
- Cross-functional collaboration
- Continuous improvement
- Scalable leadership
- Technical excellence
- Operational discipline

# Organization Overview

```text
Founder

│

Executive Leadership

│

Department Heads

│

Engineering Managers

│

Team Leads

│

Individual Contributors
```

# Decision-Making Model

Decision authority is distributed according to responsibility.

| Level | Primary Responsibility |
|--------|------------------------|
| Founder | Vision & Strategic Direction |
| Executive Leadership | Company Strategy |
| Department Heads | Department Execution |
| Engineering Managers | Team Delivery |
| Team Leads | Technical Leadership |
| Individual Contributors | Execution & Ownership |

---

# Communication Model

Communication should be:

- Transparent
- Timely
- Documented
- Respectful
- Actionable
- Accessible

Important decisions should always be documented.

---

# Organizational Growth

As MIANX grows:

- Teams may split into specialized units.
- New departments may be created.
- Leadership layers may expand.
- Processes should evolve while maintaining simplicity.

Growth should not reduce accountability.
