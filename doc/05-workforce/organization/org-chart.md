# Organization Chart

> This document defines the official organizational hierarchy of MIANX. It provides a visual representation of reporting relationships, leadership structure, departmental ownership, and organizational scalability.

The organization chart ensures every employee understands where they belong, who they report to, and how departments interact across the company.

---

# Purpose

The Organization Chart establishes a clear reporting hierarchy while supporting collaboration, accountability, and future organizational growth.

---

# Objectives

The organization chart aims to:

- Define reporting relationships
- Clarify leadership hierarchy
- Improve organizational transparency
- Reduce ownership ambiguity
- Support workforce scaling
- Simplify communication paths
- Enable operational efficiency
- Standardize department structures

---

# Organizational Philosophy

MIANX is designed as an **engineering-first company** where leadership exists to enable teams—not create bureaucracy.

Leadership responsibilities include:

- Removing blockers
- Setting direction
- Mentoring teams
- Improving execution
- Supporting growth
- Making strategic decisions

# Reporting Principles

Every employee should have:

- One direct manager
- Clearly defined responsibilities
- Documented performance expectations
- Access to leadership
- Transparent communication channels

Dual reporting should only exist for temporary project-based initiatives.

---

# Span of Control

Recommended management ratios:

| Role | Recommended Team Size |
|------|------------------------|
| Team Lead | 5–8 Engineers |
| Engineering Manager | 8–15 People |
| Director | 20–50 People |
| Executive | Multiple Departments |

These values may vary depending on team maturity and business needs.

# Organizational Guidelines

The organization should:

- Minimize unnecessary hierarchy
- Encourage direct communication
- Delegate decision-making
- Empower technical leaders
- Support cross-functional collaboration
- Promote leadership through expertise

---

# Best Practices

Recommended:

- Keep reporting lines simple
- Maintain clear ownership
- Review organization annually
- Adapt structure as the company grows
- Promote leaders based on capability
- Document every organizational change
- Encourage collaboration across departments
- Avoid excessive management layers

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Multiple direct managers
- Undefined reporting structures
- Organizational silos
- Excessive hierarchy
- Role duplication
- Unclear ownership
- Frequent unnecessary restructuring
- Leadership without accountability

---

# Future Evolution

Planned organizational improvements include:

- Global regional offices
- AI-assisted workforce planning
- Skills-based team formation
- Dynamic project teams
- Internal talent marketplace
- Organization analytics
- Automated workforce dashboards
