# Roles & Responsibilities

> This section defines the role architecture of MIANX. It describes every organizational role, its responsibilities, authority, reporting structure, required competencies, and expected outcomes. The objective is to create clarity, accountability, consistency, and scalability across the company.

Every employee should clearly understand their responsibilities, decision-making authority, performance expectations, and career progression.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Roles documentation is to standardize job responsibilities across MIANX and ensure every position contributes effectively toward organizational goals.

---

# Objectives

This documentation aims to:

- Define organizational roles
- Clarify responsibilities
- Eliminate role ambiguity
- Improve accountability
- Support hiring
- Improve onboarding
- Enable career development
- Standardize performance expectations
- Support organizational scaling
- Maintain documentation consistency

```text
Executive Leadership

↓

Department Heads

↓

↓

Team Leads

↓

Senior Specialists

↓

Mid-Level Professionals

↓
```

- Reporting structure

- Related documentation

All role documents should follow a standardized format.

# Role Ownership

Every role should have:

- Defined owner
- Department assignment
- Reporting manager
- Decision authority
- Responsibility boundaries
- Performance indicators

Role ownership promotes accountability and operational clarity.

---

# Responsibilities

Responsibilities should be:

- Clearly defined
- Measurable
- Role-specific
- Business aligned
- Reviewed regularly

Responsibilities should describe outcomes rather than simply listing tasks.

---

# Decision Authority

Each role should define:

- Decisions the role can make independently
- Decisions requiring manager approval
- Budget authority (if applicable)
- Technical approval authority
- Operational approval authority

Decision boundaries help prevent confusion and delays.

---

# Career Progression

Each role should document possible growth paths.

Example:

```text
Intern

↓

Junior Engineer

↓

Software Engineer

↓

Senior Engineer

↓

Staff Engineer

↓

Principal Engineer

↓

Engineering Manager

↓

Director of Engineering
```

Career progression should be transparent and merit-based.
