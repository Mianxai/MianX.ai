# Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, decision-making scope, and performance expectations of the Chief Technology Officer (CTO) at MIANX. As an AI-first technology company, the CTO is the executive responsible for building, evolving, and protecting the company's technology ecosystem.

Unlike a traditional CTO role, the MIANX CTO owns not only software engineering but also AI infrastructure, CoreOS, platform engineering, developer experience, security architecture, cloud architecture, technical innovation, and long-term technology strategy.

---

# Purpose

The CTO ensures that MIANX builds secure, scalable, reliable, and innovative technology capable of supporting the company's long-term vision.

The CTO transforms business strategy into technical reality.

---

# Mission

The CTO exists to:

- Build world-class engineering
- Create scalable platforms
- Drive AI innovation
- Protect technical quality
- Enable developer productivity
- Build resilient infrastructure
- Improve engineering excellence
- Future-proof company technology

---

# Role Summary

The CTO provides executive leadership for every technical function across MIANX.

The CTO ensures:

- Technology supports business goals
- Architecture remains scalable
- Engineering teams operate efficiently
- AI systems remain reliable

- Technical debt is actively managed
- Innovation becomes part of company culture
