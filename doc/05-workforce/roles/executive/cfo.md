# Chief Financial Officer (CFO)

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, and performance expectations of the Chief Financial Officer (CFO) at MIANX. The CFO is responsible for protecting the company's financial health, ensuring sustainable growth, managing financial risk, and enabling strategic decision-making through accurate financial planning and analysis.

The CFO serves as the executive responsible for all financial operations and long-term financial sustainability.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the CFO role is to establish a strong financial foundation that enables MIANX to innovate, scale, and operate responsibly while maximizing long-term value.

---

# Mission

The CFO exists to:

- Protect company finances
- Ensure long-term sustainability
- Support strategic growth
- Manage financial risk
- Improve financial transparency
- Enable informed decision-making
- Optimize resource allocation
- Build financial discipline

---

# Role Summary

The CFO oversees every financial aspect of MIANX.

The CFO ensures that:

- Financial operations remain accurate
- Budgets align with strategy
- Spending is controlled
- Investments are evaluated
- Risks are identified
- Compliance requirements are met
- Financial reporting is reliable

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Objective |
|------|-----------|
| Revenue Growth | Sustainable business expansion |
| Gross Margin | Healthy profitability |
| Operating Margin | Efficient operations |
| Budget Accuracy | Reliable planning |
| Cash Flow Stability | Financial sustainability |
| Forecast Accuracy | Predictable financial performance |
| Operating Expense Ratio | Cost optimization |
| Financial Close Cycle | Efficient reporting |
| Audit Findings | Strong internal controls |
| Return on Investment (ROI) | Effective capital allocation |

KPIs should be monitored monthly, quarterly, and annually.
