# Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO)

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, and expectations of the Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO) at MIANX. The CHRO is responsible for building a world-class workforce by developing people, strengthening company culture, establishing HR governance, and ensuring that MIANX attracts, develops, and retains exceptional talent.

The CHRO is the executive responsible for every aspect of the employee lifecycle.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the CHRO role is to create an environment where talented people can perform at their highest potential while supporting MIANX's long-term strategic objectives.

---

# Mission

The CHRO exists to:

- Build exceptional teams
- Develop future leaders
- Strengthen company culture
- Improve employee experience
- Ensure fair HR practices
- Promote continuous learning
- Support organizational growth
- Build a high-performance workplace

---

# Role Summary

The CHRO leads all people operations across MIANX.

The CHRO ensures that:

- Hiring is effective
- Employees grow professionally
- Leaders receive proper support
- HR policies remain consistent
- Culture remains healthy
- Performance is continuously improved
- Workforce planning supports business growth
