# Chief Marketing Officer (CMO)

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, and expectations of the Chief Marketing Officer (CMO) at MIANX. The CMO is responsible for building the company's brand, driving sustainable growth, generating demand, and ensuring consistent communication with customers, partners, and the global market.

The CMO transforms company value into market awareness, customer acquisition, and long-term brand trust.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the CMO role is to develop and execute marketing strategies that increase brand recognition, customer engagement, and business growth while maintaining a consistent and trusted market presence.

---

# Mission

The CMO exists to:

- Build a globally recognized brand
- Generate sustainable demand
- Increase customer acquisition
- Strengthen customer relationships
- Improve market positioning
- Support revenue growth
- Expand global reach
- Build long-term brand equity

---

# Role Summary

The CMO leads every marketing function across MIANX.

The CMO ensures that:

- The brand remains consistent
- Marketing supports business objectives
- Customer acquisition is scalable
- Market opportunities are identified
- Marketing investments deliver measurable ROI
- Customer trust continuously grows

# Decision Authority

The CMO has authority over:

- Marketing strategy
- Brand standards
- Campaign approvals
- Marketing budgets (within approved limits)
- Content strategy
- Communication guidelines
- Advertising initiatives
- Marketing technologies
- Event planning
- Marketing partnerships

Major brand repositioning or strategic investments require CEO and Founder approval.

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Objective |
|------|-----------|
| Brand Awareness | Increase market recognition |
| Website Traffic | Grow qualified visitors |
| Marketing Qualified Leads (MQLs) | Increase pipeline quality |
| Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) | Optimize acquisition efficiency |
| Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) | Improve long-term value |
| Conversion Rate | Improve marketing effectiveness |
| Campaign ROI | Maximize marketing investment |
| Organic Search Growth | Increase SEO performance |
| Social Engagement | Strengthen community presence |
| Marketing Contribution to Revenue | Support business growth |

KPIs should be reviewed monthly and quarterly to ensure marketing effectiveness.
