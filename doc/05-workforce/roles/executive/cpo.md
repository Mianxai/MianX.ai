# Chief Product Officer (CPO)

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, and expectations of the Chief Product Officer (CPO) at MIANX. The CPO is responsible for transforming business vision into products that solve real customer problems while ensuring alignment between users, business goals, design, engineering, and AI innovation.

The CPO owns the complete product lifecycle—from product strategy to successful customer adoption.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the CPO role is to create products that generate long-term value for customers and support MIANX's strategic growth.

---

# Mission

The CPO exists to:

- Build outstanding products
- Understand customer needs
- Define product strategy
- Align business and technology
- Deliver customer value
- Drive innovation
- Improve user experience
- Build a product-driven culture

---

# Role Summary

The CPO leads the company's product organization and ensures every product aligns with the company's mission, customer needs, and business strategy.

The CPO connects:

- Business
- Customers
- Engineering
- Design
- AI
- Sales
- Marketing
- Customer Success

into one unified product vision.
