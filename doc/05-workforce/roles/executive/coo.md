# Chief Operating Officer (COO)

> This document defines the role, responsibilities, authority, and operational leadership of the Chief Operating Officer (COO) at MIANX. The COO is responsible for transforming company strategy into efficient day-to-day operations, ensuring that every department executes consistently, efficiently, and at scale.

The COO is the executive responsible for operational excellence across the organization.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the COO role is to build and maintain operational systems that enable MIANX to grow sustainably while delivering high-quality products and services.

---

# Mission

The COO exists to:

- Execute company strategy
- Optimize operations
- Build scalable processes
- Improve organizational efficiency
- Enable cross-functional collaboration
- Support business growth
- Remove operational bottlenecks
- Deliver operational excellence

---

# Role Summary

The COO oversees the operational execution of MIANX.

The COO ensures that:

- Teams operate efficiently
- Business processes are standardized
- Projects are delivered successfully
- Resources are utilized effectively
- Departments collaborate efficiently
- Company objectives are achieved

---

The COO leads:

- Operational Roadmap

- Process Standardization
- Business Scaling
- Operational Automation

- Delivery Excellence

# Operational Responsibilities

The COO oversees:

- Daily Operations
- Department Coordination
- Resource Allocation
- Operational KPIs
- Internal Workflows
- Company Policies
- Operational Reviews
- Vendor Relationships
- Business Performance

Operational ownership focuses on execution rather than technology strategy.

---

# Decision Authority

The COO has authority over:

- Operational policies
- Company procedures
- Resource coordination
- Operational priorities
- Internal process improvements
- Vendor approvals (within policy)
- Business process optimization
- Program execution
- Operational reporting
- Delivery management

Major strategic changes require CEO and Founder approval.

---

# Leadership Responsibilities

The COO develops:

- Operational leaders
- Department managers
- Program managers
- Delivery managers
- Process owners
- Operations specialists

Leadership should create consistency and accountability across the organization.
