# Chief Revenue Officer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Chief Revenue Officer (CRO) at MIANX.

The Chief Revenue Officer (CRO) is the executive responsible for all revenue-generating functions across MIANX. The CRO leads Sales, Business Development, Revenue Operations, Customer Success, Strategic Partnerships, and Commercial Strategy to maximize sustainable revenue growth while ensuring exceptional customer experience and long-term profitability.

The Chief Revenue Officer reports directly to the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) and serves as a member of the Executive Leadership Team.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Chief Revenue Officer role is to create a unified revenue organization that drives predictable growth, maximizes customer lifetime value, aligns commercial operations, and supports the strategic objectives of MIANX.

---

The Chief Revenue Officer exists to:

- Accelerate revenue growth
- Expand market presence
- Improve customer acquisition
- Increase customer retention
- Optimize commercial operations
- Build scalable sales organizations
- Strengthen strategic partnerships
- Maximize customer lifetime value
