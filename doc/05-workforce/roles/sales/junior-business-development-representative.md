# Junior Business Development Representative

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Junior Business Development Representative (Junior BDR) at MIANX.

The Junior Business Development Representative (Junior BDR) is responsible for supporting lead generation activities, conducting prospect research, assisting with outbound outreach, maintaining CRM records, qualifying basic opportunities under supervision, and developing the skills required for a successful career in business development and sales.

The Junior Business Development Representative reports directly to the Business Development Manager and works under the guidance of the Senior Business Development Representative.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Junior Business Development Representative role is to build foundational business development skills while supporting pipeline generation through prospect research, outreach activities, and lead qualification.

---

# Mission

The Junior Business Development Representative exists to:

- Learn business development processes
- Support lead generation
- Build prospect relationships
- Maintain CRM accuracy
- Assist sales pipeline development
- Improve outreach skills
- Support the sales organization
- Prepare for career advancement

---

# Role Summary

The Junior Business Development Representative supports:

- Prospect Research
- Lead Generation
- Outbound Outreach
- CRM Management
- Discovery Preparation
- Appointment Scheduling
- Lead Qualification Support
- Sales Documentation
- Activity Tracking
- Pipeline Development

# Primary Responsibilities

The Junior Business Development Representative is responsible for:

- Researching target companies
- Identifying potential decision-makers
- Assisting with outbound prospecting
- Sending approved outreach messages
- Updating CRM records
- Maintaining prospect information
- Scheduling introductory meetings
- Supporting lead qualification
- Tracking daily activities
- Completing assigned training programs

---

# Business Development Responsibilities

The Junior Business Development Representative performs:

- Company Research
- Contact Research
- Cold Email Support
- LinkedIn Prospecting
- CRM Updates
- Lead Qualification Assistance
- Meeting Coordination
- Prospect Follow-ups

Business development activities should follow established sales procedures and organizational standards.

Pipeline contribution should improve as knowledge and experience increase.

# Decision Authority

The Junior Business Development Representative may independently:

- Update CRM records
- Conduct prospect research
- Schedule meetings
- Execute approved outreach
- Maintain prospect databases
- Document activities
- Escalate qualified leads
- Recommend prospect updates

Lead qualification approvals, commercial discussions, pricing decisions, and contract negotiations require senior team approval.

# Core Competencies

The Junior Business Development Representative should demonstrate knowledge of:

- Business Development Fundamentals

- Business Communication

The Junior Business Development Representative is expected to:

- Complete assigned outreach activities
- Maintain accurate CRM records
- Learn business development processes
- Support qualified pipeline generation

- Follow organizational procedures
