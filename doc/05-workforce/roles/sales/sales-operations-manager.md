# Sales Operations Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Sales Operations Manager at MIANX.

The Sales Operations Manager is responsible for optimizing sales processes, managing sales systems, maintaining CRM governance, improving operational efficiency, supporting sales forecasting, and enabling the Sales organization through automation, analytics, and standardized operational practices. The role ensures that the Sales team operates efficiently, accurately, and at scale.

The Sales Operations Manager reports directly to the Head of Sales.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Sales Operations Manager role is to maximize sales productivity by designing scalable operational processes, maintaining reliable sales systems, and providing data-driven operational support for revenue growth.

---

# Mission

The Sales Operations Manager exists to:

- Improve sales efficiency
- Optimize sales processes
- Maintain CRM governance
- Deliver operational excellence
- Support revenue forecasting
- Enable data-driven decisions
- Standardize sales operations
- Scale organizational growth
