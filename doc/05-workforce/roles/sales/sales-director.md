# Sales Director

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Sales Director at MIANX.

The Sales Director is responsible for leading the operational execution of the Sales organization by translating executive revenue strategy into departmental plans, managing sales leadership, improving sales performance, and ensuring the achievement of revenue objectives. The role oversees regional or organizational sales teams while maintaining operational excellence and customer-centric selling practices.

The Sales Director reports directly to the Vice President of Sales.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Sales Director role is to lead the execution of MIANX's sales strategy by building high-performing teams, improving operational efficiency, and delivering consistent revenue growth.

---

The Sales Director exists to:

- Execute sales strategy
- Achieve revenue targets
- Build high-performing sales teams
- Improve sales operations
- Develop future sales leaders

- Improve forecasting accuracy
- Drive continuous sales improvement
