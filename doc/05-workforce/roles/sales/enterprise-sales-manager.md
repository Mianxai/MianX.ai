# Enterprise Sales Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Enterprise Sales Manager at MIANX.

The Enterprise Sales Manager is responsible for leading enterprise sales initiatives, managing strategic customer relationships, overseeing enterprise account executives, and delivering high-value commercial agreements. The role focuses on acquiring, expanding, and retaining enterprise customers while ensuring predictable revenue growth and exceptional customer experience.

The Enterprise Sales Manager reports directly to the Head of Sales.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Enterprise Sales Manager role is to drive enterprise revenue growth by managing strategic accounts, leading enterprise sales teams, developing long-term customer partnerships, and executing complex sales opportunities.

---

# Mission

The Enterprise Sales Manager exists to:

- Grow enterprise revenue
- Acquire strategic customers
- Expand existing enterprise accounts
- Improve customer retention
- Increase contract value
- Lead enterprise sales teams
- Strengthen customer relationships
- Support sustainable business growth
