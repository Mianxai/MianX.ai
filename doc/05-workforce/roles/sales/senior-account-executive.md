# Senior Account Executive

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Senior Account Executive at MIANX.

The Senior Account Executive is responsible for managing strategic sales opportunities, developing enterprise customer relationships, negotiating commercial agreements, mentoring junior sales professionals, and consistently achieving revenue targets. The role focuses on acquiring high-value customers while expanding existing accounts and supporting long-term business growth.

The Senior Account Executive reports directly to the Enterprise Sales Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Senior Account Executive role is to generate sustainable revenue through strategic customer engagement, enterprise sales execution, and successful management of complex sales opportunities.

---

# Mission

The Senior Account Executive exists to:

- Grow enterprise revenue
- Win strategic customers
- Expand existing accounts
- Build trusted customer relationships
- Mentor junior sales staff
- Improve sales execution
- Increase customer lifetime value
- Support organizational growth

---

The Senior Account Executive is responsible for:

- Business Development Representatives

# Decision Authority

The Senior Account Executive may approve:

- Opportunity Qualification
- Sales Proposals
- Customer Meetings

- Sales Forecast Updates

Pricing exceptions, contractual deviations, and non-standard commercial terms require management approval.
