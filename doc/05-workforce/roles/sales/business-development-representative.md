# Business Development Representative

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Business Development Representative (BDR) at MIANX.

The Business Development Representative (BDR) is responsible for identifying potential customers, generating qualified sales opportunities, conducting outbound prospecting, qualifying inbound leads, and scheduling meetings for the Sales team. The role serves as the bridge between Marketing and Sales by ensuring a continuous flow of qualified prospects into the sales pipeline.

The Business Development Representative reports directly to the Business Development Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Business Development Representative role is to generate high-quality sales opportunities through effective prospecting, lead qualification, and customer engagement while supporting sustainable revenue growth.

---

# Mission

The Business Development Representative exists to:

- Generate qualified leads
- Build a healthy sales pipeline
- Identify new business opportunities
- Engage prospective customers
- Support revenue growth
- Improve lead quality
- Strengthen market presence
- Support enterprise sales success

---

# Role Summary

The Business Development Representative manages:

- Lead Generation
- Outbound Prospecting
- Inbound Lead Qualification
- Discovery Calls
- CRM Management
- Meeting Scheduling
- Pipeline Development
- Prospect Research
- Sales Outreach
- Activity Reporting

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Business Development Representative is responsible for:

- Researching target accounts
- Identifying decision-makers
- Conducting outbound prospecting
- Qualifying inbound and outbound leads
- Scheduling meetings for Account Executives
- Updating CRM records
- Maintaining prospect databases
- Executing outreach campaigns
- Following established qualification frameworks
- Meeting activity and pipeline targets

---

# Business Development Responsibilities

The Business Development Representative performs:

- Cold Calling
- Cold Emailing
- LinkedIn Prospecting
- Lead Qualification
- Discovery Conversations
- Prospect Research
- Appointment Setting
- Opportunity Creation

Business development activities should consistently produce qualified opportunities for the Sales organization.

---

# Pipeline Responsibilities

The Business Development Representative contributes to:

- Qualified Lead Generation
- Pipeline Growth
- Opportunity Creation
- Prospect Engagement
- Market Intelligence
- Sales Readiness
- Lead Conversion
- Revenue Pipeline Support

Pipeline quality should be maintained through disciplined qualification and timely follow-up.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The Business Development Representative ensures:

- CRM Accuracy
- Prospect Documentation
- Activity Logging
- Qualification Compliance
- Outreach Consistency
- Timely Reporting
- Sales Process Compliance
- Data Quality

Operational consistency supports predictable sales performance.

---

# Collaboration

The Business Development Representative collaborates with:

- Business Development Manager
- Senior Business Development Representative
- Enterprise Sales Manager
- Account Executives
- Marketing Team
- Sales Operations Team
- Product Team
- Customer Success Team
- Revenue Operations
- Executive Leadership (when required)

Cross-functional collaboration ensures smooth lead handoffs and efficient customer acquisition.

---

# Decision Authority

The Business Development Representative may independently:

- Execute approved outreach campaigns
- Qualify prospects
- Schedule sales meetings
- Update CRM records
- Recommend opportunity progression
- Prioritize daily prospecting activities
- Escalate qualified opportunities
- Maintain prospect documentation

Commercial negotiations, pricing decisions, and contractual discussions remain the responsibility of Account Executives and Sales Management.

---

# Core Competencies

The Business Development Representative should demonstrate proficiency in:

- Lead Generation
- Prospect Research
- Cold Outreach
- CRM Systems
- Business Communication
- Sales Qualification
- Customer Engagement
- Time Management
- Active Listening
- Problem Solving

---

# Performance Expectations

The Business Development Representative is expected to:

- Achieve monthly lead generation targets
- Maintain outreach activity goals
- Improve lead qualification quality
- Schedule qualified sales meetings
- Maintain accurate CRM records
- Respond promptly to inbound inquiries
- Continuously improve prospecting skills
- Contribute to pipeline growth

---

| KPI | Objective |
|------|-----------|
| Qualified Leads Generated | Achieve monthly qualification targets |
| Meetings Scheduled | Deliver qualified sales meetings |
| Outreach Activity | Meet daily and weekly outreach goals |
| Lead Qualification Accuracy | Maintain high-quality opportunities |
| CRM Accuracy | Maintain complete prospect records |
| Response Rate | Improve prospect engagement |
| Pipeline Contribution | Support healthy sales pipeline growth |
| Activity Completion | Achieve prospecting targets |
| Sales Accepted Leads | Increase accepted opportunities |
| Continuous Improvement | Improve outreach effectiveness |

Performance evaluations focus on lead generation, activity consistency, qualification quality, CRM discipline, collaboration, communication, and contribution to pipeline growth.
