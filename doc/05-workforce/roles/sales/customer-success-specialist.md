# Customer Success Specialist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Customer Success Specialist at MIANX.

The Customer Success Specialist is responsible for managing assigned customer accounts, ensuring successful onboarding, supporting product adoption, maintaining customer satisfaction, monitoring customer health, and assisting with renewals and expansion opportunities. The role serves as the primary point of contact for customers after implementation and helps maximize long-term customer value.

The Customer Success Specialist reports directly to the Customer Success Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Customer Success Specialist role is to ensure customers successfully adopt MIANX products and services while improving retention, satisfaction, and long-term customer success.

---

# Mission

The Customer Success Specialist exists to:

- Deliver exceptional customer experiences
- Improve customer retention
- Increase product adoption
- Strengthen customer relationships
- Reduce customer churn
- Resolve customer issues
- Support revenue retention
- Drive customer success

# Decision Authority

The Customer Success Specialist may independently:

- Manage assigned customer accounts
- Conduct onboarding sessions
- Schedule customer meetings
- Update CRM records
- Recommend product adoption plans
- Escalate customer concerns
- Maintain customer documentation
- Recommend customer success improvements

Pricing discussions, contractual modifications, strategic account decisions, and commercial negotiations require approval from Customer Success Management or Sales Leadership.
