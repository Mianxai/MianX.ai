# Junior Account Executive

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Junior Account Executive at MIANX.

The Junior Account Executive is responsible for supporting the sales team by managing entry-level sales opportunities, assisting with customer communications, maintaining CRM records, preparing sales documentation, conducting product demonstrations under supervision, and developing the knowledge and skills required for independent account management.

The Junior Account Executive reports directly to the Enterprise Sales Manager and works under the guidance of Senior Account Executives.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Junior Account Executive role is to develop future sales professionals while supporting customer acquisition, account management, and revenue generation through structured learning and practical sales experience.

---

# Mission

The Junior Account Executive exists to:

- Learn professional sales practices
- Support revenue generation
- Build customer relationships
- Assist senior sales staff
- Maintain accurate CRM records
- Improve sales knowledge
- Deliver excellent customer experiences
- Prepare for career progression
