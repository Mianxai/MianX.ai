# Account Executive

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Account Executive at MIANX.

The Account Executive is responsible for managing qualified sales opportunities, building customer relationships, conducting product demonstrations, negotiating commercial agreements, and achieving assigned sales targets. The role serves as the primary point of contact for prospective and existing customers throughout the sales lifecycle, ensuring a professional buying experience while contributing to sustainable revenue growth.

The Account Executive reports directly to the Enterprise Sales Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Account Executive role is to convert qualified opportunities into long-term customers by delivering value-driven sales engagements, maintaining strong customer relationships, and consistently achieving revenue objectives.

---

# Mission

The Account Executive exists to:

- Achieve sales targets
- Convert qualified leads
- Build customer relationships
- Increase customer satisfaction
- Expand customer accounts
- Maintain accurate forecasts
- Improve sales performance
- Support organizational growth

# Primary Responsibilities

The Account Executive is responsible for:

- Managing assigned sales opportunities
- Conducting discovery meetings
- Delivering product demonstrations
- Preparing commercial proposals
- Negotiating standard agreements
- Closing qualified opportunities
- Maintaining CRM records
- Following up with customers
- Coordinating with internal teams
- Meeting monthly and quarterly sales targets

- Solution Selling

- Sales Negotiation
