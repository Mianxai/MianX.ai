# Senior Customer Success Specialist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Senior Customer Success Specialist at MIANX.

The Senior Customer Success Specialist is responsible for managing strategic customer relationships, ensuring successful product adoption, driving customer retention, mentoring Customer Success Specialists, and supporting renewal and expansion initiatives. The role serves as the senior individual contributor within the Customer Success team and plays a key role in maximizing customer lifetime value.

The Senior Customer Success Specialist reports directly to the Customer Success Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Senior Customer Success Specialist role is to maximize customer success by proactively managing customer relationships, improving product adoption, reducing churn, and mentoring junior team members.

---

# Mission

The Senior Customer Success Specialist exists to:

- Improve customer retention
- Increase customer satisfaction
- Drive product adoption
- Reduce customer churn
- Build trusted customer relationships
- Mentor junior specialists
- Support revenue expansion
- Deliver exceptional customer experiences

---

The Senior Customer Success Specialist manages:

- Strategic Customer Accounts
- Customer Onboarding
- Product Adoption
- Customer Health Monitoring
- Renewal Readiness
- Expansion Opportunity Identification
- Customer Success Planning

- Customer Reporting

# Primary Responsibilities

The Senior Customer Success Specialist is responsible for:

- Managing strategic customer accounts
- Leading customer onboarding activities
- Monitoring customer health scores
- Conducting business review meetings
- Supporting renewal planning
- Identifying upsell and cross-sell opportunities
- Resolving customer escalations
- Mentoring Customer Success Specialists
- Maintaining CRM records
- Preparing customer success reports

Customer success activities should consistently increase customer value realization and long-term engagement.

# Decision Authority

The Senior Customer Success Specialist may independently:

- Manage assigned customer accounts
- Develop customer success plans
- Schedule executive business reviews
- Recommend renewal strategies
- Escalate customer issues
- Update CRM records
- Mentor junior specialists
- Recommend process improvements

Commercial negotiations, contract modifications, pricing decisions, and strategic account approvals remain the responsibility of Sales Leadership.
