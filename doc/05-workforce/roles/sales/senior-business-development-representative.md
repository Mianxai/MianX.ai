# Senior Business Development Representative

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Senior Business Development Representative at MIANX.

The Senior Business Development Representative (Senior BDR) is responsible for generating qualified sales opportunities, mentoring Business Development Representatives, executing outbound prospecting strategies, and supporting strategic pipeline development. The role serves as the senior individual contributor within the Business Development team and plays a critical role in filling the enterprise sales pipeline.

The Senior Business Development Representative reports directly to the Business Development Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Senior Business Development Representative role is to consistently generate high-quality sales opportunities while improving prospecting effectiveness, mentoring junior team members, and supporting predictable revenue growth.

---

# Mission

The Senior Business Development Representative exists to:

- Generate qualified opportunities
- Expand sales pipeline
- Build strategic prospect relationships
- Improve outbound performance
- Mentor junior representatives
- Increase conversion rates
- Support enterprise sales
- Drive business growth

---

# Role Summary

The Senior Business Development Representative manages:

- Outbound Prospecting
- Lead Qualification
- Enterprise Prospecting
- Pipeline Development
- Sales Outreach
- Discovery Calls
- Prospect Research
- CRM Management
- BDR Mentorship
- Opportunity Handoffs

# Primary Responsibilities

The Senior Business Development Representative is responsible for:

- Identifying qualified prospects
- Executing outbound campaigns
- Conducting discovery calls
- Qualifying business opportunities
- Scheduling meetings for Account Executives
- Maintaining CRM accuracy
- Mentoring BDRs
- Supporting sales campaigns
- Tracking prospect engagement
- Meeting pipeline generation targets

# Business Development Responsibilities

The Senior Business Development Representative performs:

- Enterprise Prospecting
- Account Research
- Cold Outreach
- Email Campaigns
- LinkedIn Outreach
- Discovery Calls
- Opportunity Qualification
- Meeting Scheduling

Business development activities should consistently produce qualified opportunities for the Sales organization.

# Collaboration

The Senior Business Development Representative collaborates with:

- Enterprise Sales Manager

- Sales Operations

- Customer Success Team
- Revenue Operations

# Decision Authority

The Senior Business Development Representative may independently:

- Qualify prospects
- Execute outreach campaigns
- Schedule sales meetings
- Update CRM records
- Recommend opportunity progression
- Prioritize prospect lists
- Mentor junior representatives
- Escalate strategic opportunities

Pricing, contractual discussions, and commercial negotiations remain the responsibility of the Sales team.

- Improve outreach effectiveness
- Mentor junior team members
- Maintain CRM accuracy

- Increase meeting conversion
