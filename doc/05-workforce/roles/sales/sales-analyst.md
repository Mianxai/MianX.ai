# Sales Analyst

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Sales Analyst at MIANX.

The Sales Analyst is responsible for collecting, analyzing, interpreting, and reporting sales data to support strategic decision-making across the Sales organization. The role provides actionable insights into pipeline performance, revenue trends, sales productivity, customer behavior, forecasting, and operational effectiveness to improve business performance.

The Sales Analyst reports directly to the Sales Operations Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Sales Analyst role is to transform sales data into actionable business insights that improve forecasting accuracy, operational efficiency, pipeline performance, and revenue growth.

---

# Mission

The Sales Analyst exists to:

- Deliver accurate sales insights
- Improve decision-making
- Support revenue forecasting
- Monitor sales performance
- Identify growth opportunities
- Improve reporting quality
- Optimize sales operations
- Enable data-driven strategies

---

# Role Summary

The Sales Analyst manages:

- Sales Analytics
- Revenue Reporting
- Pipeline Analysis
- Forecast Modeling
- KPI Measurement
- Dashboard Development
- Performance Analysis
- Data Validation
- Market Analysis

# Primary Responsibilities

The Sales Analyst is responsible for:

- Collecting sales performance data
- Analyzing pipeline health
- Preparing executive reports
- Maintaining sales dashboards
- Monitoring KPIs
- Supporting revenue forecasting
- Identifying performance trends
- Conducting opportunity analysis
- Recommending process improvements
- Maintaining analytical documentation

# Analytics Responsibilities

The Sales Analyst performs:

- Sales Trend Analysis
- Pipeline Analytics
- Revenue Analysis
- Conversion Analysis
- Customer Segmentation
- Territory Performance Analysis
- Product Performance Analysis
- Win/Loss Analysis

Analytics should support strategic and operational business decisions.

# Reporting Responsibilities

The Sales Analyst prepares:

- Sales Performance Reports
- Revenue Reports

- KPI Dashboards
- Executive Summaries
- Pipeline Reports

- Business Intelligence Reports

# Decision Authority

The Sales Analyst may independently:

- Build dashboards
- Validate sales data

- Analyze sales trends
