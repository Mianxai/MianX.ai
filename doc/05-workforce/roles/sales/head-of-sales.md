# Head of Sales

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Head of Sales at MIANX.

The Head of Sales is responsible for managing the day-to-day execution of the Sales organization by leading sales managers, enterprise sales teams, account executives, business development teams, and sales operations. The role ensures consistent sales execution, achievement of revenue targets, customer satisfaction, pipeline growth, and continuous improvement of sales processes.

The Head of Sales reports directly to the Sales Director.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Head of Sales role is to execute departmental sales strategy, maximize team performance, improve operational efficiency, and ensure sustainable revenue growth through effective leadership and customer-focused selling.

---

The Head of Sales exists to:

- Deliver sales targets
- Improve pipeline quality
- Develop high-performing teams
- Increase customer acquisition
- Improve customer retention
- Strengthen sales execution
- Optimize operational efficiency
- Support long-term business growth
