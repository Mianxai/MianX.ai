# Customer Success Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Customer Success Manager at MIANX.

The Customer Success Manager (CSM) is responsible for ensuring customers achieve measurable value from MIANX products and services. The role focuses on customer onboarding, adoption, retention, expansion opportunities, relationship management, and long-term customer satisfaction while serving as the primary post-sales point of contact.

The Customer Success Manager reports directly to the Head of Sales.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Customer Success Manager role is to maximize customer value, improve retention, strengthen customer relationships, and identify growth opportunities throughout the customer lifecycle.

---

# Mission

The Customer Success Manager exists to:

- Improve customer satisfaction
- Increase customer retention
- Drive product adoption
- Build long-term relationships
- Reduce customer churn
- Identify expansion opportunities
- Support customer success
- Improve customer experience

---

The Customer Success Manager manages:

- Customer Onboarding

- Product Adoption

# Primary Responsibilities

The Customer Success Manager is responsible for:

- Managing customer relationships
- Leading customer onboarding
- Monitoring customer health
- Supporting product adoption
- Managing customer renewals
- Identifying upsell opportunities
- Resolving customer escalations
- Leading the Customer Success team
- Preparing customer success reports
- Maintaining customer documentation
