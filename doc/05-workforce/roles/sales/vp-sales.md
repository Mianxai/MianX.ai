# Vice President of Sales

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Vice President of Sales at MIANX.

The Vice President of Sales is responsible for leading the global Sales organization, executing the commercial strategy established by the Chief Revenue Officer, and ensuring sustainable revenue growth through effective sales leadership, operational excellence, forecasting, and customer acquisition. The role oversees all sales teams, sales leadership, enterprise sales initiatives, and sales operations.

The Vice President of Sales reports directly to the Chief Revenue Officer (CRO).

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Vice President of Sales role is to transform organizational revenue strategy into measurable sales execution by building high-performing teams, developing scalable sales processes, and consistently achieving revenue objectives.

---

The Vice President of Sales exists to:

- Execute revenue strategy
- Grow annual revenue

- Expand enterprise customers

- Drive commercial excellence
