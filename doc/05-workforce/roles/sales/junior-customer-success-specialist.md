# Junior Customer Success Specialist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Junior Customer Success Specialist at MIANX.

The Junior Customer Success Specialist is responsible for assisting with customer onboarding, supporting product adoption, maintaining customer records, responding to routine customer inquiries, monitoring customer health under supervision, and developing the knowledge and skills required for a successful career in Customer Success.

The Junior Customer Success Specialist reports directly to the Customer Success Manager and works under the guidance of the Senior Customer Success Specialist.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Junior Customer Success Specialist role is to support customer success operations while developing the skills necessary to independently manage customer relationships and contribute to long-term customer satisfaction.

---

# Mission

The Junior Customer Success Specialist exists to:

- Support customer success initiatives
- Assist customer onboarding
- Improve customer experiences
- Promote product adoption
- Maintain customer records
- Learn customer success best practices
- Support retention efforts
- Develop professional capabilities

The Junior Customer Success Specialist is responsible for:

- Responding to routine customer inquiries

- Escalating customer issues when necessary

- Completing customer success training programs

# Decision Authority

The Junior Customer Success Specialist may independently:

- Update CRM records
- Schedule customer meetings
- Conduct approved customer follow-ups
- Maintain customer documentation
- Monitor assigned customer activities
- Prepare routine reports
- Escalate customer issues
- Recommend documentation improvements

Renewal approvals, pricing discussions, commercial negotiations, customer success strategies, and strategic account decisions require approval from senior team members.
