# Sales Roles

> This directory defines the organizational structure, responsibilities, reporting hierarchy, authority, and career progression for all Sales roles within MIANX.

The Sales organization is responsible for generating revenue, acquiring customers, expanding strategic accounts, managing sales operations, developing partnerships, and maintaining long-term customer relationships. Sales teams work closely with Marketing, Product, Customer Success, Finance, and Executive Leadership to achieve sustainable business growth.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this directory is to:

- Standardize Sales role definitions
- Clarify responsibilities and ownership
- Define reporting relationships
- Establish promotion pathways
- Improve organizational consistency
- Support hiring and onboarding
- Enable workforce planning
- Maintain governance across Sales functions

---

# Sales Organization

The Sales organization consists of executive leadership, management, account management, business development, sales operations, and customer acquisition roles.

```text
Chief Revenue Officer
│
└── Vice President of Sales

        │
        └── Head of Sales
```

# Role Categories

## Executive Leadership

Responsible for organizational sales strategy, revenue growth, forecasting, partnerships, and executive decision-making.

Roles:

- Chief Revenue Officer
- Vice President of Sales

---

## Department Leadership

Responsible for departmental execution, sales planning, performance management, coaching, and operational oversight.

Roles:

- Sales Director
- Head of Sales

---

## Enterprise Sales

Responsible for managing strategic customer relationships and closing high-value opportunities.

Roles:

- Enterprise Sales Manager
- Senior Account Executive
- Account Executive
- Junior Account Executive

---

## Business Development

Responsible for pipeline generation, outbound prospecting, lead qualification, and partnership development.

Roles:

- Business Development Manager
- Senior Business Development Representative
- Business Development Representative
- Junior Business Development Representative

---

## Sales Operations

Responsible for CRM administration, reporting, forecasting, sales enablement, analytics, and operational excellence.

Roles:

- Sales Operations Manager
- Sales Operations Specialist
- Sales Analyst

---

## Customer Success

Responsible for onboarding customers, customer retention, adoption, renewals, expansion opportunities, and customer satisfaction.

Roles:

- Customer Success Manager
- Senior Customer Success Specialist
- Customer Success Specialist
- Junior Customer Success Specialist

---

# Career Progression

Typical Sales career progression:

```text
Junior Business Development Representative

↓

Business Development Representative

↓

Senior Business Development Representative

↓

Account Executive

↓

Senior Account Executive

↓

Enterprise Sales Manager

↓

Head of Sales

↓

Sales Director

↓

Vice President of Sales

↓

Chief Revenue Officer
```

Alternative career paths exist through Sales Operations and Customer Success leadership.
