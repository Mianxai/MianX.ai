# Business Development Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Business Development Manager at MIANX.

The Business Development Manager is responsible for identifying new business opportunities, developing strategic partnerships, expanding market presence, qualifying high-value prospects, and leading the Business Development Representative (BDR) team. The role focuses on creating a strong sales pipeline that supports long-term revenue growth and organizational expansion.

The Business Development Manager reports directly to the Head of Sales.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Business Development Manager role is to accelerate sustainable business growth by building qualified sales pipelines, developing strategic partnerships, and leading business development initiatives that generate long-term commercial value.

---

# Mission

The Business Development Manager exists to:

- Generate qualified sales opportunities
- Expand market reach
- Build strategic partnerships
- Develop high-performing BDR teams
- Improve lead quality
- Increase pipeline growth
- Support revenue objectives
- Drive business expansion
