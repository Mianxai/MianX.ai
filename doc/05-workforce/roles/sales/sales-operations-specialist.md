# Sales Operations Specialist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Sales Operations Specialist at MIANX.

The Sales Operations Specialist is responsible for supporting the Sales organization through CRM administration, sales reporting, process execution, data management, workflow coordination, and operational support. The role ensures that sales teams have accurate systems, reliable data, and standardized operational processes that improve productivity and revenue performance.

The Sales Operations Specialist reports directly to the Sales Operations Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Sales Operations Specialist role is to improve operational efficiency by maintaining sales systems, supporting process execution, ensuring data quality, and enabling the Sales organization with reliable operational support.

---

# Mission

The Sales Operations Specialist exists to:

- Maintain CRM accuracy
- Support sales operations
- Improve reporting quality
- Execute operational processes
- Ensure data integrity
- Support revenue operations
- Increase sales productivity
- Continuously improve operational efficiency

# Decision Authority

The Sales Operations Specialist may independently:

- Update CRM records
- Generate operational reports
