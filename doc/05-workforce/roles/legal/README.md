# Legal Department Roles

> This directory contains the official role definitions for every position within the Legal Department at MIANX.

The Legal Department is responsible for protecting the organization's legal interests, ensuring regulatory compliance, managing contracts, mitigating legal risks, overseeing corporate governance, safeguarding intellectual property, supporting strategic business initiatives, and providing legal guidance across all departments.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Legal Department is to:

- Protect organizational interests
- Ensure legal compliance
- Reduce legal risk
- Support business growth
- Manage corporate governance
- Maintain contractual integrity
- Protect intellectual property
- Enable informed decision-making

---

# Department Responsibilities

The Legal Department oversees:

- Corporate Legal Affairs
- Contract Management
- Regulatory Compliance
- Corporate Governance
- Employment Law
- Commercial Law
- Intellectual Property
- Privacy & Data Protection
- Litigation Management
- Legal Advisory Services

# Reporting Philosophy

The Legal Department operates under centralized governance while partnering closely with all business units. Legal professionals provide proactive guidance, ensure regulatory compliance, reduce organizational risk, and support business objectives without compromising legal or ethical standards.

