# Head of Legal

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Head of Legal at MIANX.

The Head of Legal is responsible for leading the organization's day-to-day legal operations, managing legal professionals, overseeing corporate legal matters, ensuring regulatory compliance, supervising contract governance, supporting executive leadership, and maintaining effective legal risk management across the organization.

The Head of Legal reports directly to the Legal Director.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Head of Legal role is to ensure consistent, high-quality legal support for business operations while strengthening governance, minimizing legal risk, and maintaining full regulatory compliance.

---

The Head of Legal exists to:

- Lead legal operations
- Protect organizational interests
- Ensure regulatory compliance
- Strengthen corporate governance
- Reduce legal risks
- Support business growth
- Develop legal professionals
- Improve legal efficiency

# Decision Authority

The Head of Legal may independently:

- Review and approve standard legal documents
- Assign legal matters
- Manage legal workloads
- Coordinate external legal support
- Recommend legal process improvements
- Resolve routine legal issues
- Approve departmental operational activities
- Escalate strategic legal matters

Major litigation decisions, enterprise legal policies, mergers and acquisitions, Board governance matters, and significant legal settlements require approval from the Legal Director or Vice President of Legal.
