# Junior Legal Counsel

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Junior Legal Counsel at MIANX.

The Junior Legal Counsel supports the Legal Department by conducting legal research, drafting standard legal documents, reviewing routine agreements, assisting with regulatory compliance activities, maintaining legal records, and providing day-to-day legal support under the supervision of senior legal professionals.

The Junior Legal Counsel reports directly to the Senior Legal Counsel.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Junior Legal Counsel role is to provide dependable legal support while developing professional expertise and contributing to efficient, compliant, and legally sound business operations.

---

# Mission

The Junior Legal Counsel exists to:

- Support legal operations
- Conduct legal research
- Draft legal documentation
- Assist contract reviews
- Maintain legal compliance
- Protect organizational interests
- Learn legal best practices
- Grow into a trusted legal advisor

---

# Role Summary

The Junior Legal Counsel supports:

- Commercial Contracts
- Legal Research
- Regulatory Compliance
- Corporate Governance
- Employment Law Support
- Legal Documentation
- Internal Policy Reviews
- Dispute Preparation
- Legal Administration
- Business Advisory Support

# Primary Responsibilities

The Junior Legal Counsel is responsible for:

- Conducting legal research
- Drafting standard contracts
- Reviewing routine legal documents
- Preparing legal memoranda
- Supporting compliance initiatives
- Maintaining legal records
- Monitoring regulatory updates
- Assisting litigation preparation
- Supporting internal legal projects
- Escalating complex legal issues

---

# Legal Advisory Responsibilities

The Junior Legal Counsel assists with:

- Contract drafting
- Legal document preparation
- Legal opinion research
- Policy reviews
- Vendor agreement reviews
- Customer agreement reviews
- Employment law research
- Compliance reviews
- Governance documentation
- Internal legal consultations

All legal work should be reviewed by Senior Legal Counsel before final approval when required.

# Risk Management Responsibilities

The Junior Legal Counsel assists with identifying:

- Contract Risks
- Regulatory Risks
- Compliance Risks
- Employment Law Risks
- Vendor Risks
- Customer Risks
- Documentation Risks
- Privacy Risks
- Intellectual Property Risks
- Operational Legal Risks

Potential legal risks should be reported immediately to the Senior Legal Counsel.

# Collaboration

The Junior Legal Counsel collaborates with:

- Senior Legal Counsel
- Legal Counsel
- Head of Legal
- Legal Operations Manager
- Compliance Manager
- Contracts Manager
- Human Resources
- Finance Department
- Procurement
- External Legal Counsel (as assigned)

Cross-functional collaboration supports legally compliant and efficient business operations.

---

# Decision Authority

The Junior Legal Counsel may independently:

- Conduct legal research
- Draft routine legal documents
- Prepare legal summaries
- Organize legal records
- Review low-risk agreements
- Assist compliance activities
- Recommend document improvements
- Escalate legal concerns

Contract approvals, legal opinions, litigation strategies, enterprise legal policies, and high-risk legal matters require approval from the Senior Legal Counsel or Head of Legal.

- Regulatory Compliance

The Junior Legal Counsel is expected to:

- Produce accurate legal work
- Complete assigned tasks on time
- Support legal operations effectively
- Maintain organized legal records
- Demonstrate continuous learning
- Communicate professionally
- Support compliance initiatives
- Improve legal knowledge continuously
