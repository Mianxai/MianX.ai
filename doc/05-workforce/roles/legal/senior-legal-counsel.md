# Senior Legal Counsel

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Senior Legal Counsel at MIANX.

The Senior Legal Counsel is responsible for providing advanced legal advice, managing complex legal matters, reviewing high-value contracts, supporting regulatory compliance, mitigating legal risks, mentoring junior legal professionals, and ensuring the organization's legal interests are protected.

The Senior Legal Counsel reports directly to the Head of Legal.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Senior Legal Counsel role is to provide expert legal guidance, reduce organizational legal risks, ensure regulatory compliance, and support strategic business decisions through high-quality legal advisory services.

---

# Mission

The Senior Legal Counsel exists to:

- Deliver expert legal advice
- Protect organizational interests
- Reduce legal risks
- Ensure regulatory compliance
- Strengthen contract governance
- Support business growth
- Mentor legal professionals
- Improve legal quality

---

The Senior Legal Counsel manages:

- Corporate Legal Advisory

# Primary Responsibilities

The Senior Legal Counsel is responsible for:

- Advising business leaders on legal matters
- Reviewing and negotiating complex contracts
- Conducting legal research
- Managing legal risk assessments
- Supporting regulatory compliance

- Reviewing corporate policies
- Mentoring junior legal staff

# Decision Authority

The Senior Legal Counsel may independently:

- Issue routine legal opinions
- Review commercial agreements
- Conduct legal research
- Recommend contract revisions
- Advise business units
- Support compliance initiatives
- Mentor junior legal professionals
- Escalate strategic legal matters

Major litigation decisions, enterprise legal policies, mergers and acquisitions, Board governance matters, and exceptionally high-risk legal issues require approval from the Head of Legal or Legal Director.

# Career Progression

Typical career progression:

```text

↓

Legal Counsel

↓

Senior Legal Counsel

↓

Head of Legal

↓

Legal Director

↓

Vice President of Legal
```

Promotion to Senior Legal Counsel requires significant corporate legal experience, advanced expertise in commercial and regulatory law, exceptional legal drafting and negotiation skills, leadership ability, sound judgment, and a consistent record of providing high-quality legal advice.
