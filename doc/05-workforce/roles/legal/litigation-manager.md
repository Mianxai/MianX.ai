# Litigation Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Litigation Manager at MIANX.

The Litigation Manager is responsible for managing all litigation, disputes, claims, investigations, arbitration, mediation, and legal proceedings involving MIANX while minimizing legal exposure, protecting organizational interests, and ensuring effective coordination between internal stakeholders and external legal counsel.

The Litigation Manager reports directly to the Head of Legal.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Litigation Manager role is to protect MIANX from legal disputes by proactively managing litigation strategy, coordinating legal proceedings, reducing legal risks, and ensuring timely resolution of disputes.

---

# Mission

The Litigation Manager exists to:

- Protect organizational interests
- Reduce litigation risks
- Manage legal disputes
- Improve case outcomes
- Ensure regulatory compliance
- Coordinate legal defense
- Strengthen legal governance
- Continuously improve litigation management

# Decision Authority

The Litigation Manager may independently:

- Coordinate litigation activities
- Assign cases to external counsel
- Review litigation documentation
- Conduct litigation risk assessments
- Recommend settlement strategies
- Manage litigation reporting
- Preserve legal evidence
- Escalate significant disputes

Major settlements, litigation strategy, regulatory enforcement actions, class-action matters, and high-value legal disputes require approval from the Head of Legal or Legal Director.
