# IP Specialist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the IP Specialist at MIANX.

The IP Specialist is responsible for supporting the management, protection, documentation, monitoring, and administration of MIANX's intellectual property assets, including trademarks, copyrights, patents, trade secrets, licensing records, and brand protection activities.

The IP Specialist reports directly to the Intellectual Property Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the IP Specialist role is to ensure MIANX's intellectual property assets are properly documented, maintained, protected, and managed throughout their lifecycle while supporting innovation and reducing intellectual property risks.

---

# Mission

The IP Specialist exists to:

- Protect intellectual property
- Support innovation
- Maintain IP records
- Reduce IP risks
- Strengthen brand protection
- Support legal compliance
- Improve portfolio management
- Promote IP awareness

---

# Role Summary

The IP Specialist supports:

- Trademark Administration
- Copyright Administration
- Patent Coordination
- IP Documentation
- Brand Protection
- Licensing Administration
- IP Portfolio Management
- IP Due Diligence
- Infringement Monitoring
- IP Compliance

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The IP Specialist is responsible for:

- Maintaining IP records
- Preparing trademark documentation
- Supporting patent filings
- Coordinating copyright registrations
- Monitoring trademark renewals
- Tracking licensing agreements
- Monitoring infringement reports
- Supporting IP audits
- Maintaining IP databases
- Escalating IP concerns

---

# Intellectual Property Responsibilities

The IP Specialist performs:

- Trademark Administration
- Copyright Documentation
- Patent Filing Support
- Trade Secret Documentation
- Brand Monitoring
- Licensing Record Management
- IP Portfolio Updates
- Domain Name Monitoring
- IP Research
- Documentation Quality Assurance

All intellectual property records should remain complete, accurate, and up to date.

---

# Governance Responsibilities

The IP Specialist supports:

- Intellectual Property Governance
- Documentation Standards
- Regulatory Compliance
- Records Management
- Brand Governance
- Innovation Governance
- Audit Preparation
- Compliance Reporting

Governance activities ensure MIANX's intellectual property assets remain organized, traceable, and legally protected.

---

# Risk Management Responsibilities

The IP Specialist assists with identifying:

- Trademark Risks
- Copyright Risks
- Patent Risks
- Licensing Risks
- Documentation Risks
- Brand Misuse
- Infringement Risks
- Vendor IP Risks
- Technology Risks
- Compliance Risks

Potential legal disputes or significant infringements must be escalated immediately to the Intellectual Property Manager.

The IP Specialist manages:

- IP Portfolio Database
- Trademark Register
- Copyright Register

- Renewal Calendar
- Licensing Records

- Documentation Repository
- IP Metrics
- Continuous Improvement Activities

Operational excellence ensures MIANX's intellectual property assets remain secure, organized, and properly maintained.

# Collaboration

The IP Specialist collaborates with:

- Intellectual Property Manager

- Compliance Manager
- Product Management
- Engineering Teams
- Marketing Department
- Brand Management
- Information Security
- Procurement
- External IP Consultants

Cross-functional collaboration supports effective protection and management of MIANX's intellectual property.

---

# Decision Authority

The IP Specialist may independently:

- Maintain IP documentation
- Prepare registration documents
- Update IP databases
- Track renewals
- Conduct IP research
- Monitor trademarks
- Support licensing administration
- Escalate significant IP concerns

Patent strategies, litigation matters, major licensing agreements, portfolio acquisitions, and enterprise IP policies require approval from the Intellectual Property Manager or Head of Legal.
