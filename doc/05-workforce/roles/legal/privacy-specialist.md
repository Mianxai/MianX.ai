# Privacy Specialist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Privacy Specialist at MIANX.

The Privacy Specialist is responsible for supporting MIANX's privacy and data protection program by monitoring privacy compliance, maintaining privacy documentation, assisting with data subject requests, performing privacy assessments, supporting incident response activities, and ensuring organizational compliance with applicable privacy laws and internal policies.

The Privacy Specialist reports directly to the Data Protection Officer.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Privacy Specialist role is to support the implementation, operation, and continuous improvement of MIANX's privacy management program while ensuring responsible handling of personal data and regulatory compliance.

---

# Mission

The Privacy Specialist exists to:

- Protect personal information
- Support privacy compliance
- Reduce privacy risks
- Maintain privacy documentation
- Improve privacy awareness
- Support regulatory readiness
- Strengthen governance
- Promote responsible data handling

---

The Privacy Specialist is responsible for:

- Monitoring privacy compliance
- Maintaining privacy documentation

- Reviewing data processing activities
- Coordinating privacy awareness training
- Tracking privacy incidents
- Supporting vendor privacy assessments
- Escalating privacy concerns

# Privacy Responsibilities

The Privacy Specialist performs:

- Privacy Compliance Reviews
- Data Processing Reviews
- Consent Verification
- Data Retention Monitoring
- Privacy Documentation Updates
- Privacy Assessment Support
- Data Subject Rights Administration
- Vendor Privacy Reviews
- Privacy Reporting
- Regulatory Monitoring

Privacy activities should ensure lawful, transparent, and secure processing of personal information.

# Governance Responsibilities

The Privacy Specialist supports:

- Privacy Governance
- Data Governance
- Records Management
- Privacy Policies
- Documentation Standards
- Audit Preparation

- Compliance Monitoring

Governance activities help maintain organizational accountability and regulatory compliance.

# Risk Management Responsibilities

The Privacy Specialist assists with identifying:

- Privacy Risks
- Data Protection Risks
- Regulatory Risks
- Third-Party Risks
- Data Retention Risks
- Consent Risks
- Documentation Risks
- Operational Risks
- Information Handling Risks
- Compliance Gaps

Material privacy risks must be escalated immediately to the Data Protection Officer.

- Consent Records

- Privacy Training Records

Operational consistency ensures effective execution of MIANX's privacy program.

# Collaboration

- Data Protection Officer

- Human Resources

- Internal Audit

Cross-functional collaboration ensures privacy requirements are integrated into business operations.

---

# Decision Authority

The Privacy Specialist may independently:

- Perform routine privacy reviews
- Maintain privacy documentation
- Coordinate data subject requests
- Monitor privacy metrics
- Conduct privacy compliance checks
- Recommend privacy improvements
- Support privacy awareness initiatives
- Escalate significant privacy concerns

Enterprise privacy policies, regulatory notifications, major privacy incidents, and strategic privacy initiatives require approval from the Data Protection Officer or Compliance Manager.
