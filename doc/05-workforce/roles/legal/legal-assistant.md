# Legal Assistant

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Legal Assistant at MIANX.

The Legal Assistant is responsible for providing administrative, operational, and documentation support to the Legal Department by maintaining legal records, preparing legal documents, coordinating schedules, organizing case files, supporting contract administration, and ensuring efficient daily legal operations.

The Legal Assistant reports directly to the Legal Operations Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Legal Assistant role is to ensure the Legal Department operates efficiently by providing reliable administrative support, maintaining organized legal documentation, and assisting legal professionals with routine operational activities.

---

# Mission

The Legal Assistant exists to:

- Support legal operations
- Maintain legal documentation
- Improve administrative efficiency
- Protect confidential information
- Coordinate legal activities
- Support legal professionals
- Strengthen operational consistency
- Enable continuous improvement

---

The Legal Assistant supports:

# Decision Authority

The Legal Assistant may independently:

- Maintain legal records
- Organize documentation
- Coordinate meetings
- Prepare administrative reports
- Manage departmental calendars
- Maintain filing systems
- Support document workflows
- Escalate operational concerns

Legal advice, legal interpretations, contract approvals, litigation decisions, and regulatory communications require approval from Legal Counsel, the Legal Operations Manager, or the Head of Legal.
