# Vice President of Legal

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Vice President of Legal at MIANX.

The Vice President of Legal is responsible for executing the organization's legal strategy, overseeing legal operations, managing legal teams, ensuring regulatory compliance, supervising legal risk management, and supporting executive leadership in achieving organizational objectives.

The Vice President of Legal reports directly to the Chief Legal Officer.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Vice President of Legal role is to translate the organization's legal strategy into effective operational execution while ensuring legal excellence, regulatory compliance, strong governance, and efficient legal service delivery.

---

The Vice President of Legal exists to:

- Execute legal strategy
- Strengthen legal governance
- Minimize legal risks
- Improve legal operations
- Support executive leadership
- Ensure regulatory compliance
- Build high-performing legal teams
- Enable sustainable business growth

# Decision Authority

The Vice President of Legal may independently:

- Approve departmental procedures
- Allocate legal resources
- Assign legal matters
- Recommend legal policy updates
- Manage legal operations
- Approve operational expenditures within delegated authority
- Recommend governance improvements
- Escalate strategic legal matters

Major legal settlements, corporate restructuring, mergers and acquisitions, enterprise legal policies, and Board-level governance matters require approval from the Chief Legal Officer or the Chief Executive Officer.
