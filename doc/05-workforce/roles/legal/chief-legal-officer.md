# Chief Legal Officer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Chief Legal Officer (CLO) at MIANX.

The Chief Legal Officer is the senior executive responsible for the organization's legal strategy, corporate governance, regulatory compliance, enterprise legal risk management, litigation oversight, contract governance, intellectual property protection, privacy compliance, and legal support for business operations.

The Chief Legal Officer reports directly to the Chief Executive Officer and serves as the principal legal advisor to executive leadership and the Board of Directors.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Chief Legal Officer role is to protect the legal interests of MIANX while enabling sustainable business growth through proactive legal guidance, effective governance, regulatory compliance, and enterprise risk management.

---

The Chief Legal Officer exists to:

- Protect organizational interests
- Ensure legal compliance
- Minimize enterprise legal risk
- Strengthen corporate governance
- Support executive decision-making
- Protect intellectual property
- Manage litigation effectively
- Build a world-class legal organization

# Decision Authority

The Chief Legal Officer may independently:

- Approve legal procedures
- Issue legal opinions
- Approve legal department initiatives
- Select external legal advisors
- Resolve legal operational issues
- Recommend governance improvements
- Approve departmental budgets
- Escalate strategic legal risks to executive leadership

Major litigation settlements, mergers and acquisitions, corporate restructuring, Board governance changes, and organization-wide legal policies require approval from the Chief Executive Officer and/or the Board of Directors.

```text

↓
```
