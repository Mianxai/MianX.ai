# Contract Specialist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Contract Specialist at MIANX.

The Contract Specialist is responsible for drafting, reviewing, coordinating, maintaining, and administering commercial contracts while ensuring compliance with organizational policies, legal requirements, and contract governance standards.

The Contract Specialist reports directly to the Senior Contract Specialist.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Contract Specialist role is to ensure contracts are prepared accurately, processed efficiently, maintained properly, and aligned with MIANX's legal, operational, and commercial objectives.

---

# Mission

The Contract Specialist exists to:

- Support contract lifecycle management
- Produce accurate contracts
- Ensure contract compliance
- Reduce contractual risks
- Maintain contract records
- Support business operations
- Improve contract efficiency
- Strengthen governance

---

# Role Summary

The Contract Specialist supports:

- Commercial Contracts
- Vendor Agreements
- Customer Agreements
- Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)
- Statements of Work (SOWs)
- Contract Administration
- Contract Repository
- Contract Compliance
- Contract Renewals
- Contract Documentation

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Contract Specialist is responsible for:

- Drafting standard agreements
- Reviewing contract requests
- Updating contract templates
- Coordinating contract approvals
- Maintaining contract repositories
- Tracking contract renewals
- Monitoring contractual obligations
- Supporting contract negotiations
- Performing document quality checks
- Escalating contractual risks

---

# Contract Management Responsibilities

The Contract Specialist performs:

- Contract Drafting
- Contract Formatting
- Contract Review Support
- Contract Amendments
- Contract Filing
- Contract Registration
- Contract Execution Coordination
- Renewal Tracking
- Version Control
- Contract Archiving

All contracts should comply with MIANX's contract management standards and approval workflows.

---

# Governance Responsibilities

The Contract Specialist supports:

- Contract Governance
- Standard Contract Templates
- Approval Matrix Compliance
- Documentation Standards
- Contract Repository Accuracy
- Delegation of Authority
- Audit Preparation
- Records Management

Governance activities ensure contracts remain accurate, traceable, and compliant.

---

# Risk Management Responsibilities

The Contract Specialist assists with identifying:

- Contract Risks
- Commercial Risks
- Financial Risks
- Vendor Risks
- Customer Risks
- Compliance Risks
- Operational Risks
- Documentation Risks
- Renewal Risks
- Confidentiality Risks

Potential high-risk agreements must be escalated to the Senior Contract Specialist.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The Contract Specialist manages:

- Contract Repository
- Contract Register
- Contract Templates
- Approval Tracking
- Contract Status Reports
- Renewal Calendar
- Clause Library Updates
- Documentation Quality
- Contract Metrics
- Continuous Improvement

Operational excellence improves efficiency and consistency throughout the contract lifecycle.

---

# Collaboration

The Contract Specialist collaborates with:

- Senior Contract Specialist
- Contracts Manager
- Legal Counsel
- Procurement Department
- Sales Department
- Finance Department
- Operations Department
- Compliance Team
- Information Security
- External Vendors

Cross-functional collaboration ensures contracts support business operations effectively.

---

# Decision Authority

The Contract Specialist may independently:

- Draft routine contracts
- Update approved templates
- Maintain contract records
- Coordinate contract approvals
- Track contract renewals
- Prepare contract summaries
- Maintain documentation quality
- Escalate contractual concerns

Non-standard clauses, strategic commercial agreements, enterprise contracts, mergers and acquisitions, and high-risk contractual matters require approval from the Senior Contract Specialist or Contracts Manager.

# Core Competencies

The Contract Specialist should demonstrate proficiency in:

- Contract Administration
- Commercial Contracts
- Contract Drafting
- Documentation Management
- Contract Lifecycle Management
- Regulatory Compliance
- Attention to Detail
- Organization
- Communication
- Time Management

# Performance Expectations

The Contract Specialist is expected to:

- Produce accurate contracts
- Meet contract processing deadlines
- Maintain repository accuracy
- Support governance compliance
- Improve contract administration
- Deliver excellent stakeholder support
- Maintain documentation quality
- Continuously improve contract knowledge
