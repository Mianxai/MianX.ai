# Contracts Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Contracts Manager at MIANX.

The Contracts Manager is responsible for overseeing the complete contract lifecycle, ensuring contractual compliance, negotiating commercial agreements, maintaining contract governance, reducing contractual risk, supporting business operations, and ensuring all contractual obligations align with organizational objectives and legal requirements.

The Contracts Manager reports directly to the Head of Legal.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Contracts Manager role is to establish a standardized, efficient, and legally compliant contract management function that protects MIANX while enabling business growth.

---

# Mission

The Contracts Manager exists to:

- Protect contractual interests
- Reduce contract risks
- Improve contract governance
- Standardize contract processes
- Support business operations
- Ensure regulatory compliance
- Improve contract visibility
- Enable efficient contract execution

---

The Contracts Manager oversees:

# Decision Authority

The Contracts Manager may independently:

- Review standard agreements
- Approve routine contractual language
- Manage contract workflows
- Maintain contract templates
- Coordinate contract negotiations
- Recommend contractual improvements
- Monitor contractual obligations
- Escalate high-risk agreements

Enterprise contracts, strategic partnerships, mergers and acquisitions, high-value commercial agreements, and non-standard contractual obligations require approval from the Head of Legal or Legal Director.
