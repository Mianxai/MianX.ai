# Legal Counsel

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Legal Counsel at MIANX.

The Legal Counsel is responsible for providing day-to-day legal advice, drafting and reviewing contracts, supporting regulatory compliance, conducting legal research, assisting with dispute resolution, protecting organizational interests, and ensuring business operations remain compliant with applicable laws and internal policies.

The Legal Counsel reports directly to the Senior Legal Counsel.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Legal Counsel role is to provide practical, accurate, and timely legal support that enables business operations while protecting MIANX from legal, contractual, and regulatory risks.

---

# Mission

The Legal Counsel exists to:

- Provide reliable legal advice
- Protect business interests
- Support regulatory compliance
- Review legal documents
- Reduce legal risks
- Assist business teams
- Strengthen governance
- Improve legal efficiency

---

# Decision Authority

The Legal Counsel may independently:

- Draft standard legal agreements
- Review routine contracts
- Provide legal guidance within delegated authority
- Conduct legal research
- Prepare legal memoranda
- Recommend contract revisions
- Support regulatory activities
- Escalate strategic legal matters

High-risk litigation, enterprise legal policies, major acquisitions, corporate restructuring, and Board-level legal matters require approval from the Senior Legal Counsel or Head of Legal.
