# Corporate Governance Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Corporate Governance Manager at MIANX.

The Corporate Governance Manager is responsible for establishing, maintaining, and continuously improving MIANX's corporate governance framework, Board administration processes, governance policies, corporate records, regulatory filings, and organizational accountability standards.

The Corporate Governance Manager reports directly to the Head of Legal.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Corporate Governance Manager role is to ensure MIANX maintains effective corporate governance practices, regulatory compliance, transparent decision-making, and strong organizational accountability.

---

# Mission

The Corporate Governance Manager exists to:

- Strengthen corporate governance
- Maintain regulatory compliance
- Support executive leadership
- Improve governance processes
- Protect organizational integrity
- Maintain corporate records
- Enable transparent decision-making
- Reduce governance risks

---

# Role Summary

The Corporate Governance Manager oversees:

- Corporate Governance Framework
- Board Administration
- Governance Policies
- Corporate Records
- Regulatory Filings
- Committee Administration
- Governance Audits
- Organizational Policies
- Delegation of Authority
- Governance Reporting

# Primary Responsibilities

The Corporate Governance Manager is responsible for:

- Managing corporate governance programs
- Coordinating Board meetings
- Preparing governance documentation
- Maintaining corporate records
- Monitoring governance compliance
- Supporting executive committees
- Managing governance calendars
- Reviewing governance policies
- Coordinating regulatory filings
- Improving governance practices

- Governance Reporting

The Corporate Governance Manager collaborates with:

- Human Resources
- Finance Department
- External Regulators

---

# Decision Authority

The Corporate Governance Manager may independently:

- Maintain governance documentation
- Schedule governance activities
- Coordinate Board meetings
- Update governance records
- Recommend governance improvements
- Monitor governance compliance
- Support governance audits
- Escalate governance risks

Changes to governance frameworks, Board policies, Articles of Organization, corporate structure, and governance strategy require approval from the Head of Legal or Legal Director.
