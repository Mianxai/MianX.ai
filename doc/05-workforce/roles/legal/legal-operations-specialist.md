# Legal Operations Specialist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Legal Operations Specialist at MIANX.

The Legal Operations Specialist is responsible for supporting the Legal Department by administering legal systems, managing legal documentation, coordinating operational workflows, maintaining compliance records, improving legal processes, supporting technology platforms, and ensuring efficient day-to-day legal operations.

The Legal Operations Specialist reports directly to the Legal Operations Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Legal Operations Specialist role is to improve the efficiency, organization, and scalability of MIANX's Legal Department by supporting operational excellence, legal technology, documentation management, reporting, and continuous process improvement.

---

# Mission

The Legal Operations Specialist exists to:

- Improve legal operations
- Maintain legal systems
- Support operational efficiency
- Strengthen governance
- Enhance legal reporting
- Improve documentation quality
- Reduce operational risks
- Enable continuous improvement

# Decision Authority

The Legal Operations Specialist may independently:

- Maintain legal systems
- Manage legal documentation
- Update operational records
- Prepare operational reports
- Monitor workflow performance
- Coordinate vendor activities
- Recommend process improvements
- Escalate operational concerns

Department strategy, legal technology procurement, major workflow redesigns, enterprise legal policies, and budget approvals require authorization from the Legal Operations Manager or Head of Legal.
