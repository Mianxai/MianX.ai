# Litigation Specialist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Litigation Specialist at MIANX.

The Litigation Specialist is responsible for supporting litigation management by coordinating legal cases, maintaining litigation documentation, preparing case materials, assisting with investigations, managing legal holds, coordinating with external counsel, monitoring court deadlines, and ensuring litigation activities comply with organizational policies and legal requirements.

The Litigation Specialist reports directly to the Litigation Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Litigation Specialist role is to provide operational and administrative support for litigation and dispute resolution activities while ensuring legal matters are accurately documented, efficiently coordinated, and managed throughout their lifecycle.

---

# Mission

The Litigation Specialist exists to:

- Support litigation activities
- Protect organizational interests
- Maintain legal documentation
- Improve case coordination
- Reduce litigation risks
- Support legal investigations
- Strengthen governance
- Improve operational efficiency

---

- Maintaining litigation files

- Managing legal hold records

- Assisting legal investigations

- Preparing litigation reports
- Escalating significant legal issues

# Litigation Responsibilities

The Litigation Specialist performs:

- Case Administration
- Court Filing Support
- Discovery Coordination
- Evidence Management
- Legal Hold Administration
- Litigation Documentation
- Hearing Preparation
- Arbitration Support
- Mediation Support
- Case Status Tracking

Every litigation activity should be documented accurately and completed within established legal timelines.

# Governance Responsibilities

The Litigation Specialist supports:

- Litigation Governance
- Documentation Standards
- Records Management
- Regulatory Compliance
- Investigation Procedures
- Legal Reporting
- Audit Preparation
- Policy Compliance

Governance responsibilities ensure litigation records remain complete, secure, and legally defensible.

# Risk Management Responsibilities

The Litigation Specialist assists with identifying:

- Litigation Risks
- Documentation Risks
- Court Deadline Risks
- Investigation Risks
- Evidence Management Risks
- Regulatory Risks
- Contractual Disputes
- Employment Disputes
- Operational Risks
- Compliance Gaps

Material legal risks must be escalated immediately to the Litigation Manager.

The Litigation Specialist collaborates with:

Cross-functional collaboration ensures litigation activities remain coordinated and efficient.

---

# Decision Authority

The Litigation Specialist may independently:

- Maintain litigation documentation
- Coordinate case schedules
- Update litigation records
- Monitor legal deadlines
- Prepare litigation reports
- Organize evidence documentation
- Support external counsel coordination
- Escalate significant litigation concerns

Settlement negotiations, litigation strategy, court representations, regulatory enforcement matters, and high-risk legal disputes require approval from the Litigation Manager or Head of Legal.
