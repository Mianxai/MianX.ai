# Legal Director

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Legal Director at MIANX.

The Legal Director is responsible for directing the organization's legal operations, managing legal teams, overseeing complex legal matters, ensuring regulatory compliance, supporting corporate governance, supervising legal projects, and providing strategic legal guidance to business leadership.

The Legal Director reports directly to the Vice President of Legal.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Legal Director role is to lead the execution of legal programs, ensure high-quality legal services, reduce organizational legal risk, and strengthen governance while enabling sustainable business operations.

---

# Mission

The Legal Director exists to:

- Lead legal operations
- Strengthen regulatory compliance
- Reduce legal risks
- Improve legal service delivery
- Support strategic initiatives
- Develop legal professionals
- Enhance governance
- Protect organizational interests

# Decision Authority

The Legal Director may independently:

- Approve routine legal procedures
- Assign legal matters
- Review and approve standard commercial agreements
- Manage legal department resources
- Coordinate external counsel
- Recommend legal policy improvements
- Approve departmental operational initiatives
- Escalate strategic legal issues

Major litigation matters, mergers and acquisitions, enterprise-wide legal policies, governance framework changes, and Board-level legal matters require approval from the Vice President of Legal or Chief Legal Officer.
