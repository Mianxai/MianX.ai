# Corporate Secretary

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Corporate Secretary at MIANX.

The Corporate Secretary is responsible for supporting the Board of Directors, maintaining statutory corporate records, coordinating board and committee meetings, managing corporate filings, administering governance documentation, and ensuring compliance with corporate governance requirements and applicable laws.

The Corporate Secretary reports directly to the Corporate Governance Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Corporate Secretary role is to ensure that MIANX maintains accurate corporate records, effective Board administration, regulatory compliance, and efficient governance processes that support executive leadership and organizational accountability.

---

# Mission

The Corporate Secretary exists to:

- Support Board governance
- Maintain statutory records
- Ensure governance compliance
- Coordinate Board meetings
- Maintain corporate documentation
- Support executive leadership
- Improve governance administration
- Protect corporate integrity

The Corporate Secretary is responsible for:

- Organizing Board meetings
- Preparing meeting agendas

- Supporting governance audits

# Decision Authority

The Corporate Secretary may independently:

- Schedule Board meetings
- Maintain statutory registers
- Prepare governance documentation
- Record Board minutes
- Manage governance records
- Coordinate routine regulatory filings
- Maintain governance calendars
- Escalate governance concerns

Changes to governance frameworks, constitutional documents, Board policies, or corporate legal structures require approval from the Corporate Governance Manager or Head of Legal.
