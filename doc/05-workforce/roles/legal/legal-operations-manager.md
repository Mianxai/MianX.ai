# Legal Operations Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Legal Operations Manager at MIANX.

The Legal Operations Manager is responsible for managing the day-to-day operations of the Legal Department, optimizing legal processes, coordinating legal projects, managing legal technologies, supporting contract administration, maintaining legal documentation, monitoring departmental performance, and improving operational efficiency.

The Legal Operations Manager reports directly to the Head of Legal.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Legal Operations Manager role is to ensure the Legal Department operates efficiently, consistently, and in alignment with organizational governance, compliance requirements, and business objectives.

---

# Mission

The Legal Operations Manager exists to:

- Improve legal operations
- Standardize legal processes
- Increase departmental efficiency
- Support legal professionals
- Strengthen governance
- Improve legal service delivery
- Maintain operational excellence
- Enable continuous improvement

# Decision Authority

The Legal Operations Manager may independently:

- Assign operational work
- Maintain legal documentation standards
- Manage legal workflows
- Coordinate legal vendors
- Recommend process improvements
- Approve routine operational procedures
- Monitor departmental performance
- Escalate strategic legal issues

Legal policy changes, major budget approvals, enterprise technology decisions, litigation strategy, and governance framework modifications require approval from the Head of Legal or Legal Director.
