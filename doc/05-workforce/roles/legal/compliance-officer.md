# Compliance Officer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Compliance Officer at MIANX.

The Compliance Officer is responsible for supporting the organization's compliance program by monitoring regulatory requirements, performing compliance reviews, maintaining compliance documentation, assisting with investigations, coordinating compliance training, and ensuring adherence to applicable laws, regulations, and internal policies.

The Compliance Officer reports directly to the Compliance Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Compliance Officer role is to help maintain an effective compliance program by supporting regulatory adherence, reducing compliance risks, and promoting a culture of integrity throughout MIANX.

---

# Mission

The Compliance Officer exists to:

- Support regulatory compliance
- Monitor policy adherence
- Reduce compliance risks
- Promote ethical behavior
- Assist compliance investigations
- Improve compliance awareness
- Maintain accurate records
- Strengthen governance

---

The Compliance Officer supports:

- Compliance Investigations
- Compliance Training

# Primary Responsibilities

The Compliance Officer is responsible for:

- Monitoring compliance activities
- Conducting compliance reviews
- Maintaining compliance records
- Assisting internal investigations
- Supporting regulatory reporting
- Coordinating compliance training
- Tracking corrective actions
- Monitoring policy implementation
- Identifying compliance risks
- Escalating compliance issues

# Compliance Responsibilities

The Compliance Officer performs:

- Regulatory Monitoring
- Policy Compliance Reviews
- Compliance Assessments
- Documentation Verification
- Internal Compliance Checks
- Corrective Action Follow-up
- Regulatory Research
- Compliance Reporting
- Ethics Monitoring
- Compliance Awareness Activities

Compliance activities should proactively identify issues before they become regulatory or operational risks.

---

# Governance Responsibilities

The Compliance Officer supports:

- Corporate Governance
- Regulatory Governance
- Internal Controls
- Policy Management
- Compliance Documentation
- Audit Preparation
- Records Management
- Governance Reporting

Governance activities help ensure accountability, transparency, and consistent compliance across MIANX.

---

# Risk Management Responsibilities

The Compliance Officer assists with identifying:

- Regulatory Risks
- Compliance Risks
- Operational Risks
- Policy Violations
- Documentation Risks
- Third-Party Compliance Risks
- Privacy Risks
- Internal Control Weaknesses
- Audit Findings
- Ethical Risks

Material compliance concerns must be reported immediately to the Compliance Manager.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The Compliance Officer manages:

- Compliance Registers
- Regulatory Tracking
- Compliance Checklists
- Corrective Action Logs
- Investigation Documentation
- Training Records
- Compliance Dashboards
- Compliance Metrics
- Policy Distribution
- Continuous Improvement Activities

Operational consistency ensures the compliance program remains effective and well documented.

---

# Collaboration

The Compliance Officer collaborates with:

- Compliance Manager
- Head of Legal
- Corporate Governance Manager
- Legal Counsel
- Human Resources
- Finance Department
- Information Security
- Procurement
- Internal Audit
- Department Managers

Cross-functional collaboration strengthens compliance across all business functions.

---

# Decision Authority

The Compliance Officer may independently:

- Perform routine compliance reviews
- Maintain compliance documentation
- Prepare compliance reports
- Monitor policy implementation
- Coordinate compliance training
- Recommend corrective actions
- Track compliance metrics
- Escalate significant compliance concerns

Compliance policy changes, regulatory disclosures, enforcement responses, and enterprise compliance initiatives require approval from the Compliance Manager or Head of Legal.

---

# Core Competencies

The Compliance Officer should demonstrate proficiency in:

- Regulatory Compliance
- Policy Interpretation
- Internal Controls
- Risk Assessment
- Compliance Auditing
- Documentation Management
- Investigation Support
- Communication
- Attention to Detail
- Analytical Thinking

# Performance Expectations

The Compliance Officer is expected to:

- Maintain accurate compliance records
- Complete compliance reviews on schedule
- Support regulatory compliance
- Promote policy awareness
- Assist compliance investigations
- Deliver quality documentation
- Identify compliance issues proactively
- Continuously improve compliance knowledge
