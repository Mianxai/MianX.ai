# Data Protection Officer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Data Protection Officer (DPO) at MIANX.

The Data Protection Officer is responsible for overseeing MIANX's data protection and privacy program, ensuring compliance with applicable data protection regulations, advising leadership on privacy matters, monitoring personal data processing activities, and protecting the rights of customers, employees, partners, and other data subjects.

The Data Protection Officer reports directly to the Compliance Manager with independent authority to escalate privacy and regulatory concerns directly to the Head of Legal when necessary.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Data Protection Officer role is to establish and maintain a comprehensive privacy governance program that safeguards personal information, ensures regulatory compliance, and builds trust across MIANX's operations.

---

# Mission

The Data Protection Officer exists to:

- Protect personal data
- Ensure privacy compliance
- Reduce privacy risks
- Promote responsible data practices
- Strengthen governance
- Support business operations
- Build customer trust
- Continuously improve privacy maturity

---

- Data Processing Activities

# Primary Responsibilities

The Data Protection Officer is responsible for:

- Developing privacy policies
- Monitoring data processing activities
- Advising leadership on privacy regulations
- Conducting Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs)
- Managing data subject requests
- Coordinating privacy incident response
- Supporting regulatory investigations
- Delivering privacy awareness training
- Monitoring third-party privacy compliance
- Reporting privacy risks and compliance status

The Data Protection Officer manages:

- Privacy Compliance

The Data Protection Officer oversees:

- Privacy Risks

# Decision Authority

The Data Protection Officer may independently:

- Conduct privacy assessments
- Review data processing activities
- Recommend privacy controls
- Monitor regulatory compliance
- Review vendor privacy practices
- Coordinate data subject requests
- Recommend corrective actions
- Escalate significant privacy risks

Enterprise privacy policies, regulatory notifications, strategic privacy initiatives, and major data processing decisions require approval from the Head of Legal or executive leadership where applicable.
