# Compliance Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Compliance Manager at MIANX.

The Compliance Manager is responsible for developing, implementing, monitoring, and continuously improving MIANX's compliance management program, ensuring adherence to applicable laws, regulations, internal policies, ethical standards, and industry best practices while minimizing regulatory and operational risks.

The Compliance Manager reports directly to the Head of Legal.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Compliance Manager role is to establish and maintain an effective compliance program that protects MIANX from regulatory, legal, financial, and reputational risks while promoting a strong culture of ethics and accountability.

---

# Mission

The Compliance Manager exists to:

- Strengthen organizational compliance
- Reduce regulatory risk
- Promote ethical conduct
- Improve governance
- Ensure policy compliance
- Support business operations
- Build compliance awareness
- Continuously improve compliance maturity

---

# Decision Authority

The Compliance Manager may independently:

- Conduct compliance reviews
- Monitor regulatory obligations
- Recommend policy improvements
- Coordinate compliance investigations
- Approve routine compliance documentation
- Deliver compliance training
- Monitor corrective actions
- Escalate material compliance issues

Enterprise compliance policies, major regulatory disclosures, legal enforcement matters, and strategic compliance initiatives require approval from the Head of Legal or Legal Director.
