# Intellectual Property Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Intellectual Property (IP) Manager at MIANX.

The Intellectual Property Manager is responsible for developing, protecting, managing, and enforcing MIANX's intellectual property portfolio, including trademarks, copyrights, patents, trade secrets, proprietary technologies, and brand assets while minimizing legal risks and maximizing the organization's intellectual property value.

The Intellectual Property Manager reports directly to the Head of Legal.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Intellectual Property Manager role is to establish and maintain a comprehensive intellectual property management program that protects MIANX's innovations, brands, products, technologies, and proprietary information.

---

# Mission

The Intellectual Property Manager exists to:

- Protect organizational intellectual property
- Strengthen competitive advantage
- Reduce IP risks
- Ensure legal compliance
- Support innovation
- Safeguard proprietary information
- Maximize IP value
- Improve IP governance

---

The Intellectual Property Manager oversees:

# Decision Authority

The Intellectual Property Manager may independently:

- Manage IP registrations
- Coordinate trademark filings
- Recommend copyright registrations
- Conduct IP risk assessments
- Review licensing documentation
- Monitor infringement activities
- Maintain IP portfolio records
- Escalate strategic IP matters

Patent filings, litigation strategy, major licensing agreements, acquisitions involving IP assets, and enterprise-wide IP policies require approval from the Head of Legal or Legal Director.
