# Paralegal

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Paralegal at MIANX.

The Paralegal is responsible for providing legal research, documentation, case preparation, contract support, regulatory research, and administrative assistance to the Legal Department while ensuring accuracy, confidentiality, and compliance with organizational policies and applicable laws.

The Paralegal reports directly to the Senior Legal Counsel.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Paralegal role is to strengthen MIANX's legal operations by supporting attorneys with legal research, documentation, case preparation, contract review, and regulatory compliance activities.

---

# Mission

The Paralegal exists to:

- Support legal professionals
- Improve legal efficiency
- Maintain legal documentation
- Strengthen compliance
- Improve research quality
- Protect confidential information
- Reduce legal risks
- Enable continuous improvement

---

The Paralegal supports:

- Legal Research

- Contract Support
- Regulatory Research
- Legal Documentation
- Corporate Records

- Compliance Documentation
- Evidence Management

The Paralegal is responsible for:

- Conducting legal research
- Preparing legal documents
- Drafting standard legal correspondence
- Organizing case files
- Reviewing supporting documentation
- Assisting contract reviews
- Supporting litigation activities
- Maintaining legal records

- Escalating legal concerns

# Legal Responsibilities

The Paralegal performs:

- Legal Research
- Statutory Research
- Regulatory Research
- Case Law Research
- Document Drafting
- Contract Support
- Evidence Organization
- Legal Filing Support
- Due Diligence Support
- Legal Documentation Review

All work should be completed accurately while maintaining attorney-client privilege and confidentiality.

# Risk Management Responsibilities

The Paralegal assists with identifying:

- Documentation Risks
- Compliance Risks
- Contract Risks
- Litigation Risks
- Regulatory Risks
- Confidentiality Risks
- Filing Risks
- Administrative Risks

---

# Decision Authority

The Paralegal may independently:

- Conduct legal research
- Prepare standard legal documentation
- Organize legal files
- Maintain legal records
- Support case preparation
- Coordinate documentation
- Track legal deadlines
- Escalate legal issues

Providing legal opinions, approving contracts, representing MIANX in legal matters, negotiating settlements, or issuing regulatory communications requires approval from Legal Counsel or the Head of Legal.
