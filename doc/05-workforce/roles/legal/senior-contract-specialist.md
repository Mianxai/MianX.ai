# Senior Contract Specialist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Senior Contract Specialist at MIANX.

The Senior Contract Specialist is responsible for drafting, reviewing, negotiating, and administering complex commercial agreements while ensuring contractual compliance, minimizing legal and commercial risks, maintaining contract governance standards, and mentoring junior contract professionals.

The Senior Contract Specialist reports directly to the Contracts Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Senior Contract Specialist role is to ensure complex contracts are negotiated, documented, and managed efficiently while protecting MIANX's legal and commercial interests.

---

# Mission

The Senior Contract Specialist exists to:

- Deliver high-quality contracts
- Reduce contractual risk
- Support commercial growth
- Ensure contractual compliance
- Standardize contract practices
- Improve contract quality
- Mentor contract professionals
- Strengthen contract governance

---

# Decision Authority

The Senior Contract Specialist may independently:

- Draft standard and complex agreements
- Negotiate approved contractual clauses
- Review contract amendments
- Recommend contractual improvements
- Coordinate contract execution
- Maintain contract documentation
- Support procurement and sales teams
- Escalate high-risk contractual matters

Non-standard legal provisions, enterprise agreements, mergers and acquisitions, strategic partnerships, and exceptionally high-risk contracts require approval from the Contracts Manager or Head of Legal.
