# Payroll and Benefits Specialist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Payroll and Benefits Specialist at MIANX.

The Payroll and Benefits Specialist is responsible for accurately processing employee payroll, administering employee benefits, maintaining payroll records, ensuring statutory compliance, supporting compensation programs, and providing employees with payroll and benefits assistance. The role ensures employees are compensated accurately, on time, and in accordance with organizational policies and legal requirements.

The Payroll and Benefits Specialist reports directly to the Compensation and Benefits Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Payroll and Benefits Specialist role is to ensure accurate payroll processing and efficient administration of employee benefits while maintaining compliance, confidentiality, and employee satisfaction.

---

# Mission

The Payroll and Benefits Specialist exists to:

- Ensure payroll accuracy
- Deliver timely salary payments
- Administer employee benefits
- Maintain payroll compliance
- Protect payroll confidentiality
- Support employee satisfaction
- Improve payroll efficiency
- Maintain accurate HR records

---

# Role Summary

The Payroll and Benefits Specialist supports:

- Payroll Processing
- Salary Administration
- Employee Benefits Administration
- Time & Attendance Management
- Payroll Compliance
- Benefits Enrollment
- Employee Deductions
- Payroll Reporting
- HRIS Payroll Data
- Employee Payroll Support
- Leave & Absence Records
- Payroll Audits

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Payroll and Benefits Specialist is responsible for:

- Processing payroll accurately and on schedule
- Verifying employee attendance and leave records
- Calculating salaries, overtime, bonuses, and deductions
- Managing employee benefits enrollment
- Maintaining payroll records
- Supporting compensation reviews
- Preparing payroll reports
- Resolving payroll discrepancies
- Responding to employee payroll inquiries
- Coordinating with Finance for payroll execution
- Supporting payroll audits
- Maintaining confidentiality of payroll information

---

# Payroll Responsibilities

The Payroll and Benefits Specialist manages:

- Monthly Payroll
- Payroll Adjustments
- Salary Calculations
- Overtime Processing
- Leave Deductions
- Tax Deductions
- Social Security Contributions
- Benefits Deductions
- Payroll Registers
- Payroll Reconciliations

Payroll activities must be completed accurately, securely, and within established payroll schedules.

---

# Benefits Responsibilities

The Payroll and Benefits Specialist administers:

- Health Insurance Enrollment
- Retirement Programs
- Employee Assistance Programs
- Leave Benefits
- Wellness Benefits
- Benefits Changes
- Benefits Claims Support
- Employee Benefits Records
- Benefits Communications
- Annual Benefits Enrollment

Benefits administration should provide employees with timely and accurate support throughout their employment lifecycle.

---

# Governance Responsibilities

The Payroll and Benefits Specialist complies with:

- Payroll Policies
- Compensation Policies
- Benefits Policies
- Employment Regulations
- Tax Regulations
- Data Privacy Policies
- Payroll Security Standards
- Record Retention Policies
- Internal Control Procedures
- Audit Requirements

All payroll and benefits activities must comply with applicable laws and organizational standards.

# Operational Responsibilities

The Payroll and Benefits Specialist manages:

- Payroll System
- HRIS Payroll Records

- Payroll Reports

- Payroll Documentation
- Payroll Calendars
- Benefits Enrollment Records
- Payroll Audit Files

Operational excellence ensures reliable payroll processing and benefits administration.

# Risk Management Responsibilities

The Payroll and Benefits Specialist monitors:

- Payroll Errors
- Incorrect Salary Payments
- Benefits Administration Errors
- Compliance Risks
- Payroll Fraud Risks
- Data Privacy Risks
- Payroll System Failures
- Attendance Data Errors
- Audit Risks
- Operational Risks

Critical payroll issues must be immediately escalated to the Compensation and Benefits Manager.

# Collaboration

The Payroll and Benefits Specialist collaborates with:

- Compensation and Benefits Manager
- HR Operations Manager
- Finance Department
- Accounting Department
- HR Business Partner
- Department Managers
- Information Technology Department
- External Payroll Providers
- Insurance Providers
- Employees

Cross-functional collaboration ensures payroll accuracy, employee satisfaction, and regulatory compliance.

---

# Decision Authority

The Payroll and Benefits Specialist may independently:

- Process approved payroll
- Update payroll records
- Maintain employee benefits records
- Resolve standard payroll inquiries
- Prepare payroll reports
- Verify attendance records
- Process approved payroll adjustments
- Recommend payroll process improvements

Salary changes, bonus approvals, executive payroll adjustments, benefits policy changes, and payroll exceptions outside approved policies require approval from the Compensation and Benefits Manager.

- Support employee satisfaction
