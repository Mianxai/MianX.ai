# HR Director

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the HR Director at MIANX.

The HR Director is responsible for leading the day-to-day strategic and operational management of the Human Resources Department. The role ensures HR programs, policies, talent initiatives, employee relations, organizational development, and workforce operations are executed consistently across MIANX while aligning with executive human capital strategy.

The HR Director reports directly to the Vice President of Human Resources.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the HR Director role is to transform enterprise HR strategy into effective departmental execution by leading HR managers, improving people operations, strengthening organizational capability, and ensuring operational excellence across all human resource functions.

---

The HR Director exists to:

- Execute HR strategy
- Build organizational capability

- Drive continuous improvement

# Decision Authority

The HR Director may independently approve:

- HR operational procedures
- Recruitment activities
- Performance review schedules
- Learning initiatives
- Workforce reports
- Department budgets within approved limits
- HR process improvements
- Employee engagement initiatives

Executive appointments, enterprise restructures, compensation philosophy changes, organization-wide workforce reductions, and major HR transformation initiatives require approval from the Vice President of Human Resources or Chief Human Resources Officer.
