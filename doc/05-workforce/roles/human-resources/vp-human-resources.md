# Vice President of Human Resources

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Vice President of Human Resources (VP HR) at MIANX.

The Vice President of Human Resources is responsible for executing MIANX's enterprise human resources strategy by leading HR operations, talent management, organizational development, workforce planning, employee engagement, and HR transformation initiatives across the organization.

The Vice President of Human Resources reports directly to the Chief Human Resources Officer.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Vice President of Human Resources role is to transform strategic HR objectives into operational excellence by leading people initiatives, developing organizational capability, and ensuring every HR function supports MIANX's long-term business goals.

---

# Mission

The Vice President of Human Resources exists to:

- Execute enterprise HR strategy
- Build high-performing teams
- Strengthen organizational capability
- Improve employee experience
- Develop future leaders
- Enhance workforce productivity
- Ensure HR operational excellence
- Support sustainable business growth

# Decision Authority

The Vice President of Human Resources may independently approve:

- HR operational initiatives
- Department workforce plans
- HR process improvements
- Recruitment strategies
- Learning initiatives
- Performance management activities
- HR operational budgets
- Employee engagement programs

Executive compensation decisions, enterprise restructures, executive appointments, company-wide workforce reductions, and major organizational redesigns require approval from the Chief Human Resources Officer or Chief Executive Officer.
