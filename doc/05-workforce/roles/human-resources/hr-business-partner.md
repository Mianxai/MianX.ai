# HR Business Partner

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the HR Business Partner (HRBP) at MIANX.

The HR Business Partner (HRBP) is responsible for partnering with business leaders to align people strategies with organizational objectives. The HRBP acts as a strategic advisor, helping departments improve workforce planning, employee engagement, leadership capability, organizational effectiveness, and overall business performance through effective human resource practices.

The HR Business Partner reports directly to the Head of Human Resources.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the HR Business Partner role is to bridge business strategy and human resources by ensuring every department has the people, leadership, and organizational support necessary to achieve its objectives.

---

# Mission

The HR Business Partner exists to:

- Align HR with business goals
- Improve organizational performance
- Strengthen leadership capability
- Support workforce planning
- Enhance employee engagement
- Improve manager effectiveness
- Drive organizational change
- Build high-performing teams

---

- Supporting succession planning

# Decision Authority

The HR Business Partner may independently:

- Recommend workforce improvements
- Facilitate performance discussions
- Support workforce planning
- Coordinate employee engagement initiatives
- Advise managers on HR policies
- Recommend development plans
- Conduct workforce analysis
- Escalate organizational risks

Final disciplinary actions, executive employment decisions, compensation adjustments outside approved policies, and organizational restructures require approval from the Head of Human Resources or HR Director.
