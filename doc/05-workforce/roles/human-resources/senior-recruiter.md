# Senior Recruiter

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Senior Recruiter at MIANX.

The Senior Recruiter is responsible for leading complex recruitment initiatives, sourcing high-quality talent, managing full-cycle hiring, mentoring recruiters, and partnering with hiring managers to fill strategic positions efficiently while ensuring an exceptional candidate experience.

The Senior Recruiter reports directly to the Recruitment Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Senior Recruiter role is to strengthen MIANX's talent acquisition capability by successfully hiring high-performing professionals, improving recruitment quality, and supporting recruitment leadership through operational excellence and mentorship.

---

# Mission

The Senior Recruiter exists to:

- Hire exceptional talent
- Improve recruitment quality
- Build strong talent pipelines
- Deliver outstanding candidate experiences
- Support recruitment leadership
- Mentor recruitment team members
- Improve hiring efficiency
- Continuously optimize recruitment practices

---

# Role Summary

The Senior Recruiter supports:

- Full-Cycle Recruitment
- Talent Sourcing
- Candidate Screening
- Interview Management
- Hiring Coordination
- Employer Branding
- Recruitment Analytics
- Recruitment Compliance
- Offer Management
- Recruiter Mentorship

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Senior Recruiter is responsible for:

- Managing end-to-end recruitment
- Sourcing qualified candidates
- Conducting recruiter screenings
- Coordinating interviews
- Evaluating candidates
- Supporting hiring managers
- Preparing hiring recommendations
- Negotiating offers within approved guidelines
- Maintaining recruitment documentation
- Mentoring recruiters
- Improving hiring processes
- Escalating recruitment challenges

---

# Recruitment Responsibilities

The Senior Recruiter performs:

- Talent Sourcing
- Candidate Outreach
- Resume Screening
- Candidate Interviews
- Skills Assessments
- Interview Coordination
- Reference Verification
- Offer Preparation
- Candidate Relationship Management
- Recruitment Reporting

Recruitment activities should consistently produce high-quality hiring outcomes.

---

# Governance Responsibilities

The Senior Recruiter supports:

- Recruitment Standards
- Candidate Evaluation Framework
- Interview Guidelines
- Recruitment Documentation
- ATS Data Accuracy
- Hiring Compliance
- Candidate Privacy
- Recruitment Metrics
- Hiring Workflows
- Employer Branding Standards

Governance responsibilities ensure consistency, fairness, and compliance throughout the recruitment lifecycle.

---

# Risk Management Responsibilities

The Senior Recruiter monitors:

- Hiring Delays
- Candidate Withdrawal Risks
- Recruitment Compliance Risks
- Interview Bias
- Talent Availability Risks
- Documentation Errors
- Offer Acceptance Risks
- Employer Branding Risks
- Recruitment Quality Risks
- Operational Risks

Significant recruitment risks must be escalated to the Recruitment Manager.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The Senior Recruiter manages:

- Candidate Pipeline
- ATS Records
- Interview Calendar
- Candidate Communications
- Recruitment Documentation
- Recruitment Metrics
- Job Posting Support
- Offer Coordination
- Hiring Reports
- Continuous Recruitment Improvements

Operational consistency enables efficient and scalable recruitment.

---

# Collaboration

The Senior Recruiter collaborates with:

- Recruitment Manager
- Talent Acquisition Manager
- Recruiters
- Junior Recruiters
- HR Business Partner
- Hiring Managers
- Department Leaders
- Finance Department
- Legal Department
- External Recruitment Partners

Cross-functional collaboration ensures successful hiring outcomes across MIANX.

---

# Decision Authority

The Senior Recruiter may independently:

- Source candidates
- Screen applicants
- Coordinate interviews
- Recommend qualified candidates
- Maintain recruitment documentation
- Mentor recruiters
- Improve recruitment workflows
- Escalate hiring concerns

Final hiring decisions, executive recruitment, compensation outside approved salary bands, and employment offers requiring policy exceptions require approval from the Recruitment Manager or Talent Acquisition Manager.

---

# Core Competencies

The Senior Recruiter should demonstrate proficiency in:

- Talent Acquisition
- Candidate Assessment
- Behavioral Interviewing
- Talent Sourcing
- Recruitment Analytics
- Employer Branding
- ATS Administration
- Communication
- Negotiation
- Stakeholder Management

# Performance Expectations

The Senior Recruiter is expected to:

- Deliver high-quality hires
- Reduce hiring timelines
- Build qualified talent pipelines
- Improve candidate experience
- Maintain recruitment compliance
- Mentor recruitment staff
- Strengthen hiring manager relationships
- Continuously improve recruitment effectiveness
