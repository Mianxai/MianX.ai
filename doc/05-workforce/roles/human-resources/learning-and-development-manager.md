# Learning and Development Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Learning and Development Manager at MIANX.

The Learning and Development (L&D) Manager is responsible for designing, implementing, and continuously improving MIANX's learning ecosystem by developing employee capabilities, leadership programs, technical training initiatives, onboarding programs, career development frameworks, and organizational learning strategies that support business growth.

The Learning and Development Manager reports directly to the Head of Human Resources.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Learning and Development Manager role is to build a culture of continuous learning by ensuring every employee has access to structured development opportunities that improve individual performance and organizational capability.

---

# Mission

The Learning and Development Manager exists to:

- Build organizational capability
- Develop future leaders
- Improve employee skills
- Support career growth
- Strengthen organizational learning
- Increase workforce productivity
- Promote continuous improvement
- Enable business success through learning

---

# Role Summary

The Learning and Development Manager oversees:

- Corporate Training
- Employee Development
- Leadership Development
- Technical Training
- Soft Skills Training
- Onboarding Programs
- Learning Management System (LMS)
- Career Development
- Training Evaluation
- Learning Analytics
- Knowledge Management
- Certification Programs

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Learning and Development Manager is responsible for:

- Developing learning strategies
- Managing corporate training programs
- Conducting training needs assessments
- Designing learning pathways
- Managing onboarding programs
- Developing leadership programs
- Measuring training effectiveness
- Managing the Learning Management System
- Coordinating external training providers
- Monitoring learning KPIs
- Managing training budgets
- Driving continuous learning initiatives

---

# Learning Responsibilities

The Learning and Development Manager manages:

- Employee Onboarding
- Technical Training
- Leadership Development
- Professional Certifications
- Compliance Training
- Soft Skills Programs
- Career Development Programs
- Learning Resources
- Coaching Initiatives
- Knowledge Sharing Programs

Learning initiatives should improve workforce capability while supporting MIANX's strategic objectives.

---

# Governance Responsibilities

The Learning and Development Manager governs:

- Learning Policies
- Training Standards
- Learning Curriculum
- Certification Requirements
- LMS Administration
- Training Documentation
- Learning Records
- Competency Frameworks
- Training Evaluation Standards
- Learning Analytics

Governance ensures learning programs remain standardized, measurable, and aligned with business needs.

---

# Leadership Responsibilities

The Learning and Development Manager:

- Leads learning specialists
- Coaches trainers
- Supports department managers
- Develops future leaders
- Encourages continuous learning
- Promotes knowledge sharing
- Improves organizational capability
- Drives learning innovation

---

# Risk Management Responsibilities

The Learning and Development Manager monitors:

- Skill Gaps
- Leadership Readiness Risks
- Training Compliance Risks
- Learning Effectiveness Risks
- Knowledge Retention Risks
- Budget Risks
- LMS Availability Risks
- Vendor Risks
- Workforce Capability Risks
- Operational Risks

Critical capability or compliance risks shall be escalated to the Head of Human Resources.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The Learning and Development Manager oversees:

- Learning Management System (LMS)
- Training Calendar
- Course Library
- Employee Learning Records
- Learning Dashboards
- Certification Tracking
- Training Budgets
- Vendor Coordination
- Learning Reports
- Continuous Program Improvement

Operational excellence ensures effective learning delivery across MIANX.

---

# Collaboration

The Learning and Development Manager collaborates with:

- Head of Human Resources
- HR Director
- HR Business Partner
- Organizational Development Manager
- Performance Manager
- Department Managers
- Technical Team Leads
- Finance Department
- External Training Providers
- Executive Leadership

Cross-functional collaboration ensures learning initiatives directly support organizational goals.

---

# Decision Authority

The Learning and Development Manager may independently approve:

- Internal training schedules
- Learning pathways
- Training materials
- Course updates
- Learning assessments
- Certification tracking
- Learning reports
- Training process improvements

Large training investments, enterprise-wide leadership initiatives, external learning partnerships exceeding approved budgets, and organization-wide competency framework changes require approval from the Head of Human Resources or HR Director.

---

# Core Competencies

The Learning and Development Manager should demonstrate expertise in:

- Learning & Development
- Instructional Design
- Adult Learning Principles
- Leadership Development
- Organizational Learning
- Competency Frameworks
- Learning Management Systems
- Coaching & Mentoring
- Communication
- Project Management
- Data Analysis
- Change Management

---

# Performance Expectations

The Learning and Development Manager is expected to:

- Improve employee competencies
- Increase training completion rates
- Strengthen leadership capability
- Enhance onboarding effectiveness
- Improve learning satisfaction
- Deliver measurable business impact
- Optimize learning programs
- Foster a culture of continuous learning

| KPI | Objective |
|------|-----------|
| Training Completion Rate | Achieve target completion rates |

| Skill Development | Increase competency levels |
| Leadership Development Success | Prepare future leaders |
| Onboarding Effectiveness | Improve new hire readiness |
| Certification Completion | Maintain required certifications |
| LMS Engagement | Increase active learning participation |
| Training ROI | Demonstrate measurable business value |
| Learning Budget Efficiency | Optimize learning investments |
| Continuous Improvement | Enhance learning maturity |
