# Compensation and Benefits Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Compensation and Benefits Manager at MIANX.

The Compensation and Benefits Manager is responsible for designing, implementing, and managing MIANX's total rewards strategy, including salary structures, incentive programs, employee benefits, compensation benchmarking, payroll coordination, rewards governance, and benefits administration to ensure competitive, equitable, and sustainable employee compensation.

The Compensation and Benefits Manager reports directly to the Head of Human Resources.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Compensation and Benefits Manager role is to develop fair, competitive, and sustainable compensation and benefits programs that attract, retain, and motivate top talent while supporting MIANX's financial and strategic objectives.

---

# Mission

The Compensation and Benefits Manager exists to:

- Build competitive compensation programs
- Improve employee satisfaction
- Ensure internal pay equity
- Support employee retention
- Optimize benefits programs
- Maintain compensation compliance
- Support business growth
- Improve total rewards effectiveness

---

# Decision Authority

The Compensation and Benefits Manager may independently approve:

- Salary benchmarking reports
- Benefits enrollment processes
- Compensation analyses
- Job evaluation recommendations
- Compensation reports
- Rewards documentation
- Benefits communications
- Process improvements

Salary adjustments outside approved salary bands, executive compensation, major benefits changes, organization-wide incentive programs, and compensation policy revisions require approval from the Head of Human Resources or HR Director.
