# Chief Human Resources Officer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO) at MIANX.

The Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO) is the executive leader responsible for developing and executing MIANX's global people strategy. The CHRO ensures the organization attracts, develops, engages, and retains exceptional talent while building a high-performance culture that supports long-term business objectives.

The Chief Human Resources Officer reports directly to the Chief Executive Officer.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Chief Human Resources Officer role is to align human capital strategy with business strategy, ensuring MIANX develops a world-class workforce capable of sustaining innovation, operational excellence, and organizational growth.

---

# Mission

The Chief Human Resources Officer exists to:

- Build a world-class workforce
- Develop future leaders
- Strengthen organizational culture
- Improve employee experience
- Drive workforce transformation
- Enable business growth
- Ensure employment compliance
- Foster continuous learning

# Decision Authority

The Chief Human Resources Officer may independently approve:

- HR strategies
- Organizational structures
- HR policies
- Executive hiring recommendations
- Workforce planning initiatives
- Leadership development programs
- HR operational budgets
- Talent management initiatives

The following require Chief Executive Officer or Board approval:

- Executive compensation
- Executive succession appointments
- Enterprise restructuring
- Company-wide workforce reductions
- Major organizational redesigns
- Enterprise compensation philosophy
- Long-term incentive programs
