# HR Generalist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the HR Generalist at MIANX.

The HR Generalist is responsible for supporting the day-to-day Human Resources operations across the entire employee lifecycle. The role provides operational support in recruitment, onboarding, employee relations, HR administration, performance management, training coordination, policy implementation, HR compliance, and employee engagement while ensuring a consistent and high-quality employee experience.

The HR Generalist reports directly to the HR Operations Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the HR Generalist role is to provide comprehensive HR support across multiple functional areas while ensuring efficient HR operations, employee satisfaction, compliance, and consistent implementation of HR policies.

---

# Mission

The HR Generalist exists to:

- Support employees throughout their employment lifecycle
- Deliver efficient HR services
- Maintain HR compliance
- Improve employee experience
- Support HR initiatives
- Ensure policy consistency
- Strengthen organizational culture
- Drive operational excellence

The HR Generalist follows and supports:

- Workplace Conduct Standards

# Risk Management Responsibilities

The HR Generalist monitors:

- Documentation Errors
- Employee Record Risks
- HR Compliance Risks
- Data Privacy Risks
- Onboarding Delays
- Policy Violations
- Attendance Administration Risks
- Operational Risks
- Employee Service Risks
- Audit Risks

Any significant HR compliance or employee-related issue must be escalated to the HR Operations Manager.

The HR Generalist collaborates with:

- Payroll and Benefits Specialist

- Finance Department
- Information Technology Department
- Employees

Cross-functional collaboration ensures efficient HR operations and a positive employee experience.

---

# Decision Authority

The HR Generalist may independently:

- Process standard HR requests
- Update employee records
- Prepare HR documentation
- Coordinate onboarding activities
- Schedule HR meetings
- Process approved leave documentation
- Generate standard HR reports
- Recommend operational improvements

Employment offers, policy exceptions, disciplinary actions, salary changes, employee terminations, and strategic HR decisions require approval from the HR Operations Manager or Head of Human Resources.
