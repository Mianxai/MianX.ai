# Human Resources Department

> The Human Resources (HR) Department is responsible for building, supporting, developing, and retaining MIANX's workforce by establishing people strategies, talent management practices, organizational development initiatives, and employee experience programs that enable sustainable business growth.

The Human Resources Department partners with every department across MIANX to attract exceptional talent, develop high-performing teams, maintain compliance with employment laws, strengthen organizational culture, and ensure employees have the resources, policies, and support necessary to succeed.

---

# Department Mission

The Human Resources Department exists to:

- Attract exceptional talent
- Develop future leaders
- Build high-performing teams
- Strengthen organizational culture
- Improve employee experience
- Ensure regulatory compliance
- Support business growth
- Enable organizational excellence
