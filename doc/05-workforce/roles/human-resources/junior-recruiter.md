# Junior Recruiter

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Junior Recruiter at MIANX.

The Junior Recruiter is responsible for supporting recruitment operations by assisting with candidate sourcing, resume screening, interview coordination, recruitment administration, candidate communications, and Applicant Tracking System (ATS) maintenance. The role provides foundational recruitment support while developing the knowledge and skills required for progression into a Recruiter role.

The Junior Recruiter reports directly to the Senior Recruiter.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Junior Recruiter role is to support efficient hiring operations by providing administrative and operational recruitment assistance while developing expertise in talent acquisition and recruitment best practices.

---

# Mission

The Junior Recruiter exists to:

- Support recruitment operations
- Build recruitment knowledge
- Deliver excellent candidate experiences
- Maintain recruitment accuracy
- Improve hiring efficiency
- Assist hiring managers
- Learn recruitment best practices
- Grow into an independent recruiter

---

# Role Summary

The Junior Recruiter supports:

- Candidate Sourcing
- Resume Screening
- Candidate Communication
- Interview Scheduling
- ATS Administration
- Recruitment Documentation
- Job Posting
- Recruitment Reporting
- Candidate Database Management
- Hiring Coordination

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Junior Recruiter is responsible for:

- Assisting with candidate sourcing
- Reviewing resumes using established criteria
- Scheduling interviews
- Coordinating interview logistics
- Maintaining ATS records
- Posting job advertisements
- Communicating with candidates
- Preparing recruitment documentation
- Supporting recruitment reporting
- Maintaining candidate databases
- Assisting recruiters with hiring activities
- Escalating recruitment issues when necessary

---

# Recruitment Responsibilities

The Junior Recruiter performs:

- Job Posting
- Resume Collection
- Candidate Database Updates
- Interview Scheduling
- Candidate Follow-up
- Recruitment Administration
- ATS Record Maintenance
- Recruitment Documentation
- Candidate Communications
- Hiring Coordination Support

Recruitment activities should be completed accurately, professionally, and within established service timelines.

---

# Governance Responsibilities

The Junior Recruiter follows:

- Recruitment Policies
- Hiring Procedures
- Interview Scheduling Standards
- Candidate Privacy Requirements
- Documentation Standards
- Data Protection Policies
- ATS Usage Guidelines
- Recruitment Compliance Requirements
- Communication Standards
- Employer Branding Guidelines

All activities shall comply with company policies and applicable employment regulations.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The Junior Recruiter manages:

- Interview Calendars
- ATS Candidate Records
- Recruitment Inbox
- Candidate Documentation
- Job Posting Platforms
- Recruitment Reports
- Candidate Communications
- Recruitment Checklists
- Administrative Records
- Daily Recruitment Tasks

Operational accuracy is essential to ensure a smooth recruitment process.

---

# Risk Management Responsibilities

The Junior Recruiter monitors:

- Interview Scheduling Conflicts
- Missing Documentation
- Candidate Communication Delays
- ATS Data Errors
- Recruitment Compliance Issues
- Administrative Mistakes
- Candidate Experience Concerns
- Recruitment Process Delays
- Data Privacy Risks
- Operational Issues

Operational risks should be reported promptly to the Senior Recruiter.

---

# Collaboration

The Junior Recruiter collaborates with:

- Senior Recruiter
- Recruiter
- Recruitment Manager
- Talent Acquisition Manager
- HR Business Partner
- Hiring Managers
- Department Coordinators
- Candidates
- External Recruitment Partners
- HR Operations Team

Effective collaboration supports efficient recruitment and a positive candidate experience.

---

# Decision Authority

The Junior Recruiter may independently:

- Schedule interviews
- Maintain ATS records
- Publish approved job postings
- Communicate standard recruitment updates
- Maintain recruitment documentation
- Coordinate interview logistics
- Update recruitment reports
- Escalate operational issues

Candidate selection decisions, employment offers, salary discussions, and hiring approvals remain the responsibility of senior recruitment leadership.

---

# Core Competencies

The Junior Recruiter should demonstrate proficiency in:

- Recruitment Administration
- Candidate Communication
- Organization
- Time Management
- Attention to Detail
- ATS Administration
- Documentation
- Professional Communication
- Collaboration
- Continuous Learning

---

# Performance Expectations

The Junior Recruiter is expected to:

- Complete recruitment tasks accurately
- Maintain professional candidate communications
- Support efficient hiring operations
- Ensure documentation quality
- Follow recruitment procedures
- Learn recruitment best practices
- Improve productivity over time
- Contribute positively to the recruitment team
