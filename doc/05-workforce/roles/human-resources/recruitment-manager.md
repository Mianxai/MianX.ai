# Recruitment Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Recruitment Manager at MIANX.

The Recruitment Manager is responsible for managing MIANX's end-to-end recruitment operations by leading recruitment teams, coordinating hiring activities, improving recruitment efficiency, maintaining candidate quality, and ensuring every hiring process aligns with organizational policies and workforce objectives.

The Recruitment Manager reports directly to the Talent Acquisition Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Recruitment Manager role is to ensure recruitment operations consistently deliver qualified talent through efficient, scalable, and standardized hiring processes while providing an exceptional experience for candidates and hiring managers.

---

# Mission

The Recruitment Manager exists to:

- Deliver exceptional hiring outcomes
- Build strong talent pipelines
- Improve recruitment efficiency
- Support workforce growth
- Enhance candidate experience
- Ensure hiring consistency
- Strengthen employer reputation
- Continuously improve recruitment operations

---

- Coordinating hiring managers
- Monitoring recruitment KPIs

- Improving hiring workflows

The Recruitment Manager manages:

- Candidate Sourcing
- Resume Screening

- Candidate Assessments
- Hiring Coordination
- Offer Preparation
- Offer Negotiation Support
- Candidate Communication
- Recruitment Reporting

# Governance Responsibilities

The Recruitment Manager governs:

- Interview Standards
- Candidate Evaluation
- Recruitment Documentation
- Hiring Compliance
- Recruitment Workflows
- Recruitment Reporting
- Recruitment KPIs

- Hiring Quality Standards

Governance responsibilities ensure hiring remains fair, transparent, compliant, and efficient.

The Recruitment Manager:

- Leads recruiters
- Coaches recruitment staff
- Supports hiring managers

- Promotes collaborative hiring

- Drives operational excellence

# Risk Management Responsibilities

The Recruitment Manager manages:

- Hiring Delays
- Recruitment Bottlenecks
- Candidate Drop-off
- Talent Shortages
- Compliance Risks
- Interview Bias
- Hiring Quality Risks
- Vendor Risks
- Documentation Risks
- Operational Risks

Critical recruitment risks must be escalated to the Talent Acquisition Manager.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The Recruitment Manager oversees:

- Recruitment Pipeline
- ATS Operations
- Candidate Database
- Interview Calendar
- Recruitment Dashboards
- Hiring Reports
- Recruitment Vendors
- Job Advertisements
- Recruitment Documentation
- Continuous Improvement Activities

Operational excellence ensures predictable, efficient, and scalable recruitment operations.

# Collaboration

The Recruitment Manager collaborates with:

- Talent Acquisition Manager
- Head of Human Resources
- HR Business Partner
- Senior Recruiter
- Recruiters
- Hiring Managers
- Department Heads
- Finance Department
- Legal Department
- External Recruitment Agencies

Cross-functional collaboration ensures hiring activities align with workforce planning and organizational priorities.

---

# Decision Authority

The Recruitment Manager may independently approve:

- Recruitment schedules
- Candidate screening progression
- Interview coordination
- Recruitment reporting
- Recruiter task assignments
- Recruitment process improvements
- Job posting schedules
- Vendor coordination

Final hiring approvals, executive recruitment, compensation outside approved salary ranges, and workforce restructuring require approval from the Talent Acquisition Manager or Head of Human Resources.
