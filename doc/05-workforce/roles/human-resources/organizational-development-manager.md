# Organizational Development Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Organizational Development Manager at MIANX.

The Organizational Development (OD) Manager is responsible for improving organizational effectiveness by designing organizational structures, developing culture initiatives, leading change management programs, enhancing leadership capability, improving employee engagement, supporting succession planning, and implementing organization-wide development initiatives.

The Organizational Development Manager reports directly to the Head of Human Resources.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Organizational Development Manager role is to strengthen MIANX's organizational capability by improving people, processes, culture, leadership, and organizational design to support sustainable business growth.

---

# Mission

The Organizational Development Manager exists to:

- Improve organizational effectiveness
- Strengthen company culture
- Develop future leaders
- Drive organizational change
- Increase employee engagement
- Build organizational capability
- Improve workforce performance
- Support long-term business growth

# Decision Authority

The Organizational Development Manager may independently approve:

- Organizational assessments
- Employee engagement initiatives
- Leadership workshops
- Competency evaluations
