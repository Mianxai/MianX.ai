# Head of Human Resources

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Head of Human Resources at MIANX.

The Head of Human Resources is responsible for managing the daily operations of the Human Resources Department by overseeing recruitment, employee relations, HR operations, performance management, learning and development, policy implementation, workforce planning, and employee engagement initiatives. The role ensures HR services are delivered consistently, efficiently, and in alignment with MIANX's strategic objectives.

The Head of Human Resources reports directly to the HR Director.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Head of Human Resources role is to lead operational HR functions, develop people programs, strengthen organizational culture, and ensure exceptional employee experiences while maintaining compliance with organizational policies and employment regulations.

---

# Mission

The Head of Human Resources exists to:

- Lead HR operations
- Strengthen organizational culture
- Improve employee experience
- Build high-performing teams
- Develop future leaders
- Ensure policy compliance
- Improve workforce capability
- Drive operational excellence

# Decision Authority

The Head of Human Resources may independently approve:

- Recruitment activities
- Employee onboarding plans
- HR operational procedures
- Training schedules
- HR reports
- Employee engagement initiatives
- Department resource allocation
- HR process improvements

Executive appointments, major restructures, enterprise-wide policy changes, compensation philosophy, and strategic workforce initiatives require approval from the HR Director or Vice President of Human Resources.
