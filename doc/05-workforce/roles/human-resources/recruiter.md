# Recruiter

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Recruiter at MIANX.

The Recruiter is responsible for executing end-to-end recruitment activities by sourcing qualified candidates, coordinating interviews, maintaining candidate relationships, supporting hiring managers, and ensuring recruitment processes comply with MIANX's hiring standards.

The Recruiter reports directly to the Senior Recruiter.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Recruiter role is to efficiently identify, evaluate, and recruit qualified professionals while delivering an exceptional candidate experience and supporting the organization's workforce growth objectives.

---

# Mission

The Recruiter exists to:

- Identify exceptional talent
- Build strong candidate pipelines
- Improve hiring efficiency
- Deliver an outstanding candidate experience
- Support hiring managers
- Maintain recruitment quality
- Ensure hiring compliance
- Contribute to organizational growth

---

# Role Summary

The Recruiter supports:

- Candidate Sourcing
- Resume Screening
- Candidate Communication
- Interview Coordination
- Candidate Assessment
- Hiring Documentation
- Recruitment Reporting
- ATS Management
- Employer Branding Support
- Recruitment Operations

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Recruiter is responsible for:

- Sourcing qualified candidates
- Reviewing resumes
- Conducting initial screening interviews
- Scheduling interviews
- Coordinating hiring activities
- Maintaining ATS records
- Preparing recruitment documentation
- Supporting offer preparation
- Communicating with candidates
- Building talent pipelines
- Monitoring recruitment metrics
- Escalating recruitment issues

---

# Recruitment Responsibilities

The Recruiter performs:

- Job Posting
- Candidate Sourcing
- Resume Screening
- Initial Candidate Interviews
- Interview Scheduling
- Candidate Follow-up
- Candidate Database Maintenance
- Hiring Documentation
- Recruitment Reporting
- Candidate Relationship Management

Recruitment activities should consistently support timely and high-quality hiring.

---

# Governance Responsibilities

The Recruiter follows:

- Recruitment Policies
- Hiring Procedures
- Candidate Evaluation Standards
- Interview Guidelines
- Employment Compliance Requirements
- Documentation Standards
- Data Privacy Policies
- ATS Standards
- Recruitment Reporting Requirements
- Employer Branding Guidelines

All recruitment activities shall comply with organizational policies and applicable employment laws.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The Recruiter manages:

- Candidate Pipeline
- Applicant Tracking System (ATS)
- Interview Calendar
- Recruitment Documentation
- Candidate Communications
- Recruitment Reports
- Job Advertisement Updates
- Candidate Records
- Recruitment Metrics
- Daily Hiring Activities

Operational consistency ensures reliable recruitment execution.

---

# Risk Management Responsibilities

The Recruiter monitors:

- Candidate Drop-offs
- Hiring Delays
- Incomplete Documentation
- Interview Scheduling Conflicts
- Compliance Issues
- Data Accuracy
- Candidate Experience Risks
- Hiring Quality Risks
- Communication Gaps
- Recruitment Bottlenecks

Significant operational risks must be reported to the Senior Recruiter.

---

# Collaboration

The Recruiter collaborates with:

- Senior Recruiter
- Recruitment Manager
- Talent Acquisition Manager
- HR Business Partner
- Hiring Managers
- Department Supervisors
- Finance Department
- Legal Department
- External Candidates
- Recruitment Agencies

Effective collaboration ensures recruitment activities support organizational hiring goals.

---

# Decision Authority

The Recruiter may independently:

- Source candidates
- Screen applications
- Schedule interviews
- Update ATS records
- Communicate with candidates
- Prepare recruitment documentation
- Recommend qualified candidates
- Maintain candidate pipelines

Final hiring decisions, compensation negotiations, executive recruitment, and policy exceptions require approval from the Senior Recruiter or Recruitment Manager.

---

# Core Competencies

The Recruiter should demonstrate proficiency in:

- Talent Sourcing
- Candidate Screening
- Behavioral Interviewing
- Communication
- ATS Administration
- Recruitment Coordination
- Organization
- Relationship Management
- Time Management
- Attention to Detail

---

# Performance Expectations

The Recruiter is expected to:

- Deliver qualified candidates
- Reduce recruitment timelines
- Maintain recruitment accuracy
- Improve candidate satisfaction
- Build quality talent pipelines
- Ensure documentation quality
- Support hiring managers
- Continuously improve recruitment practices
