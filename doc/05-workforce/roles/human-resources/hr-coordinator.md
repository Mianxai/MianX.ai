# HR Coordinator

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the HR Coordinator at MIANX.

The HR Coordinator is responsible for providing administrative and operational support to the Human Resources department by coordinating employee documentation, onboarding and offboarding activities, HR communications, scheduling, personnel records, recruitment logistics, and day-to-day HR administrative tasks. The role ensures efficient HR operations and delivers timely support to employees and managers.

The HR Coordinator reports directly to the HR Operations Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the HR Coordinator role is to ensure accurate, organized, and efficient HR administrative operations that support employees throughout the entire employment lifecycle while maintaining compliance with organizational policies.

---

# Mission

The HR Coordinator exists to:

- Support HR operations
- Deliver excellent employee service
- Maintain accurate HR records
- Coordinate HR activities
- Improve administrative efficiency
- Ensure policy compliance
- Support organizational growth
- Contribute to continuous improvement

# Decision Authority

The HR Coordinator may independently:

- Schedule interviews
- Coordinate onboarding activities
- Maintain employee records
- Prepare standard HR documentation
- Respond to routine HR inquiries
- Update HR systems
- Coordinate HR meetings
