# Employee Relations Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Employee Relations Manager at MIANX.

The Employee Relations Manager is responsible for fostering a positive, respectful, and legally compliant work environment by managing employee relations programs, resolving workplace conflicts, overseeing grievance procedures, supporting disciplinary processes, promoting employee engagement, and ensuring consistent application of employment policies across MIANX.

The Employee Relations Manager reports directly to the Head of Human Resources.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Employee Relations Manager role is to maintain healthy employer-employee relationships by ensuring fairness, trust, engagement, compliance, and effective conflict resolution throughout the employee lifecycle.

---

# Mission

The Employee Relations Manager exists to:

- Build a positive workplace culture
- Strengthen employee engagement
- Resolve workplace conflicts
- Promote fairness and transparency
- Support employee wellbeing
- Ensure policy compliance
- Protect organizational values
- Improve employee experience

# Decision Authority

The Employee Relations Manager may independently approve:

- Standard grievance investigations
- Employee mediation sessions
- Workplace communication initiatives
- Employee engagement activities
- Recognition recommendations
- Investigation reports
- Policy guidance
- Process improvement recommendations

Employee terminations, executive investigations, major disciplinary actions, legal settlements, and policy exceptions require approval from the Head of Human Resources and, where applicable, the Legal Department.
