# Training Specialist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Training Specialist at MIANX.

The Training Specialist is responsible for planning, developing, delivering, and evaluating employee training programs that improve workforce knowledge, technical competencies, professional skills, compliance awareness, and overall organizational performance. The role ensures employees receive effective, engaging, and measurable learning experiences aligned with MIANX's business objectives.

The Training Specialist reports directly to the Learning and Development Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Training Specialist role is to equip employees with the knowledge, skills, and competencies required to perform effectively while supporting continuous learning and professional growth across MIANX.

---

# Mission

The Training Specialist exists to:

- Deliver high-quality training
- Improve employee competencies
- Support organizational learning
- Enhance workforce productivity
- Promote continuous development
- Strengthen onboarding experiences
- Maintain learning standards
- Measure training effectiveness

---

# Role Summary

The Training Specialist supports:

- Employee Training
- Technical Training
- Soft Skills Development
- New Employee Onboarding
- Compliance Training
- Learning Content Development
- Training Assessments
- Learning Management System (LMS)
- Learning Analytics
- Knowledge Sharing

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Training Specialist is responsible for:

- Delivering instructor-led training
- Developing training materials
- Conducting training needs assessments
- Managing onboarding sessions
- Evaluating training effectiveness
- Maintaining training records
- Supporting LMS administration
- Coordinating training logistics
- Assisting certification programs
- Updating learning content
- Supporting leadership development initiatives
- Recommending learning improvements

---

# Training Responsibilities

The Training Specialist delivers:

- Employee Orientation
- Technical Skills Training
- Professional Development Programs
- Compliance Training
- Product Training
- Systems Training
- Leadership Workshops
- Soft Skills Programs
- Refresher Courses
- Knowledge Transfer Sessions

Training should be practical, engaging, measurable, and aligned with organizational requirements.

---

# Governance Responsibilities

The Training Specialist follows:

- Learning Policies
- Training Standards
- Competency Frameworks
- LMS Procedures
- Training Documentation Standards
- Certification Requirements
- Compliance Training Requirements
- Assessment Standards
- Attendance Policies
- Learning Evaluation Procedures

All learning activities must comply with organizational learning standards and regulatory requirements.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The Training Specialist manages:

- Training Calendar
- Classroom Sessions
- Virtual Training Sessions
- LMS Course Records
- Attendance Reports
- Assessment Results
- Training Materials
- Learning Resources
- Course Evaluations
- Training Documentation

Operational excellence ensures employees receive consistent and high-quality learning experiences.

---

# Risk Management Responsibilities

The Training Specialist monitors:

- Low Training Participation
- Knowledge Gaps
- Course Quality Risks
- Compliance Training Delays
- LMS Availability Issues
- Assessment Accuracy
- Training Documentation Errors
- Learning Resource Risks
- Scheduling Conflicts
- Operational Risks

Critical learning risks shall be escalated to the Learning and Development Manager.

---

# Collaboration

The Training Specialist collaborates with:

- Learning and Development Manager
- HR Business Partner
- Organizational Development Manager
- Performance Manager
- Department Managers
- Technical Team Leads
- Subject Matter Experts
- HR Operations Team
- External Trainers
- Employees

Collaboration ensures training programs meet operational and strategic workforce needs.

---

# Decision Authority

The Training Specialist may independently:

- Deliver approved training programs
- Schedule internal training sessions
- Update training materials
- Conduct learning assessments
- Maintain LMS records
- Prepare training reports
- Recommend content improvements
- Coordinate training logistics

Major curriculum changes, enterprise-wide learning initiatives, external training engagements, and significant budget decisions require approval from the Learning and Development Manager.

---

# Core Competencies

The Training Specialist should demonstrate proficiency in:

- Adult Learning Principles
- Instructional Delivery
- Course Facilitation
- Presentation Skills
- Communication
- Learning Management Systems
- Assessment Design
- Coaching
- Curriculum Development
- Organization
- Time Management
- Continuous Learning

---

# Performance Expectations

The Training Specialist is expected to:

- Deliver engaging training sessions
- Improve employee learning outcomes
- Maintain accurate training records
- Increase training participation
- Improve learner satisfaction
- Support organizational capability
- Ensure training compliance
- Continuously improve course quality
