# HR Operations Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the HR Operations Manager at MIANX.

The HR Operations Manager is responsible for overseeing the day-to-day operational activities of the Human Resources department, ensuring efficient HR service delivery, HR process optimization, HR systems administration, employee records management, policy implementation, compliance, and operational excellence across the entire employee lifecycle.

The HR Operations Manager reports directly to the Head of Human Resources.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the HR Operations Manager role is to ensure HR operations are efficient, compliant, scalable, and employee-focused while enabling the HR department to effectively support MIANX's business objectives.

---

# Mission

The HR Operations Manager exists to:

- Deliver operational excellence
- Standardize HR processes
- Improve HR service quality
- Ensure regulatory compliance
- Optimize HR systems
- Maintain employee records
- Support organizational growth
- Drive continuous improvement

# Decision Authority

The HR Operations Manager may independently approve:

- HR operational procedures
- Employee record updates
- HR documentation standards
- HR operational reports
- HR service workflows

Major HRIS implementations, enterprise-wide HR policy revisions, organizational restructuring, and significant operational changes require approval from the Head of Human Resources or HR Director.
