# Talent Acquisition Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Talent Acquisition Manager at MIANX.

The Talent Acquisition Manager is responsible for developing and executing MIANX's talent acquisition strategy by leading recruitment operations, employer branding, workforce planning support, candidate experience initiatives, recruitment technology, and hiring process optimization. The role ensures MIANX consistently attracts, assesses, and hires exceptional talent that supports long-term organizational growth.

The Talent Acquisition Manager reports directly to the Head of Human Resources.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Talent Acquisition Manager role is to build a scalable, efficient, and high-quality recruitment function that enables MIANX to attract top talent while providing an outstanding hiring experience for candidates and hiring managers.

---

# Mission

The Talent Acquisition Manager exists to:

- Build exceptional teams
- Attract world-class talent
- Improve hiring quality
- Strengthen employer branding
- Reduce hiring timelines
- Improve candidate experience
- Support workforce growth
- Continuously improve recruitment processes

# Decision Authority

The Talent Acquisition Manager may independently approve:

- Recruitment campaigns
- Job advertisements
- Candidate sourcing strategies
- Interview scheduling
- Recruitment workflows
- Recruitment vendor coordination
- Hiring reports
- Recruitment process improvements

Executive hiring approvals, compensation packages outside approved salary bands, workforce restructuring, and executive recruitment decisions require approval from the Head of Human Resources or HR Director.
