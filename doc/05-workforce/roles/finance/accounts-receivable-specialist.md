# Accounts Receivable Specialist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Accounts Receivable Specialist at MIANX.

The Accounts Receivable Specialist is responsible for managing customer invoicing, recording incoming payments, maintaining customer account balances, monitoring outstanding receivables, supporting collections, and ensuring accurate financial records for all revenue-related transactions.

The Accounts Receivable Specialist reports directly to the Accounting Manager or Senior Accountant.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Accounts Receivable Specialist role is to ensure accurate billing, timely collection of receivables, proper recording of customer payments, and effective management of outstanding balances while maintaining positive customer relationships.

---

# Mission

The Accounts Receivable Specialist exists to:

- Process customer invoices accurately
- Collect outstanding receivables
- Maintain accurate customer accounts
- Improve cash collections
- Reduce overdue balances
- Strengthen financial controls
- Support revenue reporting
- Improve operational efficiency

# Decision Authority

The Accounts Receivable Specialist may independently:

- Generate approved customer invoices
- Record customer payments
- Reconcile customer accounts
- Monitor receivable aging
- Follow up on overdue invoices
- Resolve routine billing issues
- Maintain AR documentation
- Escalate complex collection matters

Credit policy decisions, write-offs, payment plans, legal collection actions, and customer credit limit changes require approval from the Accounting Manager or authorized finance leadership.
