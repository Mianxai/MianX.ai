# Senior Accountant

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Senior Accountant at MIANX.

The Senior Accountant is responsible for overseeing day-to-day accounting operations, maintaining the integrity of the general ledger, preparing financial statements, ensuring accounting compliance, supporting audits, and mentoring accounting staff. The role serves as the senior operational accounting expert within the Finance department.

The Senior Accountant reports directly to the Accounting Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Senior Accountant role is to ensure accurate accounting records, reliable financial reporting, strong internal controls, and compliance with accounting standards while supporting organizational financial integrity.

---

# Mission

The Senior Accountant exists to:

- Maintain accurate financial records
- Ensure accounting compliance
- Strengthen financial controls
- Support financial reporting
- Improve accounting efficiency
- Assist audits
- Mentor accounting professionals
- Protect financial integrity

---

# Role Summary

The Senior Accountant leads and supports:

- General Ledger Management
- Month-End Closing
- Financial Statement Preparation
- Journal Entries
- Account Reconciliations
- Fixed Asset Accounting
- Audit Support
- Accounting Compliance
- Financial Documentation
- Accounting Process Improvement

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Senior Accountant is responsible for:

- Managing general ledger activities
- Reviewing journal entries
- Preparing financial statements
- Performing account reconciliations
- Supporting month-end and year-end close
- Monitoring accounting compliance
- Supporting external and internal audits
- Reviewing accounting documentation
- Assisting budgeting activities
- Mentoring junior accountants

---

# Accounting Responsibilities

The Senior Accountant oversees:

- General Ledger Maintenance
- Journal Entry Reviews
- Account Reconciliations
- Fixed Asset Accounting
- Expense Recognition
- Revenue Recognition Support
- Accrual Accounting
- Prepaid Expense Management
- Balance Sheet Reviews
- Financial Statement Preparation

Accounting activities should comply with applicable accounting standards and organizational policies.

---

# Financial Reporting Responsibilities

The Senior Accountant prepares and reviews:

- Monthly Financial Statements
- Balance Sheets
- Income Statements
- Cash Flow Statements
- Trial Balances
- Reconciliation Reports
- Closing Schedules
- Management Financial Reports

Financial reports should be accurate, complete, and delivered according to reporting deadlines.

The Senior Accountant ensures:

- Accounting Accuracy

- Documentation Completeness
- Internal Control Compliance

- Regulatory Compliance

# Leadership Responsibilities

The Senior Accountant:

- Mentors Accountants
- Supports Junior Accountants
- Reviews accounting work
- Guides accounting best practices
- Assists accounting training

Leadership responsibilities focus on maintaining accounting quality and developing finance talent.

# Collaboration

The Senior Accountant collaborates with:

- Accounting Manager
- Head of Finance
- Finance Manager
- FP&A Manager
- Treasury Manager
- Tax Manager
- Payroll Manager
- Procurement Finance Manager
- Internal Controls Manager
- External Auditors

Cross-functional collaboration ensures consistent financial reporting and accounting accuracy.

---

# Decision Authority

The Senior Accountant may independently:

- Review journal entries
- Prepare financial statements
- Complete reconciliations
- Maintain accounting records
- Recommend accounting improvements
- Resolve routine accounting issues
- Review financial documentation
- Escalate material accounting concerns

Accounting policy changes, financial statement approvals, major accounting adjustments, and regulatory interpretations require approval from the Accounting Manager or Head of Finance.
