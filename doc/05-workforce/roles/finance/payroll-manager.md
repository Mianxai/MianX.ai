# Payroll Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Payroll Manager at MIANX.

The Payroll Manager is responsible for managing the organization's payroll operations, ensuring employees are paid accurately and on time, maintaining payroll compliance, administering payroll systems, coordinating with Human Resources and Finance, and safeguarding confidential compensation information.

The Payroll Manager reports directly to the Head of Finance.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Payroll Manager role is to ensure accurate, timely, compliant, and confidential payroll operations while supporting employee satisfaction and organizational financial integrity.

---

# Mission

The Payroll Manager exists to:

- Ensure payroll accuracy
- Process salaries on time
- Maintain payroll compliance
- Protect employee compensation data
- Improve payroll efficiency
- Reduce payroll errors
- Support employees
- Strengthen payroll governance

---

# Role Summary

The Payroll Manager leads:

- Payroll Processing
- Salary Administration
- Benefits Coordination
- Payroll Compliance
- Payroll Reporting
- Payroll Systems Administration
- Payroll Audits
- Compensation Records
- Payroll Process Improvement
- Payroll Team Management

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Payroll Manager is responsible for:

- Managing payroll operations
- Processing monthly payroll
- Reviewing salary calculations
- Managing employee compensation records
- Coordinating payroll with Human Resources
- Ensuring payroll tax compliance
- Managing payroll deductions
- Preparing payroll reports
- Supporting payroll audits
- Improving payroll procedures

---

# Payroll Responsibilities

The Payroll Manager oversees:

- Salary Processing
- Overtime Calculations
- Bonus Processing
- Incentive Payments
- Payroll Deductions
- Employee Benefits Administration Support
- Leave Adjustments
- Tax Withholding
- Payroll Reconciliation
- Payroll Documentation

Payroll activities should ensure employees receive accurate and timely compensation.

---

# Compliance Responsibilities

The Payroll Manager ensures:

- Payroll Policy Compliance
- Employment Law Compliance
- Tax Compliance
- Social Security Contributions
- Pension Contributions (where applicable)
- Statutory Reporting
- Payroll Record Retention
- Audit Readiness

Compliance activities should minimize payroll risk and regulatory exposure.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The Payroll Manager manages:

- Payroll Calendar
- Payroll Systems
- Salary Verification
- Employee Payment Processing
- Payroll Queries
- Payroll Documentation
- Payroll Reporting
- Payroll Process Improvements
- Payroll Controls
- Payroll Data Security

Operational excellence ensures reliable payroll operations across the organization.

---

# Leadership Responsibilities

The Payroll Manager leads:

- Payroll Specialists
- Payroll Coordinators
- Payroll Administrators
- Payroll Improvement Initiatives
- Payroll Quality Reviews

Leadership responsibilities include coaching payroll staff, maintaining operational excellence, and continuously improving payroll processes.

---

# Collaboration

The Payroll Manager collaborates with:

- Head of Finance
- Finance Manager
- Human Resources
- Accounting Manager
- Tax Manager
- Treasury Manager
- Operations
- Legal
- External Payroll Auditors
- Government Authorities

Cross-functional collaboration ensures payroll accuracy, regulatory compliance, and employee satisfaction.

---

# Decision Authority

The Payroll Manager may independently:

- Approve routine payroll processing
- Verify salary calculations
- Review payroll adjustments
- Approve payroll documentation
- Resolve payroll discrepancies
- Assign payroll work
- Recommend payroll process improvements
- Escalate payroll compliance issues

Salary structure changes, executive compensation, payroll policy modifications, and exceptional payroll adjustments require approval from the Head of Finance and Human Resources leadership.

---

# Core Competencies

The Payroll Manager should demonstrate expertise in:

- Payroll Administration
- Compensation Management
- Employment Law
- Payroll Taxation
- Payroll Systems
- Financial Controls
- Data Privacy
- Leadership
- Process Improvement
- Regulatory Compliance

# Performance Expectations

The Payroll Manager is expected to:

- Process payroll accurately
- Meet all payroll deadlines
- Maintain payroll confidentiality
- Ensure legal compliance
- Improve payroll efficiency
- Resolve payroll issues promptly
- Develop payroll professionals
- Continuously improve payroll operations
