# Tax Specialist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Tax Specialist at MIANX.

The Tax Specialist is responsible for preparing tax calculations, supporting tax compliance, maintaining tax records, assisting with tax filings, monitoring tax regulations, and ensuring the organization fulfills all tax obligations accurately and on time.

The Tax Specialist reports directly to the Tax Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Tax Specialist role is to ensure accurate tax reporting, timely tax compliance, proper documentation, and adherence to applicable tax laws while minimizing organizational tax risks.

---

# Mission

The Tax Specialist exists to:

- Ensure tax compliance
- Prepare accurate tax calculations
- Support regulatory reporting
- Maintain tax documentation
- Reduce tax risks
- Improve tax processes
- Support financial compliance
- Monitor regulatory changes

---

The Tax Specialist manages:

- Tax Calculations
- Tax Return Preparation
- Tax Documentation
- Regulatory Reporting

- Direct Tax Compliance

- Tax Record Maintenance
- Audit Support

The Tax Specialist is responsible for:

# Tax Responsibilities

The Tax Specialist performs:

- Corporate Tax Calculations
- VAT / GST Calculations
- Sales Tax Support
- Withholding Tax Processing
- Payroll Tax Support
- Tax Account Reconciliations
- Tax Payment Scheduling Support
- Tax Return Preparation
- Tax Documentation
- Regulatory Filing Support

Tax activities should comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and organizational policies.

Compliance activities help reduce financial, legal, and reputational risks.

# Decision Authority

The Tax Specialist may independently:

- Prepare tax calculations
- Maintain tax records
- Reconcile tax accounts
- Prepare tax schedules
- Research tax regulations
- Maintain tax documentation
- Recommend process improvements
- Escalate complex tax matters

Tax policy decisions, tax planning strategies, regulatory interpretations, tax settlements, and official tax filings require approval from the Tax Manager or Head of Finance.
