# Accounting Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Accounting Manager at MIANX.

The Accounting Manager is responsible for overseeing the organization's accounting operations, ensuring accurate financial records, maintaining compliance with accounting standards, supervising accounting staff, managing financial close processes, and supporting statutory reporting and audits.

The Accounting Manager reports directly to the Head of Finance.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Accounting Manager role is to ensure the integrity, accuracy, completeness, and compliance of all accounting operations while providing reliable financial information that supports organizational decision-making.

---

# Mission

The Accounting Manager exists to:

- Maintain accurate accounting records
- Ensure financial integrity
- Strengthen internal controls
- Deliver timely financial reporting
- Support regulatory compliance
- Improve accounting processes
- Develop accounting professionals
- Reduce financial risk

---

# Role Summary

The Accounting Manager leads:

- General Ledger Management
- Month-End Close
- Year-End Close
- Journal Entries
- Account Reconciliations
- Financial Statements
- Accounting Compliance
- Audit Coordination
- Accounting Team Management
- Accounting Process Improvement

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Accounting Manager is responsible for:

- Managing daily accounting operations
- Supervising accountants
- Reviewing journal entries
- Approving account reconciliations
- Coordinating month-end close
- Preparing financial statements
- Maintaining the general ledger
- Supporting internal and external audits
- Ensuring accounting policy compliance
- Improving accounting procedures

---

# Accounting Responsibilities

The Accounting Manager oversees:

- General Ledger
- Journal Entries
- Bank Reconciliations
- Fixed Asset Accounting
- Accrual Accounting
- Revenue Recognition
- Expense Recognition
- Financial Statement Preparation
- Closing Activities
- Accounting Documentation

Accounting activities should comply with applicable accounting standards and internal financial policies.

---

# Financial Reporting Responsibilities

The Accounting Manager ensures:

- Accurate Balance Sheets
- Accurate Income Statements
- Cash Flow Statements
- Monthly Financial Reports
- Quarterly Financial Reports
- Annual Financial Statements
- Supporting Schedules
- Regulatory Financial Reporting

Financial reports must be accurate, timely, and fully documented.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The Accounting Manager manages:

- Daily Accounting Operations
- Accounting Systems
- Financial Records
- Closing Schedules
- Internal Controls
- Audit Preparation
- Documentation Standards
- Accounting Workflows
- Record Retention
- Compliance Activities

Operational excellence supports organizational financial integrity.

---

# Leadership Responsibilities

The Accounting Manager leads:

- Senior Accountants
- Accountants
- Junior Accountants
- Accounts Payable Specialists
- Accounts Receivable Specialists

Leadership responsibilities include assigning work, mentoring accounting professionals, reviewing work quality, and improving departmental performance.

---

# Collaboration

The Accounting Manager collaborates with:

- Head of Finance
- Finance Manager
- FP&A Manager
- Treasury Manager
- Payroll Manager
- Tax Manager
- Procurement Team
- Human Resources
- Operations
- External Auditors

Cross-functional collaboration ensures complete and accurate financial reporting.

---

# Decision Authority

The Accounting Manager may independently:

- Approve journal entries
- Approve reconciliations
- Review accounting documentation
- Assign accounting work
- Recommend accounting improvements
- Monitor accounting controls
- Review financial schedules
- Escalate accounting risks

Changes to accounting policies, financial reporting frameworks, material adjustments, or significant accounting judgments require approval from the Head of Finance or Finance Director.

---

The Accounting Manager should demonstrate expertise in:

- Financial Accounting
- General Ledger Management
- Financial Reporting
- Reconciliations
- Internal Controls
- ERP Systems
- Accounting Standards
- Audit Management
- Leadership
- Analytical Thinking

The Accounting Manager is expected to:

- Maintain accurate accounting records

- Produce reliable financial statements
