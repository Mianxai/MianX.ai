# Finance Roles

> This directory defines the organizational structure, responsibilities, reporting hierarchy, and role documentation for the Finance Department at MIANX.

The Finance Department is responsible for safeguarding company assets, maintaining financial integrity, managing accounting operations, budgeting, forecasting, treasury, taxation, payroll, compliance, procurement support, and providing strategic financial guidance that enables sustainable business growth.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Finance Department is to ensure the financial stability, operational transparency, regulatory compliance, and long-term fiscal health of MIANX.

---

# Mission

The Finance organization exists to:

- Protect company assets
- Maintain accurate financial records
- Support strategic decision-making
- Ensure regulatory compliance
- Manage financial risks
- Optimize resource allocation
- Improve operational efficiency
- Enable sustainable growth

---

# Department Responsibilities

The Finance Department is responsible for:

- Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A)
- Accounting Operations
- Budget Management
- Treasury Management

- Financial Reporting
- Tax Management
- Payroll Administration
- Accounts Payable
- Accounts Receivable
- Procurement Financial Support
- Financial Compliance
- Internal Controls
- Financial Risk Management
- Audit Coordination
