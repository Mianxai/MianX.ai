# Tax Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Tax Manager at MIANX.

The Tax Manager is responsible for managing the organization's tax strategy, tax compliance, tax planning, statutory filings, tax reporting, regulatory coordination, and tax risk management. The role ensures MIANX meets all applicable tax obligations while optimizing tax efficiency within legal and ethical boundaries.

The Tax Manager reports directly to the Head of Finance.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Tax Manager role is to ensure complete tax compliance, minimize tax-related risks, optimize the organization's tax position, and maintain strong relationships with tax authorities.

---

# Mission

The Tax Manager exists to:

- Ensure tax compliance
- Minimize tax liabilities legally
- Reduce tax risks
- Deliver accurate tax reporting
- Maintain statutory compliance
- Support financial planning
- Improve tax processes
- Protect organizational reputation

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Tax Manager is responsible for:

- Preparing corporate tax returns
- Managing tax calculations
- Reviewing tax provisions
- Coordinating statutory filings
- Managing indirect taxes
- Supporting financial statement tax disclosures
- Monitoring tax law changes
- Coordinating tax audits
- Advising management on tax matters
- Improving tax processes

# Tax Responsibilities

The Tax Manager oversees:

- Corporate Income Tax
- Sales Tax / VAT / GST (where applicable)
- Withholding Taxes
- Payroll Tax Coordination
- Tax Estimates
- Tax Payments
- Transfer Pricing Support (where applicable)
- Tax Reconciliations
- Tax Documentation
- Tax Compliance Calendar

Tax activities must comply with applicable laws, regulations, and organizational governance standards.

# Decision Authority

The Tax Manager may independently:

- Review tax calculations
- Prepare statutory filings
- Approve routine tax documentation
- Coordinate tax payments
- Recommend tax planning opportunities
- Resolve routine tax matters
- Maintain tax compliance calendars
- Escalate material tax risks

Corporate tax strategy, significant tax positions, legal disputes, tax settlements, and major tax policy decisions require approval from the Head of Finance, Finance Director, or Chief Financial Officer.
