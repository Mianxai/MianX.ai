# Procurement Finance Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Procurement Finance Manager at MIANX.

The Procurement Finance Manager is responsible for managing the financial governance of procurement activities, overseeing purchasing budgets, validating procurement expenditures, monitoring vendor financial performance, ensuring procurement policy compliance, and optimizing procurement costs across the organization.

The Procurement Finance Manager reports directly to the Head of Finance.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Procurement Finance Manager role is to ensure financially responsible procurement operations by maintaining cost efficiency, strengthening financial controls, supporting strategic sourcing, and maximizing value from organizational purchasing.

---

# Mission

The Procurement Finance Manager exists to:

- Optimize procurement spending
- Strengthen purchasing governance
- Improve vendor financial performance
- Ensure procurement compliance
- Reduce procurement costs
- Support strategic sourcing
- Improve procurement reporting
- Protect organizational resources

# Decision Authority

The Procurement Finance Manager may independently:

- Review procurement budgets
- Validate purchase requests
- Review procurement reports
- Recommend cost-saving initiatives
- Approve procurement documentation
- Evaluate vendor financial performance
- Assign procurement finance work
- Escalate procurement risks

Major procurement contracts, strategic sourcing decisions, capital expenditures, and procurement policy changes require approval from the Head of Finance or Finance Director.
