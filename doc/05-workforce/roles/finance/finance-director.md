# Finance Director

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Finance Director at MIANX.

The Finance Director is responsible for directing the day-to-day financial operations of MIANX, executing departmental financial strategy, overseeing finance managers, ensuring accurate financial reporting, strengthening internal controls, managing budgeting activities, and supporting executive financial decision-making.

The Finance Director reports directly to the Vice President of Finance.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Finance Director role is to ensure efficient financial operations, maintain financial integrity, support strategic initiatives, and deliver reliable financial information that enables organizational success.

---

# Mission

The Finance Director exists to:

- Lead finance operations
- Deliver accurate financial reporting
- Improve budgeting processes
- Strengthen financial governance
- Ensure regulatory compliance
- Optimize operational efficiency
- Develop finance managers
- Support business growth

---

# Role Summary

The Finance Director leads:

- Finance Operations
- Budget Management
- Financial Planning & Analysis
- Accounting Operations
- Treasury Coordination
- Payroll Oversight
- Tax Coordination
- Financial Reporting
- Internal Controls
- Financial Systems
- Finance Leadership
- Operational Governance

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Finance Director is responsible for:

- Managing daily finance operations
- Supervising finance managers
- Reviewing departmental budgets
- Overseeing month-end and year-end financial close
- Monitoring financial performance
- Preparing executive financial reports
- Improving finance processes
- Supporting audits
- Ensuring compliance with financial policies
- Leading finance projects

---

# Strategic Responsibilities

The Finance Director supports:

- Financial Strategy Execution
- Budget Planning
- Financial Forecasting
- Cost Optimization
- Resource Allocation
- Operational Planning
- Business Performance Analysis
- Financial Transformation Initiatives

Strategic execution should align departmental performance with organizational financial objectives.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The Finance Director oversees:

- Budget Execution
- Accounting Operations

- Tax Coordination

- Financial Reporting

Operational excellence ensures efficient, accurate, and compliant financial management.

- Product

Strong cross-functional collaboration ensures financial alignment across all business functions.

---

# Decision Authority

The Finance Director may approve:

- Department Budgets (within delegated limits)
- Financial Reports
- Operational Finance Procedures
- Department Resource Allocation
- Financial Process Improvements
- Internal Finance Documentation
- Finance Team Performance Reviews
- Operational Spending Requests

Corporate financial strategy, capital investments, banking agreements, and major financial commitments require approval from the Vice President of Finance or Chief Financial Officer.

- Deliver accurate financial reporting
- Improve operational efficiency
- Maintain budget discipline
- Strengthen financial controls
