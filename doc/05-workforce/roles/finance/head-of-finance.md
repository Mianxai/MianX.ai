# Head of Finance

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Head of Finance at MIANX.

The Head of Finance is responsible for managing the daily operations of the Finance Department, supervising finance managers and specialists, ensuring financial accuracy, maintaining compliance, executing approved financial strategies, and supporting business units with financial guidance.

The Head of Finance reports directly to the Finance Director.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Head of Finance role is to ensure efficient, accurate, compliant, and well-controlled financial operations while supporting organizational growth through sound financial management.

---

# Mission

The Head of Finance exists to:

- Deliver operational financial excellence
- Maintain financial accuracy
- Support business decision-making
- Improve financial processes
- Strengthen internal controls
- Ensure regulatory compliance
- Develop finance professionals
- Optimize organizational performance

---

# Role Summary

The Head of Finance manages:

- Finance Operations
- Financial Planning Execution
- Budget Monitoring
- Accounting Coordination
- Treasury Coordination
- Payroll Oversight
- Tax Administration
- Financial Reporting
- Internal Controls
- Finance Team Management
- Financial Systems
- Process Improvement

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Head of Finance is responsible for:

- Supervising finance managers and specialists
- Monitoring departmental budgets
- Reviewing financial reports
- Coordinating month-end and year-end closing
- Managing financial operations
- Supporting forecasting activities
- Improving finance workflows
- Monitoring regulatory compliance
- Coordinating internal and external audits
- Providing financial guidance to department leaders

---

# Financial Management Responsibilities

The Head of Finance oversees:

- Budget Execution
- Expense Monitoring
- Revenue Tracking
- Cash Flow Monitoring
- Financial Performance Reviews
- Financial Reporting
- Department Financial Support
- Resource Planning

Financial management activities should ensure responsible use of organizational resources.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The Head of Finance manages:

- Accounting Operations
- Treasury Activities
- Payroll Processing
- Tax Coordination
- Accounts Payable
- Accounts Receivable
- Financial Documentation
- ERP & Finance Systems
- Internal Financial Controls
- Finance Administration

Operational excellence ensures consistent, reliable, and compliant finance operations.

---

# Leadership Responsibilities

The Head of Finance leads:

- Finance Manager
- FP&A Manager
- Accounting Manager
- Treasury Manager
- Payroll Manager
- Tax Manager
- Procurement Finance Manager
- Internal Controls Manager
- Finance Analysts
- Accounting Specialists

Leadership responsibilities include coaching managers, improving operational performance, assigning priorities, and ensuring alignment with departmental objectives.

---

# Collaboration

The Head of Finance collaborates with:

- Finance Director
- Vice President of Finance
- Chief Financial Officer
- Human Resources
- Engineering
- Product
- Design
- Marketing
- Sales
- Operations
- Procurement
- Legal
- External Auditors

Strong collaboration ensures financial transparency and organizational alignment.

---

# Decision Authority

The Head of Finance may approve:

- Operational Finance Activities
- Department Expense Requests (within delegated authority)
- Budget Adjustments (within approved limits)
- Financial Reports
- Finance Procedures
- Workflow Improvements
- Team Work Assignments
- Operational Documentation

Strategic financial decisions, capital investments, financial policy changes, banking agreements, and major expenditures require approval from Finance Director or higher authority.

---

# Core Competencies

The Head of Finance should demonstrate expertise in:

- Financial Management
- Accounting
- Budget Administration
- Financial Reporting
- Treasury Fundamentals
- Payroll Administration
- Tax Compliance
- Leadership
- ERP & Finance Systems
- Regulatory Compliance

# Performance Expectations

The Head of Finance is expected to:

- Deliver accurate financial operations
- Maintain compliance with financial regulations
- Improve financial efficiency
- Ensure timely reporting
- Strengthen operational controls
- Develop finance personnel
- Support organizational planning
- Drive continuous improvement initiatives
