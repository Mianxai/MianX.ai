# FP&A Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) Manager at MIANX.

The FP&A Manager is responsible for leading the organization's financial planning, budgeting, forecasting, financial modeling, business performance analysis, and executive reporting. The role provides strategic financial insights that support executive decision-making and long-term organizational growth.

The FP&A Manager reports directly to the Head of Finance.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the FP&A Manager role is to transform financial and operational data into strategic insights that improve planning, resource allocation, profitability, and business performance.

---

# Mission

The FP&A Manager exists to:

- Lead financial planning
- Improve budgeting accuracy
- Deliver reliable forecasts
- Support executive decision-making
- Analyze business performance
- Optimize financial resources
- Improve profitability
- Enable sustainable growth

---

# Role Summary

The FP&A Manager leads:

- Annual Budget Planning
- Financial Forecasting
- Financial Modeling
- Business Performance Analysis
- Executive Reporting
- KPI Reporting
- Scenario Planning
- Cost Analysis
- Revenue Analysis
- Strategic Financial Support

# Primary Responsibilities

The FP&A Manager is responsible for:

- Preparing annual operating budgets
- Developing rolling financial forecasts
- Building financial models
- Monitoring business performance
- Preparing executive dashboards
- Supporting strategic initiatives
- Conducting profitability analysis
- Analyzing revenue and expenses
- Identifying financial risks
- Recommending financial improvements

- Strategic Financial Modeling

Financial planning activities should support organizational strategy and sustainable growth.

# Decision Authority

The FP&A Manager may independently:

- Prepare financial forecasts
- Develop financial models
- Recommend budget allocations
- Approve planning methodologies
- Publish internal analytical reports
- Recommend performance improvements
- Monitor departmental budgets
- Escalate financial risks

Budget approvals, corporate financial strategy, capital allocation decisions, and investment approvals require authorization from Finance leadership.
