# Internal Controls Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Internal Controls Manager at MIANX.

The Internal Controls Manager is responsible for designing, implementing, monitoring, and continuously improving the organization's internal control framework. The role ensures financial integrity, operational efficiency, regulatory compliance, fraud prevention, and effective risk mitigation across all business functions.

The Internal Controls Manager reports directly to the Head of Finance.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Internal Controls Manager role is to establish and maintain an effective internal control environment that protects organizational assets, supports regulatory compliance, reduces operational risk, and strengthens corporate governance.

---

# Mission

The Internal Controls Manager exists to:

- Strengthen internal controls
- Reduce organizational risk
- Protect company assets
- Improve governance
- Ensure policy compliance
- Prevent fraud
- Support audit readiness
- Promote continuous improvement

---

# Role Summary

The Internal Controls Manager leads:

- Internal Control Framework
- Risk Control Assessments
- Financial Control Reviews
- Operational Control Reviews
- Compliance Monitoring
- Internal Policy Enforcement
- Process Control Improvements
- Fraud Prevention Initiatives
- Audit Coordination
- Control Documentation

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Internal Controls Manager is responsible for:

- Designing internal control frameworks
- Assessing financial and operational risks
- Evaluating business processes
- Reviewing internal controls
- Monitoring policy compliance
- Supporting internal and external audits
- Investigating control deficiencies
- Developing corrective action plans
- Maintaining control documentation
- Improving governance processes

---

# Internal Control Responsibilities

The Internal Controls Manager oversees:

- Financial Controls
- Operational Controls
- IT Financial Controls
- Segregation of Duties
- Approval Controls
- Documentation Standards
- Control Testing
- Risk Assessments
- Compliance Monitoring
- Corrective Action Tracking

Internal controls should provide reasonable assurance regarding operational effectiveness, financial reporting accuracy, and regulatory compliance.

---

# Risk Management Responsibilities

The Internal Controls Manager manages:

- Control Risk Assessments
- Process Risk Reviews
- Fraud Risk Assessments
- Control Gap Analysis
- Risk Mitigation Planning
- Control Monitoring
- Exception Reporting
- Risk Documentation

Risk management activities should proactively identify weaknesses before they become significant organizational issues.

Cross-functional collaboration ensures consistent implementation of internal controls throughout the organization.

---

# Decision Authority

The Internal Controls Manager may independently:

- Perform internal control reviews
- Conduct control testing
- Recommend control improvements
- Review compliance documentation
- Assign internal control activities
- Issue control findings
- Monitor corrective actions
- Escalate significant control deficiencies

Enterprise risk policies, governance framework changes, major control exceptions, and organization-wide compliance initiatives require approval from the Head of Finance or Finance Director.
