# Chief Financial Officer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Chief Financial Officer (CFO) at MIANX.

The Chief Financial Officer (CFO) is responsible for the overall financial strategy, governance, fiscal health, regulatory compliance, capital management, budgeting, financial planning, accounting, treasury, taxation, risk management, and long-term financial sustainability of MIANX.

The Chief Financial Officer reports directly to the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) and serves as a member of the Executive Leadership Team.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Chief Financial Officer role is to ensure the financial stability, profitability, transparency, compliance, and long-term sustainability of MIANX while enabling strategic business growth through sound financial leadership.

---

# Mission

The Chief Financial Officer exists to:

- Protect company assets
- Maximize shareholder value
- Drive financial strategy
- Maintain fiscal discipline
- Ensure regulatory compliance
- Optimize capital allocation
- Improve profitability
- Enable sustainable business growth

---

# Role Summary

The Chief Financial Officer leads:

- Financial Strategy
- Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A)
- Accounting
- Treasury
- Budget Management
- Cash Flow Management
- Tax Strategy
- Payroll Governance
- Financial Reporting
- Audit & Compliance
- Internal Controls
- Enterprise Risk Management
- Investor & Banking Relations
- Procurement Financial Governance

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Chief Financial Officer is responsible for:

- Defining corporate financial strategy
- Leading financial planning and forecasting
- Approving annual operating budgets
- Managing capital allocation
- Maintaining liquidity and cash flow
- Overseeing accounting operations
- Ensuring financial reporting accuracy
- Leading enterprise financial risk management
- Maintaining regulatory compliance
- Advising executive leadership on financial decisions

---

# Strategic Responsibilities

The Chief Financial Officer develops:

- Long-term Financial Strategy
- Capital Investment Strategy

- Profitability Strategy
- Cost Optimization Strategy
- Financial Risk Strategy
- Corporate Budget Framework
- Enterprise Financial Governance

Strategic financial decisions should support sustainable organizational growth and long-term value creation.

# Decision Authority

The Chief Financial Officer has authority to approve:

- Financial Strategy
- Annual Budgets
- Capital Allocation
- Financial Policies
- Treasury Decisions
- Investment Recommendations
- Financial Risk Controls
- Banking Relationships
- Audit Plans
- Executive Financial Reports

The Chief Financial Officer is expected to:

- Deliver accurate financial reporting
