# Vice President of Finance

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Vice President of Finance at MIANX.

The Vice President of Finance is responsible for executing the financial strategy established by the Chief Financial Officer, overseeing finance operations, leading finance leadership teams, improving financial performance, ensuring regulatory compliance, and maintaining efficient financial processes across the organization.

The Vice President of Finance reports directly to the Chief Financial Officer (CFO).

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Vice President of Finance role is to transform financial strategy into operational execution while ensuring financial accuracy, governance, compliance, and organizational efficiency.

---

# Mission

The Vice President of Finance exists to:

- Execute financial strategy
- Improve operational efficiency
- Strengthen financial governance
- Support business growth
- Maintain financial integrity
- Optimize financial operations
- Reduce organizational risk
- Develop finance leadership

---

The Vice President of Finance leads:

# Primary Responsibilities

The Vice President of Finance is responsible for:

- Executing financial strategy
- Managing finance operations

- Reviewing departmental budgets
- Overseeing financial reporting
- Improving operational efficiency
- Supporting executive decision-making
- Managing financial compliance
- Monitoring organizational financial performance
- Driving continuous improvement initiatives

Cross-functional collaboration ensures financial alignment across the organization.

---

# Decision Authority

The Vice President of Finance may approve:

- Department Budgets
- Financial Reports
- Financial Policies (within delegated authority)
- Resource Allocation
- Operational Spending
- Finance Process Improvements
- Internal Finance Procedures
- Financial Performance Reviews

Corporate financial strategy, capital structure, investment decisions, mergers, acquisitions, and major financial commitments remain under the authority of the Chief Financial Officer and Executive Leadership.
