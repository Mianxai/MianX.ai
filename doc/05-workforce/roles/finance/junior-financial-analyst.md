# Junior Financial Analyst

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Junior Financial Analyst at MIANX.

The Junior Financial Analyst is responsible for supporting financial reporting, budgeting, forecasting, data analysis, and financial modeling activities. The role assists senior finance professionals by preparing accurate financial information, maintaining financial records, and contributing to analytical projects that support business decision-making.

The Junior Financial Analyst reports directly to the Senior Financial Analyst or FP&A Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Junior Financial Analyst role is to provide reliable financial analysis support, maintain accurate financial data, and develop analytical expertise while contributing to informed financial decision-making across the organization.

---

# Mission

The Junior Financial Analyst exists to:

- Support financial analysis
- Maintain accurate financial data
- Assist budgeting activities
- Improve reporting quality
- Learn financial planning
- Develop analytical skills
- Support business decisions
- Continuously improve finance operations

---

# Role Summary

The Junior Financial Analyst supports:

- Financial Reporting
- Budget Preparation
- Forecast Support
- KPI Reporting
- Variance Analysis
- Financial Data Management
- Spreadsheet Modeling

- Documentation

# Primary Responsibilities

The Junior Financial Analyst is responsible for:

- Preparing financial reports
- Collecting financial data
- Updating financial models
- Assisting budget preparation
- Supporting forecasting activities
- Monitoring KPIs
- Performing basic variance analysis
- Maintaining financial records
- Preparing management reports
- Supporting finance projects

---

# Financial Analysis Responsibilities

The Junior Financial Analyst assists with:

- Revenue Analysis
- Expense Analysis
- Cost Analysis
- Budget Analysis
- Variance Analysis
- Trend Analysis
- KPI Monitoring
- Forecast Support
- Financial Data Validation
- Performance Reporting

Financial analyses should be accurate, timely, and completed according to organizational standards.

# Planning Responsibilities

The Junior Financial Analyst supports:

- Annual Budget Preparation

- Budget Data Collection

- Financial Research
- Planning Documentation

Planning activities help improve financial forecasting and organizational planning accuracy.

- Analytical Working Papers

Reports should be complete, accurate, and submitted according to reporting schedules.

# Collaboration

- Payroll Team

- Department Managers
- Operations

# Decision Authority

The Junior Financial Analyst may independently:

- Prepare assigned reports
- Perform financial calculations
- Update financial models
- Collect financial data
- Maintain reporting documentation
- Identify reporting discrepancies
- Support analytical projects
- Escalate data quality issues

Budget approvals, executive reports, strategic financial recommendations, and policy decisions require review and approval from the Senior Financial Analyst or FP&A Manager.
