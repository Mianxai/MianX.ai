# Payroll Specialist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Payroll Specialist at MIANX.

The Payroll Specialist is responsible for processing employee payroll, maintaining payroll records, ensuring compliance with payroll laws and company policies, calculating salaries and deductions, managing payroll documentation, and supporting accurate and timely employee compensation.

The Payroll Specialist reports directly to the Payroll Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Payroll Specialist role is to ensure employees are compensated accurately and on time while maintaining compliance with payroll regulations, protecting confidential payroll information, and supporting efficient payroll operations.

---

# Mission

The Payroll Specialist exists to:

- Process payroll accurately
- Ensure timely salary payments
- Maintain payroll compliance
- Protect payroll confidentiality
- Support employee payroll needs
- Maintain payroll records
- Improve payroll efficiency
- Support financial accuracy

---

# Role Summary

The Payroll Specialist manages:

- Payroll Processing
- Salary Calculations
- Payroll Adjustments
- Employee Deductions
- Payroll Documentation
- Tax Withholding Support
- Benefits Deductions
- Time & Attendance Verification
- Payroll Reporting

# Primary Responsibilities

The Payroll Specialist is responsible for:

- Processing payroll cycles
- Calculating salaries and wages
- Verifying attendance and timesheets
- Applying payroll deductions
- Maintaining payroll records
- Preparing payroll reports
- Processing bonuses and overtime
- Resolving payroll discrepancies
- Supporting payroll audits
- Assisting employees with payroll inquiries

- Leave Payment Calculations

Payroll activities should ensure employees receive accurate and timely compensation every payroll cycle.

---

# Decision Authority

The Payroll Specialist may independently:

- Process approved payroll
- Calculate salaries and deductions
- Update payroll records
- Resolve routine payroll discrepancies
- Prepare payroll reports
- Maintain payroll documentation
- Support employee payroll requests
- Escalate payroll exceptions

Salary structure changes, compensation policy updates, payroll approvals, statutory interpretations, and executive payroll matters require approval from the Payroll Manager or Head of Finance.
