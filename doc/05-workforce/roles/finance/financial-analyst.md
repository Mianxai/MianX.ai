# Financial Analyst

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Financial Analyst at MIANX.

The Financial Analyst is responsible for preparing financial analyses, supporting budgeting and forecasting activities, monitoring organizational performance, producing management reports, maintaining financial models, and assisting decision-makers with accurate financial insights.

The Financial Analyst reports directly to the FP&A Manager or Senior Financial Analyst.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Financial Analyst role is to provide accurate financial information, meaningful business analysis, and performance reporting that supports operational efficiency, strategic planning, and sound financial decision-making.

---

# Mission

The Financial Analyst exists to:

- Deliver accurate financial analysis
- Support budgeting activities
- Improve reporting quality
- Assist forecasting
- Monitor business performance
- Identify financial trends
- Support strategic decisions
- Improve financial processes

---

# Role Summary

The Financial Analyst supports:

- Financial Analysis
- Budget Preparation
- Financial Forecasting
- KPI Reporting
- Variance Analysis
- Financial Modeling
- Business Performance Analysis
- Cost Analysis
- Profitability Analysis
- Executive Reporting

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Financial Analyst is responsible for:

- Preparing financial reports
- Performing variance analysis
- Assisting budget preparation
- Supporting forecasting activities
- Monitoring financial KPIs
- Building financial models
- Analyzing business performance
- Preparing management presentations
- Maintaining financial databases
- Supporting strategic projects

---

# Financial Analysis Responsibilities

The Financial Analyst performs:

- Revenue Analysis
- Expense Analysis
- Cost Analysis
- Margin Analysis
- Profitability Analysis
- Department Performance Analysis
- Trend Analysis
- Budget Performance Analysis
- Forecast Comparisons
- Investment Support Analysis

Financial analyses should provide accurate, timely, and actionable insights.

---

# Budgeting & Forecasting Responsibilities

The Financial Analyst supports:

- Annual Budget Preparation
- Quarterly Forecasts
- Monthly Forecast Updates
- Department Budget Reviews
- Budget Variance Analysis
- Financial Planning Activities
- Scenario Analysis
- Resource Allocation Analysis

Budgeting activities should support efficient allocation of organizational resources.

---

# Reporting Responsibilities

The Financial Analyst prepares:

- Monthly Financial Reports
- KPI Dashboards
- Budget Reports
- Forecast Reports
- Variance Reports
- Department Performance Reports
- Cost Analysis Reports
- Executive Financial Summaries

Reports should be timely, accurate, and aligned with organizational reporting standards.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The Financial Analyst ensures:

- Financial Data Accuracy
- Reporting Consistency
- Model Maintenance
- Documentation Quality
- Data Validation
- Process Compliance
- Reporting Timeliness
- Continuous Improvement

Operational excellence improves the quality and reliability of financial information.

---

# Collaboration

The Financial Analyst collaborates with:

- FP&A Manager
- Senior Financial Analyst
- Finance Manager
- Accounting Manager
- Treasury Manager
- Tax Manager
- Department Managers
- Sales
- Marketing
- Operations
- Human Resources

Cross-functional collaboration ensures financial reporting reflects actual business performance.

---

# Decision Authority

The Financial Analyst may independently:

- Prepare analytical reports
- Perform financial calculations
- Maintain financial models
- Analyze operational performance
- Prepare variance analyses
- Recommend reporting improvements
- Support planning activities
- Escalate financial concerns

Budget approvals, strategic financial decisions, accounting policy changes, and executive reporting approvals require approval from the Senior Financial Analyst or FP&A Manager.

- Financial Analysis

- Financial Reporting
- Spreadsheet Modeling
