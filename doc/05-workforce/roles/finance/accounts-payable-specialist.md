# Accounts Payable Specialist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Accounts Payable Specialist at MIANX.

The Accounts Payable Specialist is responsible for processing supplier invoices, maintaining vendor accounts, scheduling payments, reconciling accounts payable records, ensuring timely payment of organizational obligations, and maintaining accurate financial documentation.

The Accounts Payable Specialist reports directly to the Accounting Manager or Senior Accountant.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Accounts Payable Specialist role is to ensure accurate, timely, and compliant processing of supplier invoices and payments while maintaining strong vendor relationships and protecting the organization's financial integrity.

---

# Mission

The Accounts Payable Specialist exists to:

- Process supplier invoices accurately
- Ensure timely vendor payments
- Maintain accurate payable records
- Support financial compliance
- Strengthen vendor relationships
- Improve payment efficiency
- Protect financial controls
- Support accounting operations

---

The Accounts Payable Specialist manages:

- Invoice Processing

- Expense Verification

# Primary Responsibilities

The Accounts Payable Specialist is responsible for:

- Reviewing supplier invoices
- Verifying purchase documentation
- Matching invoices with purchase orders
- Processing approved invoices
- Preparing payment schedules
- Reconciling vendor statements
- Maintaining vendor records
- Resolving invoice discrepancies
- Supporting month-end closing
- Assisting financial audits

---

# Accounts Payable Responsibilities

The Accounts Payable Specialist performs:

- Invoice Verification
- Three-Way Matching (PO, Invoice, Receipt)
- Vendor Invoice Entry
- Payment Processing
- Vendor Statement Reconciliation
- Credit Note Processing
- Expense Classification Support
- Vendor Record Maintenance
- Outstanding Payables Monitoring
- Payment Documentation

Accounts payable activities should ensure timely, accurate, and compliant payment processing.

# Decision Authority

The Accounts Payable Specialist may independently:

- Process approved invoices
- Maintain vendor records
- Reconcile vendor accounts
- Schedule approved payments
- Resolve routine invoice discrepancies
- Maintain payment documentation
- Recommend AP process improvements
- Escalate payment exceptions

Payment approvals, vendor contract decisions, payment policy changes, and high-value transactions require approval from the Senior Accountant, Accounting Manager, or authorized financial approvers.
