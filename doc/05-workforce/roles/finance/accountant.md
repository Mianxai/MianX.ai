# Accountant

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Accountant at MIANX.

The Accountant is responsible for maintaining accurate financial records, processing accounting transactions, preparing journal entries, performing reconciliations, supporting financial reporting, and ensuring compliance with accounting policies and regulatory requirements.

The Accountant reports directly to the Senior Accountant or Accounting Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Accountant role is to ensure accurate day-to-day accounting operations, maintain reliable financial records, support financial reporting, and contribute to the organization's financial integrity.

---

# Mission

The Accountant exists to:

- Maintain accurate accounting records
- Process financial transactions
- Support financial reporting
- Ensure accounting compliance
- Improve accounting efficiency
- Assist financial close
- Strengthen financial controls
- Support organizational decision-making

---

# Role Summary

The Accountant supports:

- General Ledger Accounting
- Journal Entry Processing
- Account Reconciliation
- Financial Reporting
- Month-End Closing
- Fixed Asset Accounting
- Expense Recording
- Revenue Recording
- Audit Support
- Accounting Documentation

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Accountant is responsible for:

- Recording financial transactions
- Preparing journal entries
- Maintaining the general ledger
- Reconciling accounts
- Supporting month-end closing
- Assisting financial statement preparation
- Maintaining accounting documentation
- Supporting internal and external audits
- Monitoring accounting accuracy
- Assisting process improvements

---

# Accounting Responsibilities

The Accountant performs:

- General Ledger Posting
- Journal Entry Preparation
- Bank Reconciliations
- Account Reconciliations
- Fixed Asset Recording
- Expense Classification
- Revenue Recognition Support
- Accrual Entries
- Prepaid Expense Tracking
- Financial Record Maintenance

Accounting activities must comply with accounting standards, company policies, and financial controls.

---

# Financial Reporting Responsibilities

The Accountant supports preparation of:

- Monthly Financial Statements
- Trial Balances
- Balance Sheets
- Income Statements
- Cash Flow Reports
- Reconciliation Reports
- Closing Schedules
- Management Reports

Financial reports should be complete, accurate, and submitted according to established reporting deadlines.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The Accountant ensures:

- Transaction Accuracy
- Ledger Integrity
- Documentation Completeness
- Timely Processing
- Financial Data Accuracy
- Internal Control Compliance
- ERP Data Maintenance
- Continuous Process Improvement

Operational excellence ensures reliable accounting operations across the organization.

---

# Collaboration

The Accountant collaborates with:

- Senior Accountant
- Accounting Manager
- Finance Manager
- FP&A Team
- Treasury Team
- Payroll Team
- Tax Team
- Procurement Finance Team
- Internal Controls Team
- External Auditors

Cross-functional collaboration ensures accounting records remain accurate and aligned with business operations.

---

# Decision Authority

The Accountant may independently:

- Record routine accounting transactions
- Prepare journal entries
- Complete account reconciliations
- Maintain accounting documentation
- Prepare standard accounting reports
- Resolve routine accounting discrepancies
- Recommend accounting process improvements
- Escalate complex accounting issues

Accounting policy decisions, financial statement approvals, material accounting adjustments, and regulatory interpretations require approval from the Senior Accountant or Accounting Manager.

---

# Core Competencies

The Accountant should demonstrate proficiency in:

- Financial Accounting
- Bookkeeping
- General Ledger Accounting
- Account Reconciliation
- Financial Reporting
- ERP Systems
- Spreadsheet Applications
- Internal Controls
- Attention to Detail
- Time Management

---

# Performance Expectations

The Accountant is expected to:

- Maintain accurate accounting records
- Complete assigned work on schedule
- Support timely financial closing
- Ensure documentation quality
- Follow accounting standards
- Assist successful audits
- Collaborate effectively with finance teams
- Continuously improve accounting skills
