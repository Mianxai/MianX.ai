# Senior Financial Analyst

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Senior Financial Analyst at MIANX.

The Senior Financial Analyst is responsible for delivering advanced financial analysis, forecasting, budgeting support, financial modeling, business performance reporting, and strategic insights that enable executive and departmental decision-making. The role serves as the senior analytical resource within the Finance department and mentors junior analysts.

The Senior Financial Analyst reports directly to the FP&A Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Senior Financial Analyst role is to provide accurate financial analysis, actionable business insights, and strategic recommendations that improve financial performance and support sustainable organizational growth.

---

# Mission

The Senior Financial Analyst exists to:

- Deliver financial insights
- Improve forecasting accuracy
- Support budgeting
- Build financial models
- Analyze business performance
- Assist strategic planning
- Mentor analysts
- Improve financial decision-making

---

# Role Summary

The Senior Financial Analyst leads and supports:

- Financial Analysis
- Budget Planning
- Forecasting
- Financial Modeling
- KPI Reporting
- Variance Analysis

- Executive Reporting

# Primary Responsibilities

The Senior Financial Analyst is responsible for:

- Preparing financial analyses
- Building financial models
- Supporting annual budgets
- Preparing rolling forecasts
- Monitoring KPIs
- Performing variance analysis
- Preparing executive reports
- Identifying financial trends
- Supporting strategic initiatives
- Mentoring junior analysts

# Financial Analysis Responsibilities

The Senior Financial Analyst performs:

- Revenue Analysis
- Cost Analysis
- Margin Analysis
- Profitability Analysis
- Budget Analysis
- Forecast Analysis
- Capital Investment Analysis
- Business Case Evaluation
- Scenario Modeling
- Trend Analysis

Financial analyses should provide timely, accurate, and actionable recommendations.

---

# Planning Responsibilities

The Senior Financial Analyst supports:

- Annual Budget Development
- Quarterly Forecasts
- Monthly Forecast Updates
- Strategic Planning
- Resource Allocation Analysis
- Financial Scenario Planning
- Department Budget Reviews
- Executive Planning Sessions

Planning activities should align financial resources with organizational objectives.

The Senior Financial Analyst prepares:

- Monthly Financial Reports
- Executive Dashboards
- KPI Reports
- Variance Reports
- Forecast Reports
- Budget Performance Reports

Reports should be accurate, timely, and clearly communicate financial performance.

# Leadership Responsibilities

The Senior Financial Analyst:

- Supports Junior Financial Analysts

- Guides financial modeling standards
- Assists with analyst training
- Promotes analytical best practices

Leadership responsibilities focus on developing analytical capabilities within the Finance team.

- Treasury Manager
- Tax Manager

- Marketing
- Operations

Cross-functional collaboration ensures financial analysis reflects operational realities and strategic priorities.

---

# Decision Authority

The Senior Financial Analyst may independently:

- Prepare financial analyses
- Build financial models
- Publish analytical reports
- Recommend financial improvements
- Perform variance investigations
- Review departmental financial performance
- Support planning activities
- Escalate financial concerns

Budget approvals, financial policy decisions, strategic investments, and executive financial commitments require approval from the FP&A Manager or Head of Finance.
