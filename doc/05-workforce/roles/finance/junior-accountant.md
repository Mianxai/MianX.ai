# Junior Accountant

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Junior Accountant at MIANX.

The Junior Accountant is responsible for supporting daily accounting operations, recording financial transactions, maintaining accounting records, assisting with reconciliations, preparing supporting documentation, and contributing to accurate financial reporting while developing professional accounting expertise.

The Junior Accountant reports directly to the Senior Accountant or Accounting Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Junior Accountant role is to provide dependable operational accounting support, maintain accurate financial records, assist accounting processes, and develop the knowledge and experience required for long-term success within the Finance department.

---

# Mission

The Junior Accountant exists to:

- Maintain accurate accounting records
- Support daily accounting operations
- Learn accounting best practices
- Assist financial reporting
- Strengthen financial controls
- Ensure documentation accuracy
- Support compliance
- Continuously develop professionally

---

# Role Summary

The Junior Accountant supports:

- Bookkeeping
- General Ledger Maintenance
- Journal Entry Preparation
- Account Reconciliations
- Financial Documentation
- Expense Recording
- Revenue Recording
- Month-End Closing Support
- Audit Preparation
- Financial Data Management

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Junior Accountant is responsible for:

- Recording daily financial transactions
- Preparing routine journal entries
- Maintaining accounting records
- Assisting with account reconciliations
- Organizing accounting documentation
- Supporting month-end closing activities
- Assisting financial statement preparation
- Maintaining financial spreadsheets
- Supporting audit requests
- Performing other accounting duties as assigned

---

# Accounting Responsibilities

The Junior Accountant assists with:

- General Ledger Posting
- Journal Entry Preparation
- Bank Reconciliations
- Expense Recording
- Revenue Recording
- Fixed Asset Records
- Vendor Transaction Recording
- Customer Transaction Recording
- Supporting Schedules
- Financial Record Maintenance

Accounting activities must comply with company policies, accounting standards, and internal controls.

---

# Financial Reporting Responsibilities

The Junior Accountant supports preparation of:

- Monthly Financial Reports
- Trial Balances
- Account Reconciliation Reports
- Expense Reports
- Revenue Reports
- Supporting Financial Schedules
- Audit Documentation
- Financial Working Papers

Financial information should be accurate, complete, and delivered according to reporting schedules.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The Junior Accountant ensures:

- Accurate Transaction Processing
- Timely Record Updates
- Complete Documentation
- Financial Data Integrity
- Compliance with Procedures
- ERP Data Accuracy
- Confidentiality of Financial Information
- Continuous Learning

Operational excellence ensures reliable day-to-day accounting support.

---

# Collaboration

The Junior Accountant collaborates with:

- Senior Accountant
- Accounting Manager
- Finance Manager
- FP&A Team
- Treasury Team
- Payroll Team
- Tax Team
- Accounts Payable Team
- Accounts Receivable Team
- External Auditors (as required)

Cross-functional collaboration helps maintain accurate accounting records and efficient financial operations.

---

# Decision Authority

The Junior Accountant may independently:

- Record routine accounting transactions
- Prepare standard journal entries
- Update accounting records
- Maintain financial documentation
- Assist reconciliations
- Prepare routine accounting schedules
- Identify data discrepancies
- Escalate accounting issues requiring review

Financial statement approvals, accounting policy decisions, significant adjustments, and regulatory matters require approval from the Senior Accountant or Accounting Manager.

---

# Core Competencies

The Junior Accountant should demonstrate knowledge of:

- Accounting Fundamentals
- Bookkeeping
- General Ledger Basics
- Financial Reporting
- Spreadsheet Applications
- ERP Systems
- Data Entry Accuracy
- Attention to Detail
- Organization
- Professional Communication

# Performance Expectations

The Junior Accountant is expected to:

- Maintain accurate accounting records
- Meet assigned deadlines
- Follow accounting procedures
- Produce high-quality documentation
- Support timely financial close
- Learn accounting standards
- Collaborate effectively
- Continuously improve accounting skills
