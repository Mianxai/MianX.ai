# Finance Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Finance Manager at MIANX.

The Finance Manager is responsible for managing the day-to-day financial activities of the organization, supervising finance professionals, ensuring accurate financial reporting, maintaining budget control, supporting forecasting activities, and ensuring compliance with financial policies and regulatory requirements.

The Finance Manager reports directly to the Head of Finance.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Finance Manager role is to ensure efficient financial operations, maintain accurate financial records, support strategic financial planning, and deliver reliable financial information for business decision-making.

---

# Mission

The Finance Manager exists to:

- Manage finance operations
- Ensure financial accuracy
- Support budgeting processes
- Improve financial performance
- Maintain compliance
- Strengthen internal controls
- Develop finance team members
- Enable informed business decisions

---

# Role Summary

The Finance Manager manages:

- Finance Operations
- Budget Administration
- Financial Reporting
- Expense Management
- Revenue Monitoring
- Cash Flow Support
- Financial Compliance
- Team Supervision
- Internal Controls
- Process Improvement

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Finance Manager is responsible for:

- Supervising finance staff
- Monitoring daily financial activities
- Preparing monthly financial reports
- Managing departmental budgets
- Reviewing financial transactions
- Supporting forecasting and planning
- Monitoring expenses and revenues
- Ensuring compliance with finance policies
- Coordinating financial audits
- Improving finance processes

---

# Financial Management Responsibilities

The Finance Manager oversees:

- Budget Monitoring
- Expense Control
- Financial Analysis
- Revenue Tracking
- Cost Management
- Financial Reporting
- Cash Flow Monitoring
- Financial Performance Reviews

Financial management activities should support operational efficiency and organizational profitability.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The Finance Manager ensures:

- Accurate Accounting Records
- Timely Financial Reporting
- Budget Compliance
- Financial Documentation
- Internal Control Compliance
- Expense Verification
- Regulatory Compliance
- Financial Process Efficiency

Operational excellence ensures reliable and efficient financial management.

---

# Leadership Responsibilities

The Finance Manager leads:

- Financial Analysts
- Accountants
- Accounts Payable Specialists
- Accounts Receivable Specialists
- Payroll Specialists
- Finance Coordinators
- Junior Finance Staff

Leadership responsibilities include coaching team members, assigning work, monitoring performance, and supporting professional development.

---

# Collaboration

The Finance Manager collaborates with:

- Head of Finance
- Finance Director
- FP&A Manager
- Accounting Manager
- Treasury Manager
- Payroll Manager
- Tax Manager
- Human Resources
- Procurement
- Sales
- Marketing
- Operations

Cross-functional collaboration ensures financial alignment across all departments.

---

# Decision Authority

The Finance Manager may independently:

- Approve routine financial transactions
- Review departmental expenses
- Allocate team responsibilities
- Approve finance documentation
- Recommend process improvements
- Review financial reports
- Monitor budget utilization
- Escalate financial risks

Budget changes beyond approved limits, capital investments, financial policy changes, and strategic financial decisions require approval from the Head of Finance or Finance Director.

---

# Core Competencies

The Finance Manager should demonstrate expertise in:

- Financial Management
- Budget Administration
- Accounting Principles
- Financial Reporting
- Financial Analysis
- Leadership
- Internal Controls
- ERP Systems
- Regulatory Compliance
- Problem Solving

---

# Performance Expectations

The Finance Manager is expected to:

- Deliver accurate financial reports
- Maintain budget discipline
- Improve operational efficiency
- Ensure regulatory compliance
- Support strategic planning
- Develop finance personnel
- Strengthen financial controls
- Continuously improve finance processes
