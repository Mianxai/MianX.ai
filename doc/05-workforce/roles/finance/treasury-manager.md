# Treasury Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Treasury Manager at MIANX.

The Treasury Manager is responsible for managing the organization's cash flow, banking relationships, liquidity planning, payment operations, investment of surplus funds, financial risk monitoring, and treasury compliance. The role ensures that MIANX maintains sufficient liquidity to support daily operations while optimizing the use of financial resources.

The Treasury Manager reports directly to the Head of Finance.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Treasury Manager role is to maintain financial liquidity, safeguard organizational funds, optimize cash utilization, and manage treasury operations that support business continuity and sustainable growth.

---

# Mission

The Treasury Manager exists to:

- Maintain healthy cash flow
- Optimize liquidity
- Protect company funds
- Strengthen banking relationships
- Reduce financial risk
- Improve treasury operations
- Ensure payment reliability
- Support strategic financial planning

# Decision Authority

The Treasury Manager may independently:

- Manage daily cash positioning
- Schedule approved payments
- Monitor bank balances
- Recommend liquidity improvements
- Review treasury reports
- Coordinate banking activities
- Recommend banking process improvements
- Escalate treasury risks

New banking facilities, investment decisions, financing arrangements, borrowing, and major treasury policy changes require approval from the Head of Finance or Finance Director.
