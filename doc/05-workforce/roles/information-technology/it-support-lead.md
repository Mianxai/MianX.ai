# IT Support Lead

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the IT Support Lead at MIANX.

The IT Support Lead is responsible for supervising the IT Support Specialists and Help Desk Technicians while ensuring high-quality technical support, timely incident resolution, excellent customer service, and efficient day-to-day IT service operations across MIANX.

The IT Support Lead reports directly to the IT Operations Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the IT Support Lead role is to ensure reliable, responsive, and customer-focused IT support services that minimize downtime and maximize employee productivity.

---

# Mission

The IT Support Lead exists to:

- Deliver exceptional IT support
- Lead support operations
- Improve service quality
- Reduce incident resolution time
- Increase customer satisfaction
- Standardize support processes
- Develop support staff
- Continuously improve service delivery

---

# Role Summary

The IT Support Lead oversees:

- IT Support Team
- Help Desk Operations
- Incident Management
- Service Requests
- Technical Troubleshooting
- User Support
- Device Support
- Software Support
- Asset Coordination
- Knowledge Management
- Escalation Management
- Support Reporting

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The IT Support Lead is responsible for:

- Supervising support staff
- Assigning support tickets
- Monitoring SLA compliance
- Resolving complex technical issues
- Escalating critical incidents
- Coaching support personnel
- Managing support workloads
- Improving service quality
- Maintaining knowledge articles
- Coordinating with infrastructure teams
- Monitoring customer satisfaction
- Preparing operational reports

---

# Support Operations Responsibilities

The IT Support Lead manages:

- Help Desk Operations
- Incident Queue
- Service Requests
- User Support
- Hardware Support
- Software Support
- Remote Assistance
- Ticket Prioritization
- Escalation Workflow
- Knowledge Base

Support operations shall prioritize responsiveness, professionalism, accuracy, and customer satisfaction.

---

# Governance Responsibilities

The IT Support Lead ensures compliance with:

- IT Service Management Policy
- Service Desk Procedures
- Incident Management Policy
- Problem Management Policy
- Change Management Policy
- Asset Management Policy
- Information Security Policy
- Access Control Policy
- Acceptable Use Policy
- Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

All support activities shall comply with organizational governance, IT policies, and security standards.

---

# Leadership Responsibilities

The IT Support Lead:

- Leads daily support operations
- Coaches support staff
- Conducts performance reviews
- Coordinates shift scheduling
- Promotes customer service excellence
- Improves team productivity
- Supports professional development
- Drives operational improvements

Leadership focuses on accountability, teamwork, continuous learning, and operational excellence.

---

# Risk Management Responsibilities

The IT Support Lead monitors:

- SLA Breaches
- Ticket Backlogs
- User Satisfaction Issues
- Recurring Incidents
- Service Availability
- Support Resource Constraints
- Hardware Failures
- Software Issues
- Security Incidents
- Escalation Delays

Critical operational issues shall be escalated immediately to the IT Operations Manager.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The IT Support Lead oversees:

- Ticket Management
- Incident Resolution
- Service Request Fulfillment
- Technical Escalations
- User Communication
- Asset Coordination
- Knowledge Base Updates
- Team Scheduling
- Performance Reporting
- Continuous Improvement

Operational excellence ensures efficient, reliable, and customer-focused IT support.

---

# Collaboration

The IT Support Lead collaborates with:

- IT Operations Manager
- Infrastructure Manager
- Systems Administrators
- Network Engineers
- Cloud Engineers
- DevOps Engineers
- Information Security Manager
- Security Engineers
- Software Engineering Teams
- Human Resources
- Department Managers
- External Vendors

Cross-functional collaboration ensures rapid issue resolution and seamless IT service delivery.

---

# Decision Authority

The IT Support Lead may independently approve:

- Ticket assignments
- Incident prioritization
- Routine technical resolutions
- Knowledge base updates
- Support scheduling
- Escalation recommendations
- Support documentation
- Operational workflow improvements

Major operational policy changes, staffing decisions, significant technology upgrades, and budget-related decisions require approval from the IT Operations Manager.

---

# Core Competencies

The IT Support Lead should demonstrate expertise in:

- IT Service Management
- Technical Support
- Incident Management
- Customer Service
- Leadership
- Windows Administration
- Microsoft 365
- Hardware Troubleshooting
- Network Fundamentals
- IT Asset Management
- Problem Solving
- Communication

---

# Performance Expectations

The IT Support Lead is expected to:

- Achieve SLA targets
- Improve customer satisfaction
- Reduce ticket backlog
- Increase first-contact resolution
- Improve team productivity
- Strengthen knowledge management
- Develop support staff
- Continuously improve support services

---

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Objective |
|------|-----------|
| SLA Compliance | ≥95% |
| First Contact Resolution | ≥80% |
| Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) | ≥90% |
| Ticket Resolution Time | Meet SLA targets |
| Ticket Backlog | Maintain within target threshold |
| Escalation Rate | Minimize unnecessary escalations |
| Knowledge Base Growth | Increase documented solutions |
| Team Productivity | Improve monthly performance |
| Repeat Incidents | Reduce recurring issues |
| Continuous Improvement | Improve support maturity annually |

Performance evaluations focus on leadership effectiveness, operational efficiency, customer satisfaction, technical expertise, documentation quality, team development, and continuous improvement.
