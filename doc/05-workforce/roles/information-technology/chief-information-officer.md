# Chief Information Officer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Chief Information Officer (CIO) at MIANX.

The Chief Information Officer (CIO) is the executive leader responsible for defining MIANX's technology vision, digital transformation strategy, enterprise architecture, cybersecurity governance, IT operations, software engineering, infrastructure, technology investments, innovation initiatives, and overall technology alignment with business objectives.

The Chief Information Officer reports directly to the Chief Executive Officer.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Chief Information Officer role is to establish technology as a strategic business enabler by delivering secure, scalable, innovative, and cost-effective technology solutions that accelerate organizational growth and operational excellence.

---

# Mission

The Chief Information Officer exists to:

- Define enterprise technology strategy
- Lead digital transformation
- Drive innovation
- Protect organizational information
- Build scalable technology platforms
- Improve operational efficiency
- Support business growth
- Maintain technology governance

# Decision Authority

The Chief Information Officer may independently approve:

- Enterprise technology standards
- Technology architecture decisions
- IT operational policies
- Technology project prioritization
- Infrastructure improvements
- Technology procurement within approved budgets
- Vendor selection
- Technology governance initiatives

Executive-level capital expenditures, mergers and acquisitions technology integration, organization-wide strategic investments, and budget allocations beyond approved authority require approval from the Chief Executive Officer and Executive Leadership Team.
