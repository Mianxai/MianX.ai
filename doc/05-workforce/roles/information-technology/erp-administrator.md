# ERP Administrator

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the ERP Administrator at MIANX.

The ERP Administrator is responsible for administering, configuring, securing, maintaining, optimizing, and supporting the organization's Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) platform, ensuring reliable business operations, data integrity, process automation, and seamless integration across all departments.

The ERP Administrator reports directly to the Infrastructure Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the ERP Administrator role is to ensure the ERP platform operates efficiently, securely, and reliably while enabling standardized business processes and supporting organizational growth.

---

# Mission

The ERP Administrator exists to:

- Maintain ERP availability
- Optimize business processes
- Improve system performance
- Protect enterprise data
- Support operational continuity
- Automate workflows
- Ensure system reliability
- Drive continuous improvement

---

# Role Summary

The ERP Administrator is responsible for:

- ERP Administration
- ERP Configuration
- User Administration
- Access Management
- ERP Security
- Workflow Automation
- ERP Integrations
- System Monitoring
- Performance Optimization
- ERP Upgrades
- Vendor Coordination
- Technical Documentation

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The ERP Administrator is responsible for:

- Administering ERP environments
- Configuring ERP modules
- Managing users and permissions
- Supporting Finance, HR, Procurement, Sales, and Operations modules
- Monitoring ERP performance
- Applying updates and patches
- Coordinating ERP upgrades
- Troubleshooting ERP issues
- Managing integrations
- Supporting business users
- Maintaining ERP documentation
- Coordinating with ERP vendors

---

# ERP Administration Responsibilities

The ERP Administrator manages:

- ERP Infrastructure
- Business Modules
- User Accounts
- Role Management
- Workflow Configuration
- Business Rules
- Integrations
- Scheduled Jobs
- ERP Reporting
- System Monitoring

ERP administration shall prioritize reliability, security, performance, and operational continuity.

---

# Governance Responsibilities

The ERP Administrator complies with:

- ERP Governance Policy
- Information Security Policy
- Access Control Policy
- Change Management Policy
- Data Governance Policy
- Business Continuity Policy
- Disaster Recovery Plan
- Software Asset Management Policy
- Vendor Management Policy
- Compliance Requirements

All ERP administration activities shall comply with organizational governance and applicable regulations.

---

# Security Responsibilities

The ERP Administrator:

- Implements role-based access control
- Reviews user permissions
- Protects confidential business data
- Enforces segregation of duties
- Monitors privileged access
- Secures ERP integrations
- Supports security audits
- Reports security incidents immediately

ERP environments shall follow least-privilege and zero-trust security principles.

---

# Risk Management Responsibilities

The ERP Administrator monitors:

- ERP Availability
- User Access Risks
- Integration Failures
- Data Integrity Issues
- Workflow Failures
- System Performance
- Backup Status
- Security Vulnerabilities
- Upgrade Risks
- Vendor Support Risks

Critical ERP incidents shall be escalated immediately to the Infrastructure Manager.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The ERP Administrator supports:

- ERP Monitoring
- System Maintenance
- User Support
- Configuration Management
- Workflow Automation
- Patch Management
- Backup Verification
- Disaster Recovery Testing
- Operational Reporting
- Documentation Management

Operational excellence ensures uninterrupted enterprise business operations.

# Collaboration

The ERP Administrator collaborates with:

- Infrastructure Manager
- Head of Information Technology
- Database Administrators
- Software Engineers
- Business Systems Analysts
- Information Security Team
- Finance Department
- Human Resources
- Procurement Department
- Sales Department
- Operations Department
- ERP Vendor

Cross-functional collaboration ensures ERP solutions align with organizational objectives.

---

# Decision Authority

The ERP Administrator may independently approve:

- Standard ERP configurations
- User account provisioning
- Routine permission changes
- Scheduled maintenance
- Workflow improvements
- ERP documentation updates
- Monitoring enhancements
- Routine troubleshooting activities

Major ERP architecture changes, production module deployments, enterprise integrations, and organization-wide configuration changes require approval from the Infrastructure Manager or Head of Information Technology.

The ERP Administrator should demonstrate expertise in:

- ERP Administration
- ERP Configuration
- SQL
- Business Process Management
- User Access Management
- Workflow Automation
- System Integration

# Performance Expectations

The ERP Administrator is expected to:

- Maintain high ERP availability
- Ensure secure system operations
- Improve ERP performance
- Support business users effectively
- Reduce ERP incidents
- Maintain accurate documentation
- Ensure successful upgrades
- Continuously optimize ERP processes
