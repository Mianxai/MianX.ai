# Business Systems Analyst

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Business Systems Analyst at MIANX.

The Business Systems Analyst is responsible for analyzing business processes, gathering requirements, evaluating enterprise systems, designing functional solutions, coordinating stakeholders, and ensuring technology solutions align with organizational objectives while maximizing operational efficiency and business value.

The Business Systems Analyst reports directly to the Engineering Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Business Systems Analyst role is to bridge business needs with technology solutions by translating operational requirements into scalable, secure, and efficient enterprise systems.

---

# Mission

The Business Systems Analyst exists to:

- Align technology with business goals
- Improve operational efficiency
- Optimize business processes
- Deliver business value
- Improve enterprise systems
- Support digital transformation
- Enhance stakeholder collaboration
- Drive continuous improvement

---

# Role Summary

The Business Systems Analyst is responsible for:

- Business Requirements Analysis
- Functional Analysis
- Business Process Mapping
- Systems Analysis
- Solution Design
- Stakeholder Management
- Gap Analysis
- Workflow Optimization
- User Acceptance Coordination
- Change Support
- Business Documentation
- Process Improvement

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Business Systems Analyst is responsible for:

- Gathering business requirements
- Conducting stakeholder interviews
- Documenting functional requirements
- Creating business process maps
- Performing gap analysis
- Preparing functional specifications
- Supporting ERP and CRM implementations
- Coordinating User Acceptance Testing (UAT)
- Managing change requests
- Collaborating with development teams
- Supporting solution deployment
- Maintaining project documentation

---

# Business Systems Responsibilities

The Business Systems Analyst manages:

- Business Requirements
- Functional Specifications
- Business Workflows
- Process Improvements
- Enterprise Applications
- Requirement Traceability
- User Stories
- Acceptance Criteria
- Business Rules
- Solution Documentation

Business analysis activities shall prioritize business value, usability, scalability, and operational efficiency.

---

# Governance Responsibilities

The Business Systems Analyst complies with:

- Business Analysis Standards
- Change Management Policy
- Information Security Policy
- Data Governance Policy
- Project Governance Framework
- Documentation Standards
- Business Continuity Policy
- Risk Management Policy
- Access Control Policy
- Compliance Requirements

All business analysis activities shall comply with organizational governance standards and regulatory requirements.

---

# Security Responsibilities

The Business Systems Analyst:

- Protects confidential business information
- Incorporates security requirements into solutions
- Supports privacy-by-design principles
- Reviews access requirements
- Identifies business security risks
- Supports compliance initiatives
- Protects stakeholder information
- Reports security concerns immediately

Business solutions shall be designed with security and compliance as foundational principles.

---

# Risk Management Responsibilities

The Business Systems Analyst monitors:

- Requirement Gaps
- Process Inefficiencies
- Scope Creep
- Business Risks
- Integration Risks
- User Adoption Risks
- Change Management Risks
- Data Quality Issues
- Compliance Risks
- Project Delivery Risks

Critical business risks shall be escalated immediately to the Engineering Manager.

---

The Business Systems Analyst supports:

- Business Process Improvement
- Enterprise System Enhancements
- Requirement Management

- Stakeholder Communication

- Continuous Improvement

- Engineering Manager

- Data Engineers

- Product Managers
- Project Managers
- Information Security Team
- Department Managers

Cross-functional collaboration ensures business objectives are effectively translated into successful technology solutions.

# Decision Authority

The Business Systems Analyst may independently approve:

- Business requirement documentation
- Functional specifications
- Process flow improvements
- User stories
- Acceptance criteria
- Business documentation updates
- Requirement prioritization recommendations
- Process optimization proposals

Major business process redesigns, enterprise solution architecture decisions, organization-wide system implementations, and strategic technology initiatives require approval from the Engineering Manager or Head of Information Technology.
