# Infrastructure Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Infrastructure Manager at MIANX.

The Infrastructure Manager is responsible for planning, implementing, operating, securing, and continuously improving MIANX's IT infrastructure, including servers, cloud environments, networks, storage systems, virtualization platforms, backup solutions, disaster recovery capabilities, and enterprise infrastructure services to ensure high availability, security, and scalability.

The Infrastructure Manager reports directly to the Head of Information Technology.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Infrastructure Manager role is to provide reliable, secure, scalable, and resilient infrastructure services that enable uninterrupted business operations and support enterprise technology initiatives.

---

# Mission

The Infrastructure Manager exists to:

- Maintain reliable infrastructure
- Improve infrastructure performance
- Strengthen cybersecurity
- Ensure business continuity
- Optimize infrastructure costs
- Support cloud transformation
- Deliver operational excellence
- Enable enterprise growth

# Decision Authority

The Infrastructure Manager may independently approve:

- Routine infrastructure maintenance
- Infrastructure configuration standards
- Backup schedules
- Capacity planning adjustments
- Infrastructure monitoring improvements
- Operational infrastructure procedures
- Resource allocation within the infrastructure team
- Routine infrastructure upgrades within delegated authority

Enterprise infrastructure architecture changes, major cloud migrations, capital infrastructure investments, and strategic technology initiatives require approval from the Head of Information Technology or IT Director.

| Capacity Utilization | Maintain optimal utilization levels |
| Security Compliance | Eliminate critical infrastructure vulnerabilities |
