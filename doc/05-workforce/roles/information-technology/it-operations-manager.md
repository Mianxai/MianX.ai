# IT Operations Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the IT Operations Manager at MIANX.

The IT Operations Manager is responsible for planning, managing, monitoring, and continuously improving the daily operation of MIANX's technology infrastructure, IT services, service desk, operational support, system availability, incident management, and business continuity to ensure reliable and efficient IT service delivery across the organization.

The IT Operations Manager reports directly to the Head of Information Technology.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the IT Operations Manager role is to ensure that enterprise IT services remain available, secure, efficient, scalable, and aligned with business objectives through effective operational management and continuous service improvement.

---

# Mission

The IT Operations Manager exists to:

- Deliver reliable IT services
- Maximize system availability
- Improve operational efficiency
- Lead IT support operations
- Reduce operational risks
- Improve service quality
- Strengthen business continuity
- Drive operational excellence

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The IT Operations Manager is responsible for:

- Managing daily IT operations
- Leading IT support teams
- Ensuring service availability
- Managing operational incidents
- Coordinating problem resolution
- Overseeing IT asset lifecycle
- Monitoring operational performance
- Managing service level agreements (SLAs)
- Coordinating disaster recovery readiness
- Improving operational processes
- Managing operational vendors
- Maintaining operational documentation

# IT Operations Responsibilities

The IT Operations Manager manages:

- IT Service Desk
- Infrastructure Operations
- End-User Support
- System Availability
- Incident Management
- Problem Management
- Service Requests
- Operational Monitoring
- Asset Management
- Capacity Planning

Operations shall prioritize reliability, efficiency, availability, customer satisfaction, and continuous improvement.

# Governance Responsibilities

The IT Operations Manager ensures compliance with:

- IT Operations Policy
- IT Service Management Standards
- Change Management Policy
- Incident Management Procedures
- Problem Management Procedures
- Asset Management Policy
- Business Continuity Policy
- Disaster Recovery Plan
- Information Security Policy
- Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

All operational activities shall comply with organizational governance, security standards, and service management policies.

---

# Leadership Responsibilities

The IT Operations Manager:

- Leads IT Operations teams
- Manages Service Desk operations
- Coordinates cross-functional support
- Develops operational strategies
- Mentors IT professionals
- Oversees operational performance
- Allocates operational resources
- Promotes continuous improvement

Leadership shall focus on operational excellence, accountability, collaboration, and customer service.

# Risk Management Responsibilities

The IT Operations Manager monitors:

- Service Outages
- Infrastructure Failures
- Capacity Constraints
- Incident Trends
- Vendor Risks
- Operational Security Risks
- Hardware Failures
- Backup Failures
- Disaster Recovery Readiness
- Business Continuity Risks

Critical operational incidents shall be escalated immediately to the Head of Information Technology.

# Decision Authority

The IT Operations Manager may independently approve:

- Operational procedures
- Incident response coordination
- Routine infrastructure maintenance
- IT support scheduling
- Asset replacement recommendations
- Service improvement initiatives
- Operational documentation
- Vendor operational coordination

Enterprise infrastructure strategy, major technology investments, organization-wide service changes, and operational budget approvals require approval from the Head of Information Technology or Chief Information Officer.

The IT Operations Manager is expected to:

- Maintain high service availability
- Improve operational efficiency
- Meet SLA commitments
- Reduce recurring incidents
- Improve customer satisfaction

- Continuously improve IT services

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Objective |
|------|-----------|
| IT Service Availability | Maintain ≥99.9% uptime |
| SLA Compliance | Achieve ≥95% SLA performance |
| Mean Time to Resolve (MTTR) | Meet incident resolution targets |
| Incident Volume | Reduce recurring incidents |
| Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) | Achieve ≥90% satisfaction |
| Service Request Fulfillment | Meet defined response targets |
| Change Success Rate | ≥95% successful changes |
| Asset Accuracy | Maintain accurate asset inventory |
| Operational Cost Efficiency | Optimize operational spending |
| Continuous Improvement | Improve IT service maturity annually |

Performance evaluations focus on operational excellence, service reliability, customer satisfaction, leadership, efficiency, governance, and continuous improvement.
