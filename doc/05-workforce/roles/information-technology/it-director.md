# IT Director

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the IT Director at MIANX.

The IT Director is responsible for leading the operational management of the Information Technology department by overseeing engineering, infrastructure, cybersecurity, enterprise applications, IT operations, service delivery, technology projects, and operational governance. The role ensures technology initiatives are executed efficiently while supporting the strategic direction established by executive leadership.

The IT Director reports directly to the Vice President of Information Technology.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the IT Director role is to transform enterprise technology strategy into operational execution by ensuring technology teams deliver secure, reliable, scalable, and high-quality services that enable business success.

---

# Mission

The IT Director exists to:

- Lead technology operations
- Deliver enterprise technology initiatives
- Improve engineering performance
- Strengthen operational governance
- Optimize technology services
- Build high-performing teams
- Improve service reliability
- Drive continuous improvement

The IT Director monitors:

# Decision Authority

The IT Director may independently approve:

- Operational technology improvements
- Department resource allocation
- Project execution plans
- Infrastructure enhancements
- Standard operating procedures
- Technology procurement within delegated authority
- Operational staffing recommendations
- Service improvement initiatives

Major architectural decisions, enterprise technology strategy, executive budget approvals, and organization-wide technology initiatives require approval from the Vice President of Information Technology or Chief Information Officer.

|------|-----------|
| System Availability | Maintain ≥99.9% uptime |
| Project Delivery Rate | Deliver projects on schedule |

| Incident Resolution Time | Reduce operational downtime |

| Budget Performance | Operate within approved budget |
| Employee Engagement | Improve IT team satisfaction |
