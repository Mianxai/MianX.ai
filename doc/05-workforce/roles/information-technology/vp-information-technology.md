# Vice President of Information Technology

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Vice President of Information Technology at MIANX.

The Vice President of Information Technology is responsible for executing the enterprise technology strategy established by the Chief Information Officer, overseeing technology operations, engineering delivery, infrastructure, cybersecurity, enterprise systems, IT governance, and digital transformation initiatives while ensuring technology services support business objectives.

The Vice President of Information Technology reports directly to the Chief Information Officer.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Vice President of Information Technology role is to translate enterprise technology strategy into operational execution by delivering secure, scalable, reliable, and innovative technology services across MIANX.

---

# Mission

The Vice President of Information Technology exists to:

- Execute enterprise technology strategy
- Lead technology operations
- Deliver digital transformation
- Improve engineering excellence
- Strengthen cybersecurity
- Optimize technology investments
- Build high-performing technology teams
- Ensure operational reliability

# Decision Authority

The Vice President of Information Technology may independently approve:

- Department operational plans
- Technology implementation standards
- Infrastructure upgrades
- Software delivery priorities
- Operational budgets within delegated authority
- Vendor operational agreements
- Technology process improvements
- Resource allocation across IT departments

Enterprise-wide technology strategy, executive budget approvals, mergers and acquisitions technology decisions, and major capital investments require approval from the Chief Information Officer.
