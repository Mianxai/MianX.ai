# BI Developer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the BI Developer at MIANX.

The BI (Business Intelligence) Developer is responsible for designing, developing, maintaining, and optimizing business intelligence solutions, dashboards, reports, semantic models, and analytics platforms that transform enterprise data into meaningful insights for strategic and operational decision-making.

The BI Developer reports directly to the Engineering Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the BI Developer role is to deliver accurate, timely, and actionable business intelligence solutions that enable data-driven decision-making across MIANX.

---

# Mission

The BI Developer exists to:

- Deliver trusted business insights
- Enable data-driven decisions
- Build scalable reporting solutions
- Improve data visualization
- Ensure reporting accuracy
- Enhance analytics capabilities
- Support business performance
- Promote self-service analytics

---

# Role Summary

The BI Developer is responsible for:

- Dashboard Development
- Report Development
- Data Visualization
- Data Modeling
- KPI Development
- Business Analytics
- Semantic Models
- Data Validation
- Report Automation
- Performance Optimization
- Self-Service BI
- Technical Documentation

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The BI Developer is responsible for:

- Developing executive dashboards
- Building operational reports
- Designing data models
- Creating KPI scorecards
- Validating reporting accuracy
- Optimizing report performance
- Automating recurring reports
- Supporting business users
- Gathering reporting requirements
- Maintaining BI documentation
- Improving reporting standards
- Supporting enterprise analytics initiatives

---

# Business Intelligence Responsibilities

The BI Developer manages:

- BI Dashboards
- Executive Reports
- Operational Reports
- KPI Dashboards
- Data Models
- Semantic Layers
- Report Automation
- Data Visualizations
- Analytics Portals
- Self-Service Reporting

Business intelligence solutions shall prioritize accuracy, usability, scalability, and performance.

---

# Governance Responsibilities

The BI Developer complies with:

- Data Governance Policy
- Information Security Policy
- Data Quality Standards
- Reporting Standards
- Data Classification Policy
- Privacy Policy
- Change Management Policy
- Access Control Policy
- Documentation Standards
- Compliance Requirements

All BI activities shall comply with organizational governance, security policies, and regulatory requirements.

---

# Security Responsibilities

The BI Developer:

- Protects confidential business data
- Implements role-based report access
- Supports data privacy requirements
- Ensures secure report distribution
- Protects reporting environments
- Validates data integrity
- Follows least-privilege principles
- Reports security concerns immediately

Business intelligence solutions shall protect sensitive organizational information.

---

# Risk Management Responsibilities

The BI Developer monitors:

- Data Accuracy Issues
- Reporting Errors
- Dashboard Performance
- Data Refresh Failures
- Unauthorized Data Access
- KPI Inconsistencies
- Report Availability
- Data Quality Risks
- Visualization Errors
- Compliance Risks

Critical reporting issues shall be escalated immediately to the Engineering Manager.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The BI Developer supports:

- Dashboard Maintenance
- Report Delivery
- KPI Monitoring
- Data Validation
- Analytics Development
- Performance Optimization
- User Support
- Documentation
- Reporting Automation
- Continuous Improvement

Operational excellence ensures accurate, reliable, and timely business intelligence.

The BI Developer collaborates with:

- Engineering Manager

- Software Engineers
- Product Managers

Cross-functional collaboration ensures analytics align with business objectives and enterprise reporting requirements.

# Decision Authority

The BI Developer may independently approve:

- Dashboard enhancements
- Report formatting improvements
- Visualization standards
- KPI calculations
- Routine report updates
- Report scheduling
- Performance optimizations
- Documentation updates

Enterprise reporting architecture, organization-wide KPI definitions, analytics platform changes, and major data model modifications require approval from the Engineering Manager.
