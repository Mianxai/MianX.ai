# Head of Information Technology

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Head of Information Technology at MIANX.

The Head of Information Technology is responsible for managing the day-to-day execution of MIANX's technology operations, engineering teams, infrastructure, enterprise applications, cybersecurity coordination, technical support, and service delivery. The role converts strategic direction into operational execution while ensuring reliable, secure, and scalable technology services across the organization.

The Head of Information Technology reports directly to the IT Director.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Head of Information Technology role is to lead operational technology execution, maintain reliable IT services, improve engineering productivity, and ensure enterprise technology platforms support organizational growth.

---

# Mission

The Head of Information Technology exists to:

- Lead technology operations
- Deliver reliable IT services
- Improve engineering execution
- Strengthen operational governance
- Support digital transformation
- Optimize technology resources
- Build high-performing teams
- Promote continuous improvement

# Risk Management Responsibilities

The Head of Information Technology monitors:

- Infrastructure Risks
- Cloud Service Risks
- System Availability Risks

- Security Coordination Risks

- Capacity Risks

# Decision Authority

The Head of Information Technology may independently approve:

- Daily operational activities
- Resource scheduling
- Standard technology changes
- Infrastructure maintenance windows
- Operational process improvements
- Technical documentation updates
- Department workload distribution
- Routine vendor coordination

Major architectural changes, enterprise-wide technology initiatives, capital expenditures, and strategic technology investments require approval from the IT Director or Vice President of Information Technology.
