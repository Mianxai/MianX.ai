# Information Technology Roles

> This directory contains the official role definitions, responsibilities, authority, reporting structures, career paths, governance responsibilities, and operational expectations for every position within the Information Technology Department at MIANX.

The Information Technology Department is responsible for designing, building, securing, operating, and continuously improving MIANX's technology ecosystem. It provides reliable digital infrastructure, develops business applications, manages enterprise systems, protects organizational data, supports employees, and enables innovation across the company.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Information Technology department is to:

- Deliver reliable technology services
- Build secure digital platforms
- Support business operations
- Protect organizational information
- Improve operational efficiency
- Enable digital transformation
- Drive technology innovation
- Maintain technology governance
