# CRM Administrator

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the CRM Administrator at MIANX.

The CRM Administrator is responsible for administering, configuring, securing, maintaining, optimizing, and supporting the organization's Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform to enable efficient sales, marketing, customer success, and customer support operations.

The CRM Administrator reports directly to the Infrastructure Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the CRM Administrator role is to ensure the CRM platform remains reliable, secure, scalable, and aligned with business processes while improving customer relationship management and operational efficiency.

---

# Mission

The CRM Administrator exists to:

- Maintain CRM availability
- Improve customer data quality
- Support business automation
- Enhance sales productivity
- Improve customer experience
- Protect customer information
- Optimize CRM performance
- Drive continuous platform improvement

---

# Role Summary

The CRM Administrator is responsible for:

- CRM Administration
- CRM Configuration
- User Administration
- Security Management
- Workflow Automation
- Customer Data Management
- CRM Integrations
- System Monitoring
- Performance Optimization
- CRM Upgrades
- Vendor Coordination
- Technical Documentation

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The CRM Administrator is responsible for:

- Administering CRM environments
- Configuring CRM modules
- Managing users, roles, and permissions
- Creating custom fields, forms, and layouts
- Developing workflows and automations
- Maintaining customer records
- Monitoring CRM performance
- Managing CRM integrations
- Supporting business users
- Coordinating platform upgrades
- Maintaining CRM documentation
- Working with CRM vendors

---

# CRM Administration Responsibilities

The CRM Administrator manages:

- CRM Platform
- Customer Database
- User Accounts
- Security Roles
- Sales Pipelines
- Marketing Automation
- Customer Service Modules
- Dashboards
- Reports
- Business Workflows

CRM administration shall prioritize reliability, security, usability, performance, and scalability.

---

# Governance Responsibilities

The CRM Administrator complies with:

- CRM Governance Policy
- Information Security Policy
- Data Governance Policy
- Customer Data Privacy Policy
- Access Control Policy
- Change Management Policy
- Data Retention Policy
- Vendor Management Policy
- Software Asset Management Policy
- Compliance Requirements

All CRM administration activities shall comply with organizational governance and regulatory obligations.

---

# Security Responsibilities

The CRM Administrator:

- Implements role-based access controls
- Protects customer information
- Enforces least-privilege principles
- Monitors privileged accounts
- Reviews user permissions regularly
- Supports security audits
- Secures CRM integrations
- Reports security incidents immediately

Customer information shall be protected according to organizational privacy and security standards.

---

# Risk Management Responsibilities

The CRM Administrator monitors:

- CRM Availability
- Data Integrity Issues
- Integration Failures
- Unauthorized Access
- Workflow Failures
- Duplicate Customer Records
- Platform Performance
- Security Vulnerabilities
- Backup Status
- Compliance Risks

Critical CRM incidents shall be escalated immediately to the Infrastructure Manager.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The CRM Administrator supports:

- Platform Monitoring
- User Support
- CRM Configuration
- Workflow Automation
- Data Quality Improvements
- Integration Management
- System Maintenance
- Upgrade Management
- Documentation Management
- Operational Reporting

Operational excellence ensures efficient customer relationship management across MIANX.

---

# Collaboration

The CRM Administrator collaborates with:

- Infrastructure Manager
- Head of Information Technology
- Business Systems Analyst
- Software Engineers
- Database Administrator
- Sales Team
- Marketing Team
- Customer Success Team
- Customer Support Team
- Information Security Team
- CRM Vendor

Cross-functional collaboration ensures CRM capabilities support organizational growth and customer engagement.

---

# Decision Authority

The CRM Administrator may independently approve:

- Standard CRM configurations
- User account provisioning
- Workflow enhancements
- Dashboard improvements
- Routine maintenance
- Data cleanup activities
- Documentation updates
- Monitoring improvements

Major CRM architecture changes, enterprise integrations, production deployments, and organization-wide configuration changes require approval from the Infrastructure Manager or Head of Information Technology.

The CRM Administrator should demonstrate expertise in:

- CRM Administration
- CRM Configuration
- Customer Data Management
- Workflow Automation
- SQL Fundamentals
- Business Process Management
- User Administration
- Reporting & Dashboards
- Data Quality

The CRM Administrator is expected to:

- Maintain high CRM availability
- Improve CRM adoption
- Ensure customer data quality
- Reduce platform incidents
- Support business users effectively
- Maintain security compliance
- Produce accurate documentation
- Continuously improve CRM capabilities
