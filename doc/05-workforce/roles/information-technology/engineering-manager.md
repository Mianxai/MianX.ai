# Engineering Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Engineering Manager at MIANX.

The Engineering Manager is responsible for leading software engineering teams, managing software delivery, improving engineering processes, mentoring technical talent, coordinating cross-functional development initiatives, and ensuring the successful delivery of secure, scalable, and high-quality software solutions that align with MIANX's business objectives.

The Engineering Manager reports directly to the Head of Information Technology.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Engineering Manager role is to build and lead high-performing engineering teams that consistently deliver reliable, scalable, secure, and maintainable software products while fostering technical excellence and continuous improvement.

---

# Mission

The Engineering Manager exists to:

- Lead software engineering teams
- Deliver high-quality software
- Improve engineering productivity
- Develop engineering talent
- Promote engineering best practices
- Ensure delivery excellence
- Support business growth
- Foster continuous innovation

The Engineering Manager manages:

- Software Delivery

The Engineering Manager monitors:

- Technical Debt

# Decision Authority

The Engineering Manager may independently approve:

- Sprint planning decisions
- Engineering task assignments
- Technical resource allocation
- Development workflows
- Coding standards implementation
- Engineering process improvements
- Internal engineering documentation
- Team scheduling

Major architecture decisions, technology stack changes, strategic engineering investments, and organizational restructuring require approval from the Head of Information Technology or IT Director.
