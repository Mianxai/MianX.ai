# Chief Marketing Officer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Chief Marketing Officer (CMO) at MIANX.

The Chief Marketing Officer (CMO) is responsible for defining and executing the organization's global marketing strategy, brand vision, customer acquisition strategy, market positioning, communications, and revenue growth initiatives. The CMO leads the entire Marketing organization while ensuring alignment between marketing investments and business objectives.

The Chief Marketing Officer reports directly to the Chief Executive Officer.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Chief Marketing Officer role is to build a world-class marketing organization that drives sustainable growth, strengthens the MIANX brand, and maximizes customer value.

---

# Mission

The Chief Marketing Officer exists to:

- Build a globally recognized brand
- Drive business growth
- Increase market share
- Accelerate customer acquisition
- Improve customer retention
- Align marketing with business strategy
- Strengthen market positioning
- Create measurable business value
