# Marketing Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Marketing Manager at MIANX.

The Marketing Manager is responsible for planning, executing, monitoring, and optimizing marketing campaigns that support business growth, strengthen the MIANX brand, generate qualified leads, and improve customer engagement. The role leads marketing specialists while coordinating closely with Product, Design, Engineering, Sales, Customer Success, and Executive Leadership.

The Marketing Manager reports directly to the Head of Marketing.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Marketing Manager role is to transform marketing strategies into successful campaigns that deliver measurable business outcomes.

---

# Mission

The Marketing Manager exists to:

- Execute marketing strategies
- Generate qualified demand
- Strengthen brand awareness
- Increase customer engagement
- Improve campaign performance
- Support revenue growth
- Develop marketing talent
- Continuously optimize marketing execution

- Planning marketing campaigns

# Decision Authority

The Marketing Manager may approve:

- Campaign Plans
- Marketing Schedules
- Content Calendars
- Budget Allocation (within approved limits)

- Marketing Reports
- Campaign Optimizations

Major budget changes and strategic initiatives require approval from the Head of Marketing.
