# Junior Marketing Specialist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Junior Marketing Specialist at MIANX.

The Junior Marketing Specialist is an entry-level marketing professional responsible for supporting marketing campaigns, preparing marketing materials, assisting with digital marketing activities, maintaining marketing documentation, and learning marketing best practices. The role works under the guidance of senior marketing professionals while developing the skills required for independent campaign execution.

The Junior Marketing Specialist reports directly to the Marketing Specialist or Senior Marketing Specialist.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Junior Marketing Specialist role is to support the Marketing Department while developing practical marketing knowledge, campaign execution skills, and professional experience.

---

# Mission

The Junior Marketing Specialist exists to:

- Support marketing campaigns
- Learn marketing best practices
- Assist marketing operations
- Produce quality marketing assets
- Improve customer engagement
- Maintain marketing documentation
- Develop professional skills
- Contribute to business growth

---

The Junior Marketing Specialist is responsible for:

The Junior Marketing Specialist may:

- Update marketing documentation
