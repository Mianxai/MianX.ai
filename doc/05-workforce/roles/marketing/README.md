# Marketing Roles

> This directory defines the organizational structure, responsibilities, authority, and career progression for all Marketing roles within MIANX.

The Marketing Department is responsible for building brand awareness, generating demand, acquiring and retaining customers, strengthening market positioning, supporting revenue growth, and ensuring consistent communication across every customer touchpoint.

Marketing teams work closely with Executive Leadership, Product, Design, Engineering, Sales, Customer Success, Operations, Finance, Legal, and Human Resources.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Marketing Department is to:

- Build the MIANX brand
- Generate qualified demand
- Increase customer acquisition
- Improve customer retention
- Support revenue growth
- Expand market presence
- Strengthen customer relationships
- Deliver measurable business impact

---

# Department Mission

The Marketing Department exists to:

- Understand customer needs
- Build brand awareness
- Execute marketing campaigns
- Generate qualified leads
- Improve customer engagement
- Increase product adoption
- Support sales growth
- Optimize marketing performance
