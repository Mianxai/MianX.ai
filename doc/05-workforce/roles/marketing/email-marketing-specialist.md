# Email Marketing Specialist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Email Marketing Specialist at MIANX.

The Email Marketing Specialist is responsible for planning, creating, executing, and optimizing email marketing campaigns that support customer acquisition, onboarding, engagement, retention, and revenue growth. The role manages lifecycle communications, marketing automation, segmentation, personalization, deliverability, and campaign analytics while collaborating closely with Product, Sales, Customer Success, and Marketing teams.

The Email Marketing Specialist reports directly to the Marketing Manager or Growth Marketing Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Email Marketing Specialist role is to deliver personalized, high-performing email communications that strengthen customer relationships, improve engagement, and drive measurable business outcomes.

---

# Mission

The Email Marketing Specialist exists to:

- Improve customer engagement
- Increase email conversions
- Support customer retention
- Automate lifecycle communications
- Improve campaign performance
- Strengthen customer relationships
- Support product adoption
- Drive sustainable revenue growth
