# Public Relations Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Public Relations Manager at MIANX.

The Public Relations Manager is responsible for protecting, strengthening, and promoting the public image of MIANX through strategic communications, media relations, corporate messaging, reputation management, crisis communication, and public engagement. The role ensures that external communications accurately represent the organization's mission, vision, values, products, and achievements.

The Public Relations Manager reports directly to the Head of Marketing or Marketing Director.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Public Relations Manager role is to build trust, strengthen brand reputation, maintain positive public perception, and ensure consistent corporate communications across all external stakeholders.

---

# Mission

The Public Relations Manager exists to:

- Strengthen brand reputation
- Build positive media relationships
- Improve public trust
- Support business growth
- Manage corporate communications
- Coordinate crisis communication
- Promote organizational achievements
- Protect the MIANX brand
