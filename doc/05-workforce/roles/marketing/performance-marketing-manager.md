# Performance Marketing Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Performance Marketing Manager at MIANX.

The Performance Marketing Manager is responsible for planning, executing, measuring, and optimizing paid marketing campaigns across digital channels. The role focuses on maximizing return on advertising spend (ROAS), reducing customer acquisition costs, improving conversion rates, and driving scalable revenue growth through data-driven marketing.

The Performance Marketing Manager reports directly to the Marketing Manager or Head of Marketing.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Performance Marketing Manager role is to maximize business growth through highly optimized, measurable, and scalable paid marketing initiatives.

---

# Mission

The Performance Marketing Manager exists to:

- Drive qualified customer acquisition
- Maximize advertising ROI
- Improve conversion rates
- Optimize paid marketing channels
- Reduce acquisition costs
- Scale profitable campaigns
- Improve marketing efficiency
- Support sustainable revenue growth

---

The Performance Marketing Manager leads:

- Search Engine Marketing (SEM)
- Paid Social Campaigns

- Marketing Analytics

The Performance Marketing Manager is responsible for:

The Performance Marketing Manager optimizes:

Optimization should be continuous and data-driven.
