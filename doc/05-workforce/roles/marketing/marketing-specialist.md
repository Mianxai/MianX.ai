# Marketing Specialist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Marketing Specialist at MIANX.

The Marketing Specialist is responsible for executing marketing campaigns, producing marketing assets, coordinating promotional activities, analyzing campaign performance, and supporting business growth initiatives. The role works closely with Product, Design, Engineering, Sales, Customer Success, and Marketing leadership to ensure consistent, high-quality marketing execution.

The Marketing Specialist reports directly to the Senior Marketing Specialist or Marketing Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Marketing Specialist role is to execute marketing initiatives that increase brand awareness, generate qualified demand, improve customer engagement, and contribute to sustainable business growth.

---

# Mission

The Marketing Specialist exists to:

- Execute marketing campaigns
- Strengthen brand awareness
- Support lead generation
- Improve customer engagement
- Deliver marketing assets
- Monitor campaign performance
- Support product launches
- Continuously improve marketing execution
