# Senior Marketing Specialist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Senior Marketing Specialist at MIANX.

The Senior Marketing Specialist is responsible for leading the execution of complex marketing initiatives, mentoring junior team members, optimizing campaign performance, and supporting the Marketing Manager in delivering strategic marketing objectives. The role serves as the senior individual contributor within the Marketing Department and provides expertise across multiple marketing disciplines.

The Senior Marketing Specialist reports directly to the Marketing Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Senior Marketing Specialist role is to deliver high-impact marketing campaigns while improving marketing processes, mentoring team members, and maximizing business results.

---

# Mission

The Senior Marketing Specialist exists to:

- Execute advanced marketing campaigns
- Improve campaign performance
- Strengthen brand awareness
- Generate qualified demand
- Mentor marketing specialists
- Optimize marketing processes
- Support organizational growth
- Drive continuous improvement
