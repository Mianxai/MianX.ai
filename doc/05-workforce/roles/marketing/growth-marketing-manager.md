# Growth Marketing Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Growth Marketing Manager at MIANX.

The Growth Marketing Manager is responsible for designing, executing, measuring, and optimizing growth initiatives that accelerate customer acquisition, activation, retention, and revenue. The role combines marketing strategy, experimentation, analytics, automation, and cross-functional collaboration to achieve sustainable business growth.

The Growth Marketing Manager reports directly to the Marketing Manager or Head of Marketing.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Growth Marketing Manager role is to accelerate business growth through data-driven marketing strategies, experimentation, optimization, and scalable customer acquisition programs.

---

# Mission

The Growth Marketing Manager exists to:

- Increase customer acquisition
- Improve activation rates
- Increase customer retention
- Optimize conversion funnels
- Drive revenue growth
- Scale marketing channels
- Build experimentation culture
- Improve marketing efficiency

---

The Growth Marketing Manager leads:

- Growth Strategy
