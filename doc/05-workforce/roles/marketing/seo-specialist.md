# SEO Specialist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the SEO Specialist at MIANX.

The SEO Specialist is responsible for improving the visibility, discoverability, and organic growth of MIANX products, platforms, and digital properties through technical SEO, on-page optimization, off-page strategies, keyword research, and performance analysis. The role collaborates closely with Product, Engineering, Design, Content Marketing, and Performance Marketing teams to maximize sustainable organic traffic.

The SEO Specialist reports directly to the Performance Marketing Manager or Marketing Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the SEO Specialist role is to increase qualified organic traffic, improve search engine rankings, optimize website performance, and support long-term business growth through search engine optimization.

---

# Mission

The SEO Specialist exists to:

- Increase organic traffic
- Improve keyword rankings
- Optimize website performance
- Support content discoverability
- Improve search visibility
- Increase qualified leads
- Enhance user experience
- Drive sustainable growth

- Off-Page SEO

# Primary Responsibilities

The SEO Specialist is responsible for:

- Conducting SEO audits
- Performing keyword research
- Optimizing website content
- Improving technical SEO
- Monitoring search rankings
- Building backlink strategies
- Supporting content marketing
- Analyzing SEO performance
- Identifying growth opportunities
- Maintaining SEO documentation

---

# Technical SEO Responsibilities

The SEO Specialist manages:

- Website Crawlability
- Indexing Optimization
- XML Sitemaps
- Robots.txt Configuration
- Canonical URLs
- Structured Data
- Core Web Vitals
- Mobile Optimization

Technical SEO should improve search engine accessibility and website performance.

---

# On-Page SEO Responsibilities

The SEO Specialist optimizes:

- Page Titles
- Meta Descriptions
- Heading Structure
- URL Structure
- Internal Linking
- Image Optimization
- Content Structure
- Keyword Placement

Every page should align with SEO best practices and user intent.
