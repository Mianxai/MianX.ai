# Content Marketing Specialist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Content Marketing Specialist at MIANX.

The Content Marketing Specialist is responsible for planning, creating, publishing, and optimizing high-quality content that educates customers, strengthens the MIANX brand, supports product marketing, improves SEO performance, and generates qualified leads. The role collaborates closely with Product, Design, Engineering, Sales, Customer Success, SEO, and Performance Marketing teams.

The Content Marketing Specialist reports directly to the Marketing Manager or Performance Marketing Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Content Marketing Specialist role is to create valuable, consistent, and engaging content that attracts, educates, converts, and retains customers while supporting organizational marketing objectives.

---

# Mission

The Content Marketing Specialist exists to:

- Produce valuable content
- Strengthen brand authority
- Improve customer education
- Support product adoption
- Increase organic visibility
- Generate qualified leads
- Support marketing campaigns
- Build long-term audience engagement
