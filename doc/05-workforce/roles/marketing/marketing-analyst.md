# Marketing Analyst

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Marketing Analyst at MIANX.

The Marketing Analyst is responsible for collecting, analyzing, interpreting, and reporting marketing data to support strategic decision-making. The role provides actionable insights into customer behavior, campaign performance, market trends, and business opportunities while ensuring the accuracy and reliability of marketing analytics.

The Marketing Analyst reports directly to the Marketing Manager or Growth Marketing Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Marketing Analyst role is to transform marketing data into actionable business intelligence that improves marketing effectiveness, customer acquisition, operational efficiency, and organizational growth.

---

# Mission

The Marketing Analyst exists to:

- Deliver actionable insights
- Improve marketing decisions
- Measure campaign effectiveness
- Support strategic planning
- Monitor business performance
- Identify growth opportunities
- Improve reporting accuracy
- Enable data-driven marketing

- Developing dashboards
