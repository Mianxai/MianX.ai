# Marketing Operations Specialist

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Marketing Operations Specialist at MIANX.

The Marketing Operations Specialist is responsible for managing marketing systems, automation platforms, campaign operations, data quality, reporting, process optimization, and operational governance. The role ensures that marketing teams operate efficiently through standardized workflows, reliable data, scalable automation, and continuous process improvement.

The Marketing Operations Specialist reports directly to the Marketing Manager or Growth Marketing Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Marketing Operations Specialist role is to improve marketing efficiency, operational consistency, data quality, and campaign execution through well-managed processes, technology, and automation.

---

# Mission

The Marketing Operations Specialist exists to:

- Improve marketing efficiency
- Standardize operational processes
- Maintain marketing systems
- Ensure data quality
- Automate repetitive workflows
- Improve reporting accuracy
- Support campaign execution
- Enable scalable marketing operations
