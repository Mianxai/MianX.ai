# Marketing Director

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Marketing Director at MIANX.

The Marketing Director is responsible for leading the execution of marketing strategies, overseeing multiple marketing functions, managing department-wide initiatives, and ensuring marketing programs achieve measurable business outcomes. The role serves as the operational leader between executive marketing leadership and functional marketing teams.

The Marketing Director reports directly to the Vice President of Marketing.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Marketing Director role is to transform strategic marketing objectives into high-performing programs that strengthen the MIANX brand, generate demand, and accelerate business growth.

---

# Mission

The Marketing Director exists to:

- Execute marketing strategy
- Strengthen brand presence
- Drive customer acquisition
- Improve campaign performance
- Develop marketing leaders
- Increase operational efficiency
- Support revenue growth
- Deliver measurable business impact
