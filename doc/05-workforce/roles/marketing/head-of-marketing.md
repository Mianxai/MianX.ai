# Head of Marketing

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Head of Marketing at MIANX.

The Head of Marketing is responsible for managing the daily operations of the Marketing Department, leading marketing managers and specialists, executing departmental strategies, and ensuring marketing initiatives deliver measurable business value. The role bridges strategic leadership and operational execution while maintaining alignment with organizational objectives.

The Head of Marketing reports directly to the Marketing Director.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Head of Marketing role is to ensure the successful execution of marketing strategies through effective leadership, operational excellence, and cross-functional collaboration.

---

# Mission

The Head of Marketing exists to:

- Execute marketing initiatives
- Build high-performing teams
- Strengthen brand visibility
- Increase customer acquisition
- Improve marketing efficiency
- Support revenue growth
- Deliver measurable campaign results
- Continuously improve marketing operations

---

The Head of Marketing oversees:

- Marketing Operations
- Campaign Management
- Brand Marketing
- Product Marketing
- Digital Marketing
- Content Marketing
- Marketing Analytics

- Team Management

# Leadership Responsibilities

The Head of Marketing is responsible for:

- Managing marketing teams
- Supervising marketing managers
- Executing departmental strategies
- Monitoring campaign performance

- Coaching team members
- Supporting hiring activities
- Managing departmental priorities
- Reporting operational results

- Marketing Tools

# Campaign Responsibilities

- Customer Engagement Programs

- Department Budgets

- Budget Forecasts
