# Vice President of Marketing

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Vice President of Marketing at MIANX.

The Vice President of Marketing is responsible for translating the organization's marketing strategy into operational execution. The role oversees all marketing functions, manages departmental leaders, ensures marketing alignment with business objectives, and delivers measurable growth through integrated marketing initiatives.

The Vice President of Marketing reports directly to the Chief Marketing Officer.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Vice President of Marketing role is to execute the organization's marketing strategy through effective leadership, operational excellence, and measurable business outcomes.

---

# Mission

The Vice President of Marketing exists to:

- Execute marketing strategy
- Accelerate business growth
- Strengthen brand leadership
- Improve marketing performance
- Build high-performing teams
- Increase customer acquisition
- Improve customer engagement
- Deliver measurable business impact
