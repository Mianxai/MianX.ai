# Social Media Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Social Media Manager at MIANX.

The Social Media Manager is responsible for developing, executing, and optimizing MIANX's social media strategy across all approved platforms. The role manages brand presence, audience engagement, content publishing, community management, social campaigns, and performance analytics while ensuring alignment with business and marketing objectives.

The Social Media Manager reports directly to the Marketing Manager or Head of Marketing.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Social Media Manager role is to strengthen the MIANX brand, build engaged communities, increase audience reach, support customer acquisition, and drive measurable business outcomes through strategic social media management.

---

# Mission

The Social Media Manager exists to:

- Strengthen brand awareness
- Build online communities
- Increase audience engagement
- Support product launches
- Drive website traffic
- Improve customer relationships
- Support lead generation
- Maintain consistent brand communication

---

# Role Summary

The Social Media Manager leads:

- Social Media Strategy
- Community Management
- Content Publishing
- Social Campaigns
- Brand Communication
- Audience Engagement
- Social Analytics
- Influencer Collaboration
- Reputation Management
- Social Media Governance

# Primary Responsibilities

The Social Media Manager is responsible for:

- Developing social media strategies
- Managing social media calendars
- Publishing social content
- Engaging with online communities
- Monitoring brand mentions
- Coordinating campaign execution
- Collaborating with content creators
- Measuring social performance
- Managing platform growth
- Maintaining social media documentation

# Social Media Strategy Responsibilities

The Social Media Manager develops:

- Platform Strategies
- Content Calendars
- Audience Growth Plans
- Engagement Campaigns
- Product Promotion Plans
- Community Initiatives
- Brand Awareness Programs
- Social Media Roadmaps

Strategies should align with overall marketing and business objectives.

---

# Community Management Responsibilities

The Social Media Manager manages:

- Community Engagement
- Customer Responses
- Social Conversations
- User Feedback
- Brand Reputation
- Audience Interaction
- Community Guidelines
- Escalation Processes

Community interactions should be professional, timely, and aligned with MIANX values.

# Content Responsibilities

The Social Media Manager coordinates:

- Social Media Posts
- Campaign Content
- Product Announcements
- Educational Content
- Promotional Content
- Short-form Videos
- Visual Assets
- Live Event Coverage

Content should maintain brand consistency across all platforms.

# Analytics Responsibilities

The Social Media Manager monitors:

- Audience Growth
- Engagement Rate

- Click-Through Rate (CTR)

- Website Traffic
- Campaign Performance

- Social Media Dashboards

- LinkedIn

- Facebook

- YouTube
- TikTok
- Threads
- Other approved platforms

Platform priorities should evolve according to business goals and audience behavior.
