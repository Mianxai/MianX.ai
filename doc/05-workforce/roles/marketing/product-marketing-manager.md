# Product Marketing Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Product Marketing Manager at MIANX.

The Product Marketing Manager is responsible for developing product positioning, planning go-to-market strategies, enabling sales teams, understanding customer needs, and ensuring successful product launches. The role serves as the bridge between Product, Marketing, Sales, Customer Success, and Executive Leadership.

The Product Marketing Manager reports directly to the Marketing Manager or Head of Marketing.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Product Marketing Manager role is to maximize product adoption, customer engagement, and revenue growth through effective product positioning and go-to-market execution.

---

# Mission

The Product Marketing Manager exists to:

- Drive successful product launches
- Define product positioning
- Communicate product value
- Improve customer adoption
- Enable sales success
- Increase product awareness
- Support revenue growth
- Strengthen market competitiveness

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Product Marketing Manager is responsible for:

- Developing go-to-market strategies
- Defining product positioning

- Planning product launches
- Conducting competitive research
- Identifying customer segments
- Supporting pricing strategies
- Creating sales enablement materials
- Measuring launch performance
- Improving product adoption

# Product Marketing Responsibilities

The Product Marketing Manager manages:

- Product Launch Plans
- Positioning Documents
- Messaging Frameworks
- Customer Personas
- Competitive Intelligence
- Feature Announcements
- Product Campaigns
- Product Communications

Every initiative should clearly communicate customer value.

# Market Research Responsibilities

The Product Marketing Manager conducts:

- Customer Research
- Competitor Analysis
- Market Opportunity Analysis
- Industry Trend Monitoring
- Win/Loss Analysis
- Customer Interviews
- Market Segmentation
- Product Feedback Analysis

Research should guide marketing and product decisions.

# Go-to-Market Responsibilities

The Product Marketing Manager leads:

- Launch Planning
- Launch Readiness
- Sales Enablement
- Product Messaging
- Internal Training
- Marketing Campaign Coordination
- Cross-functional Launch Execution
- Launch Performance Analysis

Go-to-market activities should ensure successful product adoption.

# Sales Enablement Responsibilities

The Product Marketing Manager prepares:

- Product Presentations
- Sales Decks
- Battle Cards
- Product One-Pagers
- Competitive Comparisons
- Demo Scripts
- FAQ Documentation
- Customer Case Studies

Sales teams should have the resources required to effectively communicate product value.

The Product Marketing Manager may approve:
