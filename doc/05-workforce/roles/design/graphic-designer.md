# Graphic Designer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Graphic Designer at MIANX.

The Graphic Designer is responsible for creating high-quality visual assets that communicate ideas, strengthen the MIANX brand, support marketing initiatives, and enhance product communication. The role develops creative materials across digital and print media while ensuring consistency with the organization's visual identity and design standards.

The Graphic Designer reports directly to the Head of Design.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Graphic Designer role is to produce visually compelling designs that effectively communicate information, reinforce brand identity, and support organizational objectives.

---

# Mission

The Graphic Designer exists to:

- Create compelling visual content
- Strengthen brand recognition
- Support marketing campaigns
- Improve visual communication
- Maintain design consistency
- Deliver creative solutions
- Enhance customer engagement
- Continuously improve design quality

---

# Role Summary

The Graphic Designer contributes to:

- Graphic Design
- Brand Identity
- Marketing Design
- Digital Media
- Print Media
- Presentation Design
- Social Media Graphics
- Advertising Assets
- Creative Production
- Design Documentation

# Primary Responsibilities

The Graphic Designer is responsible for:

- Designing marketing materials
- Creating digital graphics
- Producing print-ready artwork
- Maintaining brand consistency
- Designing presentations
- Preparing promotional assets
- Creating illustrations and icons
- Managing design files
- Supporting campaigns
- Collaborating across departments

# Graphic Design Responsibilities

The Graphic Designer creates:

- Marketing Collateral
- Brochures
- Flyers
- Posters
- Social Media Graphics
- Website Graphics
- Infographics
- Digital Advertisements

Designs should communicate clearly while maintaining visual appeal.

# Branding Responsibilities

The Graphic Designer maintains:

- Brand Guidelines
- Visual Identity
- Logo Usage
- Typography Standards
- Color Systems
- Brand Assets
- Marketing Templates
- Design Consistency

Every design should reinforce the MIANX brand identity.

# Marketing Responsibilities

The Graphic Designer supports:

- Product Launches
- Marketing Campaigns
- Email Campaigns
- Event Promotions
- Corporate Communications
- Sales Materials
- Recruitment Campaigns
- Customer Engagement

Creative assets should align with campaign objectives and brand standards.

# Asset Management Responsibilities

The Graphic Designer maintains:

- Graphic Libraries
- Design Templates
- Brand Assets
- Marketing Resources
- Image Libraries
- Icon Collections
- Design Documentation

Assets should remain organized, reusable, and easily accessible.

- Human Resources

Cross-functional collaboration ensures consistent communication and branding.

# Decision Authority

The Graphic Designer may approve:

- Marketing Graphics
- Print Designs
- Digital Assets
- Presentation Graphics
- Icon Updates
- Template Improvements
- Asset Organization
- Creative Revisions

- Produce production-ready assets
