# Design Director

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Design Director at MIANX.

The Design Director is responsible for leading multiple design teams, ensuring consistent execution of the organization's design strategy, maintaining design quality, and aligning user experience initiatives with business objectives. The Design Director bridges executive strategy with operational delivery while mentoring Design Managers and senior designers.

The Design Director reports directly to the Vice President of Design.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Design Director role is to transform organizational design strategy into high-quality design execution while developing strong design teams and delivering exceptional customer experiences.

---
