# Head of Design

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Head of Design at MIANX.

The Head of Design is responsible for leading day-to-day design operations, managing Product Designers, UX Researchers, UI Designers, and Visual Designers, ensuring design quality, and delivering outstanding user experiences across all MIANX products. The Head of Design translates strategic direction from Design Leadership into successful execution by Design teams.

The Head of Design reports directly to the Design Director.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Head of Design role is to ensure consistent, high-quality product design execution while developing Design teams and maintaining alignment with business and customer objectives.

---

The Head of Design exists to:

- Lead design execution
- Improve customer experiences

- Drive continuous improvement
