# Motion Designer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Motion Designer at MIANX.

The Motion Designer is responsible for creating engaging animations, motion graphics, interactive transitions, product demonstrations, marketing videos, and visual storytelling assets that enhance user experience, strengthen brand identity, and improve customer engagement across MIANX products and platforms.

The Motion Designer reports directly to the Head of Design.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Motion Designer role is to communicate ideas through motion, improve digital experiences, and create engaging visual content that supports product usability and brand recognition.

---

# Mission

The Motion Designer exists to:

- Create engaging animations
- Improve product interactions
- Enhance user experiences
- Strengthen brand identity
- Support marketing campaigns
- Simplify complex concepts
- Increase customer engagement
- Continuously improve motion quality

---

# Role Summary

The Motion Designer contributes to:

- Motion Graphics
- UI Animation
- Product Animation
- Marketing Videos
- Explainer Videos
- Microinteractions
- Visual Storytelling
- Brand Animation
- Interactive Media
- Design Documentation

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Motion Designer is responsible for:

- Creating motion graphics
- Designing UI animations
- Producing product demonstrations
- Developing explainer videos
- Creating animated marketing assets
- Designing transitions
- Maintaining animation libraries
- Supporting product launches
- Optimizing media assets
- Collaborating across teams

---

# Motion Design Responsibilities

The Motion Designer creates:

- UI Animations
- Loading Animations
- Product Walkthroughs
- Interactive Transitions
- Feature Demonstrations
- Animated Icons
- Motion Graphics
- Product Videos

Motion should improve usability without distracting users.

- Customer Success
