# Visual Designer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Visual Designer at MIANX.

The Visual Designer is responsible for creating compelling visual experiences that strengthen the MIANX brand across digital products, marketing assets, presentations, and corporate communications. The role combines creativity with design systems to deliver visually engaging, accessible, and consistent experiences.

The Visual Designer reports directly to the Head of Design.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Visual Designer role is to communicate ideas, strengthen brand identity, and enhance user engagement through high-quality visual design.

---

# Mission

The Visual Designer exists to:

- Strengthen brand identity
- Create engaging visual experiences
- Improve product aesthetics
- Maintain visual consistency
- Support marketing initiatives
- Enhance communication
- Promote accessibility
- Continuously improve design quality

---

The Visual Designer contributes to:

# Primary Responsibilities

The Visual Designer is responsible for:

- Creating visual assets
- Designing marketing materials
