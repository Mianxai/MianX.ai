# Vice President of Design (VP Design)

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Vice President of Design (VP Design) at MIANX.

The Vice President of Design is responsible for translating the enterprise design vision into operational execution across all Design teams. The VP Design oversees Design Directors, establishes department objectives, ensures consistent design quality, and drives collaboration between Product, Engineering, Marketing, and Executive Leadership.

The Vice President of Design reports directly to the Chief Design Officer (CDO).

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Vice President of Design role is to operationalize the organization's design strategy by building high-performing teams, establishing scalable design operations, and delivering exceptional user experiences.

---

The Vice President of Design exists to:

- Execute enterprise design strategy
- Build high-performing design teams
- Improve customer experiences

- Drive continuous improvement
