# Design Department Roles

> This document provides an overview of all Design Department roles within MIANX and defines the reporting hierarchy, responsibilities, career progression, and related documentation.

The Design Department is responsible for creating intuitive, accessible, consistent, and user-centered experiences across all MIANX products, platforms, brands, and customer touchpoints. The department collaborates closely with Product, Engineering, Marketing, Customer Success, and Executive Leadership to transform business objectives into exceptional user experiences.

---

# Purpose

The Design Department exists to:

- Create world-class user experiences
- Improve customer satisfaction
- Increase product usability
- Establish consistent design systems
- Strengthen brand identity
- Support product innovation
- Improve accessibility
- Deliver customer-centered design

---

# Mission

The Design Department strives to:

- Understand users
- Solve customer problems
- Deliver intuitive experiences
- Maintain design consistency
- Improve usability
- Enable product success
- Foster innovation
- Continuously improve design quality

# Career Progression

Typical Design career progression:

```text
Junior Product Designer

↓

Product Designer

↓

Senior Product Designer

↓

UX Architect

↓

Head of Design

↓

Design Director

↓

VP Design

↓

Chief Design Officer
```

Specialist progression:

```text
Junior Designer

↓

UI / UX / Visual / Motion / Graphic Designer

↓

Senior Product Designer

↓

UX Architect

↓

Head of Design
```

Career advancement depends on design quality, customer impact, leadership, collaboration, innovation, technical expertise, and business contribution.
