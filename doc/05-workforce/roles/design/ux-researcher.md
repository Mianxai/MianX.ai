# UX Researcher

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the UX Researcher at MIANX.

The UX Researcher is responsible for understanding customer behavior, identifying user needs, validating product decisions, and providing actionable insights that improve product usability and customer satisfaction. The UX Researcher collaborates closely with Product Designers, Product Managers, Engineers, and stakeholders throughout the product lifecycle.

The UX Researcher reports directly to the Head of Design.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the UX Researcher role is to ensure product decisions are supported by reliable user research, behavioral insights, and evidence-based recommendations.

---

# Mission

The UX Researcher exists to:

- Understand user needs
- Improve customer experiences
- Validate design decisions
- Reduce product risk
- Generate actionable insights
- Promote user-centered design
- Support product innovation
- Drive continuous learning

---

# Role Summary

The UX Researcher contributes to:

- User Research
- Customer Interviews
- Usability Testing
- Behavioral Analysis
- Product Discovery
- Journey Mapping
- Research Documentation
- Survey Design
- Experience Evaluation
- Data-driven Decision Making

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The UX Researcher is responsible for:

- Planning research studies
- Conducting user interviews
- Performing usability testing
- Designing surveys
- Analyzing qualitative data
- Analyzing quantitative data
- Creating research reports
- Presenting research findings
- Supporting product discovery
- Maintaining research repositories

---

# User Research Responsibilities

The UX Researcher conducts:

- Customer Interviews
- User Interviews
- Contextual Inquiries
- Field Studies
- Remote Research
- Diary Studies
- Card Sorting
- Tree Testing

Research activities should uncover user behaviors, motivations, and pain points.

---

# Usability Testing Responsibilities

The UX Researcher performs:

- Moderated Testing
- Unmoderated Testing
- Prototype Validation
- Accessibility Testing
- Navigation Testing
- Workflow Evaluation
- Feature Validation
- Experience Benchmarking

Testing should identify usability issues before product release.

---

# Research Analysis Responsibilities

The UX Researcher analyzes:

- Customer Feedback
- Survey Results
- User Behavior
- Product Metrics
- Session Recordings
- Heatmaps
- Journey Analytics
- Research Trends

Insights should support informed product decisions.

---

# Documentation Responsibilities

The UX Researcher produces:

- Research Plans
- Interview Guides
- Survey Questionnaires
- Research Reports
- Journey Maps
- Personas
- Usability Reports
- Research Repositories

Documentation should be clear, organized, and reusable.

---

# Collaboration

The UX Researcher collaborates with:

- UX Architect
- Product Designers
- Senior Product Designers
- Product Managers
- Product Owners
- Software Engineers
- QA Engineers
- Marketing Teams
- Customer Success
- Executive Stakeholders

Collaboration ensures research findings influence product strategy and execution.

---

# Decision Authority

The UX Researcher may approve:

- Research Plans
- Interview Scripts
- Survey Designs
- Research Methodologies
- Research Documentation
- Participant Selection
- Testing Procedures
- Research Recommendations

Final product decisions remain the responsibility of Product and Design Leadership.

---

# Core Competencies

The UX Researcher should demonstrate expertise in:

- User Research
- Qualitative Research
- Quantitative Research
- Interview Techniques
- Survey Design
- Usability Testing
- Behavioral Analysis
- Data Interpretation
- Communication
- Critical Thinking

---

# Performance Expectations

The UX Researcher is expected to:

- Deliver high-quality research
- Produce actionable insights
- Improve product usability
- Support customer-centered decisions
- Maintain research quality
- Collaborate effectively
- Meet project deadlines
- Continuously improve research practices
