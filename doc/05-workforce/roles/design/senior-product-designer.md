# Senior Product Designer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Senior Product Designer at MIANX.

The Senior Product Designer is responsible for leading complex product design initiatives, creating intuitive user experiences, mentoring designers, and ensuring every design solution aligns with customer needs, business objectives, and the organization's design system.

The Senior Product Designer serves as the technical leader for product design projects and collaborates closely with Product Managers, UX Researchers, Engineers, and Design Leadership.

The Senior Product Designer reports directly to the Head of Design.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Senior Product Designer role is to deliver exceptional product experiences while establishing high standards for usability, accessibility, consistency, and design excellence.

---

The Senior Product Designer exists to:

- Create outstanding user experiences

- Improve usability
