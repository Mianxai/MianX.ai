# Chief Design Officer (CDO)

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Chief Design Officer (CDO) at MIANX.

The Chief Design Officer (CDO) is the executive responsible for the organization's overall design vision, user experience strategy, design systems, brand experience, and design culture. The CDO ensures every customer interaction across products, platforms, services, and communications reflects a consistent, intuitive, accessible, and high-quality experience aligned with MIANX's business objectives.

The Chief Design Officer reports directly to the Chief Executive Officer (CEO).

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Chief Design Officer role is to establish a world-class design organization that creates exceptional customer experiences while enabling business growth through design excellence.

---

# Mission

The Chief Design Officer exists to:

- Define enterprise design strategy
- Build customer-centered products
- Improve user experience
- Strengthen brand consistency
- Promote accessibility
- Foster innovation
- Develop design leaders
- Drive organizational excellence
