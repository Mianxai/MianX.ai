# UX Architect

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the UX Architect at MIANX.

The UX Architect is responsible for designing the overall user experience architecture across MIANX products and platforms. The role establishes scalable UX frameworks, information architecture, interaction models, design systems, and usability standards that enable Product Designers and Engineering teams to deliver intuitive, accessible, and consistent customer experiences.

The UX Architect reports directly to the Head of Design.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the UX Architect role is to establish scalable user experience architecture that enables exceptional digital products while ensuring consistency, usability, accessibility, and long-term maintainability.

---

# Mission

The UX Architect exists to:

- Design enterprise UX architecture
- Improve customer experiences
- Standardize interaction patterns
- Build scalable design systems
- Simplify complex workflows
- Promote accessibility
- Enable product innovation
- Improve usability
