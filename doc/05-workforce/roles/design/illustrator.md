# Illustrator

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Illustrator at MIANX.

The Illustrator is responsible for creating original illustrations, icons, characters, diagrams, infographics, conceptual artwork, and visual storytelling assets that support MIANX products, marketing initiatives, documentation, educational content, and brand identity. The role transforms ideas into engaging visual content while maintaining artistic consistency and alignment with organizational design standards.

The Illustrator reports directly to the Head of Design.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Illustrator role is to communicate ideas visually through original artwork that enhances user understanding, strengthens brand identity, and improves customer engagement.

---

# Mission

The Illustrator exists to:

- Create original artwork
- Support visual storytelling
- Strengthen brand identity
- Simplify complex concepts
- Enhance product communication
- Support marketing initiatives
- Improve customer engagement
- Continuously improve creative quality
