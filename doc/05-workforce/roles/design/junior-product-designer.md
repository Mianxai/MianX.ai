# Junior Product Designer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Junior Product Designer at MIANX.

The Junior Product Designer is responsible for supporting the Design team in creating user-centered digital experiences by producing wireframes, UI mockups, prototypes, and design assets while learning industry best practices. The role works under the guidance of senior designers and contributes to product development through research, collaboration, and continuous improvement.

The Junior Product Designer reports directly to the Head of Design.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Junior Product Designer role is to develop foundational product design skills while contributing to the delivery of intuitive, accessible, and visually consistent digital products.

---

# Mission

The Junior Product Designer exists to:

- Learn professional product design
- Support product development
- Improve user experiences
- Follow design standards
- Develop UX and UI skills
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams
- Maintain design quality
- Continuously improve professionally

---

# Role Summary

The Junior Product Designer supports:

- Product Design
- User Experience Design
- User Interface Design
- Wireframing
- Interactive Prototyping
- Design Documentation
- User Research
- Design System Adoption
- Accessibility
- Design Reviews

# Primary Responsibilities

The Junior Product Designer is responsible for:

- Creating wireframes
- Designing UI screens
- Building basic prototypes
- Updating design files
- Preparing design assets
- Supporting usability testing
- Following design guidelines
- Maintaining documentation
- Participating in design reviews
- Learning industry best practices

# Product Design Responsibilities

The Junior Product Designer assists with:

- Product Interfaces
- Dashboard Designs
- Mobile Screens
- Web Screens
- Feature Designs
- Landing Pages
- Form Design
- Responsive Layouts

Design work should prioritize clarity, usability, and consistency.

- Interaction Design

- UX Improvements

# Decision Authority

The Junior Product Designer may approve:

- Minor UI Updates
- Design File Maintenance
- Asset Organization
- Documentation Updates
- Prototype Revisions
- Component Usage
- Design Suggestions
- Minor UX Improvements

Major design decisions require approval from a Product Designer or Senior Product Designer.
