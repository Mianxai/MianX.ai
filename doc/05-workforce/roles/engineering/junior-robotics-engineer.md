# Junior Robotics Engineer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Junior Robotics Engineer at MIANX.

A Junior Robotics Engineer is an entry-level engineering professional responsible for assisting in the development, testing, deployment, maintenance, and support of robotic software, autonomous systems, robotic hardware integrations, perception systems, and motion control solutions while building expertise in robotics engineering.

The role emphasizes structured learning, engineering discipline, robotics safety, secure software development, and collaboration under the guidance of senior engineering staff.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Junior Robotics Engineer role is to develop capable robotics engineers through practical experience, mentorship, and continuous learning while contributing to reliable and production-ready robotic systems.

---

# Mission

The Junior Robotics Engineer exists to:

- Learn robotics engineering
- Assist robotic software development
- Support autonomous systems
- Improve robotics reliability
- Follow engineering standards
- Produce quality documentation
- Collaborate across engineering teams
- Prepare for the Robotics Engineer role
