# Platform Engineer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Platform Engineer at MIANX.

A Platform Engineer is responsible for building, maintaining, automating, and improving the Internal Developer Platform (IDP), shared engineering services, developer tooling, platform infrastructure, and engineering automation that enable software teams to deliver applications efficiently.

Platform Engineers work closely with DevOps Engineers, Cloud Engineers, Software Engineers, Security Engineers, and Product Teams to improve developer productivity and platform reliability.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Platform Engineer role is to provide reliable, scalable, secure, and self-service engineering platforms that simplify software development and reduce operational complexity.

---

# Mission

The Platform Engineer exists to:

- Build engineering platforms
- Improve developer productivity
- Automate engineering workflows
- Maintain shared platform services
- Improve platform reliability
- Support software teams
- Enhance platform security
- Continuously optimize engineering systems

---

The Platform Engineer develops and maintains:

- Platform Portals
- Self-Service Services
