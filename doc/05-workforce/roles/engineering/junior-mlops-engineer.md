# Junior MLOps Engineer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Junior MLOps Engineer at MIANX.

A Junior MLOps Engineer is an entry-level engineering professional responsible for assisting in the deployment, automation, monitoring, maintenance, and support of enterprise machine learning platforms and operational infrastructure while developing expertise in MLOps engineering practices.

The role focuses on learning CI/CD for machine learning, infrastructure automation, Kubernetes, cloud platforms, model deployment, monitoring, security, and operational excellence under the guidance of senior engineering staff.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Junior MLOps Engineer role is to develop skilled MLOps engineers through structured mentorship, practical engineering experience, and continuous learning while supporting reliable production machine learning operations.

---

# Mission

The Junior MLOps Engineer exists to:

- Learn MLOps engineering
- Support ML deployments
- Assist platform operations
- Improve automation
- Maintain production systems
- Follow engineering standards
- Maintain documentation
- Prepare for the MLOps Engineer role

---
