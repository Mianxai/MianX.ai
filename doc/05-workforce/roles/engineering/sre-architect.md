# SRE Architect

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the SRE Architect at MIANX.

The SRE Architect is responsible for designing and governing the organization's Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) strategy, ensuring software systems are reliable, scalable, resilient, observable, and capable of meeting defined Service Level Objectives (SLOs).

Unlike a DevOps Architect who focuses primarily on CI/CD and deployment automation, the SRE Architect focuses on operational excellence, reliability engineering, production resilience, observability, incident management, and continuous service improvement.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the SRE Architect role is to establish engineering practices that maximize system availability, operational reliability, performance, scalability, and customer trust.

---

# Mission

The SRE Architect exists to:

- Build reliable systems
- Improve production resilience
- Define reliability standards
- Reduce operational risk
- Improve observability
- Standardize incident management
- Enable engineering scalability
- Drive operational excellence

---

The SRE Architect provides technical leadership for reliability engineering across the organization.

- Reliability Governance

# Reliability Architecture Responsibilities

The SRE Architect designs:

- Highly Available Systems
- Fault-Tolerant Architectures
- Multi-region Deployments
- Resilient Service Topologies
- Auto-healing Infrastructure
- Load Distribution Strategies
- Redundancy Models
- Reliability Patterns

Architectures should minimize downtime and eliminate single points of failure.

---

# Service Reliability Responsibilities

The SRE Architect establishes:

- Service Level Indicators (SLIs)
- Service Level Objectives (SLOs)
- Error Budgets
- Availability Targets
- Reliability Policies
- Operational Standards
- Reliability Metrics
- Continuous Improvement Plans

Reliability objectives should align with business priorities.

---

# Observability Responsibilities

The SRE Architect governs:

- Metrics Collection
- Centralized Logging
- Distributed Tracing
- Alerting Standards
- Dashboards
- Health Monitoring
- Performance Monitoring
- Operational Analytics

Observability should enable rapid issue detection and diagnosis.

---

# Incident Management Responsibilities

The SRE Architect defines:

- Incident Response Procedures
- Escalation Policies
- On-call Processes
- Severity Classification
- Root Cause Analysis (RCA)
- Postmortem Standards
- Incident Communication
- Recovery Playbooks

Incident processes should encourage learning rather than blame.

---

# Disaster Recovery Responsibilities

The SRE Architect develops:

- Business Continuity Strategies
- Disaster Recovery Architecture
- Backup Policies
- Multi-region Failover
- Recovery Automation
- Recovery Testing
- Recovery Documentation
- Business Impact Analysis

Recovery capabilities should satisfy organizational RTO and RPO requirements.

---

# Capacity Planning Responsibilities

The SRE Architect leads:

- Capacity Forecasting
- Resource Planning
- Infrastructure Scaling
- Traffic Modeling
- Performance Benchmarking
- Cost Optimization
- Growth Planning
- Scalability Reviews

Capacity planning should anticipate future organizational growth.

---

# Production Readiness Responsibilities

The SRE Architect establishes production readiness standards including:

- Deployment Readiness Reviews
- Operational Checklists
- Monitoring Validation
- Security Verification
- Scalability Testing
- Reliability Validation
- Rollback Planning
- Operational Documentation

Production readiness should be verified before every major release.

---

# Decision Authority

The SRE Architect has authority over:

- Reliability standards
- SLO definitions
- Observability architecture
- Incident management standards
- Reliability governance
- Capacity planning strategy
- Disaster recovery standards
- Production readiness requirements
- Operational excellence initiatives
- Reliability tooling standards

Enterprise technology strategy remains under the CTO.

The SRE Architect provides leadership by:

- Mentoring SRE teams
- Leading reliability initiatives
- Reviewing production architecture
- Driving operational improvements
- Defining engineering standards
- Supporting engineering leadership

Leadership is demonstrated through reliability improvements and operational excellence.
