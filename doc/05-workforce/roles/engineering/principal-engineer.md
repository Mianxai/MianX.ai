# Principal Engineer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, and expectations of the Principal Engineer at MIANX. The Principal Engineer is the highest-level Individual Contributor (IC) within the engineering organization and serves as the company's foremost technical authority below the Chief Technology Officer (CTO).

Principal Engineers solve the organization's most complex engineering challenges while shaping the long-term technical direction of MIANX.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Principal Engineer role is to establish technical vision, guide architectural evolution, improve engineering excellence, and enable scalable innovation across the company.

---

# Mission

The Principal Engineer exists to:

- Define long-term technical direction
- Solve organization-wide engineering challenges
- Build scalable technology platforms
- Drive engineering innovation
- Establish technical standards
- Mentor engineering leaders
- Improve system reliability
- Enable sustainable growth

---
