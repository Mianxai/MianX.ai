# Junior DevOps Engineer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Junior DevOps Engineer at MIANX.

A Junior DevOps Engineer is an entry-level infrastructure and platform engineer responsible for assisting in the deployment, automation, monitoring, and maintenance of cloud infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, container platforms, and production systems while learning enterprise DevOps practices under the guidance of experienced engineers.

The role focuses on developing strong skills in Linux, cloud computing, infrastructure automation, Kubernetes, monitoring, security, and operational excellence.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Junior DevOps Engineer role is to develop capable DevOps professionals through structured mentorship, hands-on operational experience, and exposure to enterprise infrastructure.

---

# Mission

The Junior DevOps Engineer exists to:

- Learn DevOps engineering
- Support infrastructure operations
- Automate repetitive tasks
- Improve deployment reliability
- Monitor production systems
- Follow operational standards
- Collaborate with engineering teams
- Prepare for the DevOps Engineer role

---

# Role Summary

Junior DevOps Engineers contribute to infrastructure operations while working under the supervision of Senior DevOps Engineers and the DevOps Architect.

Typical work includes:

- Infrastructure deployment
- CI/CD support
- Monitoring
- Cloud administration
- Automation scripting
- Documentation
- Incident support
- Learning platform operations
