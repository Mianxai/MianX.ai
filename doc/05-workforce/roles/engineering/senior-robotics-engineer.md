# Senior Robotics Engineer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Senior Robotics Engineer at MIANX.

A Senior Robotics Engineer is a senior Individual Contributor (IC) responsible for designing, developing, deploying, optimizing, and maintaining robotic software, autonomous systems, perception pipelines, motion control systems, and hardware-integrated robotic platforms.

Senior Robotics Engineers provide technical leadership while ensuring robotic systems are reliable, safe, scalable, secure, maintainable, and production-ready.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Senior Robotics Engineer role is to build enterprise-grade robotic systems that enable autonomous operation, industrial automation, intelligent navigation, and advanced robotic capabilities.

---

# Mission

The Senior Robotics Engineer exists to:

- Build robotic platforms
- Develop autonomous systems
- Improve robotic reliability
- Optimize perception and control
- Strengthen robotics safety
- Mentor engineering teams
- Deliver production-ready robotic software
- Drive engineering excellence
