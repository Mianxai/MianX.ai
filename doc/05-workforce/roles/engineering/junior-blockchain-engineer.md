# Junior Blockchain Engineer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Junior Blockchain Engineer at MIANX.

A Junior Blockchain Engineer is an entry-level engineering professional responsible for assisting in the development, testing, deployment, maintenance, and support of blockchain applications, smart contracts, decentralized applications (dApps), and distributed ledger infrastructure while building practical expertise in blockchain engineering.

The role emphasizes structured learning, engineering discipline, secure software development, blockchain fundamentals, and collaboration under the guidance of senior engineers.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Junior Blockchain Engineer role is to develop highly capable blockchain engineers through hands-on experience, mentorship, and continuous learning while contributing to reliable enterprise blockchain solutions.

---

# Mission

The Junior Blockchain Engineer exists to:

- Learn blockchain engineering
- Assist smart contract development
- Support decentralized applications
- Maintain blockchain infrastructure
- Improve engineering skills
- Follow security standards
- Produce quality documentation
- Prepare for the Blockchain Engineer role
