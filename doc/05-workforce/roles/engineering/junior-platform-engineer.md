# Junior Platform Engineer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Junior Platform Engineer at MIANX.

A Junior Platform Engineer is an entry-level engineering professional responsible for assisting in the development, maintenance, automation, and support of the Internal Developer Platform (IDP), developer tooling, shared platform services, and engineering infrastructure while learning enterprise Platform Engineering practices.

The role focuses on building strong knowledge of platform engineering, cloud-native technologies, automation, developer experience, infrastructure, and operational excellence.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Junior Platform Engineer role is to develop skilled platform engineers through structured mentorship, hands-on engineering experience, and participation in building internal engineering platforms.

---

# Mission

The Junior Platform Engineer exists to:

- Learn platform engineering
- Support engineering platforms
- Improve developer experience
- Build platform automation
- Maintain shared services
- Follow engineering standards
- Collaborate across teams
- Prepare for the Platform Engineer role

---

# Role Summary

Junior Platform Engineers contribute to platform engineering initiatives while working under the guidance of Senior Platform Engineers and the Platform Architect.
