# Staff Engineer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, and expectations of the Staff Engineer at MIANX. Staff Engineers are senior Individual Contributors (ICs) who provide technical leadership across multiple engineering teams without direct people management responsibilities.

The Staff Engineer bridges the gap between engineering execution and long-term technical strategy.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Staff Engineer role is to improve engineering excellence across the organization by solving complex technical problems, designing scalable systems, mentoring engineers, and driving technical consistency.

---

# Mission

The Staff Engineer exists to:

- Lead through technical excellence
- Solve organization-wide engineering challenges
- Improve system architecture
- Mentor engineers
- Increase engineering productivity
- Reduce technical complexity
- Build reusable platforms
- Strengthen engineering standards

---

# Role Summary

The Staff Engineer works across multiple teams and projects.

Unlike a Technical Lead, who primarily guides one team, a Staff Engineer influences engineering across departments by:

- Defining technical direction
- Reviewing architecture
- Solving difficult technical problems
- Driving engineering initiatives
- Standardizing best practices
- Mentoring senior engineers

---

# Technical Leadership

- Leading large technical initiatives
- Reviewing complex designs
- Guiding architectural decisions

Leadership is based on technical influence rather than organizational authority.
