# Senior Blockchain Engineer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Senior Blockchain Engineer at MIANX.

A Senior Blockchain Engineer is a senior Individual Contributor (IC) responsible for designing, developing, securing, deploying, and maintaining enterprise blockchain platforms, smart contracts, decentralized applications (dApps), and distributed ledger infrastructure.

Senior Blockchain Engineers provide technical leadership across blockchain initiatives while mentoring engineering teams and ensuring blockchain solutions are scalable, secure, compliant, and production-ready.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Senior Blockchain Engineer role is to deliver enterprise-grade blockchain solutions that enable trusted digital transactions, decentralized applications, digital asset management, and secure distributed systems.

---

The Senior Blockchain Engineer exists to:

- Develop secure smart contracts
