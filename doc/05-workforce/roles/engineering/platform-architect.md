# Platform Architect

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Platform Architect at MIANX.

The Platform Architect is responsible for designing, governing, and evolving the organization's Internal Developer Platform (IDP), engineering infrastructure, developer tooling, automation ecosystem, and shared platform services.

Unlike a DevOps Architect who primarily focuses on deployment infrastructure and operational automation, the Platform Architect focuses on improving developer experience, engineering productivity, platform standardization, and reusable engineering capabilities across the organization.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Platform Architect role is to build a secure, scalable, self-service engineering platform that enables development teams to deliver software faster, more reliably, and with minimal operational overhead.

---

# Mission

The Platform Architect exists to:

- Design internal developer platforms
- Improve developer experience
- Standardize engineering workflows
- Build reusable platform services
- Increase engineering productivity
- Automate platform operations
- Strengthen engineering governance
- Support organizational scalability
