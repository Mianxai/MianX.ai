# Blockchain Engineer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Blockchain Engineer at MIANX.

A Blockchain Engineer is responsible for developing, testing, deploying, maintaining, and supporting blockchain-based applications, smart contracts, decentralized applications (dApps), and distributed ledger infrastructure. The role focuses on implementing secure, scalable, and production-ready blockchain solutions that integrate with enterprise systems.

Blockchain Engineers collaborate closely with Backend, Platform, Security, DevOps, Cloud, QA, and Product teams to deliver reliable blockchain-powered services.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Blockchain Engineer role is to implement secure, maintainable, and scalable blockchain solutions that support enterprise applications, digital assets, decentralized systems, and business innovation.

---

The Blockchain Engineer exists to:
