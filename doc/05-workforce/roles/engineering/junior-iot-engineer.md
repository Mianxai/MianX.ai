# Junior IoT Engineer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Junior IoT Engineer at MIANX.

A Junior IoT Engineer is an entry-level engineering professional responsible for assisting in the development, testing, deployment, maintenance, and support of Internet of Things (IoT) platforms, connected devices, edge applications, telemetry systems, and cloud-connected services while developing practical expertise in IoT engineering.

The role emphasizes structured learning, engineering discipline, secure software development, device connectivity, operational reliability, and collaboration under the guidance of senior engineering staff.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Junior IoT Engineer role is to develop highly capable IoT engineers through practical experience, mentorship, and continuous learning while contributing to secure, reliable, and scalable connected solutions.

---

# Mission

The Junior IoT Engineer exists to:

- Learn IoT engineering
- Assist connected device development
- Support edge computing solutions
- Maintain IoT platforms
- Improve engineering skills
- Follow security standards
- Produce quality documentation
- Prepare for the IoT Engineer role
