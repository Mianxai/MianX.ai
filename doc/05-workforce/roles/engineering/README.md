# Engineering Roles

> This section defines every engineering role within MIANX. It establishes responsibilities, authority, reporting structures, required competencies, career progression, and collaboration standards for all engineering positions.

Engineering is the foundation of MIANX. Every engineer contributes to building secure, scalable, reliable, AI-first products and platforms.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this section is to standardize engineering roles across the organization and provide a clear framework for hiring, career development, performance evaluation, and organizational growth.

---

# Objectives

This documentation aims to:

- Define engineering responsibilities
- Standardize role expectations
- Improve hiring consistency
- Support career progression
- Establish engineering standards
- Improve cross-team collaboration
- Build scalable engineering teams
- Maintain technical excellence

# Engineering Career Levels

Engineering careers typically progress through the following levels:

```text
Intern

↓

Junior Engineer

↓

Engineer

↓

Senior Engineer

↓

Lead Engineer

↓

Staff Engineer

↓

Principal Engineer

↓

Architect

↓

Engineering Manager

↓

Director

↓

VP Engineering

↓

Chief Technology Officer
```

Career progression depends on technical excellence, leadership, ownership, collaboration, and business impact.

# Reporting Structure

Engineering roles generally follow this structure:

```text
Chief Technology Officer

↓

Director of Engineering

↓

Engineering Manager

↓

Technical Lead

↓

Senior Engineers

↓

Engineers

↓

Junior Engineers

↓

Interns
```

Some specialist roles may report through architecture, platform, AI, or security organizations.
