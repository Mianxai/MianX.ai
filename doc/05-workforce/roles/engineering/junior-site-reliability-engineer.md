# Junior Site Reliability Engineer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Junior Site Reliability Engineer at MIANX.

A Junior Site Reliability Engineer (Junior SRE) is an entry-level engineering professional responsible for assisting in the operation, monitoring, automation, maintenance, and continuous improvement of production systems while learning modern Site Reliability Engineering practices.

The role focuses on building expertise in Linux, cloud infrastructure, Kubernetes, observability, incident response, automation, networking, and production operations under the guidance of senior engineers.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Junior Site Reliability Engineer role is to develop skilled reliability engineers through structured mentorship, practical operational experience, and continuous technical learning while supporting production infrastructure.

---

# Mission

The Junior Site Reliability Engineer exists to:

- Learn Site Reliability Engineering
- Support production infrastructure
- Improve operational reliability
- Assist with automation
- Strengthen observability
- Follow engineering standards
- Collaborate across engineering teams
- Prepare for the Site Reliability Engineer role

---

Junior Site Reliability Engineers contribute to daily production operations while working under the supervision of Site Reliability Engineers and Senior Site Reliability Engineers.
