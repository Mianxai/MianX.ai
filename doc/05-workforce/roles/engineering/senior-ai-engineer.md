# Senior AI Engineer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Senior AI Engineer at MIANX.

A Senior AI Engineer is a senior Individual Contributor (IC) responsible for designing, developing, deploying, and optimizing enterprise AI systems, Large Language Model (LLM) applications, AI agents, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) platforms, intelligent automation solutions, and AI-powered products.

Senior AI Engineers provide technical leadership, mentor AI engineers, establish engineering best practices, and ensure AI solutions are reliable, scalable, secure, cost-efficient, and aligned with business objectives.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Senior AI Engineer role is to deliver production-grade AI solutions that improve business operations, automate workflows, enhance customer experiences, and accelerate organizational innovation.

---

# Mission

The Senior AI Engineer exists to:

- Build production AI systems
- Deliver enterprise AI solutions
- Improve AI reliability
- Optimize AI performance
- Mentor AI engineers
- Advance AI engineering practices
- Support responsible AI adoption
- Enable intelligent automation
