# AI Engineer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the AI Engineer at MIANX.

An AI Engineer is responsible for designing, developing, deploying, and maintaining AI-powered applications, Large Language Model (LLM) integrations, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems, AI agents, intelligent automation workflows, and enterprise AI services.

AI Engineers transform business requirements into production-ready AI solutions while ensuring reliability, security, scalability, cost efficiency, and compliance with organizational AI standards.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the AI Engineer role is to build reliable AI-powered software that enhances products, automates business processes, improves customer experiences, and supports data-driven decision-making.

---

# Mission

The AI Engineer exists to:

- Build enterprise AI applications
- Develop intelligent automation
- Integrate LLMs into products
- Improve AI reliability
- Optimize AI performance
- Support responsible AI
- Deliver business value
- Continuously improve AI systems
