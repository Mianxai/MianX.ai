# Junior Embedded Engineer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Junior Embedded Engineer at MIANX.

A Junior Embedded Engineer is an entry-level engineering professional responsible for assisting in the development, testing, deployment, maintenance, and support of embedded software, firmware, device drivers, and hardware-integrated systems while building expertise in embedded systems engineering.

The role emphasizes structured learning, engineering discipline, secure firmware development, hardware interaction, real-time programming, and collaboration under the guidance of senior engineering staff.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Junior Embedded Engineer role is to develop skilled embedded engineers through practical experience, mentorship, and continuous learning while supporting the delivery of reliable embedded products.

---

# Mission

The Junior Embedded Engineer exists to:

- Learn embedded systems engineering
- Assist firmware development
- Support hardware integration
- Improve software quality
- Maintain embedded platforms
- Follow engineering standards
- Produce quality documentation
- Prepare for the Embedded Engineer role
