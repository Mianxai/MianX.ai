# Senior Site Reliability Engineer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Senior Site Reliability Engineer at MIANX.

A Senior Site Reliability Engineer (Senior SRE) is a senior Individual Contributor (IC) responsible for improving production reliability, availability, scalability, observability, automation, and operational excellence across all engineering platforms.

Senior SREs lead reliability initiatives, mentor engineers, reduce operational risk, improve incident response, and ensure production systems consistently meet established Service Level Objectives (SLOs).

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Senior Site Reliability Engineer role is to build highly reliable, scalable, observable, and resilient production systems while enabling engineering teams to deliver software with confidence.

---

# Mission

The Senior Site Reliability Engineer exists to:

- Improve system reliability
- Increase service availability
- Automate operations
- Strengthen observability
- Reduce operational risk
- Improve incident response
- Mentor SRE engineers
- Drive operational excellence
