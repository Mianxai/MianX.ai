# Engineering Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, leadership expectations, and performance standards of the Engineering Manager (EM) at MIANX.

The Engineering Manager is responsible for leading engineering teams, enabling engineers to perform at their highest potential, ensuring reliable software delivery, and aligning engineering execution with the company's technical strategy.

Unlike a Technical Lead, an Engineering Manager focuses primarily on **people, delivery, planning, and team health**, while remaining technically capable enough to support engineering decisions.

---

# Purpose

The Engineering Manager exists to build high-performing engineering teams that consistently deliver secure, scalable, reliable, and maintainable software.

---

# Mission

The Engineering Manager exists to:

- Build great engineering teams
- Enable developer success
- Improve engineering productivity
- Deliver projects successfully
- Grow future technical leaders
- Maintain engineering quality
- Improve team collaboration
- Scale engineering processes

---

# Role Summary

The Engineering Manager bridges business objectives and engineering execution.

The role balances:

- People Leadership
- Delivery Management
- Technical Oversight
- Engineering Culture
- Team Growth
- Cross-functional Collaboration

The Engineering Manager is accountable for team success—not individual heroics.

---

# Primary Responsibilities

The Engineering Manager is responsible for:

- Team Leadership
- Sprint Planning
- Delivery Management
- Resource Planning
- Hiring Support
- Performance Reviews
- Career Development
- Technical Coordination
- Risk Management
- Engineering Operations

---

# Leadership Responsibilities

The Engineering Manager leads:

- Software Engineers
- Senior Engineers
- Junior Engineers
- Interns
- Technical Leads *(where applicable)*

Responsibilities include:

- Coaching engineers
- Removing blockers
- Delegating work
- Resolving conflicts
- Encouraging ownership
- Supporting professional growth
- Maintaining team morale

---

# Engineering Responsibilities

The Engineering Manager oversees:

- Sprint execution
- Project timelines
- Code quality
- Technical documentation
- Release readiness
- Engineering standards
- Development processes
- Team collaboration

The Engineering Manager should understand technical decisions without becoming the bottleneck.

---

# Delivery Responsibilities

The Engineering Manager ensures:

- Projects remain on schedule
- Risks are identified early
- Dependencies are managed
- Priorities remain aligned
- Stakeholders stay informed
- Releases meet quality standards

Delivery should never sacrifice long-term engineering quality.

---

# Technical Responsibilities

The Engineering Manager supports:

- Architecture discussions
- Technical planning
- Code review processes
- Engineering standards
- CI/CD adoption
- Engineering automation
- Technical debt management
- Production readiness

Final architectural authority belongs to the Technical Lead, Architect, or CTO depending on project scope.

---

# Decision Authority

The Engineering Manager has authority over:

- Sprint planning
- Team workload
- Task assignments
- Delivery scheduling
- Team processes
- Performance feedback
- Engineering ceremonies
- Hiring recommendations
- Resource allocation
- Team improvement initiatives

Major architectural decisions require Technical Lead or CTO approval.

---

# Hiring Responsibilities

The Engineering Manager participates in:

- Interviewing candidates
- Technical evaluations
- Hiring recommendations
- Onboarding
- Mentoring new engineers
- Probation reviews

Hiring should prioritize long-term team quality over short-term staffing needs.

---

# Performance Management

The Engineering Manager conducts:

- One-on-one meetings
- Quarterly reviews
- Goal setting
- Career planning
- Performance feedback
- Development planning
- Promotion recommendations

Feedback should be regular, constructive, and actionable.
