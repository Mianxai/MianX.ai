# Blockchain Architect

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Blockchain Architect at MIANX.

The Blockchain Architect is responsible for designing, governing, and evolving the organization's blockchain architecture, distributed ledger platforms, smart contract standards, tokenization frameworks, decentralized application (dApp) architecture, and enterprise blockchain strategy.

The Blockchain Architect ensures blockchain solutions are secure, scalable, interoperable, compliant, and aligned with organizational business objectives.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Blockchain Architect role is to establish a secure, scalable, and enterprise-ready blockchain ecosystem that enables trusted digital transactions, decentralized applications, smart contracts, digital identity, and distributed business processes.

---

# Mission

The Blockchain Architect exists to:

- Design enterprise blockchain architecture
- Standardize blockchain engineering
- Build secure decentralized systems
- Improve distributed trust
- Govern blockchain platforms
- Enable Web3 innovation
- Strengthen blockchain security
- Drive enterprise adoption
