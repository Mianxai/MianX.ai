# Senior IoT Engineer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Senior IoT Engineer at MIANX.

A Senior IoT Engineer is a senior Individual Contributor (IC) responsible for designing, developing, deploying, optimizing, and maintaining enterprise Internet of Things (IoT) solutions, connected devices, edge computing services, telemetry platforms, and cloud-integrated IoT systems.

Senior IoT Engineers provide technical leadership while ensuring IoT platforms remain secure, scalable, resilient, observable, and production-ready throughout the device lifecycle.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Senior IoT Engineer role is to deliver secure, reliable, and scalable IoT solutions that connect intelligent devices with enterprise platforms, enabling real-time monitoring, automation, analytics, and operational efficiency.

---

# Mission

The Senior IoT Engineer exists to:

- Build enterprise IoT platforms
- Develop connected device solutions
- Improve edge computing systems
- Strengthen IoT security
- Optimize telemetry pipelines
- Mentor engineering teams
- Deliver production-ready IoT software
- Drive engineering excellence
