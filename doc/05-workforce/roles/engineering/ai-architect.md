# AI Architect

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the AI Architect at MIANX.

The AI Architect is responsible for designing, governing, and evolving the organization's artificial intelligence architecture, ensuring AI systems are scalable, secure, reliable, ethical, cost-efficient, and aligned with business objectives.

Unlike AI Engineers who implement AI solutions, the AI Architect defines enterprise-wide AI architecture, model integration standards, inference infrastructure, AI governance, agent architecture, prompt engineering standards, and long-term AI strategy.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the AI Architect role is to establish a secure, scalable, and maintainable AI ecosystem that accelerates business innovation while ensuring responsible and reliable AI adoption.

---

The AI Architect exists to:

- Build enterprise AI architecture
- Enable AI-powered products
