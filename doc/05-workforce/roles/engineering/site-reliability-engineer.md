# Site Reliability Engineer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Site Reliability Engineer (SRE) at MIANX.

A Site Reliability Engineer (SRE) is responsible for maintaining highly available, scalable, observable, secure, and resilient production systems through automation, monitoring, incident management, performance optimization, and operational excellence.

Site Reliability Engineers bridge software engineering and infrastructure operations by applying engineering principles to production systems instead of relying on manual operational work.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Site Reliability Engineer role is to improve production reliability, reduce operational toil, automate infrastructure management, and ensure services consistently meet defined Service Level Objectives (SLOs).

---

# Mission

The Site Reliability Engineer exists to:

- Improve production reliability
- Increase system availability
- Automate operational tasks
- Enhance observability
- Reduce operational risk
- Improve incident response
- Support engineering teams
- Continuously optimize production systems
