# Senior Platform Engineer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Senior Platform Engineer at MIANX.

A Senior Platform Engineer is a senior Individual Contributor (IC) responsible for designing, building, operating, and continuously improving the Internal Developer Platform (IDP), shared engineering services, automation systems, and developer tooling.

Senior Platform Engineers provide technical leadership, mentor platform engineers, improve developer productivity, and ensure engineering platforms remain secure, scalable, maintainable, and highly available.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Senior Platform Engineer role is to build and evolve reliable engineering platforms that accelerate software delivery, simplify development workflows, and enable engineering teams to operate efficiently at scale.

---

# Mission

The Senior Platform Engineer exists to:

- Build reliable platform services
- Improve developer experience
- Automate engineering workflows
- Standardize engineering platforms
- Enhance platform security
- Mentor platform engineers
- Improve engineering productivity
- Deliver long-term platform value
