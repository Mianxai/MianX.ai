# Junior AI Engineer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Junior AI Engineer at MIANX.

A Junior AI Engineer is an entry-level engineering professional responsible for assisting in the development, testing, deployment, and maintenance of AI-powered applications, Large Language Model (LLM) integrations, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems, AI agents, and intelligent automation solutions while learning modern AI engineering practices.

The role focuses on building practical experience in AI software development, prompt engineering, API integrations, vector databases, AI security, monitoring, and responsible AI under the guidance of senior AI engineers.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Junior AI Engineer role is to develop highly skilled AI engineers through structured mentorship, hands-on engineering experience, and continuous learning while supporting the delivery of reliable enterprise AI solutions.

---

# Mission

The Junior AI Engineer exists to:

- Learn AI engineering
- Build AI applications
- Support enterprise AI platforms
- Improve AI quality
- Assist intelligent automation
- Follow responsible AI practices
- Maintain engineering documentation
- Prepare for the AI Engineer role
