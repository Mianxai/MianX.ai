# Robotics Engineer

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Robotics Engineer at MIANX.

A Robotics Engineer is responsible for designing, developing, testing, deploying, maintaining, and supporting robotic software, autonomous systems, perception pipelines, motion control systems, and hardware-integrated robotic platforms.

Robotics Engineers collaborate with Embedded Engineers, AI Engineers, Platform Engineers, Cloud Engineers, Security Engineers, QA Engineers, Manufacturing teams, and Product Managers to deliver reliable, secure, and scalable robotic solutions.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Robotics Engineer role is to develop intelligent robotic systems that enable automation, autonomous operation, precision control, and real-world interaction while meeting enterprise quality, safety, and operational standards.

---

# Mission

The Robotics Engineer exists to:

- Build robotic software
- Develop autonomous capabilities
- Integrate robotic hardware
- Improve perception systems
- Optimize motion control
- Support production robots
- Follow engineering standards
- Deliver reliable robotic solutions
