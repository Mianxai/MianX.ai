# Head of Product

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Head of Product at MIANX.

The Head of Product is responsible for leading one or more product domains, managing Product Managers and Product Owners, executing product strategy, and ensuring successful delivery of customer-centric products. The role bridges executive strategy with operational product execution while maintaining alignment with business objectives.

The Head of Product reports to the Director of Product.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Head of Product role is to lead product teams in delivering innovative, high-quality products that achieve customer satisfaction, business growth, and strategic objectives.

---

# Mission

The Head of Product exists to:

- Lead product teams
- Execute product strategy
- Deliver customer value
- Drive product innovation
- Improve product execution
- Build high-performing teams
- Align products with business goals
- Foster continuous improvement

# Product Strategy Responsibilities

The Head of Product executes product strategy through:

- Translating strategic goals into actionable roadmaps
- Defining product objectives
- Prioritizing initiatives
- Validating market opportunities
- Monitoring competitive trends
- Supporting innovation initiatives
- Measuring business outcomes
- Aligning products with organizational strategy

Strategic execution should remain aligned with organizational priorities.
