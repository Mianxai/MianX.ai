# Product Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Product Manager at MIANX.

The Product Manager is responsible for planning, developing, launching, and continuously improving products that solve customer problems and deliver measurable business value. The Product Manager works closely with Engineering, Design, Marketing, Sales, Customer Success, and other stakeholders to ensure products meet customer needs and organizational objectives.

The Product Manager reports to the Head of Product or Senior Product Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Product Manager role is to transform business objectives and customer needs into successful products by leading product planning, execution, and continuous improvement throughout the product lifecycle.

---

# Mission

The Product Manager exists to:

- Understand customer needs
- Deliver valuable products
- Execute product strategy
- Prioritize product initiatives
- Coordinate cross-functional teams
- Improve customer satisfaction
- Increase product adoption
- Drive continuous improvement

---

The Product Manager is responsible for:

- Product Discovery
- Product Requirements
- Roadmap Execution
- Backlog Management
- Feature Prioritization
- Product Delivery
- Stakeholder Communication
- Product Analysis
- Continuous Improvement

---

# Product Planning Responsibilities

The Product Manager is responsible for:

- Defining product goals
- Maintaining product roadmaps
- Prioritizing product features
- Preparing product requirements
- Defining user stories
- Establishing acceptance criteria
- Planning product releases
- Measuring product outcomes

Planning activities should align with organizational strategy and customer needs.

---

# Customer Responsibilities

The Product Manager ensures:

- Customer interviews
- User research
- Market research
- Feedback collection
- Requirement validation
- Customer satisfaction
- Product usability
- Customer value delivery

Customer insights should influence all product decisions.

---

# Product Delivery Responsibilities

The Product Manager coordinates:

- Sprint Planning
- Feature Delivery
- Release Planning
- Product Reviews
- Product Demonstrations
- Feature Validation
- Delivery Tracking
- Product Launch Activities

Product delivery should be predictable, transparent, and customer-focused.

---

# Product Lifecycle Responsibilities

The Product Manager manages:

- Product Discovery
- Product Development
- Product Launch
- Product Growth
- Product Optimization
- Product Maintenance
- Product Retirement
- Product Performance Reviews

Lifecycle management should maximize long-term product value.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The Product Manager oversees:

- Product Backlogs
- Product Metrics
- Feature Tracking
- Roadmap Updates
- Delivery Status
- Product Risks
- Stakeholder Updates
- Documentation Maintenance

Operational activities should support efficient product execution.

---

# Decision Authority

The Product Manager may approve:

- Product Backlog Items
- User Stories
- Acceptance Criteria
- Feature Priorities
- Sprint Scope Recommendations
- Product Documentation
- Minor Product Improvements
- Product Release Readiness

Major roadmap or investment decisions require approval from the Head of Product.

---

# Collaboration

The Product Manager collaborates with:

- Senior Product Manager
- Technical Product Manager
- Product Owners
- Engineering Teams
- UX/UI Designers
- QA Engineers
- Marketing
- Sales
- Customer Success
- Data & Analytics Teams

Cross-functional collaboration is essential for successful product delivery.

---

# Core Competencies

The Product Manager should demonstrate expertise in:

- Product Management
- Product Discovery
- Agile Methodologies
- Scrum Framework
- Roadmap Planning
- Business Analysis
- Customer Research
- Communication
- Prioritization
- Problem Solving

# Performance Expectations

The Product Manager is expected to:

- Deliver product roadmap commitments
- Improve customer satisfaction
- Increase product adoption
- Deliver valuable features
- Maintain product quality
- Improve stakeholder communication
- Support business objectives
- Continuously improve products

---

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Objective |
|------|-----------|
| Roadmap Delivery | Complete planned roadmap objectives |
| Feature Delivery | Deliver features on schedule |
| Product Adoption | Increase active product usage |
| Customer Satisfaction | Improve user experience |
| Feature Success Rate | Deliver high-value features |
| Product Quality | Reduce defects and issues |
| Stakeholder Satisfaction | Improve collaboration |
| Documentation Quality | Maintain complete product documentation |
| Business Value | Deliver measurable outcomes |
| Continuous Improvement | Improve product processes |

Performance evaluations emphasize execution quality, customer value, collaboration, product success, and business contribution.
