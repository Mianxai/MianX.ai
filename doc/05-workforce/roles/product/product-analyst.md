# Product Analyst

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Product Analyst at MIANX.

The Product Analyst is responsible for collecting, analyzing, and interpreting product, customer, and business data to support product decisions. The role provides actionable insights that help Product Managers prioritize features, improve user experience, optimize product performance, and achieve business objectives.

The Product Analyst reports to the Product Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Product Analyst role is to transform product and customer data into meaningful insights that improve product strategy, decision-making, and business outcomes.

---

# Mission

The Product Analyst exists to:

- Analyze product performance
- Understand customer behavior
- Generate actionable insights
- Improve product decisions
- Support product planning
- Monitor business metrics
- Identify growth opportunities
- Drive data-informed decision making

---

# Role Summary

The Product Analyst supports:

- Product Analytics
- Customer Analytics
- Business Analysis
- Product Research
- KPI Reporting
- Product Dashboards
- Market Analysis
- Feature Performance Analysis
- Product Experiments
- Decision Support

# Primary Responsibilities

The Product Analyst is responsible for:

- Product Data Analysis
- KPI Monitoring
- Dashboard Development

- Market Research

- Business Intelligence

# Product Analytics Responsibilities

The Product Analyst performs:

- Feature Usage Analysis
- Funnel Analysis
- User Journey Analysis
- Product Adoption Analysis
- Retention Analysis
- Engagement Analysis
- Cohort Analysis
- Product Performance Monitoring

Analytics should provide clear and actionable recommendations.

---

# Customer Analysis Responsibilities

The Product Analyst supports:

- Customer Segmentation
- User Behavior Analysis
- Customer Satisfaction Analysis
- Customer Feedback Analysis
- Churn Analysis
- Retention Measurement
- Customer Journey Mapping
- User Persona Development

Customer insights should influence product strategy.

---

# Business Analysis Responsibilities

The Product Analyst contributes to:

- Business Metrics
- Revenue Analysis
- Conversion Analysis
- Market Research
- Competitive Analysis
- Opportunity Identification
- Product Forecasting
- Business Case Support

Business analysis should align with organizational objectives.

---

# Reporting Responsibilities

The Product Analyst prepares:

- KPI Dashboards
- Product Reports
- Executive Reports
- Weekly Metrics
- Monthly Performance Reports
- Customer Reports
- Feature Performance Reports
- Product Health Reports

Reports should be accurate, timely, and understandable.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The Product Analyst manages:

- Product Metrics
- Analytics Dashboards
- Data Validation
- Report Automation
- Data Quality Monitoring
- Documentation
- Stakeholder Requests
- Product Insights Repository

Operational activities should improve decision-making efficiency.

---

# Decision Authority

The Product Analyst may:

- Recommend Product Improvements
- Suggest KPI Changes
- Identify Product Opportunities
- Recommend Process Improvements
- Prepare Business Reports
- Publish Product Dashboards
- Conduct Product Research
- Recommend Experiments

Strategic product decisions remain the responsibility of Product Management.

---

# Collaboration

The Product Analyst collaborates with:

- Product Managers
- Product Owners
- Engineering Teams
- UX/UI Designers
- Marketing
- Sales
- Customer Success
- Data Engineering
- Business Intelligence
- Executive Leadership

Cross-functional collaboration ensures analytical insights translate into business value.

The Product Analyst should demonstrate expertise in:

# Performance Expectations

The Product Analyst is expected to:

- Deliver accurate product insights
- Improve reporting quality
- Support product decisions
- Maintain high-quality dashboards
- Improve KPI visibility
- Identify optimization opportunities
- Collaborate effectively
- Promote data-driven decision making

# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Objective |
|------|-----------|
| Report Accuracy | Maintain accurate reporting |
| Dashboard Availability | Ensure reliable analytics dashboards |
| KPI Coverage | Monitor all critical product metrics |

| Customer Analysis | Improve understanding of customer behavior |
| Data Quality | Maintain reliable product data |
| Report Timeliness | Deliver reports on schedule |
| Stakeholder Satisfaction | Improve confidence in analytics |
| Product Decision Support | Contribute valuable business insights |
| Continuous Improvement | Enhance analytics capabilities |
