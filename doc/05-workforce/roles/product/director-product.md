# Director of Product

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Director of Product at MIANX.

The Director of Product is a senior product leader responsible for managing one or more product portfolios, leading Product Managers, aligning execution with business strategy, and ensuring successful product delivery across multiple teams.

The Director of Product reports to the Vice President of Product and acts as the bridge between executive strategy and day-to-day product execution.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Director of Product role is to ensure strategic product initiatives are successfully planned, executed, measured, and continuously improved while maximizing customer and business value.

---

# Mission

The Director of Product exists to:

- Execute product strategy
- Lead product organizations
- Deliver customer value
- Improve product execution
- Build high-performing teams
- Align business and technology
- Improve operational excellence
- Drive continuous innovation

# Product Strategy Responsibilities

The Director of Product is responsible for:

- Translating strategic objectives into product initiatives
- Defining portfolio roadmaps
- Prioritizing product investments
- Identifying growth opportunities
- Managing product lifecycle decisions
- Supporting innovation initiatives
- Monitoring market trends
- Aligning execution with company objectives

Strategic execution should support organizational priorities established by executive leadership.

---

# Product Portfolio Responsibilities

The Director of Product oversees:

- Multiple Product Lines
- Product Roadmaps
- Feature Prioritization
- Product Releases
- Product Lifecycle
- Customer Value Delivery
- Portfolio Performance
- Product Health Metrics

Portfolio decisions should maximize long-term business value.
