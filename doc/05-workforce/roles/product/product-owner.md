# Product Owner

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Product Owner at MIANX.

The Product Owner is responsible for maximizing product value by managing the Product Backlog, defining product requirements, prioritizing work, and ensuring the Engineering team delivers solutions that satisfy customer needs and business objectives.

The Product Owner acts as the primary liaison between business stakeholders and delivery teams, ensuring that every development iteration contributes measurable value.

The Product Owner reports to the Product Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Product Owner role is to ensure development teams always work on the highest-value initiatives while maintaining a clear, prioritized, and well-defined Product Backlog.

---

# Mission

The Product Owner exists to:

- Maximize product value
- Own the Product Backlog
- Prioritize customer needs
- Support agile delivery
- Improve product quality
- Reduce delivery ambiguity
- Align stakeholders
- Enable successful product releases

# Product Backlog Responsibilities

The Product Owner owns:

- Product Backlog

- User Stories
- Acceptance Criteria

- Bug Prioritization

The Product Owner may approve:
