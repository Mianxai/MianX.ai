# Chief Product Officer (CPO)

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Chief Product Officer (CPO) at MIANX.

The Chief Product Officer (CPO) is the executive responsible for the overall Product organization. The CPO defines the company's product vision, strategy, portfolio, roadmap governance, customer value proposition, and product operating model while ensuring that every product initiative aligns with MIANX's mission, business strategy, and long-term growth objectives.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Chief Product Officer role is to establish and execute a world-class product strategy that enables sustainable business growth, exceptional customer experiences, and market leadership.

---

# Mission

The Chief Product Officer exists to:

- Define product vision
- Lead product strategy
- Align products with business goals
- Drive customer-centric innovation
- Maximize product value
- Build high-performing product teams
- Improve product execution
- Ensure long-term product success

---

# Role Summary

The Chief Product Officer provides executive leadership for the entire Product organization.

The role oversees:

- Product Vision
- Product Strategy
- Product Portfolio
- Product Roadmaps
- Product Governance
- Product Operations
- Customer Value
- Product Innovation
- Product Organization
- Product Performance

The Chief Product Officer is responsible for:

- Enterprise Product Strategy
- Product Portfolio Management
- Executive Product Governance
- Product Investment Decisions
- Product Organization Leadership
- Customer Experience Strategy
- Product Innovation
- Business Alignment
- Executive Reporting

# Product Strategy Responsibilities

The Chief Product Officer defines:

- Long-term Product Vision
- Strategic Objectives
- Product Portfolio Direction
- Market Positioning
- Customer Segmentation
- Competitive Strategy
- Product Differentiation
- Growth Opportunities

All strategic decisions should align with MIANX's corporate objectives.

---

# Product Portfolio Responsibilities

The Chief Product Officer oversees:

- Product Portfolio
- Product Lifecycle
- Product Investments
- Product Prioritization
- Product Expansion
- Product Sunset Decisions
- Innovation Programs
- Strategic Initiatives

The portfolio should maximize customer value while supporting sustainable business growth.

---

# Product Governance Responsibilities

The Chief Product Officer establishes governance for:

- Product Standards
- Roadmap Approval
- Product Funding
- Product Reviews
- Product Policies
- Product Metrics
- Portfolio Governance
- Executive Decision Making

Governance should ensure transparency, accountability, and organizational alignment.

---

# Customer Responsibilities

The Chief Product Officer ensures:

- Customer-Centric Decision Making
- Customer Research
- User Feedback Integration
- Customer Satisfaction
- Product Adoption
- Customer Retention
- Value Delivery
- Continuous Product Improvement

Customer outcomes remain central to every product decision.

---

# Leadership Responsibilities

The Chief Product Officer leads:

- VP Product
- Directors of Product
- Heads of Product
- Product Managers
- Product Operations
- Cross-functional Leadership

Leadership responsibilities include mentoring executives, developing future leaders, and building a high-performance product organization.

---

# Financial Responsibilities

The Chief Product Officer contributes to:

- Product Budget Planning
- Investment Strategy
- Portfolio ROI
- Business Case Reviews
- Resource Allocation
- Growth Forecasting
- Revenue Strategy
- Executive Planning

Financial decisions should maximize long-term business value.

---

# Decision Authority

The Chief Product Officer has authority over:

- Product Strategy
- Product Portfolio
- Product Investments
- Product Roadmaps
- Product Organization
- Product Governance
- Executive Product Priorities
- Product Standards
- Product Policies
- Strategic Product Initiatives

The Chief Product Officer is expected to:

- Deliver successful product strategy
- Increase customer satisfaction
- Improve product adoption
- Maximize portfolio value
- Strengthen organizational capability
- Improve execution quality
- Drive innovation
- Deliver measurable business outcomes
