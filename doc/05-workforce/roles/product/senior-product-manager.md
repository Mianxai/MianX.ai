# Senior Product Manager

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Senior Product Manager at MIANX.

The Senior Product Manager is responsible for leading strategic products, mentoring Product Managers, defining product roadmaps, and delivering business value through customer-focused product development. The role combines product strategy, execution, leadership, and cross-functional collaboration to ensure successful product outcomes.

The Senior Product Manager reports to the Head of Product.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Senior Product Manager role is to lead strategic product initiatives that maximize customer value, business growth, and product excellence while developing the next generation of Product Managers.

---

# Mission

The Senior Product Manager exists to:

- Lead strategic products
- Deliver customer value
- Execute product strategy
- Mentor Product Managers
- Improve product quality
- Increase product adoption
- Strengthen cross-functional collaboration
- Drive continuous improvement

---

# Role Summary

The Senior Product Manager oversees:

- Strategic Product Roadmaps
- Product Vision Execution
- Product Lifecycle
- Product Backlogs
- Feature Prioritization
- Customer Research
- Product Metrics
- Product Releases
- Stakeholder Communication
- Product Performance

# Primary Responsibilities

The Senior Product Manager is responsible for:

- Product Strategy Execution
- Roadmap Ownership
- Feature Prioritization
- Product Discovery
- Customer Validation
- Product Delivery
- Product Performance Analysis
- Stakeholder Management
- Product Documentation
- Team Mentorship

---

# Product Strategy Responsibilities

The Senior Product Manager is responsible for:

- Defining product objectives
- Aligning roadmaps with business goals
- Identifying market opportunities
- Evaluating customer needs
- Supporting innovation initiatives
- Prioritizing high-value features
- Measuring product success
- Improving competitive positioning

Product decisions should balance customer value, technical feasibility, and business impact.

---

# Product Delivery Responsibilities

The Senior Product Manager manages:

- Product Backlogs
- Feature Priorities
- Sprint Objectives
- Release Planning
- Product Roadmaps
- Product Milestones
- Cross-functional Dependencies
- Delivery Reviews

Delivery should be predictable, measurable, and customer-focused.

---

# Customer Responsibilities

The Senior Product Manager ensures:

- Customer Research
- User Interviews
- Feedback Analysis
- Product Validation
- Customer Satisfaction
- Product Adoption
- Feature Success Measurement
- Continuous Product Improvement

Customer insights should guide roadmap decisions.

---

# Leadership Responsibilities

The Senior Product Manager:

- Mentors Product Managers
- Coaches Associate Product Managers
- Supports Product Owners
- Reviews Product Documentation
- Shares product best practices
- Promotes collaboration
- Improves product processes
- Supports hiring activities

Leadership is demonstrated through influence, coaching, and execution rather than direct organizational authority.

---

# Operational Responsibilities

The Senior Product Manager manages:

- Quarterly Planning
- Product Reviews
- Feature Tracking
- Delivery Metrics
- Product Risks
- Roadmap Updates
- Stakeholder Reporting
- Continuous Process Improvement

Operational excellence supports efficient product delivery.

---

# Decision Authority

The Senior Product Manager may approve:

- Product Backlogs
- Feature Priorities
- User Stories
- Acceptance Criteria
- Product Requirements
- Release Scope
- Product Documentation
- Product Improvements

Major strategic roadmap changes require approval from the Head of Product.

The Senior Product Manager collaborates with:

- Head of Product
- Technical Product Manager
- Product Managers
- Engineering Managers
- Software Architects
- UX/UI Designers
- QA Engineers
- Marketing
- Sales
- Customer Success
- Data Analytics Teams

Cross-functional collaboration ensures successful product delivery.

The Senior Product Manager is expected to:

- Deliver successful product initiatives
- Increase customer satisfaction
- Improve product adoption
- Deliver roadmap commitments
- Mentor product team members
- Strengthen product quality
- Improve delivery predictability
- Generate measurable business value
