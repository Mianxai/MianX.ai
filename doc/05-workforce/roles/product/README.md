# Product Roles

> This directory defines the Product career framework at MIANX.

The Product organization is responsible for defining product vision, strategy, roadmap, customer value, business alignment, feature prioritization, and successful product delivery across the MIANX platform ecosystem.

The Product career framework establishes standardized responsibilities, authority, expectations, reporting structures, and career progression for every Product role within the organization.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this directory is to provide standardized documentation for Product roles to ensure:

- Consistent job responsibilities
- Clear ownership boundaries
- Defined reporting structures
- Standardized career progression
- Performance expectations
- Hiring consistency
- Promotion guidelines
- Organizational alignment

---

# Guiding Principles

Every Product professional at MIANX is expected to:

- Be customer-centric
- Make data-informed decisions
- Align products with business goals
- Collaborate across departments
- Deliver measurable value
- Prioritize quality over quantity
- Communicate clearly
- Continuously improve products
- Encourage innovation
- Maintain accountability

# Career Levels

The Product career framework consists of the following levels:

| Level | Role |
|--------|------|
| Executive | Chief Product Officer |
| Executive Leadership | VP Product |
| Department Leadership | Director of Product |
| Department Leadership | Head of Product |
| Senior Individual Contributor | Technical Product Manager |
| Senior Individual Contributor | Senior Product Manager |
| Professional | Product Manager |
| Professional | Product Owner |
| Entry Level | Associate Product Manager |
| Entry Level | Product Analyst |

# Career Progression

Typical Product career progression:

```text
Product Analyst

↓

Associate Product Manager

↓

Product Manager

↓

Senior Product Manager

↓

Technical Product Manager

↓

Head of Product

↓

Director of Product

↓

VP Product

↓

Chief Product Officer
```

Career progression is based on demonstrated leadership, business impact, product expertise, strategic thinking, collaboration, and organizational contribution.

- Roadmaps align with business strategy
