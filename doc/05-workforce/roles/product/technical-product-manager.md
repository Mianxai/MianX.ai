# Technical Product Manager (TPM)

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Technical Product Manager (TPM) at MIANX.

The Technical Product Manager (TPM) is responsible for managing highly technical products, platforms, APIs, developer tools, infrastructure capabilities, and engineering initiatives that require deep technical understanding. The TPM serves as the bridge between Product, Engineering, Architecture, Platform, Security, Data, and DevOps teams to ensure technically sound and business-aligned product delivery.

The Technical Product Manager reports to the Head of Product.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Technical Product Manager role is to translate complex technical capabilities into valuable products by aligning engineering execution with business objectives and customer needs.

---

# Mission

The Technical Product Manager exists to:

- Bridge business and engineering
- Define technical product strategy
- Deliver scalable technical solutions
- Improve developer experience
- Enable engineering excellence
- Drive platform evolution
- Reduce technical complexity
- Deliver measurable business value
