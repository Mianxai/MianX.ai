# Vice President of Product (VP Product)

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Vice President of Product (VP Product) at MIANX.

The Vice President of Product is the senior product executive responsible for translating the company's product vision into executable strategies, leading multiple product organizations, and ensuring successful delivery of product initiatives that support business growth, customer success, and operational excellence.

The VP Product serves as the primary operational leader of the Product organization, reporting directly to the Chief Product Officer.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Vice President of Product role is to transform strategic product vision into measurable business outcomes through effective leadership, product execution, and cross-functional collaboration.

---

# Mission

The VP Product exists to:

- Execute product strategy
- Lead product organizations
- Deliver business value
- Improve product execution
- Build high-performing teams
- Strengthen customer focus
- Drive product innovation
- Achieve strategic objectives

# Product Strategy Responsibilities

The VP Product translates strategic objectives into operational execution by:

- Defining annual product objectives
- Developing execution plans
- Aligning departmental roadmaps
- Managing strategic initiatives
- Coordinating product investments
- Monitoring strategic progress
- Managing execution risks
- Supporting innovation initiatives

Execution should remain aligned with the vision established by the Chief Product Officer.
