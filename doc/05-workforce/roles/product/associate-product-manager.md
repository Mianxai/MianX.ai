# Associate Product Manager (APM)

> This document defines the responsibilities, authority, expectations, and performance standards of the Associate Product Manager (APM) at MIANX.

The Associate Product Manager (APM) is an early-career Product professional responsible for supporting Product Managers in product discovery, planning, delivery, customer research, and product analysis. The APM develops practical product management skills while contributing to successful product execution across the organization.

The Associate Product Manager reports to the Product Manager or Senior Product Manager.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Associate Product Manager role is to develop future Product Leaders by providing hands-on experience in product management, customer research, product delivery, and cross-functional collaboration.

---

# Mission

The Associate Product Manager exists to:

- Support product delivery
- Learn product management practices
- Understand customer needs
- Assist product planning
- Improve product quality
- Analyze product performance
- Strengthen collaboration
- Build product expertise

---

# Decision Authority

The Associate Product Manager may:

- Draft User Stories
- Recommend Feature Priorities
- Prepare Product Documentation
- Conduct Research
- Maintain Product Backlogs
- Recommend Process Improvements
- Assist Release Activities
- Update Product Records

Final product decisions remain the responsibility of the Product Manager or higher leadership.
