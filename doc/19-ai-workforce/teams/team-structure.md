# Mianx.ai Enterprise Human and AI Team Structure Standard

> **This document defines the governed target-state structure for Human,
> AI Agent, and hybrid Human-AI Teams across Mianx.ai, including Team
> identity, purpose, ownership, leadership, membership, Role allocation,
> capability allocation, capacity, reporting, Department relationships,
> Product and Project relationships, Customer and Tenant boundaries,
> lifecycle, evidence, audit, and Production activation gates.**

# 8. Team Structure Principles

All Team design should follow:

1. Founder sovereignty;
2. qualified Human accountability;
3. purpose before membership;
4. Team identity before activation;
5. Role before responsibility assignment;
6. capability before workload commitment;
7. capacity before additional work;
8. least privilege;
9. Customer isolation;
10. Tenant isolation;
11. Product and Project scope separation;
12. explicit reporting;
13. explicit decision rights;
14. explicit escalation;
15. separation of duties where required;
16. Human and Agent accountability must remain distinguishable;
17. Team membership must be versioned;
18. Team changes must be auditable;
19. dormant Teams must not retain unnecessary authority;
20. documentation must not be represented as runtime activation.

---

# 9. Team Authority

Team authority defines what a Team may:

- execute;
- decide;
- recommend;
- approve;
- request;
- delegate;
- escalate;
- access;
- communicate;
- coordinate;
- own operationally.

Authority must be derived from:

- enterprise Governance;
- Department authority;
- Role authority;
- Product authority;
- Project authority;
- Customer scope;
- Tenant scope;
- workflow authority;
- Task authority.

---

# 10. Founder Sovereignty

The Founder retains authority over matters including:

- Company identity;
- Company vision;
- Company mission;
- AI Constitution;
- Founder-reserved executive authority;
- enterprise structure;
- major autonomous workforce strategy;
- unrestricted AI autonomy;
- existential Company Risk;
- matters explicitly reserved to the Founder.

No Team may:

- create Founder authority;
- claim Founder representation without evidence;
- override Founder-reserved decisions;
- convert Team consensus into Founder approval.

---

# 11. Qualified Human Accountability

Qualified Human accountability remains mandatory for high-impact Team activity
involving:

- legal matters;
- financial commitments;
- employment decisions;
- Security;
- Privacy;
- Ethics;
- Compliance;
- Customer contracts;
- Production approval;
- high-impact Agent autonomy;
- major Incident closure.

AI-majority Teams do not remove Human accountability requirements.

# 14. Team Version

Team structure changes must be versioned.

Recommended format:

```text
MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH
```

Examples:

```text
1.0.0

1.1.0

2.0.0
```

---

# 15. Team Version Change Rules

Increase `MAJOR` when materially changing:

- Team purpose;
- Team authority;
- parent Department;
- permanent ownership;
- major operating model;
- Customer isolation model.

Increase `MINOR` when materially changing:

- membership;
- capabilities;
- Role composition;
- Tool access;
- Model access;
- Project assignments.

Use `PATCH` for:

- non-material metadata corrections;
- formatting;
- clarification.
