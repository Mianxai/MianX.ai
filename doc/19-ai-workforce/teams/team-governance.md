# 8. Governance Principles

Team Governance must follow:

1. Founder sovereignty;
2. qualified Human accountability;
3. authority before action;
4. decision rights before autonomous decisions;
5. least privilege;
6. least authority;
7. explicit delegation;
8. no delegation beyond delegator authority;
9. no self-approval where separation is required;
10. inherited policies remain binding;
11. inherited standards remain binding;
12. Customer isolation;
13. Tenant isolation;
14. Product and Project authority separation;
15. Human and Agent authority remain distinguishable;
16. high autonomy requires stronger evidence;
17. exceptions must be time-bounded;
18. violations must be auditable;
19. Governance changes must be versioned;
20. documentation must not be represented as runtime enforcement.

# 10. Founder Sovereignty

The Founder retains ultimate reserved authority over matters explicitly
assigned to the Founder, including:

- Company identity;
- Company vision;
- Company mission;
- AI Constitution;
- enterprise-level structural direction;
- Founder-reserved executive appointments;
- unrestricted AI autonomy;
- existential Company Risk;
- fundamental Company strategy;
- matters explicitly reserved to the Founder.

No Team may:

- fabricate Founder approval;
- infer Founder approval;
- impersonate the Founder;
- convert Team consensus into Founder authority;
- extend a Founder decision outside its approved scope.

# 25. Team Autonomy Model

Recommended Team autonomy levels:

```text
TA0 — OBSERVE

TA1 — ASSIST

TA2 — EXECUTE-WITH-APPROVAL

TA3 — EXECUTE-WITHIN-BOUNDED-RULES

TA4 — BOUNDED-AUTONOMOUS-OPERATION

TA5 — HIGH-AUTONOMY-WITH-ENTERPRISE-GATES
```

No autonomy level creates unrestricted authority.

---

# 26. TA0 — Observe

The Team may:

- read approved information;
- analyze;
- monitor;
- report;
- recommend.

The Team may not execute governed operational actions.

---

# 27. TA1 — Assist

The Team may:

- prepare work;
- draft outputs;
- propose decisions;
- prepare actions;
- provide Human decision support.

Execution requires authorized Human or system action.

---

# 28. TA2 — Execute with Approval

The Team may prepare and stage approved actions.

Each material execution requires valid approval before completion.

---

# 29. TA3 — Execute Within Bounded Rules

The Team may execute approved classes of work when:

- rules are explicit;
- thresholds are defined;
- Customer scope is exact;
- Tenant scope is exact;
- monitoring is active;
- escalation is available.

---

# 30. TA4 — Bounded Autonomous Operation

The Team may autonomously:

- assign approved Tasks;
- coordinate members;
- execute approved workflows;
- make bounded operational decisions;
- escalate exceptions.

TA4 requires stronger runtime controls and evidence.

---

# 31. TA5 — High Autonomy with Enterprise Gates

TA5 is reserved for highly controlled Team operation.

It requires:

- explicit Enterprise Governance authorization;
- exact autonomy envelope;
- continuous monitoring;
- hard stops;
- Human override;
- tested suspension;
- tested rollback;
- evidence;
- Founder approval where required.

TA5 does not mean unrestricted autonomy.

---
