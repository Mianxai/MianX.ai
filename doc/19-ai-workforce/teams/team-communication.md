# Mianx.ai Enterprise Human and AI Team Communication Standard

> **This document defines the governed target-state communication model for
> Human, AI Agent, and hybrid Human-AI Teams across Mianx.ai, including
> Team Message identity, Human-to-Human, Human-to-Agent, Agent-to-Human,
> Agent-to-Agent communication, Team decisions, approvals, Tasks, workflows,
> dependencies, Product and Project communication, Customer and Tenant
> boundaries, meetings, notifications, alerts, acknowledgements,
> confidentiality, evidence, monitoring, audit, and Production gates.**

# 8. Communication Principles

Team communication must follow:

1. Founder sovereignty;
2. qualified Human accountability;
3. identity before authority;
4. authority before commitment;
5. minimum necessary recipients;
6. minimum necessary Data;
7. exact Team scope;
8. exact Product and Project scope;
9. exact Customer and Tenant scope;
10. approved channels;
11. source attribution;
12. clear message classes;
13. decision and approval separation;
14. acknowledgement where required;
15. escalation where required;
16. confidentiality by default for governed internal material;
17. AI-generated communication must remain bounded;
18. material communication must remain traceable;
19. records must preserve context;
20. documentation must not be represented as active runtime enforcement.

# 10. Founder Sovereignty

No Team communication may:

- fabricate Founder statements;
- fabricate Founder approval;
- impersonate the Founder;
- imply Founder consent from silence;
- broaden Founder instructions beyond scope.

Founder-reserved communication must be traceable to valid Founder authority.

|---|---|
