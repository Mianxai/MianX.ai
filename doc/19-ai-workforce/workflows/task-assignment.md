# Mianx.ai Enterprise Human and AI Workforce Task Assignment Standard

> **This document defines the governed target-state standard for assigning
> Tasks to Humans, AI Agents, Teams, or approved hybrid execution structures
> across Mianx.ai. It defines Task identity, assignment authority,
> eligibility, Role, Skill, Capability, Tool, Model, memory, availability,
> capacity, workload, priority, deadline, Risk, Product, Project, Customer,
> Tenant, candidate generation, filtering, scoring, selection, acceptance,
> rejection, reassignment, substitution, delegation relationships,
> separation of duties, isolation, evidence, monitoring, audit, and
> Production Task Assignment gates.**

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of Task Assignment is to determine:

```text
WHO
```

is eligible and authorized to become responsible for executing a governed
Task.

The standard exists to ensure assignments are not based solely on:

- availability;
- speed;
- low cost;
- Agent confidence;
- personal preference;
- Team convenience;
- previous successful execution.

A valid assignment should consider:

- Task identity;
- Task requirements;
- Role;
- skills;
- capabilities;
- authority;
- Tools;
- Models;
- memory;
- autonomy;
- Product;
- Project;
- Customer;
- Tenant;
- Risk;
- capacity;
- workload;
- deadline;
- conflicts;
- separation of duties;
- evidence.

This document does not independently:

- create a Task;
- route a Task through the enterprise;
- execute a Task;
- complete a Task;
- verify Task completion;
- grant Human authority;
- activate an Agent;
- authorize Production operation.

```text
Valid Task
+
Valid Assignment Authority
+
Eligible Candidate
+
Required Role
+
Required Skills
+

+

+

+
```

Assignment Eligibility does not mean Assignment Decision.

Assignment Decision does not mean Task Acceptance.

- speed;
- cost;
- convenience;
- utilization;
- historical performance.

# 7. Assignment Objectives

Task Assignment should optimize for:

- correct actor;
- valid authority;
- appropriate Role;
- sufficient capability;
- safe autonomy;
- correct Customer/Tenant context;
- sufficient capacity;
- predictable delivery;
- quality;
- reliability;
- cost efficiency;
- evidence;
- continuity.

Correctness and authority take precedence over optimization.

---

# 8. Task Assignment Authority

Task Assignment authority determines who or what may assign a Task.

Potential authorized assignment sources may include:

- Workflow Engine;
- Task Governance;
- Team Lead;
- Team Manager;
- Department authority;
- approved Human owner;
- approved orchestration component;
- approved automated assignment engine.

Assignment authority must itself be explicit.

---

# 9. Founder Sovereignty

Task Assignment must not assign Tasks that imply Founder-reserved authority
to subjects without explicit delegation.

Founder-reserved decisions remain governed separately.

---

# 10. Qualified Human Accountability

Qualified Human accountability may be required for Tasks involving:

- Production;
- high-Risk changes;
- financial decisions;
- legal obligations;
- privileged Security;
- sensitive Privacy;
- external Customer commitments;
- high-autonomy AI operation;
- irreversible actions.

---

# 11. Task Assignment Engine Boundary

The Task Assignment Engine determines:

```text
WHO SHOULD RECEIVE RESPONSIBILITY FOR A TASK
```

It does not determine all movement of the Task through the enterprise.

Detailed movement and destination-path selection belong to:

```text
doc/19-ai-workforce/workflows/task-routing.md
```

- Task ID;

- Task owner;

- Step where applicable;
- Product;

- Customer;

# 13. Task ID

Task IDs must be immutable after creation.

```text
TASK-{DOMAIN}-{SEQUENCE}
```

The authoritative format requires Registry approval.

- evaluate multiple eligible candidates;
- use one approved scoring model version;
- preserve all component scores;
- prove hard eligibility failures cannot be overridden;
- record selected candidate;
- preserve evidence.

# 132. Separation-of-Duties Proof

A controlled proof should attempt to assign one conflicting subject to both:

```text
EXECUTION
AND
INDEPENDENT APPROVAL / VERIFICATION
```

where prohibited.

The second assignment must be rejected.

```text
Customer A
Customer B
```

- Customer A eligible candidate;
- Customer A authorization;
- Customer B candidate without Customer A authority;
- Customer B candidate rejected;
- correct assignment retained;
- evidence preserved.
