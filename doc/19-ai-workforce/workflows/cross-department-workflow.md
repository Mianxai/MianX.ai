# Mianx.ai Enterprise Human and AI Workforce Cross-Department Workflow Standard

> **This document defines the governed target-state standard for workflows
> that span two or more Mianx.ai Departments. It establishes Department
> identity, originating and receiving Departments, Workflow ownership,
> Human accountability, Department participation, Teams, Human and AI Agent
> actors, RACI relationships, Task Routing, Task Assignment, handoffs,
> dependencies, Shared Services, service contracts, approvals, decisions,
> escalation, conflict resolution, Product, Project, Customer, Tenant,
> Customer Edition, Data, memory, Tool, Model, Security, Privacy, Ethics,
> Compliance, Risk, evidence, observability, audit, recovery, and
> Production Cross-Department Workflow gates.**

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to ensure that work moving across
Department boundaries remains:

- owned;
- accountable;
- traceable;
- authorized;
- isolated;
- measurable;
- recoverable;
- auditable.

Cross-Department execution exists because enterprise work frequently
requires capabilities owned by different organizational units.

Examples may include:

```text
PRODUCT
→
ENGINEERING
→
QA
→
SECURITY
→
DEVOPS
```

or:

```text
SALES
→
LEGAL
→
FINANCE
→
OPERATIONS
→
CUSTOMER SUCCESS
```

Cross-Department coordination must not erase Department ownership or
authority boundaries.

# 5. Core Cross-Department Rule

Every Cross-Department Workflow must answer:

```text
WHO OWNS THE WORKFLOW?

WHICH DEPARTMENT OWNS EACH RESPONSIBILITY?

WHO IS ACCOUNTABLE?

WHAT IS BEING HANDED OFF?

TO WHOM?

UNDER WHAT AUTHORITY?

WHAT MUST BE ACKNOWLEDGED?

WHAT HAPPENS IF THE HANDOFF FAILS?
```

before execution begins.

# 8. Cross-Department Workflow Authority

Cross-Department authority may govern:

- Department participation;
- Workflow ownership;
- Workflow initiation;
- Task movement;
- handoff;
- dependency resolution;
- approval gates;
- escalation;
- suspension;
- cancellation.

It must not create:

- Founder authority;
- unrestricted Department authority;
- unrestricted Customer authority;
- unrestricted Tenant authority;
- unrestricted Agent autonomy.

---

# 9. Founder Sovereignty

Founder-reserved matters remain Founder-reserved even when several
Departments participate.

Cross-Department consensus does not override Founder authority where a
matter is reserved.

---

# 10. Qualified Human Accountability

Every material Cross-Department Workflow should have an identified
qualified Human Accountable Owner.

Human accountability is especially required where workflows involve:

- Production;
- privileged Security;
- sensitive Personal Data;
- legal commitments;
- financial commitments;
- external Customer commitments;
- high-autonomy AI Agents;
- irreversible operations.

---

# 11. Department Identity

Every participating Department must resolve to one governed Department
identity.

Required references should include:

- Department ID;
- Department name;
- Department owner;
- Department purpose;
- authority scope;
- active status.

# 13. Originating Department

The originating Department is the Department from which the governed
Cross-Department Workflow originates.

It may:

- initiate the Workflow;
- create initial context;
- create initial Task;
- identify required downstream capabilities.

Originating Department does not automatically own every downstream Task.

---

# 14. Receiving Department

A receiving Department accepts work, context, dependency, or service demand
through a governed handoff or route.

Receiving Department responsibilities must be explicit.

---

# 15. Participating Departments

Every participating Department should have:

- participation reason;
- responsibility;
- expected inputs;
- expected outputs;
- authority;
- SLA where applicable;
- escalation path.

- counting upstream completion as end-to-end completion;
- counting Workflow completion before verification.
