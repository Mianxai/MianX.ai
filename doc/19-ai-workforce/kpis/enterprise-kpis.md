# Mianx.ai Enterprise Key Performance Indicators

> **This document defines the governed target-state KPI framework used to
> measure the strategic, Product, Project, Customer, financial, operational,
> workforce, capability, quality, reliability, Security, privacy, compliance,
> Risk, resilience, innovation, growth, Governance, and autonomous-enterprise
> creation performance of Mianx.ai as an enterprise.**

hierarchy:

built on the Mianx.ai platform and AI Operating System.

They are not the complete identity of Mianx.ai.

# 6. Core Enterprise KPI Principles

## 6.1 Enterprise Performance Must Be Strategy-Based

Enterprise KPIs must measure progress against approved:

- vision;
- mission;
- strategic objectives;
- operating model;
- Product portfolio;
- Customer commitments;
- Risk appetite;
- financial plan;
- Governance obligations.

---

## 6.2 Documentation Is Not Enterprise Performance

The following do not independently prove enterprise success:

- document count;
- repository size;
- Agent count in planning;
- Department count in planning;
- capability count in planning;
- Roadmap length;
- architecture completeness;
- prompt count;
- template count.

---

## 6.3 Product Activity Is Not Product Success

The following do not independently prove Product success:

- Product idea;
- Product document;
- landing page;
- repository;
- deployment attempt;
- demo;
- unverified user;
- unverified Customer;
- internal test.

---

## 6.4 Customer Records Must Be Verified

A lead, prospect, test account, or internal stakeholder must not be reported as
a Production Customer without valid evidence.

---

## 6.5 Revenue Must Be Evidence-Based

Revenue must be supported by:

- valid transaction;
- accounting record;
- contract where applicable;
- Customer identity;
- Product or service attribution;
- date;
- currency;
- recognized-revenue policy.

---

## 6.6 Enterprise Averages Must Not Hide Critical Failures

A healthy aggregate score must not hide:

- critical Security failure;
- critical privacy failure;
- critical Customer harm;
- fabricated evidence;
- fabricated approval;
- financial misstatement;
- legal breach;
- regulatory breach;
- cross-Tenant leakage;
- unauthorized Production action.

---

## 6.7 Lower-Level Evidence Must Remain Traceable

Enterprise KPIs must remain traceable to:

- Products;
- Projects;
- Customers;
- Departments;
- Teams;
- Agents;
- Humans;
- capabilities;
- Tasks;
- workflows;
- evidence records.

---

## 6.8 Baselines Must Precede Targets

Numeric Enterprise targets require:

- operating instrumentation;
- verified Data quality;
- sufficient operating period;
- measured baseline;
- financial review;
- Risk review;
- Founder approval.

---

## 6.9 Planned Values Must Be Explicit

Planned Enterprise values must be labelled:

```text
PROPOSED

FORECAST

NOT MEASURED

NOT VERIFIED

NOT APPROVED

NOT A RUNTIME RESULT
```

---

## 6.10 Enterprise KPIs Must Be Comparable

Comparison requires compatible:

- enterprise phase;
- Product portfolio;
- Customer portfolio;
- workforce size;
- capability maturity;
- market;
- financial model;
- Risk;
- period;
- Data quality.

---

## 6.11 Enterprise Performance Must Include Full Cost

Enterprise cost must include:

- Human cost;
- Agent cost;
- Model cost;
- Tool cost;
- infrastructure cost;
- development cost;
- Customer acquisition cost;
- support cost;
- compliance cost;
- incident cost;
- failure cost;
- rework cost;
- capital cost where applicable.

---

## 6.12 Enterprise KPIs Must Be Founder-Reviewable

The Founder must be able to see:

- strategic progress;
- verified results;
- assumptions;
- forecasts;
- Risks;
- limitations;
- critical failures;
- required decisions;
- supporting evidence.
