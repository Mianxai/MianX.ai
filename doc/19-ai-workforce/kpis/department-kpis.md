# Mianx.ai AI Workforce Department Key Performance Indicators

> **This document defines the governed target-state KPI framework used to
> measure the performance, capability coverage, workforce health, delivery,
> quality, reliability, Security, privacy, compliance, cost, capacity,
> collaboration, Customer contribution, Product contribution, Project
> contribution, learning, Governance, and enterprise alignment of each Mianx.ai
> AI Workforce Department.**

---

- prove Department runtime operation;
- establish measured baselines;

# 5. Core Department KPI Principles

## 5.1 Department Performance Must Be Mandate-Based

A Department must be measured against its approved:

- purpose;
- mandate;
- responsibilities;
- Products;
- Projects;
- capabilities;
- service obligations;
- Risk obligations.

---

## 5.2 Department Performance Is Not Agent Activity

The following do not independently prove Department performance:

- number of Agents;
- number of Tasks;
- number of messages;
- number of documents;
- number of Tool calls;
- number of meetings;
- number of hours active.

---

## 5.3 Department Averages Must Not Hide Critical Failures

A healthy average must not hide:

- one confirmed cross-Tenant leak;
- one critical Security incident;
- one critical privacy incident;
- one fabricated approval;
- one fabricated evidence event;
- one unauthorized Production action;
- one unresolved Customer-critical failure.

---

## 5.4 Department KPIs Must Preserve Lower-Level Evidence

Department reporting must retain traceability to:

- Teams;
- Agents;
- workflows;
- Products;
- Projects;
- Tenants;
- Customers;
- Tasks;
- evidence records.

---

## 5.5 Department Performance Must Include Dependencies

Department performance must account for:

- incoming dependencies;
- outgoing dependencies;
- shared services;
- executive decisions;
- Human review;
- Tool availability;
- Model availability;
- budget;
- capacity;
- Product and Project constraints.

---

## 5.6 Failures Must Stay with Their Responsible Scope

A Department must not transfer KPI ownership merely by:

- reassigning a failed Task;
- moving a defect to backlog;
- changing a Task label;
- changing a Project owner;
- excluding an incident from reporting.

---

## 5.7 Baselines Must Precede Numeric Targets

Numeric targets require:

- functioning instrumentation;
- verified Data quality;
- sufficient samples;
- measured baseline;
- workload classification;
- Risk review;
- Human approval.

---

## 5.8 Planned Values Must Be Explicit

Planned Department values must be labelled:

```text
PROPOSED

NOT MEASURED

NOT APPROVED

NOT A RUNTIME RESULT
```

---

## 5.9 Department KPIs Must Be Comparable

Comparison requires compatible:

- Department mandate;
- Department type;
- workload;
- Product;
- Project;
- maturity;
- Risk;
- capacity;
- budget;
- environment;
- Customer obligations.

---

## 5.10 Department KPIs Must Be Auditable

Every material result must be traceable to:

- source records;
- formulas;
- inclusion rules;
- exclusions;
- measurement window;
- Data-quality status;
- evidence;
- reviewer.

# 22. Mandate Coverage

Mandate coverage should identify:

- obligations with active ownership;
- obligations without ownership;
- obligations with missing capabilities;
- obligations with missing Roles;
- obligations with missing Skills;
- obligations blocked by dependencies.

---

# 23. Capability Coverage

```text
Capability Coverage Rate
=
Required Department Capabilities with Valid Approved Coverage
/
Required Department Capabilities
```

A documented capability without runtime proof must not count as active coverage.

---

# 24. Critical Capability Gap Rate

```text
Critical Capability Gap Rate
=
Open R3 and R4 Capability Gaps
/
Required R3 and R4 Capabilities
```

One critical gap may trigger escalation even when average coverage is high.

---

# 25. Role Coverage

```text
Role Coverage Rate
=
Required Roles with Eligible Assigned Coverage
/
Required Department Roles
```

A Role document does not count as assigned Role coverage.

---

# 26. Critical Role Vacancy

A critical Role vacancy exists when:

- a mandatory Role has no eligible Agent or Human;
- separation of duties cannot be maintained;
- required review independence is unavailable;
- emergency coverage is unavailable.

---

# 27. Skill Coverage

```text
Skill Coverage Rate
=
Required Skills with Sufficient Valid Proficiency
/
Required Department Skills
```

Expired evaluations and certifications must not count.

---

# 28. Skill Concentration Risk

```text
Skill Concentration Risk
=
Critical Skills Held by Only One Eligible Performer
/
Critical Department Skills
```

High concentration may indicate a resilience weakness.
