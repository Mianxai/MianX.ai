# Mianx.ai AI Workforce Team Key Performance Indicators

> **This document defines the governed target-state KPI framework used to
> measure mission fulfilment, membership health, leadership effectiveness,
> Role coverage, Skill coverage, capability coverage, capacity, delivery,
> quality, reliability, safety, Security, privacy, compliance, cost,
> collaboration, coordination, communication, handoffs, evidence, learning,
> lifecycle, Product contribution, Project contribution, Tenant contribution,
> and Customer contribution for each Mianx.ai AI Workforce Team.**

# 5. Core Team KPI Principles

## 5.1 Team Performance Must Be Mission-Based

A Team must be measured against its approved:

- mission;
- responsibilities;
- Product scope;
- Project scope;
- Customer obligations;
- capability obligations;
- workflow obligations;
- Risk obligations;
- delivery commitments.

---

## 5.2 Team Activity Is Not Team Performance

The following must not independently count as Team success:

- message count;
- Task count;
- document count;
- meeting count;
- token count;
- Tool-call count;
- working time;
- member count;
- Agent count.

---

## 5.3 Team Performance Is Not the Sum of Agent Activity

Team performance includes:

- coordination;
- handoffs;
- role coverage;
- shared delivery;
- conflict handling;
- dependency handling;
- evidence;
- collective outcomes;
- Team-level Risk.

---

## 5.4 Team Averages Must Not Hide Critical Member Failures

A healthy average must not hide:

- one fabricated approval;
- one fabricated evidence event;
- one confirmed cross-Tenant leak;
- one critical Security event;
- one critical privacy event;
- one unauthorized Production action;
- one ignored suspension;
- one material Customer harm event.

---

## 5.5 Team KPIs Must Preserve Member Attribution

Every material Team result must remain traceable to:

- Human members;
- Agent members;
- Roles;
- Tasks;
- workflows;
- handoffs;
- reviews;
- approvals;
- evidence.

---

## 5.6 Team KPIs Must Include Coordination Cost

Team cost and performance must include:

- planning;
- coordination;
- communication;
- handoffs;
- reviews;
- conflicts;
- duplicate work;
- rework;
- escalation;
- recovery.

---

## 5.7 Team KPIs Must Preserve Product and Project Boundaries

Team measurements must not combine unrelated:

- Products;
- Projects;
- Tenants;
- Customers;
- environments;
- Data classes;
- cost centres.

---

## 5.8 Baselines Must Precede Numeric Targets

Numeric Team targets require:

- functioning instrumentation;
- valid member attribution;
- verified Data quality;
- sufficient samples;
- measured baseline;
- workload classification;
- Risk review;
- qualified approval.

---

## 5.9 Planned Values Must Be Explicit

Planned Team KPI values must be labelled:

```text
PROPOSED

NOT MEASURED

NOT APPROVED

NOT A RUNTIME RESULT
```

---

## 5.10 Team KPIs Must Be Comparable

Comparison requires compatible:

- Team type;
- mission;
- workload;
- Product;
- Project;
- Tenant;
- Customer;
- environment;
- Team size;
- Role composition;
- maturity;
- Risk;
- measurement window.

---

## 5.11 Failures Must Remain Visible

The Team KPI system must include:

- failed Tasks;
- rejected outputs;
- timeouts;
- retries;
- rollbacks;
- partial results;
- failed handoffs;
- duplicate work;
- unresolved conflicts;
- Human corrections;
- Customer corrections;
- incidents.

---

## 5.12 Teams Must Not Approve Their Own Final Scorecards

A Team may produce evidence.

It must not be the sole authority for:

- KPI Definition;
- baseline approval;
- target approval;
- final scorecard approval;
- Production promotion;
- suspension removal.
