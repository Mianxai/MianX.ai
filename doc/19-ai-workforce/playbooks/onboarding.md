# Mianx.ai Enterprise Workforce Onboarding Playbook

> **This playbook defines the governed target-state procedure through which
> Human workers, AI Agents, Teams, Roles, Department participants, Product and
> Project contributors, Customer-support participants, Tenant operators, and
> approved external collaborators may be identified, validated, prepared,
> trained, scoped, provisioned, tested, reviewed, activated, monitored, and
> formally accepted into the Mianx.ai enterprise operating environment.**

---

# 1. Purpose

This playbook establishes the official onboarding procedure for the Mianx.ai
Human and AI Workforce.

It defines:

- onboarding authority;
- onboarding identity;
- onboarding ownership;
- onboarding subjects;
- onboarding requests;
- eligibility;
- identity verification;
- Role validation;
- responsibility validation;
- reporting relationships;
- escalation relationships;
- capacity validation;
- Skill validation;
- Capability validation;
- training;
- evaluation;
- certification;
- Tool access;
- Model access;
- memory access;
- Data access;
- Product scope;
- Project scope;
- Customer scope;
- Tenant scope;
- environment scope;
- financial boundaries;
- Risk boundaries;
- Security controls;
- privacy controls;
- legal and compliance controls;
- knowledge transfer;
- documentation access;
- supervised execution;
- readiness assessment;
- controlled activation;
- probation and observation;
- monitoring;
- restriction;
- suspension;
- onboarding failure;
- rollback;
- evidence;
- audit;
- Production onboarding gates;
- current-state boundaries.

This playbook ensures that a worker or Agent is not treated as onboarded merely
because:

- a name exists;
- an email account exists;
- a Role document exists;
- an Agent Definition exists;
- an Agent is listed in a registry;
- credentials were generated;
- a Tool was connected;
- a training document was opened;
- a Team invitation was sent;
- a Product or Project assignment exists;
- a prompt was executed;
- documentation is marked complete.

This playbook does not independently:

- employ a Human;
- create a legal relationship;
- appoint an executive;
- activate an AI Agent;
- approve a Role;
- grant Production access;
- authorize Customer access;
- authorize Tenant access;
- approve financial authority;
- prove operational readiness;
- prove Production operation.

# 3. Strategic Alignment

The onboarding process must preserve the exact Mianx.ai hierarchy:

```text
Mianx.ai Company and Governance
↓
MianX Core Platform
↓
Mianx.ai AI Operating System
↓
Shared AI Workforce
↓
Industry Operating Systems
↓
Customer Editions
↓
Autonomous Enterprise Creation at Scale
```

Onboarding must not allow:

- a Product to redefine the Company;
- a Project to create permanent enterprise authority;
- a Customer to own Mianx.ai workforce identities;
- a Tenant to access another Tenant;
- an AI Agent to claim Founder or Human authority;
- an onboarding coordinator to create unapproved Production authority;
- access provisioning to replace Governance approval.

# 10. Founder Authority Boundary

Founder approval or Founder-reserved authority may be required for:

- Founder Office Roles;
- major executive Roles;
- major AI executive-support Roles;
- unrestricted enterprise authority;
- high-risk autonomous Agent Roles;
- major Production activation;
- major Customer or enterprise commitments;
- AI Constitution exceptions;
- existential Risk;
- authority not assigned to another valid Human owner.

Onboarding must not fabricate Founder approval.

---

# 11. Human Accountability Boundary

Qualified Human accountability remains mandatory for onboarding involving:

- legal employment or contractor status;
- financial authority;
- employment decisions;
- Customer contractual authority;
- critical Security access;
- critical privacy access;
- regulated activity;
- destructive Production permissions;
- material residual Risk;
- external legal or regulatory obligations.

AI Agents may assist with evidence collection, validation, training, and
monitoring but must not replace required Human authority.

# 17. Lifecycle State Rules

An onboarding case must not move to:

- `IDENTITY-VALIDATION` without a valid subject record;
- `ROLE-VALIDATION` without a requested Role;
- `TRAINING-IN-PROGRESS` without defined learning requirements;
- `ACCESS-PROVISIONING` without approved scope;
- `CONTROLLED-READINESS-TEST` without training and access controls;
- `CONTROLLED-ACTIVATION` without explicit approval;
- `ONBOARDED` without readiness evidence;
- `PERIODIC-REVALIDATION` without an active approved onboarding state.

# 35. Onboarding Risk Classification

Recommended onboarding Risk classes:

```text
OR-0 — Informational or Observation-Only

OR-1 — Low-Risk Internal Participation

OR-2 — Controlled Operational Participation

OR-3 — Sensitive Product, Project, Customer, or Tenant Participation

OR-4 — Critical Security, Privacy, Financial, Legal, or Production Participation

OR-5 — Founder-Reserved, Executive, or Existential Authority
```

Higher Risk requires stronger Human review, independent verification, and
narrower access.

---

# 36. Risk-Based Onboarding Paths

| Risk Class | Typical Controls |
|---|---|
| OR-0 | Identity, confidentiality, restricted read-only access |
| OR-1 | Role validation, basic training, bounded internal access |
| OR-2 | Capability validation, supervised execution, review and monitoring |
| OR-3 | Customer or Tenant isolation, enhanced Security and privacy review |
| OR-4 | Independent verification, dual approval, restricted Production access |
| OR-5 | Founder or executive approval, qualified Human accountability, exceptional evidence |

These are target-state controls, not active guarantees.
