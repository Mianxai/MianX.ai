# Mianx.ai Enterprise Human and AI Workforce Training Framework

> **This document defines the target-state governed framework for training
> Humans, AI Agents, Teams, Departments, and other authorized workforce
> participants across Mianx.ai. It establishes training authority, domains,
> competency models, learning objectives, onboarding, Role-based learning,
> Agent training, Tool and Model training, Product and Project training,
> Customer and Tenant training, Security, Privacy, Ethics, Compliance,
> simulation, supervised practice, evaluation, remediation, evidence,
> audit, and Production-readiness relationships.**

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this framework is to define how Mianx.ai develops and
maintains workforce competency in a controlled, measurable, auditable, and
role-appropriate manner.

It governs training for:

- Human workers;
- Human leaders;
- AI Agents;
- Human-AI Teams;
- Departments;
- Roles;
- skills;
- capabilities;
- Tools;
- Models;
- memory systems;
- workflows;
- communication;
- coordination;
- Products;
- Projects;
- Shared Services;
- Customers;
- Tenants;
- Customer Editions;
- Security;
- Privacy;
- Ethics;
- Compliance;
- Risk;
- Incident Response;
- operational readiness.

This framework does not independently:

- grant employment;
- grant Role authority;
- grant Agent authority;
- activate Agents;
- authorize Tools;
- authorize Models;
- authorize Customer access;
- authorize Tenant access;
- approve a capability;
- issue a certification;
- authorize Production operation.

# 7. Training Principles

Training Governance must follow:

1. Founder sovereignty;
2. qualified Human accountability;
3. Role-based relevance;
4. least necessary access during training;
5. controlled environments for Risky practice;
6. Customer isolation;
7. Tenant isolation;
8. explicit learning objectives;
9. evidence-based completion;
10. evaluation separate from attendance;
11. verification separate from training completion;
12. remediation after material failure;
13. recency-aware competency;
14. version-aware Tool and Model training;
15. Production authorization separate from training completion.

- create training content;
- propose training programs;
- approve training programs;
- assign training;
- supervise practice;
- evaluate learners;
- record completion;
- require remediation;
- require retraining;
- approve training waivers;
- audit training evidence.

Training authority must not automatically create operational authority.

# 10. Qualified Human Accountability

- Production systems;
- Security-sensitive actions;
- Privacy-sensitive Data;
- legal obligations;
- regulated activities;
- financial authority;
- employment actions;
- Customer commitments;
- high-autonomy AI Agents;
- high-Risk workflows.

- marking content view as completion;
- marking completion as evaluation pass;

- marking certification as Production authorization;

- fake simulation evidence;
- repeated easy assessments designed only to improve pass rate;
- excluding failed learners from metrics;
- hiding remediation;
- using outdated training content;
- using outdated Tool or Model versions;
- counting theoretical Agent behavior as trained capability.

- training without learning objectives;
