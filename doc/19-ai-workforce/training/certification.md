# Mianx.ai Enterprise Human and AI Workforce Certification Standard

> **This document defines the governed target-state standard for issuing,
> maintaining, renewing, suspending, revoking, reinstating, and auditing
> Mianx.ai workforce certifications. It covers Human workers, AI Agents,
> Roles, skills, capabilities, Tools, Models, workflows, Products,
> Projects, Customers, Tenants, Customer Editions, Security, Privacy,
> Ethics, Compliance, Incident readiness, and Production-readiness
> certifications while preserving exact separation between certification,
> authority, access, activation, and Production authorization.**

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this standard is to establish a controlled credential model
for proving that a subject has satisfied a defined certification standard
at a specific point in time.

Certification may provide evidence that a Human, AI Agent, Role holder, or
other governed subject has satisfied approved requirements involving:

- training;
- Learning Paths;
- evaluations;
- skills;
- capabilities;
- practical exercises;
- Tool use;
- Model use;
- workflow execution;
- Security;
- Privacy;
- Ethics;
- Compliance;
- Customer isolation;
- Tenant isolation;
- Incident readiness;
- Production-readiness prerequisites.

This standard does not independently:

- appoint a Human to a Role;
- grant employment;
- activate an AI Agent;
- authorize an Agent for Production;
- grant Tool access;
- grant Model access;
- grant Customer access;
- grant Tenant access;
- approve a workflow for Production;
- approve a Product release;
- grant financial authority;
- create Founder authority;
- authorize Production operation.

- establish trusted internal credentials;
- make competency claims traceable;
- distinguish evaluated competency from authority;
- support Role eligibility;
- support Agent readiness;
- support Tool and Model readiness;
- support workflow readiness;
- support Product and Project readiness;
- support Customer and Tenant readiness;
- support Security, Privacy, Ethics, and Compliance readiness;
- support Incident readiness;
- support Production-readiness decisions;
- enforce credential validity;
- detect stale credentials;
- revoke invalid credentials;
- preserve certification history;
- support audit.

# 7. Certification Principles

Every governed certification should follow:

1. Founder sovereignty;
2. qualified Human accountability;
3. exact subject identity;
4. exact certification identity;
5. version-controlled certification requirements;
6. pre-defined eligibility;
7. evidence-backed issuance;
8. separation of evaluation and issuance where required;
9. separation of certification and authority;
10. explicit certification scope;
11. explicit restrictions;
12. expiry where competence may become stale;
13. Customer isolation;
14. Tenant isolation;
15. revocation where trust is no longer justified;
16. permanent preservation of historical evidence subject to retention rules.

- determine eligibility;
- issue certificates;
- renew certificates;
- trigger recertification;
- suspend certificates;
- revoke certificates;
- reinstate certificates;
- recognize equivalent credentials;
- hear appeals;
- audit certification evidence.

Certification authority must itself be governed.


- eligibility requirements;
- required evaluations;
- required capabilities;

- certification scope.

A `MINOR` change may apply to:

- Tool version requirements;
- Model version requirements;
- Learning Path changes;
- evaluation details;
- renewal requirements.

A `PATCH` may cover non-material corrections.

# 115. Anti-Gaming Controls

Certification Governance must prevent:

- issuing certificates without verified eligibility;
- issuing certificates based only on attendance;
- using expired Evaluation Results;
- omitting failed attempts;
- extending validity solely to avoid recertification;
- changing scope after issuance;
- deleting suspension history;
- hiding revocation;
- issuing multiple certificates to inflate workforce capability;
- using certificate count as direct proof of workforce capacity;
- reusing Customer A certification for Customer B;
- reusing Tenant A certification for Tenant B;
- certifying an Agent version and silently applying it to another version;
- treating certification as Production authorization.
