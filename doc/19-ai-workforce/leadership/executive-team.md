# Mianx.ai Executive Team Governance Standard

> **This document defines the governed target-state composition, authority,
> membership, operating process, decision participation, meeting structure,
> evidence, accountability, review, restriction, succession, and Production
> controls of the Mianx.ai Executive Team while preserving Founder sovereignty
> and Human accountability.**

---

The Founder Office is responsible for:

- preserving the enterprise mission;
- maintaining company identity;

- approving strategic direction;

- approving executive removals;

- approving major restructuring;
- approving succession for critical executive seats.
