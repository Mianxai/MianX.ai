# Mianx.ai AI Workforce Leadership Model

> **This document defines the governed target-state leadership model for
> Mianx.ai, preserving Founder sovereignty and Human accountability while
> allowing bounded AI executive, Department, Product, Project, and Team
> leadership support inside explicit authority, delegation, evidence,
> escalation, Security, and Production controls.**

---

- a marketing agency;

# 4. Foundational Leadership Principle

The foundational leadership rule is:

```text
Founder Sovereignty
>
Delegated Human Executive Authority
>
Governed AI Executive Support
>
Department, Product, Project, and Team Leadership
>
Operational Execution
```

No lower layer may silently override a higher valid authority.

# 5. Founder Sovereignty

The Founder is the highest governing authority within the Mianx.ai enterprise
model, subject to applicable law and formally adopted Governance obligations.

Founder sovereignty includes final authority over:

- enterprise vision;
- enterprise mission;
- strategic direction;
- company identity;
- AI Constitution approval;
- major Governance changes;
- executive appointment;
- executive removal;
- Product portfolio direction;
- major investment;
- major financial commitments;
- major legal commitments;
- enterprise Risk appetite;
- Production activation;
- autonomous-operation limits;
- company restructuring;
- company sale, merger, dissolution, or equivalent material change.

Founder authority must remain:

- explicit;
- attributable;
- reviewable;
- evidence-backed;
- non-transferable through silent automation.

- be explicit;
- be time-bounded;
