# Mianx.ai Governed Human and AI Team Definition Template

> **This document defines the reusable governed template for creating,
> reviewing, registering, activating, changing, suspending, dissolving, and
> auditing Human, AI Agent, and hybrid Teams across Mianx.ai. It standardizes
> Team identity, leadership, membership, Roles, capabilities, Tools, Models,
> memory, authority, autonomy, capacity, Product and Project relationships,
> Customer and Tenant boundaries, communication, coordination, workflows,
> Tasks, Security, Privacy, Ethics, Compliance, performance, evidence, and
> Production authorization.**

---

# 1. Purpose

This template establishes one enterprise-grade structure for defining
Mianx.ai Teams consistently.

It defines:

- Team identity;
- Team ID;
- Team version;
- Team name;
- Team type;
- Team purpose;
- Team Charter;
- parent Department;
- Team Owner;
- Team Lead;
- Team Manager;
- Human Accountable Owner;
- Human members;
- AI Agent members;
- Roles;
- Job Descriptions;
- skills;
- capabilities;
- Tools;
- Models;
- memory;
- authority;
- prohibited authority;
- autonomy;
- decision rights;
- approval rights;
- delegation rights;
- communication rights;
- capacity;
- workload;
- Product scope;
- Project scope;
- Shared Services;
- Customer scope;
- Tenant scope;
- Customer Edition scope;
- communication;
- coordination;
- workflows;
- Tasks;
- dependencies;
- handoffs;
- escalation;
- separation of duties;
- Security;
- Privacy;
- Ethics;
- Compliance;
- Risk;
- Team KPIs;
- lifecycle;
- creation;
- review;
- registration;
- activation;
- observation;
- membership changes;
- restructuring;
- suspension;
- dissolution;
- retirement;
- evidence;
- audit;
- Production Team gates.

This template does not independently:

- create a Team;

- register a Team;
- activate a Team;
- activate Human workers;

- authorize Tools;
- authorize Models;
- grant Department authority;
- grant Product authority;
- grant Project authority;
- grant Customer authority;
- grant Tenant authority;
- prove Team capacity;
- prove runtime coordination;
- authorize Production operation.

# 3. Strategic Alignment

Every Team Definition created from this template must preserve:

```text
Mianx.ai Company and Governance
↓
MianX Core Platform
↓
Mianx.ai AI Operating System
↓
Shared AI Workforce
↓
Industry Operating Systems
↓
Customer Editions
↓
Autonomous Enterprise Creation at Scale
```

Teams exist inside this architecture.

A Team must not redefine:

- Company Governance;
- MianX Core Platform;
- AI Operating System;
- Shared AI Workforce ownership;
- Product ownership;
- Customer ownership;
- Tenant ownership.
