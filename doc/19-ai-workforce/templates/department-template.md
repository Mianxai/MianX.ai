# Mianx.ai Governed Enterprise Department Definition Template

> **This document defines the reusable governed template for specifying,
> reviewing, registering, activating, restructuring, suspending, dissolving,
> and auditing Mianx.ai Departments containing Human workers, AI Agents,
> Teams, Roles, capabilities, Products, Projects, Shared Services, Customer
> scopes, Tenant scopes, decision rights, budgets, workflows, controls,
> evidence, and Production gates.**

---

# 1. Purpose

This template establishes one reusable enterprise-grade structure for
defining Departments across Mianx.ai.

It standardizes:

- Department identity;
- Department ID;
- Department version;
- Department name;
- purpose;
- mission;
- organizational parent;
- Department Owner;
- executive sponsor;
- reporting hierarchy;
- authority;
- prohibited authority;
- decision rights;
- delegation;
- approvals;
- Teams;
- Team Owners;
- Human workforce;
- AI workforce;
- Roles;
- Job Descriptions;
- skills;
- capabilities;
- Tools;
- Models;
- memory;
- Product relationships;
- Project relationships;
- Shared Services;
- Customer scope;
- Tenant scope;
- Customer Edition scope;
- workflows;
- communication;
- dependencies;
- escalation;
- separation of duties;
- budget boundaries;
- cost-center relationships;
- Security;
- Privacy;
- Ethics;
- Compliance;
- Risk;
- performance;
- Department KPIs;
- capacity;
- lifecycle;
- creation;
- approval;
- activation;
- restructuring;
- merger;
- split;
- transfer;
- suspension;
- dissolution;
- retirement;
- evidence;
- audit;
- Production Department gates.

This template does not independently:

- create a Department;
- approve a Department;
- register a Department;
- activate a Department;
- appoint an executive;
- hire Human workers;
- activate AI Agents;
- create a budget;
- create Customer access;
- create Tenant access;
- create Product authority;
- create Project authority;
- prove runtime operation;
- authorize Production.

# 8. Department ID

Recommended pattern:

```text
DEPT-{FUNCTION}-{SEQUENCE}
```

Examples:

```text
DEPT-ENG-001

DEPT-SEC-001

DEPT-FIN-001

DEPT-DATA-001
```

Examples are illustrative only.

The Department Registry must define the authoritative identifier.

---

# 9. Department Version

Recommended format:

```text
MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH
```

Increase `MAJOR` for material changes involving:

- Department purpose;
- Department authority;
- parent organization;
- ownership;
- major workforce model;
- major Product responsibilities.

Increase `MINOR` for material changes involving:

- Teams;
- Roles;
- capabilities;
- workflows;
- Product/Project assignments;
- Customer/Tenant operating scope.

Use `PATCH` for non-material corrections.
