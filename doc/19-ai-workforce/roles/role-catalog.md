# 3. Strategic Alignment

Roles must preserve the exact Mianx.ai hierarchy:

```text
Mianx.ai Company and Governance
↓
MianX Core Platform
↓
Mianx.ai AI Operating System
↓
Shared AI Workforce
↓
Industry Operating Systems
↓
Customer Editions
↓
Autonomous Enterprise Creation at Scale
```

Role design must not allow:

- a Customer Edition Role to override Company Governance;
- an Industry Operating System Role to override Core Platform authority;
- a Project Role to redefine permanent Product ownership;
- a Customer Role to control another Customer;
- a Tenant Role to access another Tenant;
- an AI Agent Role to claim Founder authority;
- an operational Role to create legal authority;
- a system permission to create business authority;
- a delegated Task to permanently expand a Role.

# 10. Founder Sovereignty

Founder authority is preserved for:

- Company identity;
- Company ownership;
- enterprise vision;
- AI Constitution;
- enterprise Governance model;
- Founder-reserved executive appointments;
- existential Risk;
- unrestricted AI autonomy;
- major legal or financial commitments;
- Company-wide Production authorization;
- enterprise dissolution;
- matters explicitly reserved to the Founder.

No Role definition, assignment, delegation, Agent output, executive decision,
or system permission may fabricate or silently replace Founder authority.
