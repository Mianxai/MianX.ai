# Mianx.ai Enterprise Human and AI Workforce Career Framework

> **This document defines the governed target-state framework for Human
> career development, AI Agent maturity progression, hybrid Human-Agent
> operating paths, job families, levels, grades, tracks, readiness,
> promotion, mobility, succession, downgrade, suspension, retirement,
> evidence, fairness, and Production progression assurance across Mianx.ai.**

---

This document establishes the official target-state Enterprise Human and AI

- an Agent temporarily handles complex work;
- documentation is marked complete.

This framework does not independently:

- create employment;
- create compensation;
- create a Role;
- create a Job Description;
- assign a Role;
- assign a position;
- create authority;
- grant system permissions;
- activate an Agent;
- promote a Human;
- advance an AI Agent;
- approve executive appointment;
- approve financial limits;
- prove readiness;
- prove performance;
- prove potential;
- authorize Production operation.

# 3. Strategic Alignment

Career and maturity progression must preserve the exact Mianx.ai hierarchy:

```text
Mianx.ai Company and Governance
↓
MianX Core Platform
↓
Mianx.ai AI Operating System
↓
Shared AI Workforce
↓
Industry Operating Systems
↓
Customer Editions
↓
Autonomous Enterprise Creation at Scale
```

Career design must not allow:

- a Customer Edition level to override Company Governance;
- an Industry Operating System title to create Core Platform authority;
- a Project promotion to redefine permanent Product ownership;
- a Customer-specific assignment to create cross-Customer authority;
- a Tenant-specific assignment to create cross-Tenant authority;
- AI Agent maturity to replace qualified Human accountability;
- career level to create Founder authority;
- grade to create unrestricted financial authority;
- performance pressure to bypass Security, Privacy, Ethics, Compliance, or
  Production controls.

# 11. Founder Sovereignty

Founder authority is preserved for:

- enterprise vision;
- AI Constitution;
- Company ownership;
- Founder Office structure;
- Founder-reserved career paths;
- major executive appointments;
- unrestricted AI autonomy;
- critical enterprise leadership succession;
- major organization redesign;
- Company-wide Production authority;
- existential Risk;
- matters explicitly reserved to the Founder.

No career decision, Agent recommendation, executive recommendation, succession
plan, performance score, or system record may fabricate Founder approval.

# 12. Qualified Human Accountability

Qualified Human accountability is mandatory for:

- Human promotion decisions;
- Human demotion decisions;
- compensation decisions;
- employment decisions;
- executive appointments;
- financial authority changes;
- legal or compliance authority changes;
- high-impact workforce decisions;
- Agent maturity advancement into high-impact work;
- Agent downgrade after material failure;
- Production progression authorization;
- Founder-reserved matters.

AI Agents may support analysis and evidence preparation but must not replace
required Human accountability.

# 21. Human Level H0

`H0 — Entry Candidate or Pre-Assignment` means:

- the individual is under consideration;
- no active career-level claim is proven;
- eligibility review may be pending;
- no Role authority is created;
- no Production access is created.

- foundational knowledge;
- supervised execution;
- narrow scope;
- limited complexity;
- frequent review;
- required escalation;
- no independent high-impact authority.
