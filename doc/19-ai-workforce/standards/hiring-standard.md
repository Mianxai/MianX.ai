# 10. Founder Sovereignty

Founder authority is preserved for matters including:

- Company identity;
- executive appointments where Founder-reserved;
- major organizational design;
- strategic workforce model;
- unrestricted AI autonomy;
- high-impact enterprise Agent authority;
- existential workforce Risk;
- matters explicitly reserved to the Founder.

No Human or AI Agent may:

- fabricate Founder hiring approval;
- fabricate Founder Agent approval;
- infer Founder approval from silence;
- infer Founder approval from candidate seniority;
- infer Founder approval from repository documentation.
