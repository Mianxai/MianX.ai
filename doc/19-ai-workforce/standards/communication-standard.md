# Mianx.ai Enterprise AI Workforce Communication Standard

> **This document defines the governed target-state standard for Human,
> AI Agent, Team, Department, Executive, Product, Project, Customer, Tenant,
> Shared Service, workflow, alert, approval, acknowledgement, escalation,
> Incident, and emergency communication across Mianx.ai while preserving
> Founder sovereignty, qualified Human accountability, exact authority,
> Customer and Tenant isolation, confidentiality, traceability, and evidence.**

# 10. Founder Sovereignty

The Founder retains authority for communications involving:

- Founder decisions;
- Company identity;
- Company vision;
- Company mission;
- AI Constitution;
- Founder-reserved strategy;
- enterprise hierarchy;
- Founder appointments;
- existential Company Risk;
- major enterprise direction;
- matters explicitly reserved to the Founder.

No Human or AI Agent may:

- fabricate Founder communication;
- impersonate the Founder;
- imply Founder approval without evidence;
- broaden a Founder statement beyond its scope;
- summarize a Founder decision in a way that changes its meaning.
