# Mianx.ai AI Workforce Ethics Policy

> **This policy defines the governed target-state ethical requirements for
> Human workers, AI Agents, Teams, Departments, Products, Projects, Customer
> Editions, Customers, Tenants, Tools, Models, memory systems, Data systems,
> providers, research activities, workflows, Tasks, recommendations,
> decisions, communications, and Production actions operating within the
> Mianx.ai enterprise ecosystem.**

---

This policy establishes the official AI Workforce Ethics requirements for
Mianx.ai.

This policy ensures that conduct is not considered ethical merely because:

- it is technically possible;
- it is commercially useful;
- a Customer requested it;
- a Human approved it;
- an Agent followed a prompt;
- a Model produced a confident answer;
- a Tool completed an action;
- consent was collected;
- Data was legally accessible;
- a decision was explainable;
- an outcome was profitable;
- no complaint was received;
- documentation is marked complete.

This policy does not independently:

- determine applicable law;
- provide legal advice;
- create ethical approval;
- authorize high-impact automated decisions;
- authorize surveillance;
- authorize profiling;
- authorize sensitive inference;
- approve a Tool or Model;
- approve research involving Humans;
- approve Customer or Tenant processing;
- prove fairness;
- prove non-discrimination;
- prove Production ethical enforcement.

# 3. Strategic Alignment

Ethical operation must preserve the exact Mianx.ai hierarchy:

```text
Mianx.ai Company and Governance
↓
MianX Core Platform
↓
Mianx.ai AI Operating System
↓
Shared AI Workforce
↓
Industry Operating Systems
↓
Customer Editions
↓
Autonomous Enterprise Creation at Scale
```

Ethical controls must not allow:

- a Customer Edition to override Company Governance;
- an Industry Operating System to override Core Platform ethical controls;
- a Project to redefine Product ethical ownership;
- a Customer request to create unrestricted Agent authority;
- one Customer’s objective to harm another Customer;
- one Tenant’s interest to override another Tenant’s rights;
- an AI Agent to claim Human, Founder, legal, or moral authority;
- financial gain to override Human dignity;
- operational urgency to normalize unethical conduct.

Mianx.ai systems must treat Humans as persons with dignity rather than merely:

- Data points;
- behavioural targets;
- productivity units;
- revenue opportunities;
- Risk scores;
- automation obstacles;
- training examples;
- manipulation targets.

Human dignity requires respect in:

- communication;
- decision-making;
- monitoring;
- profiling;
- support;
- employment;
- Customer treatment;
- dispute handling;
- correction;
- escalation.

- control choices;
- hide meaningful alternatives;
- exploit confusion;
- exploit urgency;
- exploit vulnerability;
- create false dependency;
- prevent Human intervention;
- suppress disagreement;
- remove valid appeal;
