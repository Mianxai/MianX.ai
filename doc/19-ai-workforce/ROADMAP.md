# Mianx.ai AI Workforce Roadmap

> This Roadmap defines the controlled sequence for completing, reviewing,
> approving, implementing, validating, activating, operating, scaling, and
> continuously improving the Mianx.ai AI Workforce under Human authority.

documentation structure to a governed, secure, measurable, and operational AI

hierarchy:

```text

            ↓

            ↓
Mianx.ai AI Operating System
            ↓
Mianx.ai Shared AI Workforce
            ↓
Industry Operating Systems
            ↓

            ↓
Governed Autonomous Enterprise Creation
```

The AI Workforce:

- operates through the AI Operating System;
- uses approved MianX Core Platform services;
- supports approved Products and Projects;
- remains subordinate to Founder and Human authority;
- must not become an independent strategic authority;

- must produce verifiable evidence;
- must remain suspendable.

# 4. Roadmap Principles

The following principles govern this Roadmap.

## 4.1 Documentation Before Implementation

Material AI Workforce capabilities must be documented sufficiently before
implementation.

```text
Documentation
      ↓
Review
      ↓
Approval
      ↓
Architecture
      ↓
Implementation
      ↓
Testing
      ↓
Controlled Activation
      ↓
Operation
```

Documentation alone does not prove implementation.

---

## 4.2 Governance Before Authority

No Agent may receive authority before:

- identity is defined;
- ownership is defined;
- Role is approved;
- permissions are approved;
- Risk is classified;
- escalation is defined;
- monitoring is available;
- suspension is available;
- Human approval is recorded where required.

---

## 4.3 Security Before Scale

The Workforce must not scale beyond its:

- identity controls;
- authorization controls;
- Tenant isolation;
- Project isolation;
- secret protection;
- monitoring;
- audit;
- cost controls;
- incident response;
- recovery;
- Governance capacity.

---

## 4.4 Evidence Before Claims

The following must remain separate:

```text
Documented
≠
Approved
≠
Implemented
≠
Tested
≠
Deployed
≠
Active
≠
Live-Tested
≠
Production Operational
```

All material claims must be supported by evidence matching the exact scope.

---

## 4.5 Human Authority Before Autonomy

Increasing AI autonomy requires increasing:

- Governance;
- monitoring;
- evaluation;
- evidence;
- accountability;
- security;
- recovery;
- Human oversight.

AI authority must never increase silently.

---

## 4.6 Reuse Before Duplication

Approved Workforce roles, capabilities, tools, models, workflows, standards,
and templates should be reused where appropriate.

Reuse must not weaken:

- Product boundaries;
- Tenant isolation;
- Project isolation;
- security;
- privacy;
- accountability;
- domain ownership.

---

## 4.7 Controlled Progression

No phase should be marked complete only because files exist.

Every phase requires:

- defined outputs;
- acceptance criteria;
- evidence;
- review;
- approval;
- status updates;
- Changelog entry;
- next-phase authorization.
