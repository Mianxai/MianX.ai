# Mianx.ai Enterprise Department Structure Standard

> **This document defines the governed target-state structure through which
> Mianx.ai Departments may be proposed, approved, staffed, activated, funded,
> measured, coordinated, restricted, restructured, suspended, dissolved, and
> retired while preserving Founder sovereignty, Human accountability, Shared
> AI Workforce ownership, Product and Project boundaries, Customer and Tenant
> isolation, Security, privacy, financial control, evidence, and Production
> safety.**

# 3. Strategic Alignment

The Department Structure must preserve the exact Mianx.ai hierarchy:

```text
Mianx.ai Company and Governance
↓
MianX Core Platform
↓
Mianx.ai AI Operating System
↓
Shared AI Workforce
↓
Industry Operating Systems
↓
Customer Editions
↓
Autonomous Enterprise Creation at Scale
```

Departments support this hierarchy.

Departments must not replace or redefine it.

A Department must not:

- redefine Mianx.ai as a single-industry business;
- own the Founder;
- override Company Governance;
- treat a Product as the whole Company;
- treat a Project as a permanent Department;
- transfer Mianx.ai Agent ownership to a Customer;
- operate outside approved Product, Project, Customer, or Tenant boundaries.

# 7. Department Definition

A Department is a stable enterprise organizational unit responsible for an
approved enduring business, technical, operational, Governance, Customer, or
support mandate.

A valid Department requires:

- unique identity;
- approved mandate;
- accountable owner;
- executive sponsor where required;
- approved Roles;
- verified capacity;
- Teams;
- capabilities;
- budget;
- reporting;
- decision rights;
- escalation;
- evidence;
- lifecycle controls.
