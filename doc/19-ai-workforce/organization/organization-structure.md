# Mianx.ai Enterprise Organization Structure Standard

> **This document defines the governed target-state organizational structure
> through which Mianx.ai Company and Governance, the MianX Core Platform,
> Mianx.ai AI Operating System, Shared AI Workforce, Industry Operating

> boundaries, Security, privacy, financial control, evidence, or Production
> safety.**
