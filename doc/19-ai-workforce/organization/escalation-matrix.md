# Mianx.ai Enterprise Escalation Matrix Standard

> **This document defines the governed target-state framework through which
> unresolved work, authority gaps, responsibility gaps, reporting failures,
> capacity shortages, capability failures, Product and Project conflicts,
> Customer and Tenant risks, Security incidents, privacy incidents, legal
> obligations, financial exposure, provider failures, Production failures, and
> other material conditions may be escalated to the correct Human,
> organizational, Governance, executive, Founder, or legally required external
> authority.**

```text
↓
Shared AI Workforce
↓
```

Escalation routing must not allow:

- a Customer Edition to override Company Governance;
- an Industry Operating System to override the Core Platform;
- a Project escalation to redefine Product ownership;

# 7. Escalation Definition

An escalation is a governed action that raises a condition beyond the current
owner’s:

- authority;
- capacity;
- capability;
- Risk limit;
- decision rights;
- response ability;
- time limit;
- organizational scope.

An escalation should be used when:

- normal resolution has failed;
- time-sensitive harm may occur;
- authority is missing;
- capacity is unavailable;
- a hard stop is triggered;
- a material control fails;
- a required decision is overdue;
- Customer or Tenant harm is possible;
- regulatory or legal action may be required;
- Production safety is threatened.

---

# 8. Escalation Authority

Escalation authority defines who may:

- raise an escalation;
- classify an escalation;
- change severity;
- assign an Escalation Owner;
- apply temporary controls;
- pause work;
- restrict access;
- route to an executive;
- route to the Founder;
- notify a Customer;
- notify an external authority;
- de-escalate;
- close;
- reopen;
- suspend escalation automation.

Escalation authority must be:

- explicit;
- current;
- scope-bounded;
- Risk-bounded;
- Product-specific;
- Project-specific;
- Customer-specific where applicable;
- Tenant-specific where applicable;
- environment-specific;
- time-bounded where delegated;
- evidence-backed;
- revocable.

---

# 9. Founder Authority Boundary

Founder escalation is mandatory where a matter concerns:

- Company identity;
- Company ownership;
- AI Constitution;
- major enterprise strategy;
- major executive authority conflict;
- major organizational restructuring;
- unrestricted Production autonomy;
- existential financial exposure;
- existential legal exposure;
- enterprise dissolution;
- Founder-reserved decisions.

Founder notification must not be represented as Founder approval.

---

# 10. Human Accountability Boundary

Qualified Human authority is mandatory for escalations involving:

- legal obligations;
- financial commitments;
- employment matters;
- Customer contractual commitments;
- critical Security;
- critical privacy;
- regulated activity;
- destructive Production actions;
- material residual Risk;
- external authority notification;
- Founder-reserved matters.

AI Agents may detect, classify, summarize, route, and monitor escalations within
approved boundaries.

# 16. Primary Escalation Owner

The Primary Escalation Owner is responsible for:

- escalation registration;
- route validation;
- acknowledgement tracking;
- response coordination;
- temporary controls;
- evidence;
- deadline monitoring;
- escalation to a higher level;
- de-escalation;
- closure preparation;
- reopening where required.

A material escalation must have exactly one Primary Escalation Owner.

---

# 17. Response Owner

The Response Owner is responsible for:

- acknowledging the escalation;
- evaluating immediate impact;
- confirming temporary controls;
- identifying required participants;
- preparing the response plan;
- providing status updates;
- escalating further when necessary.

The Response Owner may differ from the Primary Accountable Owner.

---

# 18. Resolution Owner

The Resolution Owner coordinates or owns the corrective resolution.

The Resolution Owner must have:

- valid authority;
- appropriate capability;
- available capacity;
- approved scope;
- clear acceptance criteria;
- evidence responsibilities.

Resolution ownership does not automatically transfer original accountability.

---

The Receiving Authority is the Human, Role, Team, Department, Governance body,
executive, Founder, or legally required external authority receiving the
escalation.

Receiving Authority must be determined by:

- subject;
- severity;
- urgency;
- organizational scope;
- financial exposure;
- Security impact;
- privacy impact;
- legal impact;
- Customer impact;
- Production impact;
- Founder-reserved boundaries.

---

# 22. ES-0 — Informational

ES-0 may include:

- non-material anomaly;
- minor documentation inconsistency;
- low-impact observation;
- issue requiring visibility but not intervention.

ES-0 normally requires tracking rather than urgent action.

---

# 23. ES-1 — Low

ES-1 may include:

- routine blocker;
- low-impact missed deadline;
- minor capacity variance;
- non-critical reporting delay;
- reversible low-risk failure.

ES-1 should be handled by the current owner or Team level.

---

# 24. ES-2 — Moderate

ES-2 may include:

- repeated blocker;
- Product or Project delay;
- material review backlog;
- moderate Customer impact;
- significant capacity shortage;
- control degradation.

ES-2 normally requires Department, Product, Project, or Customer ownership
intervention.

---

# 25. ES-3 — High

ES-3 may include:

- major Product or Project failure;
- material Customer impact;
- material financial exposure;
- serious Security or privacy concern;
- executive decision delay;
- cross-Department conflict;
- major provider outage.

ES-3 requires urgent Human authority and temporary controls.

---

# 26. ES-4 — Critical

ES-4 may include:

- active critical Security incident;
- active critical privacy incident;
- Production outage;
- cross-Tenant exposure;
- material legal breach;
- major financial loss;
- severe Customer harm;
- destructive or irreversible action risk.

ES-4 requires immediate containment and executive or specialist authority.

---

# 27. ES-5 — Existential or Founder-Reserved

ES-5 may include:

- Company ownership;
- Company dissolution;
- AI Constitution conflict;
- enterprise existential Risk;
- major executive authority conflict;
- unrestricted AI autonomy;
- company-wide catastrophic exposure.

ES-5 requires Founder involvement and qualified Human Governance.

---

# 28. Urgency Levels


Urgency determines timing.

They must be assessed separately.

# 29. Proposed Response-Time Baseline

The following values are proposed target-state defaults only.

They are not active service commitments until separately approved and
implemented.

| Severity | Acknowledgement Target | Initial Response Target | Containment Target | Resolution Target |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ES-0 | 2 business days | 5 business days | Not normally required | 15 business days |
| ES-1 | 1 business day | 3 business days | As required | 10 business days |
| ES-2 | 4 hours | 1 business day | 1 business day | 5 business days |
| ES-3 | 1 hour | 4 hours | 4 hours | 24 hours or approved plan |
| ES-4 | 15 minutes | 30 minutes | 1 hour | 4 hours or approved emergency plan |
| ES-5 | Immediate routing | Immediate Human acknowledgement | Immediate safe control | Founder-directed |

Business-hour assumptions must be separately defined.

Critical and emergency escalations may require 24/7 handling.

# 30. Escalation Levels

```text
EL-0 — Local Work Owner or Automated Safe Alert

EL-1 — Team Lead or Functional Owner

EL-2 — Department, Product, Project, Customer, or Tenant Owner

EL-3 — Human Executive or Independent Control Authority

```

EL-5 is used only where required by law, contract, safety, or approved
emergency policy.

---

# 31. EL-0 — Local Resolution

EL-0 applies where:

- the issue is within assigned authority;
- sufficient capacity exists;
- Risk remains low;
- no hard stop is triggered;
- no external notification is required.

Failure to resolve within the defined time must move the escalation upward.

---

# 32. EL-1 — Team or Functional Escalation

EL-1 may route to:

- Team Lead;
- Team Owner;
- functional owner;
- workflow owner;
- capability owner;
- immediate Human supervisor.

EL-1 should resolve routine blockers and local coordination issues.

---

# 33. EL-2 — Department, Product, Project, Customer, or Tenant Escalation

EL-2 may route to:

- Department Director;
- Department Owner;
- Product Owner;
