# Mianx.ai Research Lab Experiments — Experiment Results

> **Experiment Results are not the raw outputs produced by a run, and they are not automatically the conclusions a Research team wants to draw.**
>
> Mianx.ai should preserve the complete chain:
>
> ```text
> RAW
> OBSERVATIONS
>
> ↓
>
> VALIDATED
> MEASUREMENTS
>
> ↓
>
> METRIC
> OBSERVATIONS
>
> ↓
>
> AGGREGATED
> RESULTS
>
> ↓
>
> STATISTICAL
> INTERPRETATION
>
> ↓
>
> CLAIM
>
> ↓
>
> CONCLUSION
>
> ↓
>
> DECISION /
> KNOWLEDGE
> TRANSFER
> ```
>
> Each transition requires Evidence and explicit reasoning.

# 219. Negative Verification Scenarios

Containment, correction, reanalysis or invalidation should occur when:

* raw Model response is copied into a report and described as validated Experiment Result.
* secondary metric succeeds after primary metric fails and the Experiment is reported as successful without qualification.
* guardrail safety failure is hidden because quality improved strongly.
* timed-out Runs are dropped from denominator and reliability is reported only on successful completed Runs.
* successful retry is counted as first-pass success.
* missing Human evaluation scores are converted to zero without defined rule.
* rare catastrophic failure is removed as an outlier because it harms average performance.
* exclusions are invented after inspecting Results to make preferred Model win.
* protocol deviation changes Prompt for half the Experiment and all Runs are aggregated together without limitation.
* Dataset correction occurs after Experiment but prior Results are not versioned or reanalyzed.
* p-value falls below a threshold and report says there is a 99% probability hypothesis is true.
* non-significant difference is presented as proof two Models are equivalent.
* tiny but statistically significant improvement is presented as major business gain without cost analysis.
* hundreds of exploratory metrics are tested and only positive ones are reported as confirmatory.
* average quality improves but one important language subgroup materially deteriorates and subgroup Result is omitted.
* Project A Result is claimed as universal Mianx.ai performance.
* Tenant A evidence is published as a cross-Tenant finding without valid aggregation authority.
* a Judge Model prefers verbose outputs and its Result is treated as objective quality truth.
* Tool API returns success code but real side effect is incorrect and Experiment still marks Tool success.
* average latency is low while p99 is unacceptable and only average is reported.
* no attacks succeed in a limited Security experiment and system is described as secure.
* null Result is labeled proof that treatment has no effect despite wide uncertainty.
* contradictory replication is ignored because original Experiment was more favorable.
* original Result is overwritten after reanalysis with no history.
* invalidated Result remains in Knowledge Base and continues influencing Agent decisions.
* live Experiment dashboard trend is presented as final Result before stopping rule completes.
* a graph uses manipulated axis to exaggerate a small effect.
* Research team publishes only successful Experiments and suppresses negative Results.
* Result is accepted by Research Governance and is immediately treated as Production authorization.
