# Mianx.ai Research Lab

> **The Mianx.ai Research Lab is the governed research, experimentation, benchmarking, discovery, innovation and knowledge-transfer system of the Mianx.ai enterprise.**
>
> Its purpose is to systematically reduce uncertainty, discover better technologies and operating methods, test important assumptions, evaluate emerging capabilities, produce defensible evidence and transfer validated knowledge into Mianx.ai's Core Platform, AI Operating System, AI Workforce, Industry Operating Systems and future enterprise products.
>
> **Research creates evidence and options. Research does not automatically create authority.**
