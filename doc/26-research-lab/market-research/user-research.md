# 247. Negative Verification Scenarios

Containment, correction, re-analysis or escalation should occur when:

* Researcher asks users whether they like a proposed solution before establishing whether the underlying problem matters.
* only enthusiastic existing customers are recruited and findings are described as Market-wide.
* convenience sample is called statistically representative.
* participant agrees to interview but recording starts without recording-specific consent.
* full names are removed while employer, role and location still make participants easily identifiable, yet Dataset is labeled anonymous.
* participant says they perform a workflow daily and Research report treats this as observed behavior.
* leading interview questions repeatedly name the desired AI solution.
* hypothetical willingness to use is reported as validated adoption.
* one operator's workflow becomes the canonical workflow for the entire Industry OS.
* user asks for an AI Agent and team interprets the requested implementation as the underlying need.
* support tickets are counted as Market prevalence.
* a survey posted to existing followers is treated as population estimate.
* 1,000 biased survey responses are described as more reliable than a smaller but properly designed sample.
* usability participant completes task only after moderator hints, but session is scored as independent success.
* high-fidelity prototype success is described as Production readiness.
* one vivid quote is placed in executive presentation as proof that most users share the same need.
* six participants mention similar issue and report says "60% of Market" without valid denominator/population basis.
* no new theme appears in final interviews and team declares statistical saturation.
* persona characteristics are invented for storytelling and later cited as Research findings.
* AI transcription changes a material participant statement and quote is not verified.
* LLM summary invents a concern that no participant raised.
* AI-generated synthetic persona says users will pay a certain price and finding is treated as willingness-to-pay Evidence.
* Research tool merges notes from two participants and attribution is lost.
* minority accessibility issue is ignored because most participants were unaffected.
* users strongly prefer solution but security/procurement buyers reject it; Research report includes only end-user view.
* Project A Research is copied to an unrelated Project B without revalidation.
* Tenant A Research recording is used to train shared systems without Data-use authority.
* research findings are entered into Memory without date/scope and later treated as current authority.
* Product team requests a feature and researcher frames interview guide to confirm it.
* User Research insight is transferred to Product and implementation starts without separate Product approval.
* Founder receives Research report, does not respond, and document status is changed to Founder Approved.
