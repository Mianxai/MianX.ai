# Mianx.ai Research Lab Market Research — Opportunity Analysis

> **An Opportunity is not merely a good idea.**
>
> A credible Opportunity exists only when a sufficiently important problem, reachable customer, differentiated solution path, economically plausible model, strategic fit and governed execution path intersect.

# 228. Negative Verification Scenarios

Containment, correction, revalidation, pivot or rejection should occur when:

* a Market is large and team assumes it automatically contains a strong Mianx.ai Opportunity.
* a Founder idea is labeled "validated" before customer or Market Research.
* one customer asks for a feature and team redesigns core platform for the entire Market.
* competitor does not offer a feature and absence is interpreted as obvious demand.
* a new AI Model capability is available and team creates a product thesis without identifying a painful customer problem.
* interviews confirm a problem exists but buyers refuse to allocate budget.
* target users love a prototype but economic buyer sees no business value.
* customer says they would pay a target price but paid Pilot only closes after a deep discount.
* technical prototype uses hand-cleaned sample Data and team claims Production Data readiness.
* AI performs well on curated examples but fails under real workflow variability.
* task is technically automatable but has unacceptable authority or safety risk.
* required Tenant Data exists but contract does not permit reuse for shared model/feature development.
* large TAM causes a weak strategic-fit Opportunity to outrank core platform work.
* high Opportunity score averages away a critical Security or regulatory gate.
* cost model counts Model inference only and ignores implementation, support and Human oversight.
* competitor public list price is copied as achievable Mianx.ai selling price.
* sales cycle is ignored when modeling Opportunity economics.
* Project A use case is generalized to all Industry OS products without validation.
* Tenant A workflow is assumed to represent all customers.
* Opportunity score weights are changed after results to make favored idea rank first.
* positive Evidence is preserved while Counter-Evidence is removed from decision memo.
* technical feasibility improves and team assumes Market desirability also improved.
* Market demand improves and team assumes technical feasibility also improved.
* Opportunity ranks first but key core platform dependency does not exist.
* parked Opportunity is later reactivated using stale assumptions without revalidation.
* a successful prototype is presented as a Product.
* a successful controlled Pilot is described as Product-Market Fit.
* opportunity decision says "transfer to product" and engineering starts Production implementation without separate authorization.
* Founder received the Opportunity document but no explicit approval exists and status is changed to Founder Approved.
