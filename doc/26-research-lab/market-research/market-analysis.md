# 214. Negative Verification Scenarios

Containment, correction, re-analysis or escalation should occur when:

* Market is defined broadly enough to inflate TAM by including unrelated customer groups.
* TAM is presented to leadership as expected annual Mianx.ai revenue.
* global Market report is used directly as local Pakistan Market estimate without geographic adjustment.
* SAM and SOM are copied from TAM using arbitrary percentages without Evidence.
* bottom-up estimate counts the same multi-location customer multiple times without method.
* public competitor list price is used as evidence of realized enterprise price.
* one enthusiastic interview participant is described as proof of broad Market demand.
* survey from existing fans is generalized to all potential customers.
* search trends rise because of news attention and are described as purchase demand.
* analyst forecast is copied into documentation as future fact.
* three websites repeat the same analyst number and are counted as three independent sources.
* source was published recently but underlying Market Data is five years old.
* competitor marketing ROI claim is cited as verified customer economics.
* competitor count is low and team concludes Market is attractive without investigating lack of demand.
* large Market is selected despite unacceptable regulatory or Data-rights barriers.
* Industry has repetitive manual work and documentation concludes autonomous Industry OS is already validated.
* Market growth is high but customer acquisition channels are inaccessible.
* customers report willingness to pay but paid Pilot fails to convert.
* successful small Pilot is generalized to national/global demand.
* fast-growing Market is assumed profitable despite high support/infrastructure cost.
* Market analysis ignores manual processes and "do nothing" as substitutes.
* value-chain actor is mistaken for economic buyer.
* Project A Market insights are copied to Project B despite different industry/customer context.
* Tenant A confidential pricing information is used in general Market benchmarking.
* weighted attractiveness score is tuned after Results to force a preferred Market to rank first.
* contradictory sources are averaged despite using incompatible Market definitions.
* missing evidence is filled with precise-looking invented numbers.
* Research recommendation to validate an opportunity is treated as approval to build Production system.
* controlled Market Pilot succeeds and roadmap is marked Production-authorized without separate governance.
