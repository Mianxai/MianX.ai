# Mianx.ai Research Lab Ethics — AI Ethics

> **Mianx.ai should not ask only whether an AI system can perform an action.**
>
> It should also ask:
>
> * whether the action should be performed;
> * who may be affected;
> * whether people retain meaningful autonomy;
> * whether risks and benefits are distributed fairly;
> * whether consent and expectations are respected;
> * whether the system can be challenged or corrected;
> * and whether the use is consistent with Mianx.ai governance and values.
>
> Ethics is therefore not a decorative review after technical success.
>
> It is part of Research design, execution, validation and deployment governance.

# 180. Negative Verification Scenarios

Containment or correction should occur when:

* technically impressive AI capability is approved solely because no law explicitly prohibits it.
* high business ROI is used to waive ethical review.
* overall Model accuracy is used as proof that all demographic groups are treated fairly.
* protected attribute is removed but strong proxy variables remain and discrimination review is skipped.
* public social-media posts are used to infer sensitive traits because they are public.
* general product terms are treated as consent for employee emotion monitoring.
* restaurant customer engagement Agent exploits inferred financial or emotional vulnerability to increase purchases.
* employee monitoring system continuously tracks activity because technology allows it.
* Human reviewer receives hundreds of AI decisions per hour and oversight is still described as meaningful.
* final employment recommendation is automatically executed because a Human technically could override afterward.
* generated rationale is treated as faithful explanation without validation.
* Data quality report passes and ethically questionable Dataset collection method is ignored.
* Agent achieves task goal by misleading customer about being Human.
* multiple Agents reach consensus on discriminatory recommendation and consensus is treated as ethical validation.
* AI Workforce efficiency estimate automatically triggers Human role elimination.
* cloned executive voice is generated for external communication without clear authority and disclosure.
* face-recognition capability is used because accuracy Benchmark is high.
* emotion classifier output is treated as objective ground truth about employees.
* children or vulnerable users receive manipulative personalized messaging optimized for conversion.
* public demand for a surveillance feature is treated as ethical justification.
* competitor deploys invasive feature and Mianx.ai copies it without independent ethics review.
* dual-use Research artifact with material misuse capability is automatically published in full because open science is valued.
* ethical exception expires but Research continues.
* Ethics review document exists but safeguards were never implemented.
* ethics HALT is recorded while Agent tasks and public Pilot continue operating.
* a successful bounded Pilot is presented as Production Responsible AI authorization.
