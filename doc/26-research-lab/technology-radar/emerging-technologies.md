# 3. Emerging Technology Definition

For Mianx.ai:

> An Emerging Technology is a technology, technical capability, architecture, platform, standard, protocol, Model, Agent paradigm, infrastructure approach or tooling category whose capabilities, economics, ecosystem, operational characteristics or enterprise implications are still materially evolving.
