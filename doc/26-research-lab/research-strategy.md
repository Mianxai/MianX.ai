# Mianx.ai Research Lab Strategy

> **This document defines how Mianx.ai should move from the Research Lab Vision toward a governed, AI-native, continuously learning enterprise Research and Innovation capability.**
>
> The strategy prioritizes high-value uncertainty reduction, reusable research infrastructure, empirical Model and Agent evaluation, systematic experimentation, knowledge compounding and disciplined transfer of validated research into Mianx.ai's Core Platform, AI Operating System, AI Workforce and Industry Operating Systems.
>
> **This document defines strategic direction. It does not authorize budget, resources, experiments, deployments, Production changes or autonomous research authority.**
