# Mianx.ai Research Lab Roadmap

> **This document defines the recommended phased roadmap for building the Mianx.ai Research Lab from documentation foundation into a governed, secure, measurable and eventually Production-authorized Research platform.**
>
> It is deliberately evidence-based.
>
> A roadmap describes what should happen and in what order.
>
> It does **not** prove that those steps have already happened.
>
> The Research Lab should advance through explicit maturity gates rather than jumping from Markdown documentation directly to claims of autonomous Production Research.
>
> **Every major phase ends with an evidence gate.**
