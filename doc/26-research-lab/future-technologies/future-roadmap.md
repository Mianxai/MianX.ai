# Mianx.ai Research Lab Future Technologies — Future Roadmap

> **The Future Technologies Roadmap is not a promise that every emerging technology will be built, purchased or deployed.**
>
> It is a governed mechanism for deciding:
>
> * what Mianx.ai should watch;
> * what it should Research;
> * what it should Experiment with;
> * what it should prototype;
> * what it should prepare architecture for;
> * what it should deliberately avoid;
> * and what may eventually become part of the Mianx.ai AI Operating System.
>
> The roadmap should preserve optionality without allowing hype to become architecture.

---
