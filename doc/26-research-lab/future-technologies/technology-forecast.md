# Mianx.ai Research Lab Future Technologies — Technology Forecast

> **Technology forecasting is disciplined uncertainty management, not fortune telling.**
>
> The objective is not to predict one exact future.
>
> The objective is to help Mianx.ai make better decisions before the future is fully known by preserving:
>
> * Evidence;
> * uncertainty;
> * assumptions;
> * alternative scenarios;
> * trigger conditions;
> * and revision history.
>
> A forecast should be useful even when it turns out to be wrong, because the error should teach Mianx.ai which assumptions, signals or forecasting methods failed.

# 202. Negative Verification Scenarios

Containment or correction should occur when:

* forecast says Technology is likely and report rewrites statement as Technology will definitely happen.
* a speculative scenario is shown to leadership as committed future state.
* vendor roadmap says feature arrives next year and Mianx.ai treats date as guaranteed.
* one highly cited paper becomes basis for enterprise maturity forecast without replication or adoption Evidence.
* trending open-source repository is forecast to dominate enterprise because star count is rising.
* patent volume is treated as proof commercially viable Product is imminent.
* competitor invests heavily and team treats Technology as inevitable.
* weak signal is upgraded to established trend because media attention increases.
* recent exponential growth is extended indefinitely without saturation or constraint analysis.
* probability changes from 30% to 80% but old 30% forecast is deleted.
* forecast outcome occurs and historical forecast probabilities are retroactively changed to appear more accurate.
* forecast is resolved according to looser criteria than originally defined.
* one 90% forecast fails and forecaster is immediately labeled inaccurate without portfolio-level calibration review.
* backtesting method uses Data that was unavailable at historical forecast date.
* AI forecasting Agent fabricates Research sources and those sources enter evidence package unchecked.
* AI produces confident forecast prose and confidence is interpreted as calibrated probability.
* expert majority suppresses credible minority scenario.
* positive signals are retained while contradictory Security or cost signals are excluded.
* high forecast probability automatically moves Technology to `ADOPT`.
* roadmap architecture becomes tightly dependent on a Technology because forecast says it is likely.
* Agent capability forecast is interpreted as permission to increase current Agent autonomy.
* Model benchmark growth is treated as direct evidence that Tool-using Agent reliability will grow equally.
* falling token price is treated as proof total AI operating cost will fall proportionally.
* global adoption forecast is applied equally across all jurisdictions.
* Tenant-specific Data is exposed in shared forecasting dashboard.
