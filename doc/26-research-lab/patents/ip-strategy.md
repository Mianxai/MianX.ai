# 34. Patent vs Trade Secret

Potential comparison:

| Question              | Patent-Oriented Consideration                  | Trade-Secret-Oriented Consideration            |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Disclosure            | Public disclosure is inherent to patent system | Secrecy is central                             |
| Detectability         | May help where infringement can be observed    | More useful when use cannot easily be observed |
| Reverse engineering   | Public claims may still provide protection     | Weak if easily reverse engineered              |
| Duration              | Governed by legal patent lifecycle             | Potentially long while secrecy persists        |
| Cost                  | Filing/prosecution/maintenance costs           | Operational confidentiality/control costs      |
| Competitive signaling | Public portfolio may signal capability         | Less visible                                   |
| Knowledge sharing     | Publication may increase disclosure            | Sharing must remain controlled                 |

This table is strategic, not legal advice.
