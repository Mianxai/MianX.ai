# Mianx.ai Academic Research Collaborations

> **This document defines the target collaboration model for academic Research within the Mianx.ai Research Lab.**
>
> Collaboration can accelerate discovery, expand expertise, provide independent challenge and connect Mianx.ai with the broader Research ecosystem.
>
> Collaboration also introduces significant risk:
>
> * unclear ownership;
> * confidential Data exposure;
> * Intellectual Property disputes;
> * Project or Tenant leakage;
> * publication conflicts;
> * attribution disputes;
> * incompatible Research standards;
> * external Prompt Injection or malicious content;
> * compliance obligations;
> * misrepresentation of Research findings;
> * authority confusion.
>
> Therefore, external Research collaboration must be **structured, scoped, governed and traceable**.
>
> **Collaboration must increase learning without weakening Mianx.ai's authority, Security or ownership boundaries.**
