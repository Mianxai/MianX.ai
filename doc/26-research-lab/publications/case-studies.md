# 3. Case Study Definition

For Mianx.ai:

> A Case Study is a structured Research publication that documents a bounded situation, intervention, system, implementation, Prototype, MVP, customer workflow or operational change using traceable Evidence, explicit limitations and controlled claims.

# 203. Negative Verification Scenarios

Containment, correction, withdrawal, retraction or escalation should occur when:

* a successful customer is selected solely because it produces the strongest marketing story and selection bias is hidden.
* Case Study says a customer had "zero baseline" when historical Data was simply unavailable.
* several process changes occur at once and all improvement is attributed to Mianx.ai.
* Model alias is listed but materially different Model versions were used during the case.
* Agent says a task is complete and the case records a verified business outcome without source-system Evidence.
* Tool returns success and the Case Study reports the external action as complete without read-back.
* only successful Agent runs are included while failures are excluded.
* customer satisfaction quote is treated as evidence of Product-Market Fit.
* Pilot performance is described as Production-scale performance.
* one customer result is generalized to the entire market.
* Before/After comparison is written as causal proof despite major confounders.
* percentage improvement omits the denominator or comparator.
* AI cost claim excludes Human review, retry and infrastructure cost.
* raw customer Data used for operations is copied into a public Case Study without separate publication authority.
* customer name is removed but a unique industry/location/detail combination makes re-identification trivial.
* customer approves one quotation and team assumes entire document is approved.
* customer approved version 1.0 but version 2.0 adds new claims without renewed approval.
* internal technical review passes but public release occurs without required security, privacy, legal or IP review.
* Case Study publishes architecture details that expose sensitive security controls.
* Case Study contains an API key or secret in screenshot/source excerpt.
* public version removes limitations to make marketing copy shorter.
* sales presentation expands a verified 20% result into "up to 10x improvement" without Evidence.
* Model Benchmark pass is represented as business outcome improvement.
* no security incident in the observed period is written as "fully secure."
* a Prototype demonstration is described as a deployed Product.
* a controlled MVP is described as a proven scalable Production platform.
* a published Case Study becomes stale after major architecture change but remains marketed as current.
* core Data is later found invalid but publication is not corrected or retracted.
* retracted Case Study remains in sales decks and social content.
* Case Study publication is described as Founder-approved without approval Evidence.
* document generated in chat is described as saved to repository without filesystem Evidence.
