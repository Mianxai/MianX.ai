# Mianx.ai Research Lab Competitive Intelligence — Industry Trends

> **Industry Trends Research exists to help Mianx.ai detect meaningful change before strategic decisions become reactive.**
>
> A trend is not simply:
>
> * something popular;
> * something discussed frequently;
> * a viral technology;
> * a vendor announcement;
> * or a temporary spike in attention.
>
> A useful strategic trend should be supported by persistent, multi-source Evidence that indicates meaningful change in behavior, technology, economics, regulation, customer expectations or market structure.
