# Mianx.ai Research Lab Knowledge Transfer — Internal Training

> **Training is a capability-development mechanism, not an authority-generation mechanism.**
>
> The preferred flow is:
>
> ```text
> VALIDATED
> RESEARCH
> KNOWLEDGE
>
> ↓
>
> COMPETENCY
> REQUIREMENT
>
> ↓
>
> LEARNING
> OBJECTIVE
>
> ↓
>
> TRAINING
> CONTENT
>
> ↓
>
> GUIDED
> PRACTICE
>
> ↓
>
> ASSESSMENT
>
> ↓
>
> COMPETENCY
> EVIDENCE
>
> ↓
>
> SUPERVISED
> APPLICATION
>
> ↓
>
> CONTINUOUS
> LEARNING /
> REVALIDATION
> ```
>
> without silently turning training completion into permission to operate high-risk Research or Production systems.

# 234. Negative Verification Scenarios

Containment, correction, retraining or governance escalation should occur when:

* researcher completes onboarding and immediately receives unrestricted high-risk Research access.
* employee watches video and system records advanced competency without assessment.
* quiz score alone is used to grant Production Tool access.
* internal Research certification is presented externally as regulated professional accreditation.
* training slide contradicts current Research policy but remains active.
* training material removes uncertainty from preliminary Research finding.
* outdated Model/provider guidance remains in curriculum after material platform change.
* researcher is trained to use Dataset A and assumes same Dataset can be used in every Project.
* training lab uses raw Tenant Data even though synthetic data would suffice.
* real API credentials are embedded in Tool-use exercise.
* learner passes statistics quiz but cannot distinguish correlation from causation in practical case.
* one successful sandbox Experiment is accepted as Production Experiment competence.
* employee takes a course about fine-tuning and documentation records that the employee "trained the Model."
* Prompt update is incorrectly represented as Model fine-tuning.
* Agent instruction update is recorded as Human Researcher training.
* Human completes Agent operations module and is given Agent governance authority automatically.
* Agent memory containing stale policy is used in training and presented as current governance.
* training explains a Security exploit to broad audience without classification or need-to-know controls.
* Responsible AI training completion is used to skip actual high-impact system review.
* compliance training is used as substitute for qualified legal review.
* IP training is treated as approval to publish patent-sensitive Research.
* Project A customer configuration appears in general internal training and becomes visible across Projects.
* Tenant A incident case exposes identifiable Tenant information to Tenant B team.
* AI grades its own generated Research task and passes learner without independent quality control.
* curriculum has 100% pass rate because assessment is trivial, yet dashboard reports excellent competency.
* learner dissatisfaction is interpreted as proof course ineffective despite strong practical outcomes.
* course satisfaction is interpreted as competence despite poor practical performance.
* stale curriculum is re-rendered with new date and dashboard reports content current.
* corrected training slide is published but affected learners are not retrained.
* training is halted in LMS but downloaded copies remain active and no reconciliation occurs.
* controlled training Pilot succeeds and Production access automation is enabled without separate authorization.
