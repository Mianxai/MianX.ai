# 208. Negative Verification Scenarios

Containment, correction, reprioritization or escalation should occur when:

* Priority is marked approved because it received the highest score.
* high-priority Research automatically receives budget without valid authority.
* a Founder idea is described as empirically validated because it came from the Founder.
* a customer asks for a feature and Research marks the market need proven.
* a competitor launches an Agent feature and Mianx.ai immediately prioritizes copying it without strategic analysis.
* new Model is trending publicly and becomes top priority without decision relevance.
* large TAM is treated as proof Research will create high enterprise value.
* an urgent customer issue displaces foundational security Research without portfolio review.
* high strategic fit hides the fact that required Data rights do not exist.
* Research is ranked numerically even though key inputs are `UNKNOWN`, and Unknown is treated as zero.
* priority receives an `8.937` score from subjective inputs and is represented as scientifically precise.
* score weights are silently changed to favor a preferred project.
* impact is inflated and cost understated to move a proposal upward.
* a priority with a critical Tenant-isolation hard gate proceeds because its composite score is high.
* Project A priority is treated as enterprise-wide without evidence.
* Tenant-specific need is used to justify cross-Tenant Data access.
* Core platform need is duplicated separately in multiple Industry OS programs without considering shared Research.
* domain-specific compliance need is incorrectly forced into generic Core architecture without domain validation.
* all portfolio slots are consumed by short-term feature Research and foundational AI OS Research is starved.
* all portfolio slots are consumed by speculative future technology with no near-term decision value.
* a single vendor becomes dominant in every Research priority without concentration-risk review.
* old Research debt is prioritized solely because it is old.
* prototype succeeds and priority is automatically moved into Product build.
* priority is placed in `NOW` and team starts execution without authorization.
* priority is paused and is reported as failed.
* priority is accelerated and normal security controls are bypassed.
* Research program is killed and underlying Evidence is deleted.
* priority is routed to Founder and status is changed to Founder-approved without approval Evidence.
* Founder does not respond and system assumes approval.
* AI-generated portfolio summary invents budget approval or Product commitment.
* stale priority remains active after underlying architecture or market assumptions change.
* generated priority document is described as saved to repository without filesystem Evidence.
