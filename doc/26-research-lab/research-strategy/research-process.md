# 201. Negative Verification Scenarios

Containment, correction, invalidation, revalidation or escalation should occur when:

* Research begins because intake was accepted but authorization is missing.
* requester is treated as an approver without mandate.
* Research Question is written to prove a predetermined preferred solution.
* Project A Evidence is silently generalized to Project B.
* Tenant A Data is accessed during Tenant B Research.
* Data is available in storage and team assumes Research rights exist.
* old literature is treated as current Runtime Evidence.
* hypothesis is written into report as fact before execution.
* team changes Benchmark cases after seeing poor results and does not record the revision.
* Model version changes during Research without a new configuration identity.
* Prompt changes between runs but results are aggregated as one Prompt.
* Agent has a Tool available and uses it outside authorized scope.
* Multi-Agent agreement is treated as independent verification.
* Tool returns success and Research records side effect as verified without source-system Evidence.
* untrusted retrieved content tells the Agent it has elevated authority and the Agent obeys.
* sensitive Human participant Data is collected without applicable privacy controls.
* Research executes while a critical security hard gate is unresolved.
* Experiment runs many times but produces no meaningful uncertainty reduction and is reported as high productivity.
* Benchmark score is presented as real-world Product performance.
* simulation result is presented as Runtime evidence.
* Prototype success is treated as Product validation.
* raw observation and analyst interpretation are merged without distinction.
* missing Data is treated as zero.
* only positive runs are preserved.
* Counter-Evidence is removed from final analysis because it weakens preferred conclusion.
* negative result is marked Research failure rather than a valid outcome.
* statistical significance is presented as practical significance.
* global average hides a critical Tenant subgroup failure.
* Model self-critique is described as independent verification.
* Research author approves their own high-risk conclusion as independent review.
* review completion is treated as Founder approval.
* silence from Founder is interpreted as approval.
* validated Research is automatically transferred to Engineering.
* Engineering handoff is described as Production-ready implementation.
* Research enters Memory and becomes treated as permanently true despite superseding Evidence.
* publication candidate is published without publication authority.
* Patent candidate is described as Patent filed.
* HALT request is logged but Research continues and system reports it as halted.
* generated Research Process document is described as saved to repository without filesystem Evidence.
* controlled Research Process Pilot succeeds and Production control plane is claimed authorized.
