# Product Documentation

> **The Product module defines how Mianx.ai discovers, designs, builds, delivers, and continuously improves products throughout their lifecycle.**

---

# Purpose

The Product documentation establishes the product development framework for Mianx.ai.

It provides a consistent methodology for transforming ideas into enterprise-grade products through structured planning, architecture, engineering, validation, deployment, and continuous improvement.

Every product developed by Mianx.ai must follow this framework.

---

# Objectives

The Product module aims to:

- Standardize product development
- Define product planning processes
- Establish product documentation standards
- Align business and engineering
- Reduce development risk
- Build reusable product knowledge

---

# Scope

This module covers:

- Product Vision
- Product Requirements
- Product Roadmap
- Feature Specifications
- Product Lifecycle
- Release Planning
- Product Metrics
- Product Documentation Standards
- Future Product Portfolio

---

# Product Philosophy

Every product should solve a real business problem.

Products should be:

- Human-centered
- AI-powered
- Enterprise-ready
- Secure by design
- Modular
- Scalable
- Well documented
- Continuously improving

Every product should also contribute reusable knowledge back to the Mianx.ai platform.

---

# Product Lifecycle

Every product follows the same lifecycle.

```text
Idea
    ↓
Research
    ↓
Validation
    ↓
Planning
    ↓
Requirements
    ↓
Architecture
    ↓
Design
    ↓
Development
    ↓
Testing
    ↓
Deployment
    ↓
Operations
    ↓
Continuous Improvement
```

Each phase must be documented before moving to the next.

---

# Product Documentation Structure

Every product should include the following documentation:

| ID | Document | Purpose | Status |
|----|----------|---------|--------|
| PRD-001 | Product Requirements Document | Define product requirements | ⏳ Pending |
| PRD-002 | Product Roadmap | Define long-term roadmap | ⏳ Future |
| PRD-003 | Feature Specifications | Describe features in detail | ⏳ Future |
| PRD-004 | Product Lifecycle | Define development lifecycle | ⏳ Future |

Additional documents may be added as products become more complex.

---

# Product Success Criteria

Every product should aim to:

- Solve a measurable business problem
- Deliver value to users
- Be maintainable
- Scale efficiently
- Produce reusable components
- Improve organizational knowledge

Success is measured by outcomes rather than feature count.
