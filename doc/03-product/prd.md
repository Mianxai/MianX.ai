# Master Product Requirements Document (PRD)

> **This document defines the vision, scope, requirements, and success criteria for the Mianx.ai platform. It is the single source of truth for product development.**

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define exactly what Mianx.ai is, what problems it solves, who it serves, and how it will evolve over time.

Every product decision, engineering decision, and implementation should align with this document.

---

# Product Overview

Mianx.ai is an AI-native enterprise platform that enables businesses to build, operate, and scale using autonomous AI workforces under human leadership.

The platform combines enterprise software, AI agents, knowledge management, automation, and business operations into a unified ecosystem.

---

# Vision

Build the world's most trusted AI-native enterprise platform.

The platform should enable organizations to:

- Build software
- Operate businesses
- Automate workflows
- Manage AI teams
- Capture organizational knowledge
- Continuously improve through reusable intelligence

---

# Problem Statement

Modern businesses face common challenges:

- Fragmented software
- Repetitive manual work
- Knowledge loss
- Slow delivery cycles
- High operational costs
- Disconnected teams
- Difficult scaling

These problems reduce productivity and slow business growth.

---

# Solution

Mianx.ai provides one integrated platform that combines:

- Enterprise ERP
- AI Workforce
- AI Operating System
- Knowledge Management
- Automation Engine
- Project Delivery Framework
- Analytics & Reporting

Instead of using multiple disconnected tools, organizations work from one intelligent platform.

---

# Target Users

Primary Users

- Founders
- Startups
- SMEs
- Software Houses
- Enterprise Organizations

Secondary Users

- Developers
- Designers
- Product Managers
- Marketing Teams
- Operations Teams
- AI Engineers
- Consultants

---

# Product Goals

The platform should:

- Increase productivity
- Reduce operational costs
- Improve software quality
- Standardize workflows
- Preserve organizational knowledge
- Enable AI-driven execution
- Scale efficiently

---

# Core Platform Modules

The first version of Mianx.ai will include the following major modules.

## Enterprise ERP

Purpose:

Manage business operations.

Future modules include:

- CRM
- HRM
- Finance
- Sales
- Procurement
- Inventory
- Projects
- Assets
- Documents
- Reporting

---

## AI Workforce

Purpose:

Manage specialized AI departments.

Examples:

- Executive AI
- Product AI
- Engineering AI
- Marketing AI
- Sales AI
- Finance AI
- HR AI
- Customer Success AI

---

## AI Operating System

Purpose:

Coordinate communication and execution between AI agents.

Responsibilities:

- Task orchestration
- Context management
- AI memory
- Knowledge retrieval
- Workflow execution

---

## Knowledge Platform

Purpose:

Capture reusable organizational intelligence.

Knowledge includes:

- Documentation
- Architecture
- Templates
- Lessons Learned
- SOPs
- Client Patterns
- Engineering Standards

---

## Automation Platform

Purpose:

Automate repeatable business processes.

Examples:

- Project setup
- Client onboarding
- Documentation generation
- Deployment workflows
- Reporting
- Notifications

---

# Functional Requirements

The platform must support:

- User authentication
- Organization management
- Department management
- Project management
- AI workforce management
- Knowledge management
- Workflow automation
- Task management
- API integration
- Analytics dashboard
- Reporting
- Notifications

---

# Non-Functional Requirements

The platform must be:

- Secure
- Scalable
- Modular
- Reliable
- Performant
- Extensible
- Well documented
- Enterprise-grade

---

# Constraints

The platform should:

- Support modular development
- Minimize technical debt
- Follow documentation-first development
- Use reusable architecture
- Support future AI expansion

---

# Success Metrics

The platform is successful when:

- AI performs the majority of operational work.
- Human leadership focuses on strategy.
- Development becomes increasingly reusable.
- Documentation remains current.
- Customer satisfaction improves.
- Delivery time decreases.
- Knowledge continuously compounds.

---

# Out of Scope (Version 1)

The following are not part of the initial release:

- Public AI Marketplace
- Third-party plugin ecosystem
- Multi-region deployment
- White-label platform
- Industry-specific editions

These will be considered in future releases.

---

# Risks

Potential risks include:

- Scope expansion
- AI reliability
- Security threats
- Integration complexity
- Documentation maintenance
- Organizational change management

Mitigation plans will be documented separately.

---

# Assumptions

This PRD assumes:

- Documentation-first development
- Human oversight for strategic decisions
- Modular architecture
- Continuous delivery
- Continuous learning

---

# Dependencies

This product depends on:

- Governance
- Company Structure
- System Architecture
- AI Workforce
- Engineering Standards

---

# Acceptance Criteria

The product will meet this PRD when:

- All planned modules are implemented.
- Documentation is complete.
- AI departments operate successfully.
- Enterprise workflows are functional.
- Core business operations are supported.
- Quality standards are achieved.

Future releases may include:

- Enterprise Integrations
