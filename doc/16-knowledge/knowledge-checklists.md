# Knowledge Checklists

---

# Purpose

This document provides the operational checklists required to ensure the MIANX-AI Knowledge Platform is complete, secure, governed, AI-ready, and production-ready.

These checklists standardize verification activities before deployment, audits, AI integration, and enterprise releases.

---

# Objectives

The Knowledge Checklists ensure:

- Documentation completeness
- Knowledge quality
- Governance compliance
- AI readiness
- RAG readiness
- Security compliance
- Operational readiness
- Production readiness
- Continuous improvement
- Enterprise standardization

# Best Practices

- Complete every checklist before production.
- Automate checklist verification where possible.
- Review checklists regularly.
- Track unresolved items.
- Integrate checklist completion into CI/CD.
- Keep evidence for every completed item.
- Review governance periodically.
- Test disaster recovery regularly.
- Validate AI quality continuously.
- Improve checklists after every audit.

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Deploying without checklist completion.
- Skipping approvals.
- Ignoring failed validation.
- Missing audit evidence.
- Incomplete metadata.
- Unverified AI knowledge.
- Missing backups.
- Disabled monitoring.
- Ignoring security findings.
- Treating checklists as one-time activities.
